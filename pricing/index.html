Pricing questions

Straight Answers About Service Pricing

Are these guaranteed final prices?

No. They are published starting prices or standard rates designed to give clients a realistic entry point. Final pricing depends on the actual property and scope.

Does Bakerss provide free quotes?

Yes. For services requiring a custom scope, Bakerss provides a no-obligation quote or property assessment before work begins.

How much is an HVAC diagnostic?

A standard HVAC diagnostic starts at $99. Emergency or after-hours HVAC diagnostic service starts at $175. Repairs, parts, refrigerant and additional labor are separate.

How much are property maintenance plans?

Property Maintenance Plans start at $149 per month. Pricing varies by visit frequency, property size and included services.

Can I combine multiple Bakerss services?

Yes. Homeowners, second-home owners and managed properties can combine recurring services into a customized property-care plan.

Where does Bakerss provide service?

Bakerss serves Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Murrells Inlet, Garden City, Socastee, Conway, Carolina Forest, Pawleys Island, Litchfield Beach and surrounding areas in Horry and Georgetown Counties.
