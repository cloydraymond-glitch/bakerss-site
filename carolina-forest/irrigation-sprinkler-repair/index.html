Best-fit repair situations

When Carolina Forest Properties Should Request Irrigation Repair

Dry Spots or Patchy Grass

If turf is browning in patterns or certain areas stay dry, the problem may be sprinkler coverage, clogged heads, zone pressure or timing.

Soggy Areas or Standing Water

Wet areas may indicate leaks, broken heads, damaged lines, stuck valves or overscheduled irrigation zones.

Before Sod Installation

New sod needs working irrigation. Checking coverage before sod work helps protect the investment after installation.

After Mower or Landscape Damage

Sprinkler heads can be damaged during mowing, edging, bed work, landscaping, construction or vehicle traffic.
