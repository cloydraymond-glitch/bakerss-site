Coastal Exterior Cleaning

Why Myrtle Beach Properties Need More Than Basic Pressure Washing

Myrtle Beach is one of the toughest exterior-maintenance markets in the Southeast. Salt air settles on siding, windows, railings, concrete and paint. Humidity allows algae and mildew to grow fast. Pollen, sand, oak debris, pine straw, irrigation stains and storm residue collect on driveways, patios and walkways. A property can look clean in March and visibly dirty again by mid-summer.

That is why Bakerss does not treat every surface the same. Concrete, brick and some stone can handle controlled pressure. Vinyl siding, stucco, roofs, painted trim, screens and wood require soft washing. The goal is not to blast a surface; the goal is to clean it correctly, protect the material, remove organic growth at the source and leave the property looking sharp without creating damage.

For homeowners, this protects curb appeal and surface life. For vacation rentals, it protects the first impression guests see before they walk inside. For HOAs, apartments and commercial properties, it reduces complaints, slip hazards and the appearance of deferred maintenance.

Soft Wash vs Pressure Wash

The Right Technique for Every Surface

Low Pressure + Cleaning Solution

Soft Washing

Soft washing uses low pressure and professional cleaning solution to remove algae, mildew, mold, pollen and organic buildup from delicate exterior surfaces. It treats the cause of the stain instead of just pushing surface dirt around.

  • Vinyl siding and painted siding
  • Stucco, Hardie board and trim
  • Asphalt shingles and roof surfaces
  • Screen enclosures and porch ceilings
  • Wood decks and painted surfaces
  • Beach homes exposed to salt air
Controlled High Pressure

Pressure Washing

Pressure washing is used on durable surfaces where force is safe and effective. Concrete, brick, stone and pavers can hold dirt and algae deep in the surface texture.

  • Concrete driveways and sidewalks
  • Parking pads and curbs
  • Brick and masonry
  • Stone patios and pavers
  • Commercial sidewalks
  • Dumpster pads and service areas

Coastal Problems We Solve

Exterior Cleaning Built for Myrtle Beach Conditions

Salt Air

Coastal Film & Surface Staining

Ocean air carries salt that settles on siding, railings, concrete, windows and paint. Regular exterior cleaning helps reduce buildup that makes properties look older and accelerates wear.

Humidity

Algae, Mildew & Mold Growth

Warm humid conditions allow green algae and black mildew to grow quickly on shaded siding, driveways, patios, pool decks, stairs and walkways.

Pollen Season

Spring Yellow Film

Pine and oak pollen can coat every surface in Myrtle Beach. A seasonal wash removes pollen residue from siding, concrete, porches and outdoor living areas.

Storm Season

Sand, Dirt & Debris Lines

Heavy rain and tropical weather can leave dirt streaks, sand, mud splash and organic residue on homes, walkways and rental property exteriors.

Vacation Rentals

Guest-Ready First Impressions

Guests judge exterior cleanliness before they open the front door. Driveways, entryways, patios and pool decks should be clean before high-volume rental seasons.

Safety

Slip Hazard Reduction

Algae on concrete, stairs, pool decks and walkways can become slick. Cleaning high-traffic surfaces is a maintenance and safety issue, not only a curb appeal issue.

FAQ

Pressure Washing Questions — Answered

How much does pressure washing cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pressure washing starts at $99 for standard concrete cleaning. House soft washing usually starts around $149, roof soft washing starts around $299, and larger or heavily stained properties are quoted after a free estimate.

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution for siding, roofs and delicate surfaces. Pressure washing uses controlled higher pressure for durable surfaces like concrete, brick, stone and pavers.

Do you offer commercial pressure washing?

Yes. We clean storefronts, sidewalks, entrances, dumpster pads, building exteriors, apartment communities, clubhouses and HOA common areas.

Do you pressure wash apartment communities and HOAs?

Yes. Bakerss provides exterior cleaning for apartment communities, condominium properties, HOA common areas, clubhouse entries, pool decks, breezeways, sidewalks and managed properties throughout Horry County.

Can you pressure wash vinyl siding safely?

Yes, but we do not blast vinyl siding with high pressure. Vinyl siding should be soft washed to avoid water intrusion behind seams, damage to caulking and unnecessary surface wear.

How often should Myrtle Beach homes be washed?

Most homes benefit from annual house washing and driveway cleaning. Ocean-adjacent homes, shaded homes, vacation rentals and properties with heavy tree cover may need exterior cleaning twice per year.

Can you combine pressure washing with other Bakerss services?

Yes. Common combinations include gutter cleaning, lawn care, landscaping, pool maintenance, painting prep, Airbnb cleaning, sod installation, multi-family and commercial maintenance, and full property maintenance plans.
