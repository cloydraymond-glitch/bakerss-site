Coastal Exterior Cleaning

Why Myrtle Beach Properties Need More Than Basic Pressure Washing

Myrtle Beach properties face recurring coastal exterior buildup. Salt air settles on siding, windows, railings, concrete and paint. Humidity allows algae and mildew to grow fast. Pollen, sand, oak debris, pine straw, irrigation stains and storm residue collect on driveways, patios and walkways. A property can look clean in March and visibly dirty again by mid-summer.

That is why Bakerss does not treat every surface the same. Concrete, brick and some stone can handle controlled pressure. Vinyl siding, stucco, roofs, painted trim, screens and wood need a surface-specific review and may require lower-pressure cleaning. The goal is not to blast a surface; the goal is to clean it correctly, protect the material, treat organic buildup and improve the property’s appearance while accounting for surface condition and manufacturer guidance.

For homeowners, this protects curb appeal and surface life. For vacation rentals, it protects the first impression guests see before they walk inside. For HOAs, apartments and commercial properties, it reduces complaints, slip hazards and the appearance of deferred maintenance.

Soft Wash vs Pressure Wash

The Right Technique for Every Surface

Low Pressure + Cleaning Solution

Soft Washing

Soft washing uses low pressure and professional cleaning solution to remove algae, mildew, mold, pollen and organic buildup from delicate exterior surfaces. It treats the cause of the stain instead of just pushing surface dirt around.

  • Vinyl siding and painted siding
  • Stucco, Hardie board and trim
  • Asphalt shingles and roof surfaces
  • Screen enclosures and porch ceilings
  • Wood decks and painted surfaces
  • Beach homes exposed to salt air
Controlled High Pressure

Pressure Washing

Pressure washing is used on durable surfaces where force is safe and effective. Concrete, brick, stone and pavers can hold dirt and algae deep in the surface texture.

  • Concrete driveways and sidewalks
  • Parking pads and curbs
  • Brick and masonry
  • Stone patios and pavers
  • Commercial sidewalks
  • Dumpster pads and service areas

What homeowners ask before booking

Know What to Expect Before Exterior Cleaning

The right scope, safe methods and realistic expectations matter more than using the strongest pressure. Here is what to discuss before scheduling exterior cleaning around a Myrtle Beach home or commercial property.

Will pressure washing damage siding, paint or plants?

Direct high pressure can harm painted, weathered or delicate surfaces. We review materials and visible condition before recommending washing methods. Tell us about fragile plants, irrigation controls, loose paint, aging caulk and sensitive fixtures so protection and appropriate rinsing can be planned. Pre-existing damage cannot be repaired by washing.

Do I need to be home or provide water?

Access and water arrangements are confirmed before the visit. Let us know about gates, pets, outdoor outlets, hose bibs, parking, tenant notices and HOA restrictions. We will confirm whether an owner or property representative needs to be present.

How long will my pressure washing take?

Duration depends on size, surface type, buildup, accessibility and whether specialty stains need treatment. A small concrete area and a full house soft wash require different preparation and cleaning time. Share photos and your scheduling deadline when requesting an estimate.

Can you remove rust, oil, irrigation stains or oxidation?

Some stains require specialty products or may be permanent. Oxidized, chalky siding and deteriorated coatings can react differently from ordinary dirt or algae. We evaluate the surface and explain where additional treatment may be appropriate; complete removal is not guaranteed.

How should I prepare before the crew arrives?

Move vehicles, outdoor cushions and loose decorations from the agreed areas, close windows, secure pets and share gate instructions. Report electrical concerns, water intrusion, damaged siding, unsealed openings and plantings that need particular care.

What is included—and what costs extra?

Your written proposal identifies the areas being washed, the cleaning method and any specialty treatment. Heavy rust or oil staining, elevated work, unusual access, roof cleaning, and extra surfaces can require separate approval. We discuss scope before work begins.

Pressure Washing Questions — Answered

How much does pressure washing cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pricing depends on surface area, material, buildup, access and specialty stains. Our current advertised starting prices include standard driveway cleaning from $99 and house soft washing from $149, subject to a property-specific written estimate.

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing relies on lower pressure and surface-appropriate cleaning solutions for more delicate materials. Pressure washing uses controlled higher pressure on suitable hard surfaces such as many concrete driveways and sidewalks.

Can you clean green algae and mildew from siding?

We assess siding material, condition and buildup before selecting a cleaning method. Organic growth often responds to appropriate soft-wash treatment, although pre-existing discoloration or damage may remain.

Can you remove oil or rust stains from a driveway?

Oil, rust and irrigation stains may need specialty treatment beyond a standard wash. Share photos for a quote; complete stain removal cannot be guaranteed.

Do you clean commercial sidewalks and dumpster pads?

Yes. Bakerss quotes storefront approaches, sidewalks, dumpster areas, breezeways, HOA common areas and apartment or commercial exterior cleaning, including recurring service plans.

How often should a Myrtle Beach house be washed?

Many properties benefit from an annual inspection and cleaning as needed. Shaded or ocean-exposed homes and busy rentals may require more frequent attention depending on surface condition.

Is pressure washing safe for vinyl siding or roofs?

Direct high-pressure washing can damage some siding and roofing materials. We assess surface condition and manufacturer guidance and use appropriate low-pressure methods when suitable. Roof cleaning is quoted subject to access and surface review.

Can pressure washing be scheduled before painting or a rental arrival?

Yes. Tell us your painting, listing photography or guest-arrival deadline when requesting a quote. Scheduling is subject to availability and the time needed for surfaces to dry.

Do I need to be home for pressure washing?

Not necessarily. Property access, water availability, gates, pets and whether a responsible contact must be present are confirmed before scheduling.

How long does pressure washing take?

Timing varies based on square footage, buildup, surface condition, access and specialty treatments. Share photos and any time-sensitive deadlines for an estimate.

Do you use my water supply?

Water and access requirements are discussed before work begins. Confirm available outdoor spigots and any property restrictions when requesting your quote.

Will house washing harm my plants or paint?

Cleaning methods depend on surface condition. We review sensitive landscaping, fragile paint, fixtures and other concerns before recommending a method. Pre-existing damage and permanent discoloration may remain.

Can pressure washing remove oxidation from vinyl siding?

Oxidation is different from ordinary dirt or algae and can require specialized methods. We evaluate the siding and explain any additional treatment or limitations before quoting.

Do you offer recurring pressure washing for HOAs and commercial properties?

Yes. Bakerss can discuss recurring exterior cleaning for shared walkways, entrances, breezeways, pool surrounds, dumpster pads and other agreed areas, subject to scope and availability.
