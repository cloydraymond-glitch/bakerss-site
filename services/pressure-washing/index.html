Why It Matters Here

Coastal Exteriors Get Dirty Faster — and Dirtier

The same climate that makes the Grand Strand worth living in works against every exterior surface you own. Humidity that rarely drops feeds algae on shaded siding. Salt air carries a film that dulls color and grips dirt. Live oaks and pines drip tannins and pollen across driveways ten months a year. Left alone, that growth doesn't just look bad — mildew degrades paint and siding, algae makes concrete slick, and a grimy exterior quietly drags down both curb appeal and appraisal photos.

An annual professional wash is the cheapest exterior maintenance a coastal home can buy. For rentals, it's even simpler math: listings with clean exteriors photograph better, and guests judge the property before they reach the door.

Questions

Pressure Washing FAQ

How much does pressure washing cost in Myrtle Beach?

Driveways from $99, house washing from $199, soft washing from $199. Final pricing is by square footage and condition — free estimates, and bundled services on one visit earn package pricing.

Pressure washing vs. soft washing — which do I need?

Concrete, brick, and stone take high pressure. Siding, roofs, screens, stucco, and anything painted get soft washed — low pressure with solutions that kill the growth instead of blasting at it. Most coastal homes need both, which is why we price them to bundle.

How often should a Grand Strand home be washed?

Annually for most homes; shaded or tree-heavy lots and rentals with high listing standards often go every 6–9 months. Coastal growth returns faster than inland — waiting until it's visibly green means it's been feeding on your paint for months.

Is it safe for my plants, windows, and paint?

Yes — protection is step two of every job. Plants get pre-watered and rinsed, sensitive surfaces get the soft-wash method, and pressure never touches anything that can't take it.

Do you handle commercial washing?

Storefronts, sidewalks, parking lots, dumpster pads, and building exteriors — one-time or on rotation. It can also fold into a commercial maintenance retainer with parking lot cleaning included.
