Why It Matters Here

Coastal SC Properties Need Consistent Exterior Cleaning

Myrtle Beach's subtropical coastal climate is one of the most demanding environments for exterior surfaces on the East Coast. Salt air accelerates mildew, algae, and surface staining on siding, driveways, patios, and decks faster than any inland market. High summer humidity creates visible algae and black mold growth on exterior surfaces within weeks. Fifty-plus inches of annual rainfall deposits pollen, organic debris, and storm dirt on every horizontal and vertical surface of your property.

Left uncleaned, these deposits don't just affect curb appeal — they accelerate surface degradation, shorten paint life, void roofing warranties, and create slip hazards on walkways and decks. Annual professional pressure washing is the single most cost-effective maintenance service a Myrtle Beach property owner can schedule. We use professional-grade equipment and the right technique for every surface — soft wash for delicate areas like siding and roofs, high-pressure for concrete and masonry where force is safe and effective.

Soft Wash vs. Pressure Wash

The Right Technique for Every Surface

💧 Soft Washing

Low pressure (under 500 PSI) combined with a biodegradable surfactant that kills algae, mold, and mildew at the source rather than just blasting it off the surface. More effective long-term — treats the biology, not just the stain.

Used on:

  • Roof shingles
  • Vinyl, stucco, and painted siding
  • Wood decking
  • Painted surfaces
  • Screen enclosures

🔫 High-Pressure Washing

High pressure (1,500–4,000+ PSI) for hard, durable surfaces where force is safe and effective. Removes embedded dirt, oil stains, tire marks, and tough grime that soft washing alone can't address.

Used on:

  • Concrete driveways & sidewalks
  • Brick and stone masonry
  • Pavers
  • Concrete patios
  • Parking lots

Pressure Washing Questions — Answered

How much does pressure washing cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pressure washing in Myrtle Beach starts at $99. Final pricing depends on the surface type, square footage, and condition. Driveways, house washes, and roofs are all priced differently. Free estimates always provided before any commitment.

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure and a biodegradable cleaning solution to remove algae, mold, and mildew — used on roofs, siding, and delicate surfaces. High-pressure washing uses stronger force for hard surfaces like concrete driveways and stone patios. We use the right technique for every surface.

How often should I pressure wash my home in Myrtle Beach?

In coastal SC, most homeowners benefit from an annual house wash and driveway cleaning. The salt air, humidity, and pollen here accelerate algae and mildew growth compared to inland areas. After storm season is a particularly good time to schedule. Vacation rentals often benefit from twice-yearly exterior cleaning.

Can you pressure wash vinyl siding without damaging it?

Yes. We use soft wash technique on vinyl siding — low pressure with professional-grade cleaning solution that removes buildup safely without risking damage to seams, caulking, or paint. High pressure on vinyl can cause water intrusion behind siding — we never do it.

Do you pressure wash vacation rentals and Airbnb properties?

Yes. We work with many STR owners and property managers across Horry County. Exterior cleaning can be coordinated between guest stays or scheduled seasonally. A clean exterior is part of the first impression every guest gets before they walk in the door.

Are you insured for pressure washing in South Carolina?

Do you offer commercial pressure washing in Myrtle Beach?

Yes — storefronts, sidewalks, parking lots, building exteriors, and dumpster pads. Commercial pressure washing is available as a one-time service or as part of a monthly commercial maintenance retainer. Free quotes for all commercial properties.
