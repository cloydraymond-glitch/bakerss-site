The Coastal Cleaning Challenge

Why Myrtle Beach Exterior Surfaces Need More Frequent Cleaning

The Grand Strand's coastal environment creates a perfect storm of surface-degrading conditions that don't exist in most markets. Salt particles carried inland from the Atlantic deposit on every exterior surface — siding, concrete, wood, metal, and painted finishes. These microscopic salt crystals are hygroscopic: they pull moisture out of the air and hold it against your surfaces continuously. Combined with Myrtle Beach's subtropical humidity, this creates the ideal growth environment for the mildew, algae, and organic staining that turn a well-maintained home into something that looks neglected within a single season.

Then there's the rain. Myrtle Beach receives over 50 inches of rainfall annually — 32% more than the national average. Heavy rainfall doesn't clean exterior surfaces; it accelerates the growth of whatever biological life is already establishing itself. Algae blooms faster in wet conditions. Tannin staining from fallen leaves penetrates deeper into concrete. And every rain event adds to the organic film building up on your home's siding.

Professional pressure washing and soft washing — done at the right frequency with the right technique for each surface — is the single most cost-effective maintenance investment for a Myrtle Beach property. It preserves paint, extends concrete life, prevents mildew from becoming structural, and maintains the curb appeal and guest first impression that drive vacation rental revenue.

Salt Air Exposure

The Coastal Accumulation Problem

Salt particles deposit on every surface facing the ocean. The closer to the water, the more aggressive the accumulation. Even inland Myrtle Beach neighborhoods receive measurable salt deposition from prevailing easterly winds. Annual washing removes the buildup before it causes long-term damage to paint, wood, and metal.

Mildew & Algae

The Subtropical Growth Machine

Myrtle Beach's combination of heat, humidity, and moisture creates ideal conditions for biological growth on all exterior surfaces. Mildew appears on siding within weeks of washing without treatment. Green algae colonizes driveways and pool decks. Black algae stains concrete so deeply that standard washing won't remove it. We use the right chemistry for each situation.

UV Degradation

Sun & Surface Damage

South Carolina's year-round sun intensity breaks down paint film chemistry faster than northern climates. Surfaces with salt and biological growth accumulation degrade even faster — the combination accelerates oxidation and chalking. Clean surfaces repel UV damage more effectively than contaminated ones.

The Most Important Distinction

Soft Washing vs Pressure Washing — Which Surface Gets Which

The single most important technical decision in exterior cleaning is matching the method to the surface. Using high pressure on the wrong surface doesn't just produce inferior results — it causes damage that can cost thousands to repair. Water forced behind vinyl siding at 3,000 PSI creates moisture intrusion that leads to mold inside your walls. High pressure on painted wood strips the finish and drives water into the wood grain. Pressure washing asphalt shingles strips granules that prematurely age your roof.

Bakerss uses two distinct approaches — and we apply each only to the surfaces they're designed for.

High-Pressure Washing — 1,500–4,000 PSI

For Hard, Durable Surfaces

  • Concrete driveways and parking areas
  • Brick and concrete block retaining walls
  • Concrete walkways, patios, and pool decks
  • Metal fences and railings
  • Brick exterior (lower pressure, careful technique)

These surfaces handle the mechanical force needed to remove embedded staining. The pressure does the work.

Soft Washing — Under 500 PSI + Chemistry

For Delicate or Painted Surfaces

  • Vinyl siding — any and all colors
  • Wood siding and painted wood surfaces
  • Fiber cement (HardiePlank) siding
  • Stucco and EIFS exterior systems
  • Roofing materials (shingles, metal)
  • Screened enclosures and pool cages
  • Outdoor furniture and cushions

Cleaning chemistry kills mildew organisms at the root. Results last significantly longer than pressure rinsing alone.

Why soft washing produces longer-lasting results: High-pressure washing removes visible mildew and algae from the surface — but leaves the organisms' roots (mycelium) behind. They regrow within weeks. Soft washing's cleaning chemistry kills the organisms entirely, including the root structure. A properly soft-washed surface stays cleaner significantly longer than a pressure-rinsed surface.

Common Questions

Pressure Washing FAQ

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?+
Pressure washing uses high water pressure (1,500–4,000 PSI) to mechanically remove staining from hard, durable surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure (under 500 PSI) combined with cleaning chemistry — surfactants, mildewcides, and algaecides — that kill biological growth at the root rather than just blasting it off. Soft washing produces results that last significantly longer because it addresses the organism, not just the visible staining.
How much does pressure washing cost in Myrtle Beach?+
Driveway cleaning starts at $99. House washing (soft wash) starts at $199 for single-story homes. Deck cleaning starts at $150. A full exterior package — driveway, house, and deck in one visit — typically runs $500–$950 depending on home size. All estimates are free and provided before any work begins.
Can pressure washing damage my siding?+
High-pressure washing applied to vinyl siding, painted wood, stucco, or fiber cement will cause damage — forcing water behind the siding, stripping paint, and creating moisture intrusion problems. Bakerss soft washes all siding surfaces at under 500 PSI. We never apply high pressure to materials that can't handle it. This is the most common mistake made by inexperienced washing services.
How often should my Myrtle Beach home be pressure washed?+
For most Myrtle Beach residential homes, annual exterior washing is the appropriate standard. Oceanfront and ICW-adjacent properties benefit from twice-yearly service due to elevated salt and humidity exposure. Vacation rental properties benefit from pre-season washing before photos and booking season begins. Heavily shaded properties under pine or oak coverage may need twice-yearly driveway cleaning.
Do you pressure wash roofs?+
We soft wash roofs — we never use high pressure on roofing materials. Asphalt shingles lose granules under high pressure, prematurely aging the roof by years. Low-pressure soft washing with appropriate chemistry removes black algae streaks (Gloeocapsa magma) and moss effectively without any granule loss or warranty issues.
Do you wash vacation rental properties in Myrtle Beach?+
Yes — vacation rental exterior washing is one of our most active services in Myrtle Beach and Horry County. We coordinate with your rental calendar to schedule service during vacancy windows. Pre-season washing dramatically improves listing photo quality, which directly drives bookings during Myrtle Beach's competitive summer season.
