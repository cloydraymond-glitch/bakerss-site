Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does exterior painting cost in Myrtle Beach?+
Exterior painting in Myrtle Beach varies based on home size, number of stories, surface condition, and materials. A standard single-story home typically runs $2,500–$5,000. Two-story homes run $4,000–$9,000+. We provide free, detailed estimates based on your specific property. We also factor in the coastal-grade materials needed to withstand Myrtle Beach's salt air and humidity.
What kind of paint do you use for coastal homes?+
For coastal South Carolina properties, we use premium exterior paints with mildewcide additives and high humidity and UV resistance ratings. Sherwin-Williams Emerald or Duration lines are among our go-to exterior products for coastal applications. These cost more upfront but deliver significantly longer life between repaint cycles.
How often does a coastal home need to be repainted?+
In Myrtle Beach's coastal environment, exterior paint typically needs refreshing every 5–8 years, though oceanfront or direct salt-air-exposure properties may need attention sooner. Using the right primer and premium paint products, and applying them correctly, significantly extends the repaint cycle.
Do you paint vacation rental properties?+
Yes. Vacation rental painting is a specialty — we understand the turnaround pressures and can schedule paint jobs between rental seasons or in tight windows between bookings. A fresh interior paint job can materially improve your listing photos and guest reviews.
Can you match existing paint colors?+
Yes. We can color-match existing paint using spectrophotometer matching at the paint store. If you have the original paint brand and color name, matching is straightforward. We can also help you select new colors if you're ready for an update.
Do you handle drywall repair before painting?+
Yes. Minor drywall repair, caulking, filling nail holes, and surface prep are included in our painting process. If you have significant drywall damage, we can assess and quote the repair work as part of the overall painting project.
Can you paint commercial buildings in Myrtle Beach?+
Yes. We paint commercial interiors and exteriors including offices, retail spaces, restaurants, and warehouses. We schedule commercial painting work around your business hours to minimize disruption.
Do you offer deck and fence staining?+
Yes. Staining and sealing wood decks, fences, and pergolas is a common add-on to exterior painting projects. Proper staining protects coastal wood from moisture intrusion, UV damage, and the accelerated weathering that comes with salt air exposure.
