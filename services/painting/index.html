Painting in Myrtle Beach Requires More Than Paint

Painting a coastal property is not the same as painting an inland home. Myrtle Beach properties deal with salt air, high humidity, heavy summer UV exposure, wind-driven rain, mildew growth, storm season and constant wear from guests, renters, residents and visitors. Those conditions punish shortcuts.

A quality paint job starts before the first coat goes on the wall. Exterior surfaces may need soft washing, scraping, sanding, caulking, spot priming and minor repairs. Interior surfaces may need drywall patching, trim repair, nail-pop repair, stain blocking, caulk lines and careful protection of flooring, furniture and fixtures.

Bakerss approaches painting as part of property maintenance. The goal is not just a fresh color on installation day. The goal is a cleaner, sharper, more durable finish that protects the property, improves presentation and supports the property's long-term value.

Exterior Painting Process

Our Exterior Painting Process for Coastal Properties

Exterior painting fails early when prep is rushed. Bakerss builds every exterior painting project around surface condition, moisture, caulk failure, mildew, salt residue and long-term protection.

1

Inspect

Review siding, trim, caulk lines, wood damage, chalking, mildew, peeling, access and surface condition.

2

Wash

Soft wash or clean surfaces to remove dirt, salt, algae, mildew and chalk before coating.

3

Repair

Address minor trim issues, failing caulk, cracks, holes, loose material and areas needing prep.

4

Prime

Spot-prime bare wood, repairs, stains or surfaces that need adhesion and coverage support.

5

Paint

Apply finish coats using an appropriate product and application method for the surface.

6

Review

Final cleanup, walkthrough, touchups and confirmation that the scope was completed.

Vacation Rental Painting

Painting for Airbnb, VRBO & Vacation Rental Properties

Vacation rentals in Myrtle Beach take more interior wear than standard residential homes. Suitcases hit corners, furniture rubs walls, guests scuff hallways, bathrooms hold humidity and high-turnover properties accumulate small marks quickly.

Fresh paint directly affects listing photos, guest perception and review confidence. A rental that looks bright, clean and maintained is easier to market than one with scuffed walls, stained trim, faded doors or patchy touchups.

Bakerss can schedule vacation rental painting around guest calendars, offseason windows, maintenance blocks and turnover schedules. Painting can also be combined with Airbnb cleaning, handyman repairs, pressure washing and property maintenance plans.

How much does painting cost in Myrtle Beach?

Painting is priced by scope, surface condition, prep work, number of rooms, number of colors, trim detail, number of stories and product selection. Bakerss provides free estimates so the price matches the actual property rather than a generic online range.

Do you offer both interior and exterior painting?

Yes. Bakerss provides interior painting, exterior painting, trim painting, door painting, ceiling painting, deck staining, fence staining and painting for residential, rental, commercial and managed properties.

Do you paint vacation rental properties?

Yes. Vacation rental refresh painting is available for Airbnb, VRBO, beach houses, condos and short-term rental properties. Work can be scheduled around guest calendars and offseason maintenance windows.

Do you repair drywall before painting?

Yes. Minor drywall patches, nail pops, dents, scuffs, cracks, caulking and surface prep can be included before painting. Larger repairs can be reviewed during the estimate.

Do you pressure wash before exterior painting?

Exterior surfaces often need washing or soft washing before painting to remove salt, mildew, algae, chalking and dirt. Proper prep helps the new coating bond better and last longer.

Yes. Painting is often paired with handyman repairs, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, landscaping, interior cleaning, Airbnb cleaning and full property maintenance plans.
