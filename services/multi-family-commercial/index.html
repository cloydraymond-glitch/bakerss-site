Are you an apartment maintenance company in Myrtle Beach?

Do you carry insurance for commercial maintenance work?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance LLC is fully licensed and insured in South Carolina. General liability and worker's compensation documentation is available on request. We issue certificates of insurance naming your management company, HOA or ownership entity as additionally insured when required by your vendor compliance program.

Do you provide DHEC-compliant pool maintenance for HOAs and apartments?

Yes. South Carolina DHEC requires HOA and apartment community pools to maintain documented chemical testing logs and inspection records. Bakerss provides fully DHEC-compliant pool maintenance with chemical testing, water balance documentation, required log records and equipment inspection on every visit. This protects your board or management company from compliance liability during inspections.

Do you offer net-30 invoicing for commercial accounts?

Yes. Net-30 invoicing is available for approved commercial accounts including property management companies, HOA boards and apartment operators. We provide itemized billing per property and per work order to support your AP workflow and property accounting systems.

Can you maintain multiple HOA communities or apartment properties?

Yes. Bakerss provides multi-property scheduling and coordinated service routes for property management companies overseeing multiple HOA communities or apartment properties across Myrtle Beach and Horry County. Consolidated or per-property invoicing available depending on your accounting preference.

What HOA communities do you serve in Myrtle Beach?

Bakerss serves HOA communities throughout Horry County including Grande Dunes, Plantation Lakes, Berkshire Forest, Waterbridge, The Farm at Carolina Forest, Covington Lake, Indigo Bay, Barefoot Resort, Prince Creek, Blackmoor, International Club, Arcadian Shores and Carolina Waterway Plantation. We also serve HOA common areas in Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Murrells Inlet, Conway and all cities in Horry County.
