Frequently asked questions

Commercial & Multi-Family Maintenance FAQ

Do you provide HOA maintenance in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. Bakerss supports HOA communities with grounds maintenance, cleaning, pool service, pressure washing and other recurring property needs. See our HOA maintenance section.

Do you provide apartment maintenance in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. Bakerss supports apartment communities with grounds maintenance, common-area cleaning, pool service, work orders and recurring maintenance. See our apartment maintenance section.

Can you provide insurance documentation for commercial accounts?

Yes. Commercial insurance and certificate-of-insurance documentation can be provided when required for vendor onboarding or property-management compliance.

Can Bakerss handle multiple properties for one management company?

Yes, subject to route capacity and scope. We can coordinate recurring service across multiple managed properties and structure invoicing around the account's needs.

Do you offer recurring commercial maintenance agreements?

Yes. Recurring maintenance scopes can combine compatible services such as grounds, cleaning, pool service, exterior care and general work-order support.

How does service documentation work?

For supported accounts, Bakerss can provide technician notes, job photos, visit timing and other service documentation through its client-facing workflow and portal.
