Why It Matters

What Happens When Gutters Clog

Gutters protect your home's foundation, siding, and landscaping. When they fail, the damage is costly.

01

Foundation Damage

Overflow water pools at your foundation and causes erosion, cracking, and basement water intrusion over time.

02

Fascia & Soffit Rot

Standing water in gutters saturates wood fascia boards and soffits, leading to rot that spreads to roof decking.

03

Landscape Erosion

Uncontrolled overflow dumps concentrated water onto landscaping beds, washing out mulch and plant material.

04

Pest Infestation

Standing water and decomposing debris in gutters attract mosquitoes, carpenter ants, and other pests to your roofline.
