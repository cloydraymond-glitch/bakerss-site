Why It Matters Here

Horry County Gutters Fill Faster Than Most Markets

Myrtle Beach and Horry County combine pine trees, live oaks, Spanish moss, pollen, humidity, tropical storms and heavy rainfall into one demanding gutter-maintenance environment. Gutters do not only clog in fall. Pine needles, oak tassels, roof granules, pollen mats and storm debris can pack gutters throughout the year.

When gutters stop draining, water backs up into fascia boards, spills into soffits, washes out landscape beds, stains siding, erodes mulch, creates mosquito habitat and dumps water directly beside the foundation. For coastal homes, rentals, HOA communities and managed properties, gutter cleaning is not cosmetic. It is preventive property maintenance.

Conway, Socastee, Forestbrook and Wild Wing properties under tree canopy may need more frequent service. Coastal Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach and Garden City properties benefit from post-storm gutter checks after significant wind or tropical weather.

The Damage Clogged Gutters Create

Water Damage

Fascia & Soffit Rot

Standing water saturates fascia and soffit materials, leading to rot, paint failure, roof-edge damage and costly repairs.

🏠

Overflowing gutters dump water beside the structure, increasing erosion, settlement risk and moisture problems.

🌱
Curb Appeal

Landscape Washout

Clogged gutters release concentrated water into beds, washing out mulch, pine straw, rock, plants and edging lines.

🦟
Pests

Mosquitoes & Insects

Standing water and decomposing debris create habitat for mosquitoes, ants, wasps and other pests near the roofline.

FAQ

Gutter Cleaning Questions — Answered

How much does gutter cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?

Gutter cleaning starts at $150 for standard single-story homes. Two-story homes commonly range from $200 to $300, and large or complex rooflines can range from $250 to $400 or more depending on access, height, linear footage and debris load.

How often should gutters be cleaned in Horry County?

Most homes benefit from gutter cleaning twice per year. Properties under heavy pine or oak coverage may need three to four visits annually, especially in Conway, Socastee, Forestbrook and wooded communities.

Do you flush downspouts?

Yes. Downspout flushing and flow testing are included so water drains properly through the system instead of backing up and overflowing at the roofline.

Do you clean gutters for HOAs, apartments and commercial properties?

Yes. Bakerss provides gutter cleaning and exterior maintenance for HOA communities, condominiums, apartment communities, commercial buildings, clubhouses and property management accounts.

Do hurricanes affect gutter maintenance?

Yes. Tropical storms can fill gutters with leaves, sticks, Spanish moss, roof granules and wind-blown debris. Post-storm gutter cleaning is recommended after significant weather events.

Do you offer gutter brightening?

Yes. Gutter brightening is available as an add-on to clean the exterior face of gutters where black streaks, oxidation and staining affect curb appeal.

Can gutter cleaning be combined with other Bakerss services?

Yes. Common combinations include pressure washing, roof soft washing, lawn care, landscaping, pool maintenance, handyman repairs, sod installation and recurring property maintenance.
