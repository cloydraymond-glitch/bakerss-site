The Service

What Every Gutter Cleaning Includes

Gutter cleaning in Myrtle Beach isn't optional maintenance — it's structural protection. The Grand Strand's subtropical climate produces over 50 inches of rainfall annually. When that rain hits a clogged gutter, the water has nowhere to go except over the edge and directly against your home's structure. Fascia rot, foundation water pooling, and landscape erosion are the entirely preventable results.

1

Complete Debris Removal

All pine needles, oak leaves, seed pods, Spanish moss, and organic material removed from gutters by hand and blower. We don't just flush — we remove debris first so it doesn't block downspouts on the way out.

2

Downspout Flushing — Every Single One

Every downspout is flushed with water to confirm it's fully clear and draining correctly. A gutter with clean troughs but a blocked downspout still causes water damage. We verify complete drainage before we leave.

3

Inspection — While We're Up There

We inspect gutter condition during every cleaning — checking for sagging sections, improper pitch, separated joints, holes, and fascia board damage. We note anything found and report it to you in writing on request.

4

Minor Repairs (If Needed)

Many minor gutter issues — loose hangers, open end caps, separated seams — can be addressed during the same visit. We quote any repair work before doing it. No surprise charges.

Why More Often Here

Why Myrtle Beach Gutters Need Cleaning More Frequently

The national recommendation of twice-yearly gutter cleaning assumes a moderate climate with one heavy leaf-drop season. Myrtle Beach's environment is fundamentally different — and many Horry County properties need more frequent service than the national standard suggests.

Pine Trees

Year-Round Needle Fall

Longleaf and loblolly pines dominate Horry County's tree canopy. Unlike deciduous trees, they drop needles continuously — not seasonally. Properties in Carolina Forest, Forestbrook, and Conway under heavy pine coverage can fill gutters in 6–8 weeks.

Live Oaks

Near-Continuous Debris

Live oaks shed leaves, twigs, acorns, and Spanish moss almost year-round. Murrells Inlet's Prince Creek corridor and Wachesaw Plantation are particularly affected — we recommend 3–4 cleanings annually for these properties.

Hurricane Season

June 1 – November 30

A single tropical storm deposits months of debris in hours. Pre-hurricane season cleaning (May) ensures gutters function correctly when a major rain event arrives. Post-storm cleaning should be immediate.

The Forestbrook rule: Homes in Forestbrook (Socastee) under the densest pine and oak coverage need gutter cleaning 3–4 times annually. Quarterly service prevents the continuous pine needle accumulation from ever creating dangerous blockages.

Warning Signs

Signs Your Gutters Need Immediate Attention

  • Water overflowing over the front lip during rain
  • Plants or moss visibly growing from gutter troughs
  • Gutters sagging or pulling away from fascia
  • Black or brown streaking on siding below gutters
  • Water pooling at the foundation after rain
  • Soft or discolored fascia boards (early rot)
  • Last cleaning was more than 6 months ago
  • Visible debris load from the ground

FAQ

Gutter Cleaning Questions

How often should Myrtle Beach gutters be cleaned?+
Twice yearly (spring and fall) is the minimum for most Horry County properties. Homes under heavy pine or live oak coverage — particularly in Forestbrook, Carolina Forest, and Murrells Inlet — benefit from 3–4 cleanings annually due to continuous debris fall. Post-storm cleaning is recommended after any significant tropical weather event.
Does the service include downspout cleaning?+
Yes, always. Every gutter cleaning visit includes flushing every downspout and confirming it drains completely. A gutter with clear troughs but a blocked downspout still causes water damage — flushing is not optional in our service.
Can you respond after a storm?+
Yes. Post-storm gutter cleaning is an emergency service we provide throughout Horry County. Call or text 843-467-7136 any time — we answer 24/7 and respond as quickly as possible to storm-related requests.
Do gutter guards eliminate the need for cleaning?+
No. The best gutter guard products (micro-mesh systems) can reduce cleaning frequency from 3–4 times per year to 1–2 times per year in Myrtle Beach. They don't eliminate it. Pine needles and granules from aging shingles pass through most guard systems. Foam and brush inserts typically make the situation worse in our subtropical climate.
