Why It Matters Here

Horry County Gutters Fill Faster Than Most of the Country

Myrtle Beach and Horry County's tree canopy, pollen load, and rainfall combine to create one of the most demanding gutter maintenance environments on the East Coast. Longleaf pines and live oaks shed year-round — not just in fall. Pollen season in spring deposits a thick layer of pine pollen across every horizontal surface including gutter troughs. Spanish moss falls from oaks and packs gutters completely without visible warning. And Horry County's 50+ inches of annual rainfall loads every clogged gutter at full force, driving overflow water toward foundations, fascia, and landscaping beds.

Conway and Socastee properties under heavy mature canopy often need 3–4 cleanings annually. Coastal Myrtle Beach and North Myrtle Beach properties benefit from post-storm cleaning after any significant weather event. Wild Wing Plantation, Barefoot Resort, and other golf communities need consistent gutter maintenance to protect the property values their HOA standards demand.

What Clogged Gutters Cause

The Damage That Clogged Gutters Create

01

Foundation Damage

Overflow water pools at your foundation and causes erosion, cracking, and basement water intrusion that costs far more to repair than regular gutter cleaning.

02

Fascia & Soffit Rot

Standing water in gutters saturates wood fascia boards and soffits, leading to rot that spreads to roof decking and triggers expensive structural repairs.

03

Landscape Erosion

Uncontrolled overflow dumps concentrated water onto landscaping beds, washing out mulch, plant material, and concrete curbing in a single storm event.

04

Pest Infestation

Standing water and decomposing debris in gutters attract mosquitoes, carpenter ants, wasps, and other pests to your roofline and exterior walls.

FAQ

Gutter Cleaning Questions — Answered

How often should gutters be cleaned in Myrtle Beach?

Most Horry County homes benefit from gutter cleaning twice a year — late spring after pollen season and late fall after leaves drop. Homes under heavy pine or oak coverage (Wild Wing, Forestbrook, Socastee) may need 3–4 cleanings annually. After any major hurricane or tropical storm, post-storm cleaning is recommended regardless of your regular schedule.

How much does gutter cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?

Starting at $150 for standard single-story homes. Two-story homes and larger properties run $200–$400 depending on linear footage and canopy debris load. Free estimates for all Horry County properties before any commitment.

Do you flush the downspouts too?

Yes — every gutter cleaning includes downspout flushing to ensure water flows completely to the ground. Downspout blockages are the most common cause of localized overflow damage and are always addressed as part of the standard service.

Do hurricanes affect gutter maintenance?

Yes. Hurricanes and tropical storms deposit heavy debris — leaves, sticks, Spanish moss, and roof granules — directly into gutters. After any significant storm, we recommend scheduling a post-storm gutter cleaning to prevent the debris load from causing overflow damage during subsequent rainfall.

Do you clean gutters on multi-story homes?

Yes. We clean gutters on single and multi-story homes throughout Horry County. Our team has the equipment and safety approach to work at height safely on all standard residential properties.

Do you offer gutter brightening?

Yes — as an add-on. Gutter brightening cleans the exterior face of gutters to remove black streaks, algae, and oxidation that make gutters look dirty from the street. Runs $75–$125 depending on gutter linear footage and condition.
