What Is Property Maintenance?

Property maintenance is the ongoing care, inspection, repair and preservation of a home, vacation rental, commercial building, HOA common area or managed property. It is different from waiting until something breaks. A strong property maintenance plan keeps the property clean, safe, functional, attractive and ready for owners, guests, tenants, residents or customers.

In Myrtle Beach and Horry County, property maintenance matters because coastal properties deal with humidity, salt air, heavy tourism traffic, storms, fast-growing turf, seasonal rental turnover and HOA presentation standards. Lawn growth, mildew, clogged gutters, pool conditions, exterior grime, minor leaks, loose fixtures and neglected landscaping can become expensive quickly when nobody is watching.

Bakerss Property Services brings multiple services into one organized plan. Instead of separately coordinating lawn care, landscaping, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, pool maintenance, cleaning and handyman repairs, property owners can work with one local company that understands the full property.
