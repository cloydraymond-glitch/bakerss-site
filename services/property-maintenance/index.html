FAQ

Maintenance Plan FAQ

How much do property maintenance plans cost in Myrtle Beach?

Plans range from $150/month for basic residential service to $1,200/month for full-service active vacation rentals. Every plan is custom-quoted after a property consultation — we build the plan around what your property actually needs, not a generic package.

Is there a long-term contract?

Month-to-month plans are available. Annual plans receive a discount. There is no penalty for cancellation with reasonable notice. We earn retention by delivering results, not by contract terms.

Can an out-of-state owner use a maintenance plan?

Yes — this is one of our core use cases. We serve hundreds of out-of-state Horry County property owners through maintenance plans. We operate on schedule, send service reports after visits, and notify you immediately about any issues we find.

What is included in an STR maintenance plan?

Every STR plan is custom-built, but common components include: turnover cleaning dispatched automatically from your rental calendar, biweekly lawn care April–October, twice-yearly exterior washing, twice-yearly gutter cleaning, seasonal deep cleaning, and a handyman hour allocation for between-stay repairs.
