Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do property maintenance plans cost in Myrtle Beach?+
Monthly plan pricing depends entirely on the services included and the frequency of each. A basic lawn care + quarterly pressure washing plan for a standard residential property typically runs $150–$350/month. A full STR maintenance plan including weekly cleaning, lawn care, and seasonal services runs $400–$900/month depending on property size and cleaning frequency. We build every plan custom.
What services can be included in a plan?+
Any combination of: lawn care (weekly/biweekly), interior cleaning, Airbnb/STR turnover cleaning, pressure washing (monthly/quarterly/seasonal), gutter cleaning (twice yearly), painting, handyman hours, landscaping maintenance, junk removal, and property inspection visits. We include only what your property actually needs.
Is there a contract required?+
We offer month-to-month plans for flexibility. Annual plans are available at a discount for clients who want price stability. No long-term lock-in required — most clients stay because of the value and reliability, not contracts.
Can a plan include vacation rental turnover cleaning?+
Yes. Many of our most popular plans are built around STR turnover cleaning with supporting services like lawn care and seasonal pressure washing. We sync with your rental calendar so turnovers happen automatically.
Do you offer property maintenance plans for out-of-state owners?+
Yes — this is one of our most requested plan types. Out-of-state owners get a local team that handles everything: cleaning, lawn care, maintenance, and a monthly property check-in with photos. You never have to call us to remind us — we show up on schedule.
Can you manage a Myrtle Beach vacation rental property completely?+
We can handle all physical property maintenance — cleaning, lawn care, exterior maintenance, and repairs. For full property management including booking, guest communication, and revenue management, we can provide referrals to property management companies we work with. Our services integrate with any management system.
Do you offer HOA maintenance plans?+
Yes. We build custom maintenance plans for HOA communities covering common area landscaping, community pressure washing, gutter cleaning, and other shared maintenance needs. Contact us to discuss your community's requirements.
How do I get started with a property maintenance plan?+
Call or text 843-467-7136 any time. We'll schedule a property walkthrough, discuss your needs, and have a written plan proposal to you within 48 hours. No obligation until you approve the plan.
