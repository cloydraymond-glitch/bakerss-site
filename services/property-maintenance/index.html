Frequently Asked Questions

Property Maintenance Plans: Frequently Asked Questions

What does a property maintenance company do?

A full-service property maintenance company coordinates recurring upkeep and approved repairs, such as cleaning, grounds, HVAC service, pool care, exterior maintenance and handyman tasks. The exact services depend on the written agreement.

How much does property maintenance cost in Myrtle Beach?

Costs vary by property size, condition, visit frequency, systems and selected services. Bakerss provides property-specific proposals rather than a single price that may not fit the work.

What is included in a monthly or annual maintenance plan?

Plans may bundle scheduled checks, cleaning, grounds, HVAC preventive maintenance, pool service and authorized repairs. The scope, frequency, materials and approval limits are documented in the agreement.

Can one company handle lawn, pool, HVAC and cleaning?

Yes. Bakerss coordinates multiple property-service categories through one local relationship, with appropriately qualified personnel assigned to specialized work.

Is a home maintenance plan worth it?

A recurring plan may reduce the time spent coordinating vendors and make preventive tasks easier to schedule and document. Its value depends on your property needs and the scope selected.

How is property maintenance different from property management?

Property maintenance focuses on physical upkeep, repairs and service coordination. Property management may include leasing, rent collection, tenant administration and other functions not included in a standard maintenance plan.

Do you work with HOAs, apartments and commercial properties?

Yes. Bakerss supports managed communities and commercial properties with recurring services, common-area care and documented work orders. Contracts are tailored to each site.

Can you care for my second home while I am away?

Yes. Second-home plans can include scheduled visual home-watch checks, photographs, owner reporting, cleaning, exterior care and approved maintenance coordination.

How do you maintain a coastal property?

Focus on HVAC and humidity, drainage, exposed exterior hardware, salt-air wear, landscaping and storm preparation. A schedule should reflect the home’s condition and exposure.

How often should HVAC, lawn and pool services be scheduled?

Schedules depend on equipment manufacturer guidance, property use, seasonal growth, water conditions and applicable requirements. Bakerss can help develop an appropriate cadence.

Are your technicians trained, certified and licensed?

Our team includes experienced, trained and certified technicians. Where regulated work requires a particular trade or business license, assignments must comply with applicable South Carolina requirements.

Do you provide one invoice and service documentation?

Bakerss can coordinate work orders and provide service documentation through its operations systems. Consolidated billing and invoicing terms depend on the written service agreement.

How do I choose a property maintenance company?

Review the company’s service scope, insurance, applicable credentials, communication process, work-order documentation, references and written agreement.

Do you offer free property assessments?

Contact Bakerss to discuss an initial property walkthrough or assessment. We will confirm scheduling and whether any site-specific assessment fee applies before proceeding.
