Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about this service.

What is included in a Bakerss property maintenance plan?

Plans are customized by property and can combine approved recurring services such as cleaning, lawn and grounds, HVAC preventive maintenance, pool care, property checks and repair support.

Do you provide property maintenance for HOAs and apartment communities?

Yes. Bakerss supports HOAs, apartment communities and other managed properties with recurring maintenance, grounds, common-area cleaning, pool-area support and work orders.

Can Bakerss maintain a second home when the owner is away?

Yes. Property-maintenance plans can include scheduled home-watch visits, documented observations and coordination of approved maintenance services.

Do clients receive service documentation?

Recurring service can include technician notes, service photographs, work-order updates and service history through Bakerss' client-service systems.

What areas do you serve?

Bakerss serves Myrtle Beach and surrounding Grand Strand communities across Horry and Georgetown Counties.
