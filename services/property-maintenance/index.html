Service questions

Property Maintenance Services FAQ

Can Bakerss handle multiple services at the same property?

Yes. Depending on the property and approved scope, Bakerss can coordinate recurring maintenance across HVAC, grounds, cleaning, pool, home watch, preventive maintenance and repair work orders.

Do you offer HVAC preventive maintenance and air duct cleaning?

Yes. Bakerss provides HVAC diagnostics and repair, preventive-maintenance programs, recurring filter support and air duct cleaning for residential, rental and managed properties.

Can clients submit work orders through the Bakerss Client Portal?

Yes. Clients can submit requests and review live work-order status, technician activity, field notes, job photos, proposals or next steps, and service history.

Do you provide commercial, apartment and HOA maintenance?

Yes. Bakerss provides property-maintenance support for commercial sites, apartment communities, HOA-managed properties and property managers, with recurring scopes and documented work-order workflows.

Does Bakerss have skilled trade capability?

Bakerss has HVAC technicians, Certified Pool Operators, plumbers, electricians and general property-maintenance capability. Work requiring a particular trade license, permit or regulated credential is performed or coordinated through appropriately qualified personnel.

Service availability varies by property, location and scope. HOA restrictions, permits and trade licensing may apply to particular work. Bakerss will route the request through the appropriate service path after reviewing the property and requested scope.

Need One Service—or a Complete Property Maintenance Plan?

Send the property address, property type, service priorities and photos when available. Bakerss can quote one-time work or build a recurring maintenance program around the property.
