What’s Included in a Vacation Rental Turnover?

Standard guest-to-guest cleaning

  • Kitchen surfaces, sink, exterior appliance wipe-down and visible spills
  • Bathroom fixtures, mirrors, showers, toilets and floors
  • Bedroom surfaces, bed setup and guest-ready presentation
  • Vacuuming, mopping, accessible floor edges and visible sand
  • Trash removal, high-touch surfaces and final walkthrough

Optional services and special conditions

  • Owner-supplied linen changes, laundry coordination and bed inventory
  • Consumable restocking and supply purchasing by agreement
  • Patios, balconies, appliance interiors and detailed deep cleaning
  • Pet hair, heavy mess, odor or post-party cleaning assessed separately
  • Documented property maintenance concerns for owner approval
Each property receives an agreed scope. Availability for rush, same-day and weekend turnovers is confirmed in advance—not guaranteed for every reservation.

How Much Does Airbnb Turnover Cleaning Cost?

A vacation rental turnover is not the same service as a standard occupied-home clean. We quote each Airbnb, VRBO and short-term rental property against its guest-ready checklist and booking schedule, so you understand the cleaning price before service begins.

Property and cleaning scope

Bedroom/bath count, square footage, sleep capacity, kitchens, balconies, pools or outdoor areas, and the expected guest-to-guest condition.

Turnover logistics

Guest checkout/check-in windows, same-day requests, weekend dates, access procedures, parking and travel requirements.

Linen and guest supplies

Linen changes, on-site or off-site laundry, restocking, consumables, deep-clean add-ons and documentation requirements.

Clear distinction: Residential house-cleaning starting rates apply to the house cleaning price table, not automatically to short-term rental turnovers. Availability and final turnover rates are confirmed after a property review. Any additional services are agreed upon separately.

Frequently Asked Cleaning Questions

How do I switch Airbnb cleaning companies without disrupting guest bookings?

Send the property address, current checkout and check-in times, upcoming reservations, access details and any existing cleaning checklist. We review the workflow, establish responsibilities and confirm available dates before scheduling the first turnover.

Can you complete same-day or Saturday-to-Saturday turnovers?

Same-day and weekend turnovers may be available depending on property size, laundry requirements, travel time, staffing and the available checkout-to-check-in window. We confirm availability before accepting work.

What is included in vacation rental turnover cleaning?

The agreed checklist typically covers kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, floors, trash removal, high-touch surfaces, bed setup and a final guest-ready review. Laundry, outdoor areas, supply purchasing and extensive cleaning are scoped separately as needed.

Do you clean Airbnb, VRBO and privately managed beach rentals?

Yes. We discuss cleaning for Airbnb, VRBO, direct-booked vacation rentals, oceanfront condos and privately managed short-term rentals throughout the service area.

Can I get photos and damage reports after a guest checks out?

Photo documentation and visible maintenance or damage observations can be included in your service plan through Bakerss OS. This is not a formal damage investigation or comprehensive inspection.

Will you handle linens, laundry and restocking?

Bed making, linen exchanges and restocking from owner-supplied inventory can be included in an agreed checklist. Off-site laundry, purchased supplies and special inventory work are quoted as needed.

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pricing depends on bedrooms, bathrooms, condition, bedding, laundry logistics, property access, turnover frequency and requested turnaround windows. We provide a property-specific quote rather than promising a universal flat rate. See what affects your turnover cleaning quote.

Can you clean several vacation rentals for one owner or manager?

We can discuss multi-property scheduling and recurring service agreements, with capacity, travel, access and quality-control expectations confirmed during onboarding.

Do you handle post-guest maintenance problems?

Bakerss also offers separate property maintenance services. If a cleaner observes an issue such as a leaking fixture or damaged door hardware, the team can document it and help coordinate an approved maintenance request.

Do you provide deep cleaning and seasonal vacation-rental cleaning?

Pre-season, end-of-season, mid-stay and periodic deep cleans can be quoted separately from standard turnover cleaning.

Can you sync directly with Airbnb or VRBO calendars?

We can work from owner-provided reservations and scheduling information. Automated calendar synchronization is not represented as a standard feature; the specific scheduling workflow is confirmed during onboarding.

Which Grand Strand areas do you serve?

We discuss service in Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Murrells Inlet, Conway and surrounding communities, subject to location and scheduling capacity.

Are your residential house-cleaning prices the same as vacation rental turnover prices?

No. Residential cleaning starting prices cover a different scope. Vacation rental turnover rates are quoted by property size, guest turnover window, linen and laundry requirements, consumables and other checklist items.

Can your cleaners handle back-to-back reservations?

We assess the checkout-to-check-in window, property size, laundry needs and confirmed crew capacity before accepting back-to-back work. No same-day service is guaranteed until the schedule is approved.

What happens if a guest leaves an unusually dirty property?

Visible excess cleaning, stains, damage or unusual conditions can be documented and reported to the owner or manager. Additional cleaning or repairs require an agreed scope and price.
