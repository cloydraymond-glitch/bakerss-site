The Market Reality

Why Vacation Rental Cleaning in Myrtle Beach Is Different

The Myrtle Beach vacation rental market is one of the most competitive short-term rental environments in the eastern United States. With over 30,000 active listings on Airbnb and VRBO across the Grand Strand, the difference between a property that fills its calendar and one that struggles to convert browsers isn't usually the amenities or the location — it's the review score. And the review score lives and dies on the guest's cleanliness experience in the first five minutes after checkout.

Beach properties compound the standard cleaning challenge. Myrtle Beach guests bring sand into every surface of the property — it's in the bedding, inside the couch cushion covers, in the kitchen drawers, under the beds, in bathroom drains. Standard hotel-style cleaning doesn't address sand the way beach property cleaning must. Our cleaning protocol includes systematic sand removal at every step, because sand left behind is the #1 complaint in Myrtle Beach vacation rental reviews.

Then there's the scheduling reality. Peak summer season in Myrtle Beach often means back-to-back Saturday-to-Saturday bookings, same-day checkouts and check-ins, and no margin for error in the turnover window. A cleaning team that doesn't show up on time — or shows up but misses the microwave — doesn't just create a bad guest review. It creates a review that suppresses your listing's search visibility for months and costs you bookings worth multiples of what the cleaning fee saves.

The 4.8-star threshold: Airbnb's algorithm rewards properties with consistently high ratings with significantly more search visibility. The difference in impressions between a 4.6 and a 4.9 property in Myrtle Beach's competitive market can mean 25–40% more bookings at equivalent pricing. Professional, consistent cleaning is the single most reliable path to 4.9 cleanliness scores.
What's Included

The Bakerss Vacation Rental Turnover — Every Room, Every Time

Our vacation rental turnover cleaning covers every room systematically — not a speed-clean designed to look done before it's finished. Below is what guests find when they walk into a Bakerss-cleaned property.

Kitchen — Zero Tolerance

The Room Reviews Are Written About

Microwave interior cleaned completely — the single most-cited item in Myrtle Beach STR cleanliness reviews. Stovetop and burners, refrigerator interior cleared and wiped, all dishes washed and properly stored, counters cleared and sanitized, sink shined. Nothing left from previous guests, nothing sticky, nothing with a smell.

Bathrooms — Sanitized

Hotel Standard Every Turnover

Toilets scrubbed inside and out including base and behind. Shower and tub scrubbed including grout lines. Sink, faucet, and fixtures wiped and dried. Mirror streak-free. All towels replaced with fresh folded sets. Paper goods checked and restocked. Floor mopped. Nothing left from previous guests.

Bedrooms — Guest-Ready

Fresh Linens, Made Properly

All bedding stripped and replaced with fresh linens — properly made, tight corners, smooth presentation. Mattress checked for any damage or staining. All surfaces dusted including lamps, nightstands, and ceiling fans. Closets checked and cleared of any guest items left behind. Floors vacuumed thoroughly.

🏖️ Sand Protocol

Beach-Specific — Standard Everywhere

Couch cushions removed and vacuumed on all sides. Decorative pillows shaken out. Under furniture vacuumed (sand settles to floor edges). Bedroom floors vacuumed before mopping — sand scratches if mopped first. Drawer and storage areas checked. Entry and patio swept completely. This step doesn't exist in most non-beach cleaning protocols — it's essential in ours.

Living Areas

Thoroughly Cleaned & Staged

All surfaces dusted including remotes and high-touch items. Entertainment area cleaned. Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout. Trash emptied in all rooms. Paper goods checked — toilet paper, paper towels, trash bags. Final staging so the property looks welcoming and photographable when the next guest arrives.

Pre-Departure Check

Walk-Through Before We Leave

Every turnover ends with a walk-through to confirm every room meets the standard before we lock up. We also note and photograph any damage or issues for owner documentation — so you know about problems before your guest discovers them. Nothing is assumed, everything is verified.

FAQ

Vacation Rental Cleaning FAQ

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?+
Vacation rental turnover cleaning in Myrtle Beach starts at $100 for studio and 1BR units. Standard 3BR beach homes run $185–$275. Larger 4BR+ homes run $240–$500+. All pricing includes the full turnover — kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, and the beach-specific sand removal protocol. Free estimates for your specific property.
Can you sync with my Airbnb or VRBO calendar?+
Yes. We can receive calendar access directly or work from any shared calendar format. After initial setup, cleaning is dispatched automatically on every checkout day — you don't coordinate with us for each booking. This is how the best Myrtle Beach STR operators manage their cleaning: set it up once, let it run.
Do you handle same-day turnovers during busy beach season?+
Yes. Same-day turnovers — when a guest checks out at 10 AM and another arrives at 4 PM — are our norm during Myrtle Beach's peak summer season, not the exception. We plan our crew scheduling specifically for the back-to-back patterns that peak season creates. We never treat a same-day turnaround as a problem — it's just the standard beach season schedule.
What's different about beach property cleaning?+
Sand. Myrtle Beach guests bring it into every room, every piece of furniture, every storage space. A cleaning team that doesn't specifically address sand — vacuuming cushions, checking drawers, sweeping entries, vacuuming floors before mopping so the mop doesn't push sand across the surface — leaves a beach property that smells clean but still has grit in the sheets. Our beach property protocol is built specifically around the sand reality.
Do you provide deep cleaning for vacation rentals?+
Yes. In addition to regular turnovers, we provide seasonal deep cleaning 3–4 times annually — before peak season (April/May), mid-season (July), end of season (September/October), and offseason (December/January). Deep cleans add inside appliances, baseboards, window tracks, ceiling fans, and grout work that standard turnovers don't include.
