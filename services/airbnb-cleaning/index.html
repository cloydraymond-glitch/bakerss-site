Why Cleaning Defines STR Revenue

In Myrtle Beach, Turnover Cleaning Protects the Review Score

Short-term rental cleaning is not the same as normal house cleaning. A vacation rental clean is time-sensitive, guest-facing and review-driven. Guests notice floors, bathrooms, bedding, kitchen surfaces, odors, sand, hair, trash, patios, entryways and small presentation details before they notice almost anything else.

Myrtle Beach rentals create extra cleaning pressure because of sand, humidity, sunscreen, pool traffic, beach towels, outdoor living areas and compressed checkout-to-check-in windows. A property can look “mostly clean” and still receive a poor cleanliness review if floors feel gritty, bathrooms are rushed or the entry area looks neglected.

Bakerss focuses on guest-ready presentation and property-owner communication. That includes turnover cleaning, visible issue reporting and the ability to coordinate related services such as lawn care, pool maintenance, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, handyman repairs and exterior property maintenance.

Airbnb & Vacation Rental Cleaning Questions

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?

Airbnb cleaning starts around $100 for studio and one-bedroom units. Two-bedroom turnovers commonly range from $145 to $210, three-bedroom turnovers commonly range from $185 to $275, and larger four-bedroom or five-bedroom properties are quoted based on size and scope.

Do you clean VRBO and privately managed vacation rentals?

Yes. Bakerss cleans Airbnb, VRBO, direct-booked rentals, privately managed beach homes, condos and short-term rental properties throughout Horry County.

Do you handle same-day back-to-back turnovers?

Yes. Same-day turnovers are available based on property size, timing, location and schedule. Peak-season turns require strong access instructions and a clear scope.

What is your sand removal protocol?

Our sand removal protocol focuses on entry areas, floors, rugs, beds, cushions, bathrooms, sliders, patios, drawers and high-traffic guest zones where sand commonly remains after standard cleaning.

Can you report damage or maintenance issues?

Yes. Visible damage, missing items, excessive mess and maintenance concerns can be reported so owners or property managers can respond quickly.

Can Airbnb cleaning be combined with other Bakerss services?

Yes. Airbnb cleaning is often paired with lawn care, pool maintenance, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, handyman repairs, landscaping, sod installation and full property maintenance plans.
