Why Cleaning Defines STR Revenue

In Myrtle Beach's Competitive STR Market, Cleaning is Your Most Valuable Service

The Myrtle Beach vacation rental market has over 30,000 active listings competing for the same booking windows. Review score is the most powerful ranking and conversion signal. Airbnb's algorithm rewards properties above 4.8 stars with substantially increased search visibility — and penalizes properties below that threshold with reduced exposure that compounds over time. The number-one driver of vacation rental cleanliness scores is the turnover clean.

Our protocol includes systematic sand removal at every step.

STR Cleaning FAQ

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?

Vacation rental turnover cleaning starts at $100 for studio and 1BR units. Standard 3BR beach homes run $185–$275. Larger 4BR+ properties run $240–$500+. All pricing includes the full beach sand protocol, damage walk-through, and calendar-synced dispatch. Free estimates for your specific property.

Can you sync with my Airbnb or VRBO calendar?

Yes. We receive calendar access via shared iCal link. After setup, cleaning is dispatched automatically on every checkout day without any coordination from you.

Do you handle same-day back-to-back turnovers?

Yes. Same-day turnovers — checkout at 10 AM, new guest arriving at 4 PM — are our standard operating model during Myrtle Beach's peak summer season, not a special case.

What is the beach sand protocol?

Cushions removed and vacuumed on all sides, floors vacuumed before mopping, drawers and storage areas checked, outdoor entries swept completely. Properties cleaned without this protocol leave grit in the bedding and sand on floors — generating the cleanliness reviews that suppress ranking.
