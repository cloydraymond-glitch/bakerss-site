Why Cleaning Defines STR Revenue

In Myrtle Beach, Turnover Cleaning Protects the Review Score

Short-term rental cleaning is not the same as normal house cleaning. A vacation rental clean is time-sensitive, guest-facing and review-driven. Guests notice floors, bathrooms, bedding, kitchen surfaces, odors, sand, hair, trash, patios, entryways and small presentation details before they notice almost anything else.

Myrtle Beach rentals create extra cleaning pressure because of sand, humidity, sunscreen, pool traffic, beach towels, outdoor living areas and compressed checkout-to-check-in windows. A property can look “mostly clean” and still receive a poor cleanliness review if floors feel gritty, bathrooms are rushed or the entry area looks neglected.

Bakerss focuses on guest-ready presentation and property-owner communication. That includes turnover cleaning, visible issue reporting and the ability to coordinate related services such as lawn care, pool maintenance, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, handyman repairs and exterior property maintenance.

Turnover Checklist

What Is Included in an Airbnb Turnover Cleaning?

The final checklist is property-specific. Standard turnover priorities and optional services are separated to prevent surprises on a busy check-in day.

Kitchen

Guest-Ready Kitchen

Clean counters, sink, stovetop, appliance exteriors, visible food residue, dishes as agreed and trash; appliance interiors are quoted when needed.

Bathrooms

Sanitize & Reset

Clean toilets, showers, tubs, sinks, mirrors, fixtures and floors; restock approved consumables from owner-provided inventory.

Bedrooms

Bed & Linen Changeover

Make beds with clean available linens, reset guest-facing surfaces and inspect for obvious damage or left-behind items.

Living Areas

Sand, Floors & Surfaces

Vacuum and mop appropriate surfaces, address beach sand, dust, hair and high-touch points, and reset furniture.

Final Check

Entry, Trash & Presentation

Remove agreed trash, check visible windows and sliders, and review the agreed guest-ready checklist before reporting completion.

Optional

Laundry, Restocking & Extras

On-site/off-site laundry, inventory counts, patio or balcony cleaning, carpet extraction and deep cleaning can be quoted separately.

FAQ

Airbnb & Vacation Rental Cleaning Questions

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?

The original published guide lists $100–$145 for a studio or one-bedroom turnover, $145–$210 for two bedrooms and $185–$275 for three bedrooms. Actual pricing depends on bathrooms, condition, linens, supplies and timing; request a property-specific quote.

What is included in an Airbnb turnover cleaning?

A typical agreed checklist covers bathrooms, kitchen surfaces, bedrooms, bed setup, floors, trash removal, high-touch areas and a final presentation check. Linen laundry, supply purchasing, appliance interiors and patios may be separately quoted.

Do you clean VRBO and privately managed short-term rentals?

Yes. We discuss turnovers for Airbnb, VRBO, direct-booked rentals, privately managed condos and beach homes.

Can you handle same-day back-to-back Airbnb turnovers?

Same-day turnovers may be scheduled when the property size, checkout-to-check-in window, staffing and access allow. Availability is confirmed before accepting a booking.

Do you offer Saturday-to-Saturday and weekend turnovers?

Weekend and peak-season turnovers can be requested. Booking ahead improves the chance of securing the needed time window.

Can you provide photos after each vacation rental clean?

Photo reporting and visible issue notes can be included in an agreed service plan. Confirm which rooms, views and reports your property requires.

Can you manage linens, laundry and bed making?

Bed setup and linen changes can be included. Laundry logistics, owner-provided linens, off-site laundry and replacement sets are discussed during quoting.

Do you restock toiletries, toilet paper and towels?

Restocking from available owner supplies can be included in a property checklist. Supply purchasing and inventory management are separate scope items unless agreed otherwise.

How long does an Airbnb turnover take?

Timing varies with property size, bathrooms, bed count, laundry needs and condition. We review the actual checkout and check-in times before confirming a schedule.

How do you clean sand out of a beach rental?

Our beach-focused checklist addresses floors, rugs, entryways, upholstery as agreed, bathroom areas and sliding-door thresholds where sand accumulates.

Do you report damage or missing items after guests leave?

Visible damage, unusual mess, missing items and maintenance concerns can be documented and sent to the owner or property manager. A turnover is not a comprehensive property inspection.

Can you sync cleaning with my Airbnb or VRBO calendar?

Share upcoming bookings or calendar information for scheduling. Automated iCal synchronization is not represented as a standard feature; confirm available workflow options.

Do you offer vacation rental deep cleaning and mid-stay cleaning?

Yes, these can be requested separately, including pre-season, end-of-season and mid-stay visits. Scope and availability are quoted for each property.

Do you handle pet hair, post-party cleaning and odors?

Additional pet hair, heavy soil and odor concerns can be assessed. Specialized remediation or significant damage cleanup may require a separate service.

Can you clean oceanfront condos and multi-unit rental portfolios?

Yes. We can discuss condo turnover routes and multi-property scheduling in Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach and other Grand Strand areas, subject to capacity.

Can cleaning be combined with vacation home watch or maintenance?

Yes. Bakerss can coordinate separate services including vacation home watch, handyman repairs, pool care, lawn maintenance and pressure washing.

Who pays the Airbnb cleaning fee: the host or guest?

The host contracts with the cleaning company. Whether the host charges a separate guest cleaning fee depends on the rental platform, listing and pricing decisions.

How do I get a vacation rental cleaning quote?

Send the property address, bedrooms and bathrooms, approximate square footage, checkout and check-in windows, linen expectations, supply needs and expected monthly turnover volume.
