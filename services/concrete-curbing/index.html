How It Works

Our Process

01

Design Consultation

We walk your property, discuss the design you want, recommend curbing style and color, and mark out the installation path before any concrete is poured.

02

Bed Preparation

Existing edges are cleaned up, soil is prepared, and any existing plastic or metal edging removed before installation.

03

Continuous Pour

Concrete is mixed and poured in a continuous, seamless run — no seams or joints that can crack or separate.

04

Shaping & Finishing

Our crew shapes and finishes the curbing to the specified profile while the concrete is workable — smooth curves, clean edges, consistent profile throughout.

05

Curing

Concrete cures for 24–48 hours. We provide care instructions for the first week to ensure maximum strength.

06

Final Inspection

We inspect the completed curbing, clean up the site, and walk the installation with you before considering the job complete.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does concrete landscaping curbing cost in Myrtle Beach?+
Concrete curbing is priced per linear foot. Standard curving border runs approximately $4–$7 per linear foot installed, depending on design complexity and accessibility. A typical front yard installation of 50–80 linear feet runs $200–$560. Full property installations are quoted as a project. We always provide a free on-site estimate.
How long does concrete curbing last in Myrtle Beach's coastal climate?+
Properly installed and sealed concrete curbing in coastal South Carolina typically lasts 15–30+ years. Concrete is not affected by salt air the way metal and plastic edging materials are — it's one of the most durable edging options available in coastal markets. We use concrete mixes appropriate for South Carolina's climate.
What is the difference between concrete curbing and plastic edging?+
Plastic and metal edging shifts, heaves, and deteriorates over time — especially in South Carolina's freeze-thaw (limited) and heat cycles. Concrete curbing is permanent — it's poured in place and doesn't move, shift, or degrade. It also provides a more substantial visual barrier between lawn and beds and eliminates the need for re-edging year after year.
Can concrete curbing prevent mulch from washing out?+
Yes. One of the primary benefits of concrete curbing over flush or shallow edging is that it creates a raised barrier that prevents mulch from washing out during heavy Myrtle Beach rainstorms. The curb keeps mulch in beds where it belongs.
What colors does concrete curbing come in?+
We offer a range of pigmented concrete colors including natural gray, tan, terracotta, red, brown, and charcoal. Colored curbing is an additional upcharge. We show color samples during the consultation so you can choose what complements your home's exterior.
How long does curbing installation take?+
Most residential curbing installations can be completed in 1–2 days. Large commercial or multi-area properties may take 2–3 days. The concrete needs 24–48 hours of curing time after installation before the beds can be replanted or mulched.
Do you do concrete curbing for HOA communities in Myrtle Beach?+
Yes. Concrete curbing is available for HOA common areas, entry features, and community landscapes throughout Horry County. We provide volume pricing for large community projects.
Can I plant in the beds right after curbing installation?+
We recommend waiting at least 48–72 hours after installation before heavy soil disturbance near the new curbing. After full curing, the beds can be planted and mulched normally.
