Frequently Asked Questions

Answers About Irrigation and Sprinkler Repair

What sprinkler problems do you repair?

We handle common issues such as broken heads, leaking fittings, non-working zones, stuck valves, poor coverage, overspray, controller settings and visible lateral-line leaks.

How much does irrigation repair cost?

Cost depends on diagnostic time, access, number of failed components, digging conditions and required parts. We provide findings and obtain approval before material repairs whenever practical.

Why is one sprinkler zone not working?

Possible causes include controller programming, wiring, a failed solenoid, closed valve, pressure loss, broken piping or clogged heads. A zone test is needed to isolate the problem.

Why does a zone keep running after the system shuts off?

A valve may be obstructed, damaged or failing to close. Water should be shut off if the zone is flooding until the valve can be inspected.

Can you adjust sprinklers that hit the house or street?

Yes. We can adjust arcs, replace nozzles, straighten heads and recommend layout corrections to reduce overspray.

Do you install smart irrigation controllers?

We can replace and program many standard and smart residential controllers when the existing system and electrical setup are suitable.

Do you repair irrigation after sod installation?

Yes. We can inspect coverage, raise buried heads, correct damaged components and help establish a temporary watering schedule for new sod.

How often should irrigation systems be checked?

A spring startup check and periodic inspection during the growing season are advisable. Systems should also be tested after landscaping, storms or unexplained water-use increases.

Do you service HOA and commercial irrigation?

We handle selected light-commercial, HOA and multifamily irrigation repairs. Large pump stations, wells and specialized two-wire systems may require a dedicated irrigation contractor.

Can you locate underground leaks?

We can investigate visible symptoms and trace many conventional lateral-line leaks. Deep mainline, well or highly complex leak detection may require specialized equipment.

Do you work on wells and irrigation pumps?

Basic system observations are possible, but major well, pump and electrical repairs may need a licensed specialist.

Can irrigation repair be bundled with lawn care?

Yes. Irrigation checks and repairs can be coordinated with lawn care, landscaping, sod installation and property maintenance services.
