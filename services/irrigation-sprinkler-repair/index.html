Topical Authority

Why Your Sprinklers Are Not Working

If your sprinkler head will not pop up, water may be blocked by debris, pressure may be low, or the head may be damaged or buried. If an entire zone fails, the problem may involve programming, wiring, a solenoid, a valve or supply pressure. A zone that keeps running may have a valve that cannot close.

Standing water, unexpectedly high water use and dry patches can indicate underground leaks, broken lateral piping or uneven spray coverage. Bakerss checks the observable symptoms and system operation before recommending a repair.

For new turf and landscape projects, irrigation repairs are often best coordinated before installation. Explore our sod installation, lawn care and landscaping services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers About Irrigation and Sprinkler Repair

How much does sprinkler repair cost in Myrtle Beach?

The cost depends on diagnostic time, failed parts, excavation, access and system complexity. A single head replacement usually requires less work than an underground line or valve repair. Request a property-specific estimate.

Why is my sprinkler system not working?

Common causes include controller settings, a power issue, a closed water supply, wiring, valves, damaged pipes or clogged sprinkler heads. A zone-by-zone test helps narrow the cause.

How do I know if I have a sprinkler leak?

Look for soggy spots, unexplained puddles, weak sprinkler pressure, water flowing when the system is off or a sudden increase in water usage.

What should I do if a sprinkler head is geysering?

Turn off the irrigation zone or irrigation water supply if safe, avoid the damaged area and request repair. Do not leave a major leak running.

Why does one sprinkler zone not turn on?

Possible causes include a programming issue, failed solenoid, damaged wiring, closed valve, inadequate pressure or a broken line.

Why does a sprinkler zone keep running?

A valve may be stuck open or unable to seal. Shut off the irrigation supply if flooding is occurring and arrange an inspection.

Can you replace a sprinkler head damaged by a mower?

Yes, conventional damaged heads can often be replaced, repositioned or adjusted after evaluating the connection and surrounding piping.

Can you fix sprinklers that spray my driveway or house?

We can assess nozzle selection, head alignment and spray arcs to reduce overspray and improve lawn coverage.

Do you repair irrigation controllers and smart timers?

We can troubleshoot settings and assess compatible controller replacement or programming. Electrical work outside our scope is referred to an appropriately qualified provider.

How often should irrigation systems be serviced?

A seasonal startup inspection and periodic checks during active watering are sensible, particularly after mowing damage, landscaping or storms.

How long should I run sprinklers in South Carolina?

There is no universal runtime. Soil, sprinkler output, rainfall, slope, turf type and local restrictions matter. Measure actual output and adjust to the landscape rather than relying on a fixed number of minutes.

How often should I water St. Augustine grass?

Watering depends on establishment, weather, soil and local restrictions. Established turf generally benefits from appropriately timed, deeper watering rather than frequent shallow cycles; adjust to observed conditions.

Can irrigation be checked before new sod installation?

Yes. A coverage and leak check before laying sod can prevent dry strips, buried heads and avoidable disturbance afterward. See our sod installation service.

Do you repair irrigation systems supplied by wells?

We can review conventional sprinkler symptoms, but specialized well pumps, pressure systems and electrical repairs may require a qualified specialist.

Do you provide backflow preventer testing or repair?

Backflow testing and regulated repairs should be performed by appropriately certified or licensed personnel where required. We can help identify when specialist service is needed.

Can you inspect irrigation after a hurricane or major storm?

We can assess accessible heads, lines, valves, controllers and coverage for visible storm-related problems, subject to scheduling and site safety.

Do you service HOA, apartment and commercial irrigation?

We can evaluate selected HOA, multifamily and light-commercial irrigation repairs. Large systems, specialized controls and pump stations may need a dedicated contractor.

Do you offer emergency or same-day sprinkler repair?

Contact us with the symptoms and location. Scheduling depends on current workload, access and the type of repair; same-day response is not guaranteed.

Should I repair or replace my sprinkler system?

Targeted repairs are often appropriate for isolated failures. Widespread leaks, obsolete controls or poor system layout may justify a broader replacement evaluation.

Can irrigation repair be bundled with lawn care or landscaping?

Yes. Irrigation work can be coordinated with lawn care, landscaping, sod installation and recurring property maintenance when included in the agreed scope.
