Why It Matters

The Difference Between Solid Epoxy
and Everything Else at the Hardware Store

The epoxy kits at Home Depot and Lowe's are water-based paint — typically 30–50% solids, meaning nearly half of what you apply evaporates. The film left behind is thin, adhesion is poor on any concrete with moisture or previous sealer, and peeling typically begins within 12–24 months. We don't use those products. We apply 100% solids commercial-grade epoxy with a full surface preparation system — the same spec professional facilities require.

⚠️ The Big-Box Problem: A standard 2-car garage epoxy kit costs $150–$300 at the hardware store. It will peel. When it does, you'll spend $1,400+ to have it professionally removed and correctly recoated anyway — except now you've also lost the $300 and two years. We see this every season. The correct installation costs more upfront and nothing for the next two decades.
What We Use
🏆

100% Solids Commercial Epoxy

No water. No solvent. Every ounce applied stays on your floor. The film thickness and bond strength from 100% solids cannot be achieved with water-based systems. This is why our installations last 20+ years and big-box installations last 18 months.

🔬

Diamond Grinding Surface Prep

Proper adhesion requires mechanical profile on the concrete. We grind the surface with industrial diamond tooling before any coating is applied. Without this, even the best epoxy will delaminate. No exceptions.

💧

Moisture Testing Before Application

Concrete moisture is the #1 cause of epoxy failure. We test before we coat. If moisture levels exceed the manufacturer's spec, we address it — we don't coat over it and collect a check.

🎨

3D Metallic & Designer Finishes

Our metallic system creates depth and movement in the floor — every installation is unique because metallic pigments shift and flow differently in each space. Not a painted look. A flooring statement.

🛡️

Chemical & Impact Resistance

Resists oil, gasoline, transmission fluid, battery acid, and cleaning chemicals. Handles dropped tools, car jacks, and decades of tire abrasion without surface damage.

🧹

Sealed — Effortless to Clean

A non-porous epoxy surface means oil drips, spills, and dirt sit on top of the coating rather than absorbing into concrete. A mop handles everything. No staining, no sealing, no maintenance schedule.

The Process

What a Complete Epoxy Installation Includes —
from Prep to Topcoat

The installation process is where the difference between a 20-year floor and a 2-year floor is determined. Every step below happens on every project — none are skipped to reduce cost.

1

On-Site Measurement & Concrete Assessment

We measure your garage precisely, assess the concrete condition, check for previous coatings, test for moisture, and identify cracks or damage requiring repair. Color selection happens here — we bring samples so you can see options in your actual lighting before committing.

2

Crack Repair & Surface Patching

Cracks, spalls, and damaged areas are filled and repaired before grinding begins. A properly filled and ground crack is structurally sound and visually seamless under the epoxy. An unfilled crack telegraphs through every coating system applied over it.

3

Diamond Grinding — The Most Important Step

We grind the entire surface with industrial diamond tooling to create the mechanical profile that allows epoxy to chemically and physically bond to the concrete. This step cannot be replaced by acid etching for a 20-year result.

4

Moisture Barrier Primer

Myrtle Beach's coastal humidity and Horry County's water table mean concrete moisture vapor transmission is a real delamination risk. We apply a moisture-blocking primer before the epoxy system — a step frequently skipped by budget installers that causes premature peeling in our climate.

5

Full Solid Epoxy Base Coat + Color System

100% solids commercial epoxy is mixed to precise ratio and applied to the primed surface. Metallic pigments are broadcast and manipulated to create the 3D depth effect. Marble flakes or team-color blends are broadcast during this coat. Film thickness is monitored — we apply to manufacturer specification.

6

Polyaspartic Clear Topcoat

A UV-stable polyaspartic clear coat is applied over the epoxy. This provides scratch resistance, gloss, and UV protection that prevents yellowing or dulling over time. It is what you walk and drive on — the hardest, most chemical-resistant layer in the system.

7

Cure Time, Cleanup & Walk-Through

Light foot traffic in 24 hours. Vehicle traffic after 72 hours of cure. We clean the entire work area before we leave. Final walk-through with you before sign-off — you inspect the result with us, every time.

FAQ

Epoxy Garage Floor FAQ

How much does epoxy garage floor coating cost in Myrtle Beach?+

All pricing includes diamond grinding, moisture primer, full epoxy system, and polyaspartic topcoat. Free estimates for all Horry County properties — call or text 843-467-7136.
How long does epoxy garage floor coating last?+
With 100% solids commercial epoxy properly installed on a correctly prepared surface, 20–30 years is a realistic lifespan for residential garage use. Big-box water-based epoxy products typically peel within 1–3 years. The difference is solids content (100% vs 30–50%), surface preparation (diamond grinding vs acid etching), and system design (3-coat commercial vs single-coat DIY).
Why not just buy epoxy at Home Depot?+
You can — but you'll likely be calling us within 2 years anyway. Hardware store kits are water-based epoxy with 30–50% solids. The resulting film is thin and relies on chemical bonding to concrete that typically has moisture vapor transmission and no mechanical profile. Peeling in coastal South Carolina's climate is nearly inevitable with these products. When the peel happens, removal of the failed coating adds significant cost to what a correct installation would have been from the start.
How long does installation take?+
Most 1 and 2-car garages are completed in 1–2 days. 3-car and larger garages typically take 2 days. Cure time: light foot traffic in 24 hours, vehicle traffic in 72 hours. We schedule around your availability.
Do you coat areas besides garages?+
Yes — workshops, basements, pool decks, patios, laundry rooms, commercial floors, and warehouse floors all use the same solid epoxy system. Multi-area projects typically qualify for volume pricing. Ask when you call.
What if my concrete has cracks?+
Crack repair is included in our pricing. Surface cracks and spalls are filled and leveled before grinding. We assess crack severity during the estimate and are honest about structural movement cracks that may require additional remediation. We'll tell you exactly what we're working with before any work begins.
Does epoxy make the floor slippery?+
Gloss epoxy is slippery when wet — we add anti-slip aggregate to the topcoat on all residential garage installations. This creates traction when wet without significantly affecting the visual finish. For pool decks and outdoor surfaces, additional anti-slip texture is applied. We don't install slippery floors.
