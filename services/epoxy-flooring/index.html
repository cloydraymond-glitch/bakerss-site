The Bakerss Process

Two Days. Five Steps.
Zero Shortcuts.

Step 1 — Day One Morning

Diamond Grinding

Industrial diamond tooling grinds the entire slab to open the concrete's pores and create the mechanical profile epoxy needs. We never acid-etch — etching leaves the weak bond that makes DIY floors peel, especially on coastal concrete carrying salt-air mineral deposits.

Step 2 — Day One

Repair & Moisture Defense

Cracks, spalls, and pitting are cut out and filled, then the slab gets a moisture barrier primer — the layer that neutralizes the vapor transmission that coastal South Carolina's water table pushes up through every garage floor.

Step 3 — Day One Afternoon

Solid Epoxy Base Coat

The 100% solids epoxy base goes down thick — this is the structural layer that carries vehicles, jacks, toolboxes, and twenty years of daily use.

Step 4 — Day Two

Your Color System

Metallic pigments hand-worked into dimensional patterns, full-coverage flake broadcast, or clean solid color — applied at full saturation, never stretched thin.

Step 5 — Day Two

Polyaspartic Topcoat & Cleanup

A UV-stable polyaspartic wear layer seals the system — chemical-proof against gas, oil, brake fluid, and road salt. We clean up completely; light foot traffic next day, vehicles in 48–72 hours.

The Coastal Problem
🌊

Humidity + Water Table + Salt Air = A Three-Front Attack on Your Slab

Horry County concrete fights moisture from below (a high coastal water table pushing vapor up through the slab), from above (subtropical humidity most of the year), and chemically (salt air depositing minerals that block adhesion). Water-based hardware-store epoxy — 30–50% solids — has none of the film thickness or vapor resistance to survive that. We remove failed kit floors across the county every season; the homeowner always paid twice. The 100% solids system over ground, primed concrete is the one-time answer.

🧪

Moisture-Tested First

Every slab is moisture-tested before we commit a system to it. If your concrete needs an upgraded vapor barrier, you'll know at the estimate — not after the floor is down.

FAQ

Epoxy Floor Questions, Answered

How much does epoxy garage floor coating cost in Horry County?+
All-inclusive: 1-car from $1,800, 2-car from $3,000, 3-car from $4,500 — covering diamond grinding, crack repair, moisture primer, your color system, polyaspartic topcoat, and cleanup. Free on-site estimates in every Grand Strand city; your exact number comes from measured square footage.
Metallic, flake, or solid — how do I choose?+
Metallic is the one-of-a-kind marbled showpiece. Flake is the most popular garage choice — slip-resistant and the best at hiding daily life. Solid is the clean commercial look and the easiest to keep spotless. Durability is identical across all three; we bring samples to every estimate.
How long does installation take, and when can I park on it?+
Most garages are a two-day install. Light foot traffic is fine after about 24 hours; vehicles after 48–72 hours depending on temperature and humidity. We'll give you exact cure timing for your week's weather.
Why not just use a hardware-store kit?+
Kits are water-based epoxy at a fraction of the solids content, applied over acid-etched concrete. In this county's humidity and water-table conditions they typically peel within 12–24 months — then you pay for removal plus the real system. The commercial spec costs more once; the kit costs more twice.
Do you coat patios, porches, or commercial floors?+
Yes — any sound concrete slab: patios, porches, laundry rooms, workshops, retail and commercial spaces. Exterior work gets UV-stable polyaspartic topcoats so the finish never yellows in coastal sun.
