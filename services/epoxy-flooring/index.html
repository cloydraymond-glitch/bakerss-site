Epoxy Flooring in Myrtle Beach Starts With the Slab

Epoxy flooring is not just paint for concrete. A garage floor coating is a surface system that depends heavily on concrete condition, preparation, moisture, coating selection and proper installation sequence. When a concrete coating fails, the issue is usually not the color — it is adhesion, moisture, contamination or poor prep.

Myrtle Beach and Horry County properties add another layer of difficulty. Coastal humidity, salt air, slab moisture, summer heat, storm season, garage condensation and sandy foot traffic all affect concrete surfaces. That is why Bakerss reviews the concrete, existing coating condition, cracks, pitting, moisture concerns and intended use before recommending a coating system.

Our goal is a clean, durable, attractive floor that makes the garage easier to use, easier to clean and better protected from everyday wear. Whether you want a full-flake garage floor, a metallic showpiece, a clean solid-color shop floor or a commercial concrete coating, the project starts with proper preparation.

Coastal Concrete

Why Coastal South Carolina Concrete Needs Better Prep

Concrete in coastal South Carolina can be affected by moisture vapor, humidity, salt air, condensation, temperature swings and prior surface contamination. A garage floor coating installed over poorly prepared concrete may peel, bubble, chip or fail early.

That is why the most important question is not only “what color do you want?” The better question is “what condition is the slab in?” New concrete, older concrete, previously painted floors, oil-stained garages, cracked slabs and moisture-prone areas may each require a different prep approach.

Bakerss reviews the slab before making assumptions. This protects the customer and the finished floor. The right system starts with the right surface.

Moisture

Humidity & Vapor Concerns

Moisture can affect coating adhesion and cure. Coastal garages require attention to vapor, condensation and slab condition before coating.

Surface

Contamination & Existing Coatings

Oil, paint, sealers, tire residue, dust and weak concrete can interfere with bonding if not addressed during prep.

Durability

Use Case Matters

A show garage, work garage, rental property, patio or commercial floor may need different finish texture, protection and maintenance expectations.

FAQ

Epoxy Flooring Questions — Answered

How much do epoxy garage floors cost in Myrtle Beach?

Epoxy garage floor pricing depends on square footage, concrete condition, prep needs, crack repair, finish type and coating system. Bakerss provides free estimates so the price matches the actual slab and desired finish.

What is the best epoxy finish for a garage?

For most garages, full-flake epoxy is the most practical choice because it offers texture, hides dust and gives the floor a clean finished look. Metallic epoxy is better for a high-end custom appearance, while solid color works well for shops and commercial-style spaces.

Do you grind concrete before epoxy flooring?

Concrete surface preparation is critical. Depending on the slab, preparation may include grinding, cleaning, patching, crack repair or coating removal before epoxy is installed.

Can you install epoxy over an old painted garage floor?

Possibly, but the existing coating must be evaluated. Weak paint, peeling coating, contamination or poor adhesion usually needs to be removed or properly prepared before a new epoxy system is installed.

Can epoxy flooring be used outside?

Some concrete coating systems can be used on patios, porches and covered exterior areas, but exposure, drainage, UV, moisture and slip resistance must be reviewed before choosing a system.

How long before I can use my garage after epoxy?

Cure time depends on product, temperature, humidity and coating system. Bakerss will provide specific return-to-use guidance for foot traffic, storage and vehicles after installation.

Can epoxy flooring be bundled with other Bakerss services?

Yes. Epoxy flooring is often paired with garage cleanup, pressure washing, painting, handyman work, shelving installation and property maintenance services.
