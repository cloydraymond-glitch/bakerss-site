Epoxy Floor Colors for Myrtle Beach Garages

Every color in our sample library is available for installation throughout the Grand Strand — from Myrtle Beach to Conway, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Murrells Inlet, Little River, Loris, and all of Horry County. Our flake, marble, and metallic systems are all installed over diamond-ground, moisture-primed concrete using 100% solids commercial epoxy. No big-box products. No DIY kits. Learn more about our epoxy process →

Which Color System Is Right for You?

Earth Inspired & Team Inspired — broadcast flake systems are the most popular for garages. Durable, easy to clean, and available in 75+ color combinations. Marble Flakes deliver an upscale stone look for showrooms, basements, and interior spaces. 3D Metallic is a seamless, high-gloss statement floor with zero grout lines — now available in 43 colors including Ocean, Caribbean, Maui, Azure, Reef, Coral/Copper, Mango, Starfish, Driftwood, and Rum. Call us to discuss which system fits your project →

Physical Samples Available

Online samples give you a strong sense of color and texture, but we always recommend viewing physical samples in your space before committing. Lighting conditions in coastal South Carolina vary significantly between garage, interior, and exterior spaces. We bring physical samples to your property at no charge. Request a free estimate and sample visit →

Coastal Durability — Built for the Grand Strand

Myrtle Beach's 85% average overnight humidity means most DIY and big-box epoxy products fail within 12–24 months. Every color in this chart is installed with a system designed specifically for coastal South Carolina: diamond-ground surface preparation, moisture barrier primer, 100% solids commercial epoxy, and polyaspartic topcoat. 20+ year lifespan.
