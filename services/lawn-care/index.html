Local Knowledge

Mowing in Myrtle Beach Is Different — Here's Why

If you've moved to Myrtle Beach from an inland or northern market, the growing season here will surprise you. Warm-season grasses in coastal South Carolina are actively growing for nine to ten months of the year — and at peak summer conditions, Bermuda grass can produce two inches of growth per week. A lawn that was perfectly mowed can need attention again within seven days.

Bakerss adjusts mowing frequency with the seasons — more aggressive scheduling in the peak spring and summer months, relaxing to biweekly in fall and monthly in winter when warm-season grasses approach dormancy. You don't have to manage this. We do it automatically.

FAQ

Lawn Care Questions — Answered

How much does lawn care cost in Myrtle Beach?

Lawn care in Myrtle Beach starts at $50 per visit for smaller residential lots. Most standard Myrtle Beach homes (3–4 bedroom, quarter-acre lots) run $65–$90 per visit. Larger lots, HOA communities, and properties requiring extra time are quoted on-site. All estimates are free.

How often does my Myrtle Beach lawn need mowing?

During the peak growing season from May through September, most Myrtle Beach warm-season lawns need mowing every 7–10 days. Bermuda grass can require cutting every 5–7 days during July and August. We adjust frequency with the seasons — you don't have to manage the schedule after initial setup.

Do you service my specific HOA community?

Almost certainly yes. We serve HOA communities throughout Horry County including Grande Dunes, Carolina Forest, Market Common, Barefoot Resort, Tidewater Plantation, Wild Wing Plantation, Prince Creek, Wachesaw Plantation, Coastal Heights, and dozens of others. Tell us your community and we'll confirm.

What grass types do you service?

All four common warm-season grass types in Horry County: Bermuda (most common — mowed at 1.5–2 inches), Zoysia (mowed at 1–2.5 inches), St. Augustine (mowed at 3–4 inches, common in shaded and oceanfront lots), and Centipede (mowed at 1.5–2.5 inches). We apply the correct approach to your specific grass — never a one-size-fits-all cut height.

Do I need to be home when you mow?

No. Most of our clients are never home during service — including many out-of-state property owners. We access the lawn, perform the full service, and leave the property looking exactly as it should. Payment is handled separately without requiring your presence.

Do you offer weed control and fertilization?

Yes. Pre-emergent weed control applications in late February/early March and September/October are available as add-ons. Fertilization applications timed to the Horry County growing calendar are also available. These are priced separately ($45–$95) and always quoted before application.
