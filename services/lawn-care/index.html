Local Knowledge

Mowing in Myrtle Beach Is Different — Here's Why

If you've moved to Myrtle Beach from an inland or northern market, the growing season here will surprise you. Warm-season grasses in coastal South Carolina are actively growing for nine to ten months of the year — and at peak summer conditions, Bermuda grass can produce two inches of growth per week. A lawn that was perfectly mowed can need attention again within seven days.

This isn't a flaw in the grass — it's the subtropical climate working exactly as it should. The solution is consistent professional service on a schedule matched to the growth rate. Skip a mowing during July and you're looking at a difficult catch-up cut and potential HOA issues. Stay on schedule and the lawn always looks maintained.

Bakerss adjusts mowing frequency with the seasons — more aggressive scheduling in the peak spring and summer months, relaxing to biweekly in fall and monthly in winter when warm-season grasses approach dormancy. You don't have to manage this. We do it automatically.
