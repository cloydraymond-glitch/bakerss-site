Lawn Care Tailored to Your Grass Type

Coastal South Carolina is warm-season grass territory. Every lawn care decision — mowing height, fertilization timing, aeration schedule — depends on which variety you have. We know all of them.

Zoysia Grass

The most popular choice in Horry County. Dense, carpet-like growth that crowds out weeds, handles heat and drought well, and has moderate salt tolerance — ideal for most Myrtle Beach properties. Mow at 1–2 inches. Fertilize in late spring and midsummer.

Bermuda Grass

Best for full-sun, high-traffic properties. Extremely heat-tolerant and fast-recovering — popular for commercial properties and coastal homes with open, sunny yards. Requires more frequent mowing during peak season. Mow at ¾–1½ inches.

St. Augustine Grass

The go-to for shaded properties with significant tree coverage. Handles coastal humidity well and has an attractive wide-blade appearance. Requires more water than Zoysia or Bermuda and should be monitored for chinch bugs. Mow at 2½–4 inches.

Centipede Grass

A low-maintenance option for homeowners who want minimal inputs. Slow-growing, low fertilizer requirement, and decent drought tolerance — but less suited for oceanfront or salt-exposed properties. Mow at 1–2 inches. Do not over-fertilize.
