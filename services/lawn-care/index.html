Local lawn knowledge

Warm-Season Turf Care on the Grand Strand

Bermuda, centipede, zoysia and St. Augustine each respond differently to mowing height, watering, shade and seasonal stress. Maintenance should reflect the actual lawn.

Spring green-up

Clean debris, evaluate bare areas and begin mowing as active growth resumes. Fertilize based on turf type and soil needs.

Peak summer growth

Use consistent cutting intervals, avoid scalping and inspect irrigation coverage during extended heat.

Fall & winter

Reduce mowing frequency as growth slows, clear leaves and pine straw, and plan repairs for the next growing season.

Frequently Asked Questions

Lawn Care Questions — Answered

How much does lawn care cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pricing depends on lot size, turf condition, edging, obstacles, access and service frequency. Smaller routine residential visits have historically started around $50, but every property receives a current quote before scheduling.

How often should I mow my lawn in coastal South Carolina?

During active summer growth, many Bermuda, zoysia, centipede and St. Augustine lawns benefit from weekly mowing. Frequency may decrease as growth slows; avoid removing more than about one-third of the blade in a single cut.

Do you offer weekly lawn mowing and recurring plans?

Yes. We coordinate recurring mowing, edging, trimming and blowing for eligible properties. Scheduling depends on location, route availability and approved scope.

Can you care for Bermuda, centipede, zoysia and St. Augustine grass?

Yes. These warm-season grasses require different mowing heights and seasonal practices. Our lawn maintenance approach accounts for turf type, conditions and presentation requirements.

When should I fertilize warm-season lawns in South Carolina?

Fertilization is generally timed around active spring and summer growth, but the appropriate timing and product depend on the grass type and soil test. Chemical applications, when needed, must be handled by properly licensed providers.

When is the best time to aerate or overseed a coastal SC lawn?

Warm-season turf is typically aerated during active growth in late spring or early summer when conditions support recovery. Overseeding depends on turf species and objectives; Bermuda winter overseeding is different from restoring centipede or St. Augustine.

Can you help with brown patches, thin turf and dead grass?

We can assess visible conditions and discuss mowing adjustments, drainage, irrigation repair, soil preparation or sod replacement. Disease and pest identification or pesticide treatment may require a qualified specialist.

Do you provide weed control or fire ant treatments?

We can coordinate weed and pest concerns as part of a broader lawn plan. Pesticide and herbicide applications are performed only where appropriate licensing and authorization are in place.

Do you install sod, mulch or pine straw?

Sod installation, bed cleanup, mulch and pine straw may be quoted as separate landscape services based on material, preparation, access and availability.

Do you offer irrigation and sprinkler repair?

Yes. Irrigation observations and eligible sprinkler repairs can be coordinated with lawn maintenance, with work scoped and approved separately.

Can you handle HOA, apartment and commercial grounds maintenance?

Yes. Bakerss offers scoped recurring grounds maintenance for HOAs, apartment communities, commercial properties and property managers, with service documentation when included in the agreement.

Do you maintain vacation rentals and second homes?

Yes. Lawn visits can be coordinated with second-home care, vacation rental cleaning and other property maintenance services.

How can I keep my lawn green during summer heat?

Mow at a suitable height for the turf type, water deeply when needed, avoid unnecessary scalping and address irrigation coverage or drainage problems. Turf health also depends on soil conditions, pests and weather.

What is included in a standard lawn mowing visit?

A standard agreed visit can include mowing, edging, string trimming and blowing clippings off primary hard surfaces. Cleanup, hedge trimming and landscape work are typically separate unless included in the proposal.

Is DIY lawn care or professional service a better fit?

DIY can work for smaller lawns if you have equipment and time. Professional recurring service can provide consistent scheduling, trimming detail and coordinated property care, especially for rentals, HOAs and absentee owners.

Which Grand Strand communities do you serve?

Service requests are considered across Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Garden City, Murrells Inlet, Conway, Carolina Forest, Socastee, Little River, Pawleys Island and nearby areas of Horry and Georgetown counties. Route availability varies.
