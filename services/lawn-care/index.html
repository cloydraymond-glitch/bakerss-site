Grass Types

Correct Mowing for Bermuda, Centipede, Zoysia & St. Augustine

A lawn can look worse after mowing if the wrong cut height is used. Bakerss adjusts service by grass type, season and condition instead of treating every yard the same.

Fast Summer Growth

Bermuda Grass

Common on sunny Horry County lawns and golf-area properties. Requires frequent summer cutting and clean edging to keep a tight appearance.

Low Maintenance

Centipede Grass

Popular in residential lawns across the Grand Strand. Needs careful mowing to avoid stress, especially during heat or drought.

Dense Turf

Zoysia Grass

Produces a premium look when maintained correctly. Requires sharp cutting, correct height and consistent service intervals.

Shade & Coastal Lots

St. Augustine

Often found in shaded or beach-adjacent properties. Should be cut higher than Bermuda or centipede to protect turf health.

Frequently Asked Questions

Lawn Care Questions — Answered

How much does lawn care cost in Myrtle Beach?

Bakerss lawn care starts at $50 per visit for smaller properties. Standard residential lawns are usually priced based on lot size, trimming requirements, grass condition, service frequency and access. Larger HOA lots, corner lots, overgrown yards and properties with heavy trimming are quoted individually.

Do you offer weekly lawn mowing?

Yes. Weekly lawn mowing is the best fit for many Myrtle Beach and Horry County properties during peak growing season. Biweekly service may work during slower growth periods or for lower-maintenance properties.

Do you service HOAs and vacation rentals?

Yes. Bakerss services HOA homes, short-term rentals, second homes, absentee-owner properties and property-manager accounts across Horry County. Lawn care can also be paired with pool maintenance, Airbnb cleaning and full property upkeep.

Do you provide multi-family or commercial lawn maintenance?

Yes. Bakerss provides grounds maintenance for apartment communities, HOA common areas, clubhouses, office properties, retail frontage and property management portfolios. Larger scopes are priced by property layout, frequency and service expectations.

Can you install sod if my lawn is damaged?

Yes. Bakerss offers sod installation for dead, thin, damaged or newly graded lawn areas. Sod work is quoted separately based on square footage, grass type, prep needs, access and soil condition.
