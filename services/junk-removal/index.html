Myrtle Beach Junk Removal

Built for a Rental, Real Estate and Renovation Market

Junk removal in Myrtle Beach is not only garage clutter. The local market creates specific removal needs that inland communities do not see at the same volume. Vacation rental owners replace sleeper sofas, mattresses, patio furniture, dressers, televisions and damaged guest-use items on accelerated cycles. Real estate agents need homes cleared before listing photos and inspections. Estate families need respectful removal without turning a difficult situation into a logistical mess. Bakerss is positioned for that exact demand because junk removal connects directly to the rest of our property maintenance work. A move-out clean often reveals furniture, trash or abandoned items. An Airbnb turnover may expose broken patio chairs or a damaged mattress. A renovation project creates debris before painting, flooring or pressure washing can be completed. Instead of calling separate vendors, Bakerss can coordinate junk removal with cleaning, handyman repairs, pressure washing, painting, lawn care and property maintenance.

How much does junk removal cost in Myrtle Beach?

Junk removal starts at $99 for a single item. Small loads usually range from $175 to $275, medium loads from $275 to $425, full truck loads from $425 to $650 and estate cleanouts are quoted after a walkthrough.

Yes. We remove worn STR furniture, mattresses, patio furniture, grills, appliances, broken guest-use items, owner closet overflow and end-of-season replacement loads for Myrtle Beach and Horry County vacation rental owners.

Do you offer same-day junk removal?

Same-day and next-day junk removal may be available depending on schedule, route, access and load size. Text photos and the property address to 843-467-7136 for the fastest quote.

Do you handle estate cleanouts?

Yes. Bakerss handles partial-home and full-home estate cleanouts, downsizing cleanouts, garage clearing and pre-sale property clearing. Estate cleanouts are quoted after a walkthrough because scope can vary significantly.

Do you donate usable items?

Yes. Usable items are separated for donation when condition, timing and logistics allow. Donation sorting is included when practical.

What items can you not haul?

We do not haul hazardous waste, paint, chemicals, fuel, asbestos, medical waste, biological waste or regulated materials. We identify special-disposal concerns before scheduling.
