Frequently Asked Questions

Answers About Fence Installation and Repair

How much does fence installation cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pricing depends on linear footage, material, height, gates, demolition, terrain, access, post requirements and HOA specifications. A site-specific estimate is necessary.

What fence materials do you install?

Common options include pressure-treated wood, vinyl, aluminum and chain link. Availability and suitability depend on the property, design and local requirements.

Do you repair existing fences?

Yes. We repair many leaning posts, damaged pickets, rails, panels, hinges, latches and sagging gates when the remaining structure is serviceable.

Do I need HOA approval?

Many Grand Strand communities require written architectural approval before fence installation or material changes. The owner is responsible for obtaining approval unless the contract states otherwise.

Do I need a survey before installing a fence?

A current survey is strongly recommended when boundaries or easements are uncertain. Bakerss does not establish legal property lines.

Will utilities be marked?

Public utility locating should be completed before digging. Private lines such as irrigation, landscape lighting and pool equipment may require separate identification by the owner or specialist.

Can you install pool fencing?

We can install selected pool-area fence systems, but the final design must satisfy applicable barrier, gate, latch and local code requirements.

Which fence is best near the coast?

Vinyl and aluminum resist rot, while properly constructed pressure-treated wood offers privacy and repairability. The best choice depends on wind exposure, HOA rules, budget and desired maintenance.

Can you match an existing fence?

We can attempt to match dimensions, style and material, but weathering and discontinued products may prevent an exact match.

Do you remove old fencing?

Removal and disposal can be included as a separate line item based on material, volume, access and any concrete footings.

How long does fence installation take?

Duration depends on footage, material availability, weather, inspections, approvals, access and site conditions. The estimate will provide a practical project window.

Can fence work be bundled with landscaping?

Yes. Fence projects can be coordinated with landscaping, sod, irrigation repair, pressure washing and broader exterior property maintenance.
