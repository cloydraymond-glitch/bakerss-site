Fence pricing and project planning

What Determines Fence Installation Cost?

A price per linear foot is a starting comparison—not a final bid. We quote based on your site, materials and project scope.

Pricing factor

Fence Material & Height

Pressure-treated wood, cedar, vinyl, aluminum, chain link and composite have different material and installation costs. Taller privacy fencing generally requires more material and structural planning.

Pricing factor

Linear Feet, Gates & Corners

Overall footage matters, but gate openings, slopes, corners, transitions, hardware and access affect labor and supplies.

Pricing factor

Old Fence & Ground Conditions

Removal, disposal, concrete footings, sandy or wet ground, roots, grading, easements and utility clearance can change the scope.

Planning

Repair vs. Replacement

A few damaged boards or a failed hinge may be repairable. Widespread rot, unstable posts or repeated storm damage may justify replacing a full run.

Coastal fence considerations

Fence Planning for Grand Strand Conditions

Myrtle Beach properties face humid weather, wind-driven rain, occasional tropical storms, salt exposure and varied soils. No fence should be advertised as hurricane-proof.

Wind exposure

Posts, Spacing & Wind Loads

Solid privacy panels catch wind. Post depth, spacing, footing design and local code requirements need consideration.

Salt and humidity

Corrosion-Resistant Components

Appropriate fasteners, hinges, finishes and hardware help limit premature corrosion near the ocean.

Wet areas

Sandy Soil, Drainage & Wetlands

Site conditions may affect footing design; waterfront and marsh-adjacent properties can require additional setbacks or environmental approvals.

Community rules

HOA Style & Height Limits

Confirm approved heights, colors, materials and locations before ordering fencing.

Property lines

Surveys, Easements & Neighbors

Do not assume an old fence marks the legal boundary. Resolve uncertain lines with a survey or appropriate professional advice.

Pool safety

Barriers, Gates & Latches

Pool-area projects need a code-appropriate design and may require permits or inspections before use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers About Fence Installation and Repair

How much does fence installation cost in Myrtle Beach?

Fence installation pricing depends on material, height, linear footage, gates, post and footing requirements, removal, access and approvals. A site-specific quote is more reliable than a single price per linear foot.

How much does fence repair cost?

Minor hinge, latch or board repairs generally involve less work than replacing posts or full sections. The condition of nearby supports and the number of damaged areas affect cost.

What fence materials can Bakerss work with?

We can evaluate projects involving pressure-treated wood, cedar, vinyl, aluminum and chain-link fencing. Decorative, composite and specialty designs are assessed by scope and product availability.

Is vinyl, wood or aluminum best for a coastal home?

Vinyl offers low-maintenance privacy, aluminum provides open sightlines without wood rot, and treated wood offers design flexibility and repairability. Wind exposure, corrosion-resistant hardware and HOA rules matter for every choice.

Can you repair a fence blown down in a storm?

We can assess storm-damaged posts, panels and gates and propose a safe repair or replacement scope. Response time depends on weather, site safety and crew availability.

Should I repair or replace a leaning fence?

If damage is limited to one post or section, repair may be reasonable. Widespread rot, failing footings or multiple unstable posts can make replacement more practical.

Can you repair a sagging or broken fence gate?

Many gate problems can be addressed through hinge, latch, frame or post work. We inspect the support posts because adjusting hardware alone may not solve an underlying structural problem.

Can you replace individual fence panels or boards?

Yes, when matching or compatible materials are available and adjacent supports are sound. Older, weathered fencing may not match perfectly.

Do you offer fence staining, sealing or painting?

Fence preparation, staining, sealing and painting may be quoted as part of a project or coordinated separately, depending on material and surface condition.

Can you remove and haul away an old fence?

Old fence removal and disposal can be included in the written scope. Buried concrete footings and difficult access may affect cost.

Do I need a permit to build a fence in Myrtle Beach?

Requirements depend on the property jurisdiction, fence height, location, pool use and other site conditions. Verify current rules with the relevant city or county before work begins.

How tall can my fence be in an HOA?

HOA covenants and architectural guidelines may limit fence height, material, color and placement. Written approval may be required even when a local permit is not.

Do I need a survey before installing a fence?

A current survey is strongly recommended when property boundaries, easements or setbacks are uncertain. Bakerss does not determine legal property lines.

Who is responsible for a shared property-line fence?

Responsibility can depend on ownership, recorded agreements, HOA rules and applicable law. Confirm the boundary and any shared obligations before authorizing work.

Will underground utilities be located before fence posts are dug?

Public utility locating should be arranged before digging. Private irrigation, lighting, pool and other owner-installed lines may require separate locating.

Do you install pool fences and self-closing gates?

Selected pool barrier projects may be considered, but final height, openings, self-closing gates, latches and placement must comply with applicable pool safety codes and approvals.

Do you install dog-friendly privacy fencing?

Yes, we can discuss privacy layouts, secure gates and ground-clearance details to reduce escape gaps. No fence can guarantee pet containment.

Can you install automatic driveway gates?

Powered gate systems require specialized equipment, safety controls and often electrical work. We can review the project and coordinate qualified specialists when appropriate.

How long does a wood fence last in coastal South Carolina?

Service life varies considerably with material, construction, drainage, sun, moisture, wind exposure and maintenance. Regular inspection and timely repairs help extend useful life.

How long does fence installation take?

Timing depends on length, materials, gate fabrication, site access, weather, utility locating, permitting and HOA approvals. We discuss the anticipated schedule during quoting.

Do you offer emergency or same-week fence repairs?

Let us know if a damaged fence presents an immediate access or security issue. We can review scheduling options, but same-day or same-week service is not guaranteed.

Can fencing be bundled with landscaping or pool maintenance?

Yes. Fence work can be coordinated with landscaping, sod installation, irrigation repair, pressure washing and pool-area maintenance when the scopes and schedules align.
