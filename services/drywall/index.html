Drywall Repairs for Homes, Rentals, and Apartment Turnovers

Not every wall needs replacement. A practical drywall repair starts with identifying the damage, checking whether it is still active, and selecting the patching and finishing method that will produce a durable, paint-ready surface.

Common drywall problems we can assess

  • Small impact holes from doors, furniture, and moving activity
  • Wall cracks and nail pops that need an appropriate repair
  • Ceiling and wall openings after plumbing or electrical access
  • Joint damage, corner-bead problems, and failed previous patches
  • Drywall preparation before interior painting or an apartment move-in

Our repair workflow

We review the affected area, distinguish ordinary cosmetic damage from a possible underlying problem, protect nearby surfaces, and determine the appropriate repair materials. Work may include preparing and reinforcing the opening, applying joint compound in stages, sanding, priming, and coordinating the finish paint. Drying time and texture matching can affect scheduling.

Where moisture is involved, the source of the leak and the condition of surrounding materials must be addressed before a cosmetic patch is considered complete.

When Does a Drywall Repair Require More Than Patching?

A hole from a doorknob is not the same as a soft ceiling beneath a leaking drain. Repeated cracks, visible staining, damp materials, or a sagging surface can indicate movement or water intrusion. In those situations, we first identify the related trade or inspection needed. Bakerss can coordinate plumbing work, electrical access follow-up, and interior painting instead of treating the wall finish as an isolated task.

Drywall Work for Property Managers and Apartment Operators

For a rental unit, the priority is often a clean, documented handoff between repairs, painting, and cleaning. We can scope the drywall issues during a move-out walk, group repairs into a punch list, and coordinate with apartment turnover maintenance. That helps reduce unnecessary repeat visits while keeping the property owner informed about work completed and items requiring approval.

Drywall Repair Questions

Can you repair drywall and paint the same area?

Yes, the two scopes can be planned together. The finish schedule depends on compound curing, the existing wall texture, and whether the surrounding wall needs repainting for consistent appearance.

Can you guarantee that a patch will be completely invisible?

No contractor should promise a perfect match in every setting. Heavy texture, aged paint, uneven lighting, and existing wall conditions can make small differences visible. We explain the finish options before work begins.

Do you work in occupied homes and vacant apartments?

Drywall repairs can be scoped for either setting. Access, dust control, furniture protection, and scheduling affect how the job should be organized.

Which areas do you serve?

Our core service area includes Myrtle Beach, Conway, North Myrtle Beach, and surrounding Grand Strand communities, subject to scheduling and project scope.

Need drywall repair as part of a larger project? Tell us the location, approximate size and cause of the damage, and whether painting is included. Request a drywall repair estimate.
