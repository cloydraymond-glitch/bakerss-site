The Bakerss process

Clear scope. Careful preparation. Documented completion.

  1. Inspect or document vacant-unit condition and confirm paint standards, color, unit size and turnaround requirements.
  2. Quote distinct labor and materials responsibilities and specify needed wall prep, doors, trim and ceilings.

We do not advertise guaranteed one-day repaints or claim availability without reviewing each unit or property.

Common questions

Interior Painting & Apartment Repaints in Myrtle Beach, SC: FAQs

Do you repaint apartments between tenants?

Yes. Vacant-unit apartment repaints are a core fit for our property maintenance and unit-turn services. Scope and scheduling are agreed with property management.

Can you paint walls, trim and interior doors?

Yes. Those can be included in the written scope. Each property may have different paint requirements and finish standards.

Do you supply paint?

Either arrangement may be considered. The estimate specifies whether the customer or Bakerss supplies paint and materials.

Can you repair drywall before painting?

Yes. We can quote minor drywall patching, surface preparation and painting together. Larger damage is evaluated separately.

How fast can you complete a vacant unit?

Completion time depends on unit condition, scope, access, preparation and crew availability. We confirm the target turnaround before accepting the assignment.
