How to Maintain a Second Home From Out of State

When you are away for weeks or months, a coastal property still needs attention. A practical second-home care plan begins with a property-specific checklist: HVAC settings, accessible water connections, visible leaks, exterior doors and windows, humidity concerns, landscaping, pool condition and storm readiness.

Home watch is different from house sitting or continuous monitoring. It is a scheduled visual visit with agreed inspection points and owner reporting. A visit can reveal observable warning signs, but it cannot detect hidden damage, prevent every loss or substitute for insurance, security or a licensed inspection.

Bakerss can combine regular home checks with separately approved maintenance such as cleaning, lawn care, pool service, pressure washing, window cleaning and handyman repairs. Owners receive a defined scope and can authorize additional work when a problem is reported.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers About Vacation Home Watch Services

What does a home watch service do?

A home watch service provides scheduled visual checks of an unoccupied property, notes observable concerns such as leaks or HVAC problems, and reports findings to the owner. It does not replace security, a licensed home inspection or insurance.

How often should a vacant Myrtle Beach home be checked?

Weekly or biweekly visits are common starting points. The right schedule depends on the home, insurer requirements, season, humidity exposure and how long the owner is away.

How much does home watch cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pricing depends on property size, visit frequency, travel, access procedures and add-ons such as storm checks or vendor coordination. Bakerss provides a property-specific written quote rather than a universal rate.

Is home watch worth it for an out-of-state homeowner?

Scheduled checks and documented observations can help an absent owner identify visible issues sooner and coordinate approved maintenance. No inspection can guarantee every issue will be detected.

What is the difference between home watch and property management?

Home watch centers on visual visits and reporting for an unoccupied home. Leasing, rent collection, tenant representation and other property-management activities are separate services.

Can Bakerss check HVAC operation and humidity in a vacant beach house?

Visits can include thermostat readings, accessible HVAC observations and visible moisture or odor checks under the agreed checklist. Diagnostic work is handled separately by appropriately qualified personnel.

Can you prepare my home before I arrive and close it after I leave?

Pre-arrival and departure services can be quoted for cleaning, accessible utility checks, thermostat settings, exterior presentation and an owner-approved property checklist.

What happens after a hurricane or tropical storm?

When authorities permit access and conditions are safe, a scheduled post-storm visit can document visible damage and coordinate owner-authorized next steps. Access and response timing cannot be guaranteed.

Can you arrange repairs while I am out of state?

With owner approval, Bakerss can perform work within its authorized service scope or coordinate appropriately qualified specialists. Scope, pricing and approvals are documented.

Do you provide photographs after home watch visits?

Photo documentation and written visit notes can be included in the selected plan, with reporting methods and timing established in the agreement.

Do you retrieve packages or meet contractors?

Authorized package retrieval, keyholding and vendor-access coordination can be included as optional services with agreed access and security procedures.

Does home watch prevent mold or satisfy my homeowners insurance?

Home watch cannot guarantee mold prevention or satisfy an insurance policy automatically. Owners should follow insurer vacancy requirements and maintain appropriate HVAC and moisture controls.

Can you maintain the lawn, pool and exterior while the home is vacant?

Bakerss can quote coordinated lawn, landscaping, pool, pressure washing, window cleaning, cleaning and handyman services separately or as a bundled care plan.

What should be on a second-home maintenance checklist?

Include HVAC and humidity, accessible plumbing, visible water intrusion, doors and windows, exterior drainage, storm exposure, grounds, pool equipment and dated visit reporting.

Do you serve Pawleys Island, Litchfield and DeBordieu?

Bakerss serves the Grand Strand and portions of Georgetown County. Scheduling in Pawleys Island, Litchfield and DeBordieu depends on the address, access requirements and route availability.
