Coastal Deck Expertise

A Myrtle Beach Deck Must Be Built for Salt Air, Humidity & Guest Traffic

Decks in Myrtle Beach do not fail the same way decks fail inland. Coastal salt air accelerates corrosion around screws, joist hangers, railing hardware and post bases. Humidity keeps shaded deck boards damp longer after rain. UV exposure burns unfinished wood. Vacation rental guests increase traffic across stairs, railings, outdoor showers, pool decks and balcony transitions. A deck that is “good enough” inland can become a safety and appearance problem quickly near the Grand Strand.

The right deck specification starts before the first board is installed. Fasteners need to match coastal exposure. Ledger connections need proper flashing. Ground-contact lumber must be used where structural members are close to grade. Railings must be solid, properly spaced and safe for guest use. Composite decking often carries a higher upfront price but can dramatically reduce staining, sealing and splinter complaints over the life of a rental or second home.

Bakerss approaches deck projects as part of full property maintenance. Deck work can be coordinated with pressure washing, painting and staining, handyman repairs, fence repair, storm prep, property maintenance plans and vacation rental readiness.

Material Strategy

Composite vs Wood Decking in Coastal South Carolina

The best deck material depends on budget, property use, exposure, rental traffic and how much maintenance the owner wants to carry over the life of the deck.

Lowest Maintenance

Capped Composite Decking

Best fit for vacation rentals, barefoot guests, pool areas, high-traffic homes and owners who want less staining and sealing. Higher upfront cost, lower recurring maintenance, strong long-term performance when installed correctly.

Lower Upfront Cost

Pressure-Treated Wood Decking

Good fit when budget matters and the owner accepts ongoing staining, sealing and inspection. Wood must be specified correctly and maintained in Myrtle Beach’s humidity and UV exposure.

Premium Option

Hardwood & Specialty Surfaces

Premium natural-wood options can deliver high-end appearance for luxury homes, waterfront properties and custom outdoor living areas, but require correct installation and realistic maintenance expectations.

FAQ

Deck Building & Repair Questions — Answered

What is the best deck material for Myrtle Beach?

Capped composite is usually the best long-term option for Myrtle Beach vacation rentals and high-traffic coastal homes because it requires less recurring maintenance and reduces splinter issues. Pressure-treated wood is a lower upfront cost option, but it needs ongoing staining, sealing and inspections.

Why do coastal decks need better fasteners?

Salt air accelerates corrosion. Standard zinc-plated screws and hardware can rust, stain surrounding wood and loosen over time. Stainless steel or hot-dip galvanized hardware is a better specification for Myrtle Beach and coastal Horry County deck projects.

Can you repair my existing deck instead of replacing it?

Yes. We repair deck boards, stairs, railings, posts, fasteners and select framing issues when the deck is safely repairable. If the structure is too far gone, we will explain why replacement is the better option.

Do you handle deck repairs for vacation rentals?

Yes. Vacation rental decks need fast repair response because splinters, loose rails, shaky stairs and unsafe boards can create guest complaints and liability concerns. We support STR owners across Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Garden City and the wider Horry County market.

Do you offer deck staining and pressure washing?

Yes. Bakerss offers deck pressure washing, prep, staining and sealing for wood decks. We also handle related exterior services, including pressure washing, painting, fence repair, handyman work and property maintenance plans.

Do you pull permits for deck work?

Permit needs depend on the scope, location and structural changes. New builds and major structural modifications may require permits and inspections. We plan projects with permit requirements in mind and communicate what is required before work begins.
