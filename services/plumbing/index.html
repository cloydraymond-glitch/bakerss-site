Plumbing Service for Homes, Rentals, and Managed Properties

A plumbing problem can affect an entire rental, apartment, or business day. Bakerss coordinates plumbing work orders with attention to the symptom, source of the issue, property access, and the proper trade qualifications.

Plumbing issues we can help assess

  • Dripping or leaking faucets and visible fixture connections
  • Running toilets and flush problems
  • Under-sink leaks and drain connections
  • Shower, tub, and fixture problems
  • Clogged drains or backup symptoms requiring diagnosis
  • Plumbing-related make-ready punch lists and access repairs

Our approach to a plumbing service request

We begin with the reported symptom and visible site conditions, identify whether water needs to be shut off, and distinguish a limited fixture repair from a larger supply, drain, or concealed leak concern. The work scope, parts, access requirements, and any applicable trade licensing or permit needs are determined before proceeding.

Photos and clear work-order notes help owners understand the observed problem and the requested next step.

Leaks and Water Damage Require the Right Sequence

A stain on drywall or a wet cabinet base may be the symptom, not the source. Repairing only the visible finish can allow water damage to continue. Where necessary, plumbing diagnosis should precede drywall repair, cabinet work, and interior painting. Active, uncontrolled leaks require prompt shutoff and appropriate professional response.

Plumbing Maintenance for Rentals, Apartments, and Commercial Properties

High-use fixtures and rapid turnovers call for organized service. Our apartment maintenance, vacation rental turnover, and commercial maintenance teams can record fixture issues during inspections and coordinate a qualified plumbing response. This keeps a faucet drip, toilet issue, or suspected drain problem connected to the property's broader maintenance history.

Plumbing Service Questions

Can you fix a running toilet or a leaking faucet?

These are common service requests. A technician must confirm the cause, condition of the fixture, and whether repair or replacement is appropriate.

Do you respond to a leak behind a wall?

Concealed leaks need appropriate diagnosis and access before surrounding finishes are repaired. The scope may involve more than one trade.

Can you repair a sewer backup?

A backup may require specialized drain or sewer-line diagnostics. We review the situation and coordinate the correct licensed or specialized service rather than treating every backup as a simple clog.

Where do you provide plumbing service?

Service is available by scope and scheduling in Myrtle Beach, Conway, North Myrtle Beach, and other Grand Strand communities.
