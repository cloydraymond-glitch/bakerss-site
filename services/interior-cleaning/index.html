FAQ

Cleaning Questions

How much does house cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?

Standard recurring house cleaning starts at $125. Deep cleaning and move-in or move-out cleaning start around $175. Final pricing depends on square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, condition and frequency.

Do you offer recurring house cleaning?

Yes. Weekly and biweekly recurring cleaning is available for homes, condos, second homes and light commercial spaces.

Do you clean second homes and seasonal properties?

Yes. We offer pre-arrival, post-departure and recurring cleaning for second-home and out-of-state owners.

Do you clean Airbnb and vacation rentals?

Yes. Turnover cleaning is handled on our Airbnb cleaning page, with pricing starting around $100 for studios and one-bedroom units.

Do you offer commercial or multi-family cleaning?

Yes. We clean offices, clubhouses, apartment common areas and managed properties under a written scope and schedule. See commercial maintenance and apartment maintenance.

Can cleaning be combined with other Bakerss services?

Yes. Cleaning is often paired with lawn care, pool maintenance, pressure washing, handyman repairs and full property maintenance plans.
