Cleaning Built for the Grand Strand

Interior Cleaning for Homes, Rentals & Managed Properties

Cleaning in Myrtle Beach is different from cleaning in a typical inland market. Sand gets tracked into entryways, tile grout, sliders and bedrooms. Humidity increases musty odors. Vacation rental traffic creates compressed cleaning windows. Second-home owners need a trusted local team when they are not in town. Property managers need reliable communication, clear scopes and consistent presentation.

Bakerss Property Services LLC handles interior cleaning as part of a full property-maintenance platform. That means a customer can use one accountable local company for interior cleaning, Airbnb turnovers, lawn care, pool maintenance, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, landscaping, sod installation and handyman work.

For homeowners, the goal is a clean, comfortable living space. For Airbnb and VRBO owners, the goal is a guest-ready property before check-in. For commercial and multi-family clients, the goal is consistent common-area presentation without chasing multiple vendors.
