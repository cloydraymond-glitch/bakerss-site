Frequently asked questions

Can you replace baseboards after flooring?

Yes. Baseboard and transition work can be scoped alongside floor installation or as a separate repair.

Can you match existing trim?

We assess existing trim profiles and availability, then explain where a close match or wider replacement is needed.

Do you handle minor wood damage?

Minor nonstructural repairs may be included after inspection; structural or regulated work is evaluated separately.

Can you combine this with painting?

Yes. Coordinated trim and painting scopes can reduce scheduling complexity.
