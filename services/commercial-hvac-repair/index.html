Commercial HVAC FAQs

Questions Property Managers Ask Before Sending a Work Order

Clarify service scope, approval procedures, scheduling and maintenance arrangements before dispatch.

Can apartment managers submit individual HVAC work orders?

Yes. Managers can request HVAC work-order coordination for individual units or recurring community needs. Each request is reviewed for location, equipment, scope, scheduling and the credentials required.

Do you repair commercial rooftop HVAC units?

Requests involving rooftop units or other commercial equipment are reviewed case by case. Access, system type, licensing, personnel qualifications and equipment capacity must be confirmed before promising service.

Can Bakerss help manage HVAC repairs across multiple properties?

Bakerss OS is designed to help recurring clients organize jobs, notes, photos and work-order histories by property. Workflow features and account setup depend on the service arrangement.

Do you provide preventive HVAC maintenance contracts?

Bakerss has a separate commercial HVAC preventive maintenance page for recurring equipment checks, filter programs and scheduled service planning. Explore commercial HVAC maintenance programs.

Do you offer emergency commercial HVAC service?

Bakerss accepts urgent work-order requests, but same-day and after-hours attendance is not guaranteed. Availability depends on location, job scope, staffing, equipment and required authorizations.

How are commercial HVAC repairs approved and billed?

Property managers can specify approval contacts, property details and any spending thresholds for review. Diagnostics, parts, labor, materials, access and any after-hours costs should be clarified and approved under the applicable agreement before work proceeds.

Which Horry County locations do you cover?

Bakerss accepts inquiries from Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Conway, Carolina Forest, Surfside Beach, Socastee, Murrells Inlet and nearby areas. Serviceability is confirmed based on the property location and scope.

Are your commercial HVAC services appropriately licensed?

A technician’s individual credential does not by itself establish that the business is licensed for every commercial contracting scope. Bakerss reviews each requested task and arranges regulated work only where the required South Carolina authorizations are in place.
