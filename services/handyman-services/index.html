Coastal Property Expertise

Why Myrtle Beach Properties Need Handyman Repairs More Often

Coastal properties are hard on building components. Salt air and humidity affect door hardware, exterior locks, hinges, screens, fixtures, fasteners and metal finishes. Seasonal storm activity tests caulking, exterior penetrations, gutters, doors and decks. Vacation rental turnover adds another layer of wear because guests use locks, doors, toilets, showers, TVs, furniture, blinds and appliances at a higher rate than a typical owner-occupied home.

That is why a handyman service in Myrtle Beach should not only be reactive. Bakerss looks for the small issues that commonly turn into bigger problems: loose toilet bases, worn toilet fill valves, missing caulk, weak door latches, rusted hardware, failed GFCI outlets, loose handrails, cracked trim and water-stained drywall. When those items are addressed early, the property looks better and avoids unnecessary emergency calls.

Salt Air

Corrosion & Hardware Wear

Exterior locks, hinges, screws, fasteners, shower fixtures and metal hardware can wear faster near the coast. Replacement and adjustment keep the property functional.

Humidity

Caulking, Doors & Drywall

Humidity and moisture movement affect doors, trim, caulk joints, drywall patches and small wall repairs. Regular maintenance helps prevent visible deterioration.

Guest Traffic

Vacation Rental Damage

Short-term rentals generate heavy use on locks, toilets, TVs, cabinet hardware, furniture, blinds, shower fixtures and walls. Quick repairs protect guest reviews.

How much do handyman services cost in Myrtle Beach?

Handyman services from Bakerss Property Maintenance start at $95 per hour. Many common repairs and installations can be quoted before work begins. Final pricing depends on the scope, materials, access, urgency and whether the work is one item or a larger punch list.

What handyman repairs do you handle?

We handle punch lists, door repairs, drywall patches, trim repairs, caulking, ceiling fan installation, light fixture installation, outlet and switch replacement, GFCI replacement, TV mounting, shelves, blinds, curtain rods, toilet repairs, faucet replacement, showerhead replacement and other common property repairs.

Do you provide handyman services for Airbnb and vacation rentals?

Yes. Bakerss provides handyman repair support for Airbnb and vacation rental properties across Myrtle Beach and Horry County. Common requests include between-stay repairs, lock problems, door repairs, running toilets, TV mounting, damaged walls, fixture problems and guest-readiness punch lists.

Can handyman repairs be combined with property maintenance?

Yes. Handyman work can be paired with property maintenance plans, Airbnb cleaning, interior cleaning, lawn care, pool maintenance, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, painting and landscaping. This is often the best setup for out-of-state owners and property managers.

Do you handle commercial or HOA handyman work?

Yes. Bakerss supports apartment communities, HOAs, condominiums, clubhouses, leasing offices, commercial spaces and managed properties with practical repair work, common-area maintenance and punch list services.

What handyman work do you not perform?

We do not perform major electrical panel work, major rewiring, HVAC installation or major repair, structural modifications, sewer work or major plumbing reroutes. Those items require the correct licensed trade contractor. When a job is outside handyman scope, we will say so clearly.
