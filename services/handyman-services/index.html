FAQ

Home Repair & Property Maintenance FAQ

What services can Bakerss handle?

Bakerss handles handyman repairs, punch lists, decks, fences, irrigation, window cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, HVAC filter replacement, storm prep, post-storm service, seasonal décor installation and removal, new construction punch list and final clean, concrete and asphalt support, carpet cleaning, lawn care, sod installation, pressure washing, pool maintenance, cleaning and commercial maintenance.

Do you install and remove Christmas lights or seasonal décor?

Yes. Bakerss can install and remove outdoor Christmas lights, wreaths and seasonal décor. Many clients supply their own décor, and Bakerss handles the labor, setup, removal and organization.

Do you repair fences and decks?

Yes. Bakerss handles deck building and repairs plus wood, vinyl and aluminum fence installation and repair. Common requests include gate adjustment, damaged section replacement, loose board replacement, railing repair and storm-related exterior repairs.

Do you service irrigation and sprinkler systems?

Yes. Bakerss handles irrigation and sprinkler system support such as head adjustments, broken sprinkler head replacement, zone troubleshooting, startup support, winterization support and irrigation checks tied to sod installation or lawn care.

Do you offer services for vacation rental owners who are not local?

Yes. Bakerss supports out-of-state and remote owners with property checks, Airbnb cleaning, handyman repairs, HVAC filter replacement, dryer vent cleaning, window cleaning, carpet cleaning, storm prep, post-storm documentation, lawn care, pool maintenance and recurring property maintenance.

Do you provide storm prep and post-storm cleanup?

Yes. Storm prep can include outdoor furniture securing, drain clearing, shutter assistance and property checks. Post-storm services can include debris removal, tarping coordination, damage documentation, photo reporting and cleanup support.

Do you work with builders or new construction?

Yes. Bakerss can support builders, investors and new homeowners with new construction punch lists, final clean, touch-up painting, pressure washing, sod support and closing-readiness work.

What work is outside your scope?

Bakerss does not perform major electrical panel work, major rewiring, HVAC repair or installation, structural engineering, sewer work, major plumbing reroutes or specialized trade work requiring a separate licensed contractor. We will identify those limits before accepting the job.
