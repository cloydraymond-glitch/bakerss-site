Bush & Hedge Trimming for Coastal Horry County Properties

Overgrown hedges and shrubs are one of the fastest ways to make a property look neglected — particularly for vacation rentals where guest photos and first impressions matter. South Carolina's coastal climate produces rapid shrub growth through the long warm season, requiring regular trimming to maintain a sharp, well-maintained appearance.

Bakerss provides bush and hedge trimming for homes, vacation rentals, and commercial properties across Horry County. Clean lines, consistent scheduling, and one trusted team — no need to coordinate a separate landscaping company alongside your lawn service.

  • ✓ Rapid growth — coastal SC humidity and warm temperatures accelerate shrub growth requiring more frequent trimming than inland areas

  • ✓ Vacation rental presentation — sharply trimmed hedges are essential for strong guest photo impressions
