Outdoor Lighting for Walkways, Landscapes, and Common Areas

Well-planned landscape lighting helps people navigate outdoor spaces and highlights features without harsh glare. The right design accounts for how the property is used, fixture placement, water exposure, energy use, and future maintenance access.

Landscape lighting projects

  • Walkway and path lighting around residential properties
  • Entry, landscape-bed, and garden accent lighting
  • Low-voltage landscape lighting troubleshooting and upgrades
  • Exterior common-area fixtures for managed properties
  • Timer, sensor, and lighting-control review
  • Lighting maintenance coordinated with landscape refreshes

How we plan the scope

We discuss the spaces that need light, the desired appearance, and how the area is used after sunset. Fixture selection should consider drainage, wiring method, corrosion exposure, voltage drop, and accessibility for future servicing. Electrical connections and any work requiring trade licensing or permits must be handled by the appropriate professional.

For an HOA or commercial site, design approval and existing utility or electrical infrastructure may affect the proposal.

Coastal Weather Matters for Exterior Lighting

The Grand Strand's rain, humidity, irrigation, and salt exposure can affect outdoor fixtures and connections. A good maintenance plan considers water-resistant installation details, fixture orientation, vegetation growth, and simple access for inspection. Lighting should not obstruct mowing, irrigation adjustments, or routine landscape work.

Pair Lighting With a Complete Exterior Refresh

Outdoor lighting is often more useful when planned with landscaping, irrigation repairs, pressure washing, or recurring HOA common-area care. Coordinating these trades helps avoid having to disturb finished landscape beds a second time.

Landscape Lighting Questions

Do you repair existing landscape lights or only install new systems?

We can review both repair and installation requests. The appropriate solution depends on fixture age, wiring condition, transformer capacity, and the scope of the problem.

Can exterior lighting be automated?

Many systems use timers, photocells, or other controls. We can discuss available options and arrange suitable installation or troubleshooting.

Will lighting be bright enough for security?

Lighting can support visibility, but it is not a substitute for a complete security assessment. Path and accent lighting serve different purposes from dedicated security lighting.

What areas do you serve?

We evaluate outdoor lighting projects in Myrtle Beach, Conway, Murrells Inlet, and nearby Grand Strand communities.
