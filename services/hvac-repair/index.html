Urgent AC repair requests

Need Emergency AC Repair in Myrtle Beach?

If your air conditioner stops cooling during hot, humid weather, contact Bakerss with the symptoms and property location. We will check available service options and advise on the next step. Same-day and after-hours appointments are not guaranteed.

AC Blowing Warm Air

Warm air from the vents can have several causes, including airflow, control, electrical or refrigeration-system problems. A technician can identify the actual fault.

AC Leaking Water

Condensate drain backups, overflowing pans and other moisture issues can damage nearby finishes. Report active leaks promptly and avoid operating equipment that presents an electrical hazard.

Air Conditioner Frozen Up

Ice on coils or refrigerant lines may indicate airflow or other system problems. Avoid chipping ice or attempting refrigerant work yourself.

AC Not Cooling? Common Problems We Diagnose

When an AC blows warm air, leaks water, short-cycles, freezes or will not start, an accurate diagnosis comes first. We assess symptoms and system condition before recommending repairs. Contact Bakerss for a property-specific appointment and service scope.

Air Conditioner Not Cooling

Diagnosis for systems running without adequate cooling, long run times, warm supply air or poor temperature control.

Weak or Uneven Airflow

Review of filter condition, accessible blower concerns, return restrictions and visible supply-air issues.

Frozen Coil / Icing

Evaluation of airflow restrictions, dirty coils and other conditions that can contribute to freezing.

Condensate Drain Problems

Service for backups, float-switch interruptions, pan concerns and visible moisture around equipment.

Thermostat Problems

Basic diagnostic review for blank displays, inaccurate readings, incorrect settings and systems not responding.

Electrical & Component Symptoms

Diagnostic support for breaker trips, capacitor/contactors, unusual sounds and failed starts.

Coastal HVAC operating conditions

Why Myrtle Beach HVAC Systems Work Hard

Grand Strand systems deal with long cooling seasons, high humidity, salt-air exposure near the coast, vacation-rental occupancy and heavy peak-summer demand. That makes airflow, condensate management, filter condition and preventive service especially important.

Humidity Control

Cooling comfort is not only temperature. A system that runs poorly may also struggle to remove indoor moisture.

Rental Occupancy

High guest turnover can increase filter loading and make an HVAC outage a time-sensitive property-management issue.

Condensate Risk

Long cooling cycles produce significant condensate. Drain restrictions can create shutdowns, leaks or property damage.

Heat Pump Service

Many coastal homes use heat pumps for both cooling and heating, so year-round system condition matters.

Coastal Exposure

Outdoor equipment closer to salt air may require more frequent visual inspection for corrosion and debris.

Documentation

Owners and managers can keep service history, photos and recommendations connected to the property record.
