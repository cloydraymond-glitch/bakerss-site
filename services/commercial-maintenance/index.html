Built for Property Managers

Commercial Maintenance That Reduces Vendor Chaos

A property manager may need hallway cleaning, a pool-area issue, a broken gate latch, a leaking fixture, landscaping attention, pressure washing, storm debris removal and a resident or tenant work order in the same week. When those needs are divided among multiple unrelated vendors, the management team becomes the dispatcher and quality-control department.

Bakerss is built around a different model: combine approved recurring maintenance, facility support, exterior care, cleaning, general repairs and documented work orders into one written scope. The goal is fewer handoffs, clearer responsibility and faster resolution for apartment communities, HOAs, condominiums, retail sites, offices and other managed properties.

For managers and boards, the value is not only the work itself. It is visibility: documented service activity, photographs, field notes, predictable communication, approval controls and a property history that can be referenced after the technician leaves.

Commercial Property Maintenance Questions

What types of commercial properties does Bakerss maintain?

Bakerss supports apartment communities, HOA and condominium common areas, clubhouses, pool areas, office properties, retail centers, strip malls, leasing offices and other managed commercial properties across Myrtle Beach and Horry County.

Can property managers track work orders and service activity?

Bakerss uses an operations platform to manage work orders, scheduling, dispatch, technician activity and service documentation. Client reporting can include work-order status, service notes, photos, arrival and departure records and completed-service history based on the account setup.

A retainer can include recurring grounds care, common-area cleaning, pool and amenity support, general work orders, preventive maintenance, exterior services, reporting and other approved property needs. Scope, frequency, response expectations and approval thresholds are defined in writing.

Can Bakerss provide daily or recurring onsite service?

Yes. Service can be structured daily, weekly, monthly or as a hybrid schedule depending on property size, recurring needs, work-order volume and management requirements.

How are licensed or permitted trade items handled?

Bakerss performs and coordinates work within the legally permitted scope of the company and the professionals assigned. Work requiring a specific trade license, permit or specialty contractor is completed or coordinated in accordance with applicable requirements.

Do you handle urgent commercial maintenance?

Urgent issues for active commercial clients are handled according to the written service agreement, account priority and approved scope. Response expectations should be defined during onboarding.
