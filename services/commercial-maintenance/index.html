Built for Property Managers

Commercial Maintenance That Reduces Vendor Chaos

Commercial properties do not fail in neat categories. A property manager may need hallway vacuuming, pool-area checks, a broken gate latch, a parking lot trip hazard, a pressure washing visit, mulch touch-up, storm debris removal and a leaking toilet all in the same week. When those tasks are divided between five vendors, the manager becomes the coordinator, dispatcher and quality-control department.

Bakerss is designed to remove that friction. We combine recurring maintenance, exterior care, cleaning, small repairs and commercial support services into one written scope. The result is fewer handoffs, clearer accountability and faster problem resolution for apartment communities, HOAs, condos, retail sites and managed commercial properties.

For property managers and boards, the value is not only the work itself. It is consistent reporting, photo documentation, predictable communication and a local owner-led team that understands how property presentation affects resident satisfaction, guest reviews, lease-ups, inspections and board confidence.

FAQ

Commercial Maintenance Questions — Answered

What types of commercial properties does Bakerss maintain?

We maintain apartment communities, HOA common areas, condominium associations, clubhouses, pool areas, offices, retail centers, strip malls, leasing offices and managed properties across Myrtle Beach and Horry County.

What is included in a commercial maintenance retainer?

It depends on the written scope, but retainers may include grounds care, common area cleaning, pool checks, handyman work orders, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, storm prep, post-storm cleanup, filter replacement, dryer vent cleaning, inspections and reporting.

Can you provide daily onsite service?

Yes. Daily onsite service can be structured for apartment communities and managed properties that need recurring cleaning, pool checks, grounds review or work order support. The exact schedule is defined in the proposal.

Do you handle emergency commercial maintenance?

Bakerss prioritizes urgent issues for active commercial clients according to the written service agreement, especially storm events, safety concerns, common-area problems and time-sensitive work orders.

Do you replace licensed trade contractors?

No. Bakerss handles handyman-scope maintenance, cleaning, grounds, exterior services and commercial support. Major electrical, major plumbing, HVAC repair, structural work and permitted specialty trades should be completed by the appropriate licensed contractor.
