The Retainer Model

One Contract. Everything Covered.

Managing separate vendors for lawn care, pool service, cleaning, and maintenance calls is a full-time job. Retainer contracts with Bakerss replace that entire coordination overhead with a single monthly relationship.

Our recommended hybrid retainer model gives your property a guaranteed monthly base rate covering up to 30 service visits, with per-visit overage pricing beyond that threshold. Materials are billed at cost plus 15%. No surprises, no unbundled hidden charges.

Every retainer client receives a monthly service report summarizing all completed calls, materials used, open items, and trending issues — delivered by the 5th of the following month.

What's Included

Services Covered Under the Retainer

Included

Service calls & general maintenance

Door lock battery replacement, keypad troubleshooting, appliance diagnostics, HVAC filter changes, thermostat resets, minor plumbing, smoke detector service, cabinet adjustments, screen repair, and all routine unit maintenance up to the visit allowance.

Included

Pool & spa maintenance

Weekly cleaning, chemical testing and balancing (pH, chlorine, alkalinity, calcium, cyanuric acid), filter backwash, skimmer service, pump visual inspection, equipment monitoring, and chemical supply billed at cost + 15%. SC CPO compliance maintained.

Included

Grounds & exterior maintenance

Mowing, edging, trimming, common area litter removal, parking lot sweeping, bed maintenance, mulch replenishment, seasonal color, and exterior upkeep. Grounds visits scaled to season — 2× weekly in summer, weekly in shoulder months.

Included

Amenity & common area upkeep

Fitness center, clubhouse, mailroom, stairwells, elevator lobbies, package areas, courtyard, fire pit, and grilling station upkeep. Light bulb replacement, minor equipment adjustments, and general cleanliness maintained on scheduled visit cadence.

Included

Pest control coordination

Scheduling and on-site coordination of pest control visits, application of Provider-supplied spray for routine insect control (gnats, roaches, ants), wasp and bee nest removal at accessible exterior locations, and follow-up re-spray within visit allowance.

Included

Move-in coordination

Pre-occupancy walkthrough and punch list completion, lock code reset for new resident entry, appliance function check, HVAC filter change, and thermostat programming for new tenants — all included in the retainer at no additional labor charge.

Why Preventive Beats Reactive

📉

Reduces reactive call volume

Based on service call data from comparable properties, quarterly lock battery replacement alone eliminates 15–20% of monthly service call volume — the #1 category of reactive maintenance requests.

📋

Written condition reports

Each quarterly visit produces a unit-by-unit written report delivered within 5 business days. Satisfies many landlord inspection requirements and provides documentation for insurance and investor reporting.

🔒

SC compliance

Smoke detector testing, HVAC filter documentation, and plumbing inspections during quarterly visits support South Carolina residential landlord compliance requirements for multi-unit properties.
