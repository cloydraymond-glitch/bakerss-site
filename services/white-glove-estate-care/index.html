Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about this service.

What is White Glove Estate Care?

White Glove Estate Care is Bakerss' premium recurring property-care program for high-value primary homes and second homes needing coordinated cleaning, grounds, preventive maintenance, inspections and priority service support.

Is White Glove Estate Care available for second homes?

Yes. The program is especially useful for second-home owners who want documented local oversight while they are away.

Can the plan include weekly cleaning and lawn care?

Yes. The approved scope can combine recurring interior cleaning and complete grounds care with other compatible property-maintenance services.

Does the program include HVAC preventive maintenance?

HVAC preventive maintenance and system observations can be incorporated into the estate-care scope based on the property's equipment and service plan.

How do owners know what happened at the property?

Recurring clients can receive service documentation such as technician notes, property photographs, service activity and work-order history.
