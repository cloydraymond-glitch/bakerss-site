Coastal Estate Preservation

Protecting the Details That Make a Home Exceptional

Myrtle Beach estates face distinct maintenance challenges: salt exposure on hardware and railings, humidity in closed rooms, heavy HVAC demand, wind-driven rain, and sensitive stone, stucco, cedar or paver surfaces. We identify observable concerns and coordinate appropriate maintenance rather than applying a one-method-fits-all approach.

Salt Air & Exterior Finishes

Review corrosion-prone hardware, fixtures, railings and vulnerable coatings. Cleaning and protective treatments depend on the material and specialist recommendations.

Humidity & Indoor Conditions

Observe thermostat settings, cooling performance indicators, odors and visible moisture concerns; arrange qualified evaluation when needed.

Hurricane Season Readiness

Prepare an owner-approved checklist for outdoor belongings, access, drainage observations and post-storm visual visits when safe.

Estate Care FAQs

Answers for Private Homeowners & Estate Managers

What is white glove estate care?

It is a customized, recurring service relationship for private residences that coordinates property upkeep, visual checks, cleaning, grounds, technical maintenance and owner-approved vendor work.

What is the difference between an estate caretaker and a property manager?

An estate caretaker focuses on physical condition, upkeep and coordination at a private residence. Property management may also include leasing, rent collection and other regulated management functions that are not automatically part of this service.

How much does estate property maintenance cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pricing depends on the property, square footage, systems, amenities, service frequency and scope. Bakerss prepares a tailored proposal following a property assessment rather than publishing a one-size-fits-all price.

Can you care for my luxury home while I live out of state?

Yes. An approved plan can include scheduled visual home-watch visits, property photographs, service documentation, maintenance coordination and pre-arrival preparation.

Do you offer pre-arrival home preparation?

Yes, by arrangement. Options can include cleaning, accessible system observations, outdoor presentation and coordination of approved service before an owner arrives.

Do you coordinate landscaping, pool, cleaning and HVAC services?

Yes. A customized plan can coordinate these service categories, with specialized tasks performed by appropriately qualified personnel and within applicable licensing requirements.

Can you maintain oceanfront and salt-exposed homes?

Yes. Care plans can address salt-exposed surfaces, exterior presentation, humidity observations, window cleaning and appropriate maintenance of coastal materials.

Do you provide discreet access and vendor coordination?

We can establish owner-approved access procedures and coordinate scheduled vendors. Privacy expectations, authorization and access details are documented during onboarding.

Can you inspect the property after a hurricane?

Post-storm visual checks may be arranged when authorities permit access and conditions are safe. These visits are not structural, insurance or code inspections.

Are your estate care plans annual or monthly?

Plans can be structured around ongoing, seasonal or customized schedules. The final frequency, scope, billing and cancellation terms are set out in a written agreement.

Do you have trained or certified technicians?

Bakerss works with experienced technicians with relevant training and certifications. Specific credentials and applicable trade licensing can be confirmed for the work being proposed.

Do you provide background-checked staff and references?

Please ask during your consultation about current personnel-screening practices and reference availability. We do not represent every team member as background-checked or promise client references without confirmation.

Can you help prepare a luxury home for sale?

Yes. Services may include deep cleaning, exterior presentation, small repairs and owner-approved punch-list work ahead of listing or photography.

What should be on a luxury coastal home maintenance checklist?

Typical priorities include HVAC and humidity, water intrusion observations, roofline and gutter conditions, exterior finishes, grounds and irrigation, pool systems, windows, storm readiness and documented follow-up.
