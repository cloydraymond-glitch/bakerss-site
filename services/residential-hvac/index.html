Common Home Air Conditioning Problems We Assess

An AC issue is not always resolved by a tune-up or a thermostat adjustment. Tell us the symptom so the correct diagnostic scope can be confirmed.

AC Runs but Does Not Cool

Warm supply air, long run times and inadequate cooling can be caused by airflow, electrical, refrigerant or equipment problems. A proper diagnosis determines the next step.

Heat Pump Not Heating or Cooling

If your heat pump struggles in either mode, describe thermostat behavior, abnormal noises and whether the outdoor unit operates. The cause should be verified before parts are replaced.

Frozen AC Coil or Refrigerant Lines

Icing can occur alongside airflow restrictions or other equipment faults. Turn off cooling, do not chip away ice and request assessment before restarting.

AC Water Leak & Condensate Trouble

Clogged condensate drains, pans or safety switches can interrupt cooling and damage finishes. Report active water accumulation promptly.

Thermostat & System Startup Problems

Blank thermostat screens, short cycling, breaker trips and units that will not start require safe inspection of controls and system components.

Weak Airflow & Hot Rooms

Uneven cooling, restricted returns, filters and accessible duct issues can reduce comfort and affect humidity control. Report the rooms most affected.

Homeowner FAQs

Residential HVAC Repair Questions

Answers to common questions before requesting a home HVAC diagnostic visit.

How much does an AC repair visit cost in Myrtle Beach?

The cost depends on diagnostic time, equipment access, parts and the approved repair. Bakerss can explain the applicable visit or diagnostic charge when scheduling; a repair estimate follows a review of the system. No fixed repair price is promised without an assessment.

Can you help if my AC is blowing warm air?

Yes. Request a diagnostic visit and describe whether the system starts, whether the outdoor unit operates and how long the problem has occurred. Warm air can result from several issues, so the repair should follow proper diagnosis.

Do you offer same-day or emergency AC repair?

Bakerss accepts urgent requests, but same-day, evening and weekend service depend on the service location, technician availability and the scope of work. Please call to confirm the next available option.

Do you service residential heat pumps and ductless mini-splits?

You may request troubleshooting for heat pumps, ductless mini-splits and conventional cooling equipment. Confirm the equipment type and model when scheduling so qualifications and availability can be checked.

What should I do if my AC is leaking water?

If it is safe, switch off cooling when water is accumulating and protect nearby finishes. Avoid bypassing condensate safety devices or handling energized components. Request an inspection of the drain, pan and related system condition.

Should I book maintenance or an AC repair diagnosis?

Request diagnostics when equipment is not cooling, freezing, leaking, tripping breakers or making unusual noises. Preventive maintenance is better suited to functioning systems needing planned inspections, cleaning and seasonal care.

Are HVAC services performed under the required licenses?

Requirements vary by job and technician credentials. Bakerss verifies the scope of work and required South Carolina authorizations before arranging regulated work. Do not assume every service or appointment type is available.
