Minor Electrical & Plumbing FAQ

What is the difference between minor electrical and licensed electrician work?

Minor electrical scope includes outlet and switch replacement, GFCI replacement, fixture and fan installation, and smoke/CO detector replacement. Work requiring a licensed electrician includes panel replacement or upgrade, new circuit installation, and permitted rough-in. We tell you honestly which category your repair falls into.
