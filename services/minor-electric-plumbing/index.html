The Repairs Homeowners and Property Managers Actually Need

Minor electrical and plumbing repairs are the service category where timing matters. A bathroom GFCI that fails before a Myrtle Beach check-in, a running toilet in a Surfside Beach family rental, a bad garbage disposal in a Conway student rental, or a leaking supply line in a Carolina Forest home can turn into a larger operational problem fast. These are not always full trade-contractor jobs; many are common, repeatable, handyman-scope repairs that can be diagnosed, quoted and completed efficiently.

This page is designed around that exact need. Bakerss is not advertising panel upgrades, rewiring, sewer replacement or major plumbing reroutes. We focus on fixture-level electrical work, fixture-level plumbing work, part replacement, installation, troubleshooting, punch lists and property-maintenance repairs that help owners avoid delays, guest complaints and preventable damage.

Because Bakerss also provides handyman services, property maintenance plans, Airbnb cleaning, interior cleaning, pool maintenance, lawn care and commercial maintenance, we can coordinate repairs as part of a full property-care relationship instead of leaving owners to chase multiple vendors.
