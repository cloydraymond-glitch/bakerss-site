A Strong Sod Lawn Starts Before the First Roll Goes Down

Sod installation is not just placing green grass over dirt. A new lawn succeeds or fails based on soil contact, grading, drainage, irrigation, sun exposure, grass variety and the first several weeks of watering. In coastal South Carolina, sandy soil, heat, humidity, drainage patterns and shade from homes, fences and trees all matter.

Bakerss installs sod as a complete lawn replacement service. That means we evaluate the yard, remove failing turf and weeds when needed, prepare the base, correct minor grade issues, apply starter fertilizer, lay sod tightly, roll or press the surface for soil contact and provide watering guidance for establishment.

The result is a cleaner, more professional finished lawn that supports curb appeal, HOA compliance, rental presentation and long-term property value.

Post-Install Care

New Sod Needs the Right Watering, Mowing & Lawn Care

Most sod problems happen after installation because the lawn is not watered correctly, is walked on too early, is mowed too soon or is neglected during the rooting period. New sod needs consistent moisture early, then a gradual transition into normal lawn care.

Bakerss gives customers simple establishment guidance after installation. We also offer weekly lawn care and property maintenance plans so the new lawn can be maintained correctly after it roots.

Water

First Weeks Matter

New sod needs consistent watering during establishment. Hot, windy and sandy conditions can dry sod quickly if watering is missed.

Traffic

Limit Foot Traffic

Heavy use too early can shift sod, damage roots and create uneven areas before the lawn is established.

Maintenance

Mow at the Right Time

Each grass type has different mowing needs. Cutting too short or too early can stress new sod during establishment.

FAQ

Sod Installation Questions — Answered

How much does sod installation cost in Myrtle Beach?

Sod installation pricing depends on square footage, grass variety, old grass removal, grading, soil prep, irrigation, access and cleanup. Bakerss provides free estimates so the price matches the actual lawn and scope.

What sod is best for Myrtle Beach?

Centipede is a common lower-maintenance choice for coastal South Carolina. Bermuda works well in full sun and traffic. Zoysia is dense and premium. St. Augustine can be useful for some shade conditions. The best choice depends on the property.

Do you remove old grass before installing sod?

Yes. Proper lawn replacement usually includes removing old grass, weeds and weak turf before grading and installing new sod.

How often should new sod be watered?

New sod needs consistent moisture during establishment. Exact watering depends on grass type, temperature, rainfall, soil and irrigation coverage. Bakerss provides simple watering guidance after installation.

How long before new sod can be mowed?

New sod should not be mowed until it has rooted enough to resist lifting and is ready for its first cut. Timing depends on grass variety, watering, weather and establishment.

Can sod installation be bundled with landscaping?

Yes. Sod installation is often paired with mulch, bed cleanup, shrub trimming, landscaping, lawn care, pressure washing and full property maintenance.
