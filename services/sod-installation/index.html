A Strong Sod Lawn Starts Before the First Roll Goes Down

Sod installation is not just placing green grass over dirt. A new lawn succeeds or fails based on soil contact, grading, drainage, irrigation, sun exposure, grass variety and the first several weeks of watering. In coastal South Carolina, sandy soil, heat, humidity, drainage patterns and shade from homes, fences and trees all matter.

Bakerss installs sod as a complete lawn replacement service. That means we evaluate the yard, remove failing turf and weeds when needed, prepare the base, correct minor grade issues, apply starter fertilizer, lay sod tightly, roll or press the surface for soil contact and provide watering guidance for establishment.

The result is a cleaner, more professional finished lawn that supports curb appeal, HOA compliance, rental presentation and long-term property value.

Before Installation

What Needs to Be Fixed Before New Sod?

Installing fresh grass over an unresolved problem can lead to the same lawn failure again. Site readiness is part of the sod quote.

Old Grass & Weeds

Remove failed turf and vegetation where needed. Weed-control treatments, when appropriate, are evaluated separately and performed only by properly authorized applicators.

Grading & Drainage

Identify low spots, standing water and poor surface runoff. Minor finish grading may be included; engineered drainage or major regrading requires a separate assessment.

Soil & Amendments

Check compaction, root-zone quality and sandy soil conditions. Topsoil or amendments should follow site needs and, when appropriate, soil-test results.

Irrigation Coverage

Confirm access to reliable watering before delivery. Sprinkler repair or new irrigation can be coordinated or separately quoted when within scope.

Site Access & Utilities

Review gates, slopes, material staging and underground irrigation or utility conflicts before sod cutting or grading.

Ants, Pests & Lawn Disease

Identify underlying pest or disease problems before replacement; licensed pest-treatment providers may be needed rather than covering the issue with new sod.

Post-Install Care

New Sod Needs the Right Watering, Mowing & Lawn Care

Most sod problems happen after installation because the lawn is not watered correctly, is walked on too early, is mowed too soon or is neglected during the rooting period. New sod needs consistent moisture early, then a gradual transition into normal lawn care.

Bakerss gives customers simple establishment guidance after installation. We also offer weekly lawn care and property maintenance plans so the new lawn can be maintained correctly after it roots.

Water

First Weeks Matter

New sod needs consistent watering during establishment. Hot, windy and sandy conditions can dry sod quickly if watering is missed.

Traffic

Limit Foot Traffic

Heavy use too early can shift sod, damage roots and create uneven areas before the lawn is established.

Maintenance

Mow at the Right Time

Each grass type has different mowing needs. Cutting too short or too early can stress new sod during establishment.

Coastal Turf Selection

Sod for Sandy Soil, Salt Exposure, Sun & Shade

The right warm-season grass is a site decision. A sunny inland lawn, shaded backyard and exposed oceanfront property do not have identical requirements.

Salt & Oceanfront Exposure

Salt spray, wind and irrigation-water quality can limit turf choices. St. Augustine is often considered for relatively higher salt tolerance, but cultivar and actual exposure matter.

Sunny, Active Lawns

Bermuda generally prefers full sun and tolerates wear well. Its rapid growth may increase mowing needs during the growing season.

Shade & Premium Finish

St. Augustine and some Zoysia cultivars tolerate more shade than Bermuda, but no warm-season sod thrives in deep shade.

Lower-Input Coastal Yards

Centipede is frequently chosen for acidic, sandy coastal soils and moderate maintenance expectations, provided sunlight and drainage are suitable.

Specific Cultivars

Ask about Floratam St. Augustine and Empire Zoysia availability. These are cultivar-specific choices, not interchangeable labels for every St. Augustine or Zoysia product.

Storm-Damaged Turf

Assess saltwater intrusion, debris, soil condition and drainage after storms before installing replacement sod.

Plan Your Project

How Much Sod Do You Need?

Measure the Install Area

For a rectangular lawn, multiply length by width in feet to estimate square footage. Break irregular lawns into smaller shapes, then add the areas together. Exclude beds, pavement and structures.

Example: 40 ft × 50 ft = 2,000 square feet before waste allowance.

Convert Square Feet to Pallets

Many sod pallets cover approximately 400–500 square feet, but coverage varies by supplier, grass variety and pallet configuration. For 2,000 square feet, a 450-square-foot pallet would require about 4.45 pallets before cuts and waste.

Final order quantities should be based on the supplier's actual pallet coverage and the site's layout.

FAQ

Sod Installation Questions — Answered

How much does sod installation cost in Myrtle Beach?

Installed sod pricing depends on the measured square footage, grass type, delivery, old turf removal, grading, soil preparation, access and irrigation readiness. We provide a project-specific quote rather than an unverified fixed price per square foot.

How much does sod cost per pallet or per square foot?

Material pricing varies by cultivar, supplier, season, delivery distance and pallet coverage. A pallet often covers roughly 400–500 square feet, but confirm the exact coverage before ordering. Installation labor and site prep are additional costs.

What sod is best for coastal South Carolina?

Centipede, Bermuda, Zoysia and St. Augustine are common warm-season options. Sun, shade, soil, salt exposure, irrigation and traffic determine the right choice. Specific cultivars can perform differently.

Is Floratam St. Augustine or Empire Zoysia available?

Floratam and Empire are specific cultivars. Availability and suitability should be confirmed with the sod supplier and the conditions at your property before they are included in a quote.

When is the best time to lay sod in South Carolina?

Warm-season sod generally establishes best while it is actively growing, often late spring through summer, with adequate irrigation. The ideal window varies by grass variety, weather and soil conditions.

Can you lay sod during a Myrtle Beach summer?

Yes, warm-season sod can be installed in summer, but hot weather increases watering demands and the risk of drying during delivery and establishment. Reliable irrigation and rapid installation are especially important.

How long does new sod take to root?

Initial rooting may begin within about one to three weeks in suitable growing conditions, but full establishment takes longer. Cooler temperatures, shade, poor soil contact or inconsistent watering can delay rooting.

How often should I water new sod?

New sod typically needs frequent watering at first to keep the sod and upper soil consistently moist without prolonged saturation. The schedule depends on weather, grass type, soil and irrigation coverage; follow the site-specific instructions provided after installation.

How long before I can walk on new sod?

Limit traffic until the sod has anchored and no longer lifts easily. Avoid heavy play, pets and equipment during early establishment; timing depends on rooting and weather.

When can I mow newly installed sod?

Mow only once the sod is sufficiently rooted and the grass has reached an appropriate cutting height. Use a sharp blade and avoid removing too much leaf material on the first cut.

Should old grass be removed before laying new sod?

Usually yes for a full lawn replacement. Removing dead turf, weeds and excess thatch improves soil contact and helps avoid uneven grades and underlying problems.

Can new sod be installed over a dead lawn?

Installing directly over dead grass is generally not recommended. Removal and soil preparation usually provide a more reliable base for rooting and drainage.

How do I fix standing water before installing sod?

Identify whether the cause is a low spot, compacted soil, blocked runoff or a larger drainage problem. Minor finish grading may help; French drains or engineered drainage require separate evaluation and scope.

Is sod better than seed or plugs?

Sod creates an immediate lawn surface and can reduce erosion while it establishes, but costs more upfront. Seed and plugs may be less expensive and take longer to fill in. Suitability varies by grass species and site.

How much sod do I need for my yard?

Multiply measured length by width for rectangular areas, add sections for irregular lawns, and account for cuts and waste. Divide the final square footage by the supplier’s confirmed pallet coverage.

Do you install sod for new construction and builders?

Yes, new-construction sod projects can be quoted once finish grade, access, drainage and watering arrangements are reviewed. Timing depends on project readiness and sod availability.

Can you replace small patches of damaged sod?

Yes, localized patch and spot replacement can be quoted, although minimum material orders, matching grass and delivery may affect cost.

Do you install sod for HOAs and apartment communities?

Yes, we can quote shared lawns, entrances, common areas and managed-property replacements with defined scope and coordination requirements.

Can sod be installed near a pool or patio?

Yes, provided drainage, edging, irrigation overspray and access are considered. Areas subject to heavy foot traffic may need a suitable grass variety or another surface.

Do you offer same-week sod installation?

Scheduling depends on crew availability, sod supply, weather, site preparation and irrigation readiness. Ask about the earliest available installation date; same-week service is not guaranteed.

Do you offer sod delivery and installation?

Sod material delivery and installation can be included in a project quote. Material sourcing and delivery requirements are confirmed after measuring and reviewing the site.

Can sod installation be combined with landscaping and lawn care?

Yes. Sod replacement can be coordinated with project-based landscaping and followed by lawn care after establishment. See our separate landscaping and lawn care service pages for those scopes.
