What's in a Commercial Cleaning Proposal?

We review the building and your expectations before confirming cleaning frequency, tasks, access arrangements, quality checks and pricing.

  • Property type, approximate size and traffic patterns
  • Restrooms, breakrooms, lobbies, shared areas and specified rooms
  • Routine cleaning versus a deeper initial reset
  • Frequency, preferred service window and property access
  • Supplies, waste handling and any specialty-work exclusions
  • Documented findings or service notes where included in the agreement

Need a tailored cleaning plan?

Send the location, square footage, frequency, photos if available, and any problem areas. We'll review your requirements before recommending a scope.

Common questions

Commercial Cleaning FAQs

Can we schedule cleaning outside normal business hours?

Tell us your access requirements and preferred service windows. Bakerss will confirm whether the requested schedule can be supported in your written proposal.

Do you clean apartment common areas and clubhouses?

Yes. We can quote lobbies, halls, amenity spaces, clubhouses and other approved common areas, including services coordinated with broader apartment maintenance plans.

Can we request recurring commercial cleaning?

Yes. Tell us the property type, approximate square footage, desired frequency, high-traffic areas and access arrangements so we can prepare an appropriate scope.

Are supplies and specialty services included?

Supply responsibilities, equipment, floor treatments, carpet extraction, window work, deep cleaning and other specialty services are specified in the proposal; they should not be assumed to be included.

Do you provide an estimate before work begins?

Yes. We review the required tasks, property size, frequency, access and any special conditions, then discuss a proposal for approval before starting billable work.
