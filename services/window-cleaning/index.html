Frequently Asked Questions

Answers About Professional Window Cleaning

How much does window cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pricing depends on window count, pane style, accessibility, stories, screen and track scope, glass condition and whether interior and exterior service are requested. Bakersss provides property-specific estimates.

Do you clean both inside and outside windows?

Yes. Clients may schedule exterior-only service or complete interior and exterior cleaning, including selected screens, tracks, sills and frames.

Can you remove hard-water stains?

Many mineral deposits can be improved with specialty treatment, but results depend on how long deposits have remained and whether the glass is permanently etched.

Do you clean vacation-rental and condo windows?

Yes. We work with vacation rental owners, property managers and condo owners and can coordinate service around occupancy and turnover schedules.

Do you offer recurring commercial window cleaning?

Yes. Recurring storefront, office, clubhouse and common-area glass cleaning can be scheduled weekly, monthly, quarterly or at a property-specific frequency.

Are screens and tracks included?

They can be included. The estimate identifies whether the scope covers basic screen dusting, full screen washing, track vacuuming or detailed track cleaning.

Do you clean multi-story windows?

Accessible multi-story windows may be serviced using professional extension equipment. Very high, unsafe or specialty-access glass may require a dedicated high-rise contractor.

How often should coastal windows be cleaned?

Many Grand Strand homes benefit from exterior cleaning two to four times per year. Oceanfront, storefront and high-exposure properties may need more frequent service.

Will you move furniture or window treatments?

Light items may be repositioned when safe. Clients should secure valuables and provide reasonable access to windows before service.

Do you clean skylights and glass doors?

Accessible skylights, French doors, sliders, transoms and other architectural glass can be added to the scope.

Can window cleaning be bundled with pressure washing?

Yes. Window cleaning is often paired with pressure washing, gutter cleaning, interior cleaning and recurring property maintenance.

Are you insured?

Bakersss Property Services LLC operates as an insured property services company. Current certificates can be provided when required for commercial or managed-property work.
