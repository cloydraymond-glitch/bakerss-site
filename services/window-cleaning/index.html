Planning Your Service

What Owners Should Know Before Scheduling Service

Professional window cleaning in Myrtle Beach begins with an accurate scope. Property size alone does not define the work. Window count, pane design, stories, rooflines, access, glass condition, screens, tracks, prior repairs, irrigation exposure and interior furnishings can all affect the equipment, labor and sequence required.

Bakerss approaches window cleaning as part of complete property care rather than an isolated task. Owners can coordinate glass service with interior cleaning, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, vacation-rental turnovers or a recurring White Glove estate plan.

Coastal residue and sprinkler minerals require different treatment than ordinary dust or pollen. Some deposits can be improved through specialty treatment, while long-term mineral etching may be permanent. The property assessment is used to distinguish routine cleaning from restoration-oriented work.

For HOA, condominium, multifamily and commercial properties, Bakerss also reviews access procedures, approved work hours, parking, insurance documentation, tenant or guest coordination and property-management requirements before scheduling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers About Professional Window Cleaning

How much does window cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pricing depends on the number and type of windows, stories, access, glass condition, and whether you need exterior-only or interior and exterior service. Send photos or request a property-specific estimate.

Do you offer recurring storefront window cleaning?

Yes. We can quote recurring storefront and commercial glass cleaning on a weekly, biweekly, monthly or custom schedule, depending on route availability and property needs.

Do you clean interior and exterior windows?

Yes. Exterior-only and interior-and-exterior service options are available. Screens, tracks, sills and specialty stain treatment are included only when specified in your quote.

How often should windows be cleaned near the ocean?

Coastal exposure varies. Some homes choose quarterly exterior service, while oceanfront properties and busy storefronts may prefer more frequent cleaning. We can recommend a schedule after reviewing the location.

Can you clean condo, HOA and apartment windows?

We quote accessible condo, apartment, clubhouse and HOA glass cleaning. Building rules, height, access and safety requirements determine which windows can be serviced.

Can you remove hard-water stains or salt residue?

Routine cleaning can remove many surface deposits. Mineral staining may require separate treatment, and permanent etching cannot always be reversed.

Can window cleaning be combined with pressure washing?

Yes. Ask for a coordinated quote for window cleaning, pressure washing, gutter cleaning and other property maintenance services.

Do you clean vacation rental windows?

Yes. We can coordinate window cleaning for vacation homes and rentals around access and occupancy schedules. Guest turnover cleaning is handled through our separate Airbnb cleaning service.
