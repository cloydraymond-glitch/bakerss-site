Material Guide

Every Major Flooring Type — Honest Assessment for Coastal SC

⭐ Best for STR

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)

100% waterproof core. 12–20 mil wear layer handles beach sand and heavy guest traffic without visible wear for 15–20 years. Looks like real hardwood in listing photos. Handles coastal humidity without expansion. Our first recommendation for any Myrtle Beach vacation rental.

All Wet Areas

Ceramic & Porcelain Tile

The correct specification for all bathrooms, kitchens, and entry areas in coastal SC. Impervious to moisture. Handles beach sand and grit without surface damage. Easy to sanitize completely between guest stays.

Primary Residences

Engineered Hardwood

Real wood veneer over a dimensionally stable core. Significantly better humidity performance than solid hardwood in Myrtle Beach's coastal climate. Appropriate for primary residences where wood aesthetics are a priority.

⚠️ Never in Rentals

Laminate — Avoid

Not waterproof. Swells at seams from vacation rental moisture exposure and persistent coastal humidity. Requires full replacement with LVP within 2–4 years of active STR use. We specify LVP from the start to avoid this cost cycle.

FAQ

Flooring FAQ

What flooring is best for Myrtle Beach vacation rentals?

LVP with a 12–20 mil wear layer and waterproof SPC core. 100% waterproof handles beach traffic, wet feet, and spills. Handles sand abrasion without visible wear for 15–20 years. For bathrooms and kitchens: tile always. Carpet should be avoided in active STR applications.

How much does flooring installation cost in Myrtle Beach?

LVP installation: $3.50–$7.50 per square foot installed. A typical 1,200 sq ft vacation rental in standard LVP runs $4,200–$6,600. All pricing includes removal of existing flooring, subfloor preparation, and installation. Free estimates for all properties.

Why is laminate a mistake for Myrtle Beach rentals?

Laminate is not waterproof. It swells at seams from vacation rental moisture exposure and persistent coastal humidity. Most laminate installations in active Myrtle Beach rentals require full replacement with LVP within 2–4 years. We specify LVP from the start to avoid this cost cycle.

Is old flooring removal included?

Yes — removal and disposal is included in all flooring installation pricing. After removal we inspect and prepare the subfloor before installation begins.
