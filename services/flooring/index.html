Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What type of flooring is best for Myrtle Beach vacation rentals?+
Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) is the nearly universal recommendation for Myrtle Beach vacation rental properties. It's 100% waterproof — critical for beach properties with wet feet and swimsuit traffic. It's extremely scratch and dent resistant for high-traffic rental use. It looks like real wood or tile. It's easy to clean and maintain. And it's significantly more affordable than hardwood. We recommend waterproof LVP with a 12-20 mil wear layer for high-traffic rental applications.
Is real hardwood a good choice for coastal South Carolina homes?+
Solid hardwood is generally not recommended for Myrtle Beach properties due to the high humidity causing expansion, contraction, and cupping issues. Engineered hardwood — which has a real wood veneer over a stable core — performs significantly better in coastal humidity and is our recommendation when clients specifically want hardwood aesthetics.
How much does flooring installation cost in Myrtle Beach?+
Flooring costs vary by material and square footage. LVP installation typically runs $3–$6 per square foot installed (material + labor). Tile is $5–$12 per square foot installed depending on tile size and pattern complexity. Hardwood runs $6–$15+ installed. Carpet is $2–$5 installed. We provide free detailed estimates for your specific space.
Do you remove old flooring before installing new?+
Yes. Old flooring removal and disposal is included as part of our installation service (or can be quoted separately if you prefer). Subfloor inspection and leveling is included in the installation scope.
How long does flooring installation take?+
A standard room (200–300 sq ft) typically takes 1–2 days. A full home flooring replacement is typically 3–7 days depending on size and complexity. We provide a timeline with your estimate.
Do you install flooring in vacation rental properties?+
Yes. Flooring upgrades for Myrtle Beach vacation rental properties are one of our most common projects. We understand the specific durability and aesthetic requirements of STR properties.
Do you install tile in bathrooms?+
Yes. Bathroom tile installation — floor tile, shower tile, and backsplash — is available as part of our flooring services throughout Horry County.
Do you service commercial flooring needs?+
Yes. We install commercial flooring for offices, retail spaces, and commercial properties in Myrtle Beach and Horry County.
