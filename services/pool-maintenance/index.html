Why Myrtle Beach Is Different

Coastal SC Pools Need Consistent Chemistry.

Myrtle Beach's subtropical climate is one of the most demanding pool environments on the East Coast. The combination of intense summer UV, high temperatures, heavy bather load, and 85% average annual humidity creates a chemical environment that breaks down chlorine faster than virtually any inland market. A pool that was balanced Monday can be dangerously out of range by Thursday without proper weekly management.

Salt air accelerates corrosion on pool equipment and metal fittings. Horry County's warm ground temperatures extend algae season well beyond what northern pool owners experience — green water can develop in days, not weeks, when chemistry slips. And with the Grand Strand's active vacation rental market, pools frequently serve a revolving door of guests whose combined bather load would stress a lightly managed residential pool year-round.

We've maintained pools across all of Horry County and understand what Myrtle Beach's specific conditions require — not just a generic maintenance checklist, but chemistry management calibrated to this climate.

Every Visit

What's Included in Every Service Visit

Every scheduled visit covers the full checklist below — no à la carte nickel-and-diming. One visit, everything done.

✓

Surface skimming & debris removal

Skim all surface debris — leaves, insects, pollen, and organic matter. Empty skimmer and pump baskets. Brush pool walls, steps, and waterline tile to prevent scale and algae buildup.

✓

Chemical testing & balancing

Test and adjust all six parameters: free chlorine, pH, total alkalinity, calcium hardness, cyanuric acid (stabilizer), and total dissolved solids. Results logged every visit.

✓

Chemical treatment & dosing

Apply all necessary chemicals to bring water into balance — chlorine/bromine tabs, shock, pH increaser/decreaser, alkalinity adjuster, algaecide, and clarifier as needed. All chemicals billed at cost + 15%.

✓

Filter inspection & backwash

Inspect filter pressure gauge, backwash sand or DE filters when pressure rises 8–10 psi above clean baseline, clean cartridge filters on scheduled rotation. Document filter condition.

✓

Pool floor vacuuming

Vacuum pool floor to remove settled debris, fine particles, and algae matter that skimming and circulation alone don't capture. Prevents staining and reduces chemical demand.

✓

Pump & equipment inspection

Visual inspection of pump motor, filter, heater, salt cell (if applicable), automation panel, and return jets. Note unusual sounds, leaks, pressure anomalies, or error codes. Flag for follow-up repair as needed.

✓

Water level check & adjustment

Confirm water level is at mid-skimmer for optimal circulation. Top off via hose if below threshold. Flag if water loss exceeds normal evaporation rates — potential leak indicator.

✓

Monthly chemical service report

Log of every visit — chemical readings before and after treatment, products applied, quantities used, equipment notes, and any items requiring follow-up. Delivered monthly.

Pool Maintenance FAQ

Frequently asked questions about pool service in Myrtle Beach and Horry County.

How much does pool maintenance cost in Myrtle Beach?

Residential pool maintenance starts at $150–$250 per month for weekly service visits. Vacation rental pool maintenance starts at $200–$350 per month. Commercial and community pools are quoted based on pool volume, bather load, and visit frequency — typically $680–$1,400 per month for apartment complex pools. All pricing is labor only; chemicals are billed separately at cost + 15%.

How often should a pool be serviced in Myrtle Beach?

Weekly service is the minimum recommended frequency in Myrtle Beach's subtropical climate, especially April through October. High UV, 95°F+ summer temperatures, and heavy bather load break down chlorine rapidly — pools serviced less than weekly can develop algae and chemical imbalances within days during peak summer. Bi-weekly service may be appropriate for lightly-used pools during cooler months (November–March).

Do you supply pool chemicals or does the homeowner?

Bakerss supplies all chemicals. We source, transport, and apply every product needed — chlorine, shock, pH adjusters, alkalinity balancers, algaecide, and clarifier. Chemicals are billed at our actual cost plus a 15% handling fee and itemized on each invoice. You never have to buy, store, or manage pool chemicals yourself.

Do you service vacation rental pools in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. We coordinate pool maintenance with your rental calendar — scheduling visits between guest checkouts, confirming chemistry before arrivals, and performing post-storm checks. A dirty or out-of-balance pool is one of the top complaints in vacation rental reviews. We keep it guest-ready at every turnover.

Do you service commercial pools for apartment complexes?

Yes. We maintain commercial pools for apartment communities and HOAs throughout Horry County. Commercial pool service is available as a standalone service or as part of a full property maintenance retainer contract that also covers grounds care, service calls, and preventive maintenance. SC DHEC compliance is maintained on all commercial pools we service.
