Murrells Inlet Property Maintenance — Local Knowledge Matters Here

Murrells Inlet covers roughly seven and a half square miles and holds some of the most diverse real estate on the entire Grand Strand. On the waterfront side, you have creekfront homes with private docks, screened porches facing the marsh, and outdoor living spaces that take the brunt of salt air and tidal humidity year-round. In Prince Creek — a 2,800-acre master-planned community built around the state's only TPC golf course — you have everything from Bellwood Landing's gated custom homes to Seasons at Prince Creek's 55+ community, each with distinct landscaping and exterior maintenance expectations.

Waterfront and creekside properties here develop algae and mildew on siding, docks, and paving faster than anywhere inland. Screened porches and outdoor decks take constant punishment from the marsh-side humidity and salt air. The mature tree canopy throughout Prince Creek and Wachesaw fills gutters with Spanish moss, pine needles, and oak debris faster than any other part of Horry County. We know these properties because we work on them constantly — and we know what they need.
