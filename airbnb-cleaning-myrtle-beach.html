Why Myrtle Beach Is Different

Airbnb Cleaning in Myrtle Beach Isn't Like Cleaning Anywhere Else

Myrtle Beach is one of the most active short-term rental markets on the East Coast. With over 5,800 active Airbnb listings competing across the Grand Strand and roughly 20 million visitors arriving annually, the pressure on hosts to deliver a flawless guest experience is real — and it starts the moment a guest walks in the door.

The cleaning challenges here are unique. Sand finds its way into bedding, bathroom tile grout, sliding door tracks, couch cushions, and kitchen cabinets. Salt air leaves residue on windows, faucets, and exterior-facing surfaces. Summer turnovers can happen the same day — a guest checks out by 10 a.m. and a new one arrives at 3 p.m. — which means cleaning has to be fast, thorough, and dependable every single time.

Bakerss Property Maintenance is a local, veteran-owned team that understands the pace and demands of the Myrtle Beach vacation rental market. We clean properties across the Grand Strand and build our schedules around your calendar — so you're always covered, whether it's a quiet Tuesday in January or a packed Saturday in July.

What Makes Myrtle Beach Hard to Clean

The Cleaning Challenges Every Myrtle Beach Host Faces

Coastal vacation rental cleaning is not the same as residential cleaning. These are the issues that come up in Myrtle Beach properties constantly — and what we do about them.

🏖️

Sand Everywhere — Including Where You Don't Look

Sand gets into bedding, couch cushions, under appliances, inside cabinets, and in bathroom tile grout. A surface-level sweep doesn't cut it. We specifically target the places guests leave sand behind that most cleaners miss.

🌊

Salt Residue on Fixtures and Surfaces

Oceanfront and ocean-view properties accumulate salt residue on windows, faucets, sliding door tracks, and exterior-facing hardware. Left untreated it dulls surfaces and creates a film guests notice immediately.

⏱️

Tight Same-Day Turnover Windows

During peak season, guests check out at 10 a.m. and new guests check in by 3–4 p.m. That's a 4-5 hour window to clean a full property, reset linens, and restock supplies. Being late or missing something costs you a 5-star review.

💧

Humidity, Mildew, and Odor

Myrtle Beach humidity creates mildew in bathrooms, musty odors in closed-up units, and damp-smelling towels when guests leave AC off. We address humidity-related issues every turnover so the property always smells fresh on arrival.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Large Family and Group Bookings

Over 60% of Myrtle Beach Airbnb listings accommodate 6 or more guests. Large families and groups mean more laundry, heavier kitchen use, more bathrooms to clean, and more sand tracked in from the beach. We scale to property size.

🏢

Condo Building Logistics

Over 80% of Myrtle Beach short-term rentals are apartments and condos. Tight elevator access, shared parking lots, and building staff coordination add real complexity to turnover timing that local cleaners understand and out-of-towners don't.
