Why Murrells Inlet Is Different

Cleaning Vacation Rentals in Murrells Inlet Is a Different Job

Murrells Inlet is not Myrtle Beach. It's a historic Lowcountry fishing village — quieter, more character-driven, and home to guests who specifically chose it over the crowds of the Grand Strand. Guests come for the MarshWalk's half-mile of waterfront seafood restaurants, the unspoiled beaches of Huntington Beach State Park, the sculptures at Brookgreen Gardens, and the inlet's fishing and kayaking culture.

The properties here reflect that character. Rather than the oceanfront high-rise condos that dominate Myrtle Beach, Murrells Inlet vacation rentals tend to be single-family homes, marsh-view cottages, waterfront houses on the inlet, and residential-style properties near Garden City Beach. Many have screened porches, outdoor kitchens, fire pits, and dock access — all of which require thorough cleaning between guests.

The cleaning challenges are also distinct. Guests arrive from the MarshWalk with seafood smells lingering in clothing and kitchens. Marsh mud and tidal debris come in alongside beach sand. Properties with dock access or kayak storage deal with a different kind of tracked-in mess than oceanfront condos. We understand all of it — because we work in this community.

  • Marsh mud, tidal debris & seafood odor management
  • Outdoor living areas — screened porches, decks, fire pits
  • Recurring service for second homes & seasonal owners
