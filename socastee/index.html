Socastee Property Maintenance — Established Neighborhoods, Serious Standards

Socastee and the Forestbrook neighborhoods that border it are some of the most established residential areas in the greater Myrtle Beach region. These aren't new construction subdivisions — they're communities with history, mature trees, larger lots, and homeowners who have been here long enough to know exactly what good property maintenance looks like and what a bad job looks like.

The Intracoastal Waterway runs along the eastern edge of Socastee, and waterfront properties here have their own specific demands — dock maintenance, waterfront lawn care, and the humidity that comes with living adjacent to a major waterway. Forestbrook's newer construction neighborhoods like Forestbrook Estates sit on larger lots with the kind of landscaping programs that take real expertise to maintain correctly. Arrowhead's golf course community holds homeowners and HOA management to high exterior standards. And the historic Socastee village area has older homes with character that needs careful cleaning and maintenance.

We've been in these neighborhoods. We know the property types, the HOA expectations, and the specific challenges that Socastee's soil, tree canopy, and waterway proximity create for homeowners who want their properties maintained properly.
