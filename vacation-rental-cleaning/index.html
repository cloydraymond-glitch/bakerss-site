Every Turnover, Every Time

What's Included in Every Vacation Rental Clean

Every turnover follows our full guest-ready checklist. Nothing gets skipped — not on the first visit, not on the fiftieth.

🚿 Bathrooms

  • Toilet scrubbed inside and out, seat and base wiped down
  • Shower and tub scrubbed — tile, grout, glass doors, and fixtures
  • Sink and vanity cleaned and dried
  • Mirror cleaned streak-free
  • Floors mopped and dried
  • Fresh towels folded and set out (if provided by owner)
  • Toiletries restocked (if owner supplies are on-property)
  • Trash emptied and liner replaced

🍳 Kitchen

  • All dishes washed and put away (or dishwasher run and emptied)
  • Countertops wiped and sanitized
  • Stovetop and exterior of oven cleaned
  • Microwave cleaned inside and out
  • Refrigerator checked — guest food removed, shelves wiped
  • Sink scrubbed and dried
  • Cabinet fronts wiped down
  • Trash emptied and liner replaced
  • Kitchen supplies restocked (if owner supplies are on-property)

🛏 Bedrooms

  • All beds stripped of used linens
  • Fresh sheets and pillowcases put on to hotel-fold standard
  • Pillows fluffed and arranged
  • Nightstands and dressers wiped down
  • Floors vacuumed and/or mopped
  • Closets and drawers checked and cleared of guest belongings
  • Blinds and windows checked and wiped if needed

🛋 Living Areas & Common Spaces

  • All surfaces dusted and wiped — tables, shelves, TV stands
  • Sofa and cushions straightened, vacuumed if needed
  • Floors vacuumed and/or mopped throughout
  • Trash emptied throughout property
  • Remote controls, light switches, and door handles sanitized
  • Entryway swept and wiped

📸 Final Check & Photo Confirmation

  • Full property walkthrough before we leave
  • Photo confirmation sent to owner — every room, every clean
  • Any maintenance issues (broken items, damage, low supplies) flagged immediately
  • Property locked and secured

How much does vacation rental cleaning cost in Myrtle Beach?

We provide free estimates and consistent per-turnover pricing so you always know what to expect.

Do you do same-day vacation rental turnovers?

Yes. We specialize in same-day turnovers — cleaning between a morning checkout and an afternoon check-in. We understand the time pressure of short-term rental turnovers and build our schedules around your booking calendar throughout peak season.

Do you clean Airbnb and VRBO properties?

Yes. We clean all types of short-term rental properties — Airbnb, VRBO, Vacasa-managed, and privately listed vacation rentals. We serve property owners and property managers throughout Myrtle Beach and all of Horry County.

Do you send photo confirmation after cleaning?

Yes. We send photo confirmation after every turnover so you can see the property is guest-ready — even if you're managing remotely or out of state. No guessing, no follow-up calls needed.

Can you sync with my Airbnb or VRBO booking calendar?

Yes. We work directly from your booking calendar — whether that's Airbnb, VRBO, iCal, or a shared schedule. We track your checkout and check-in times and schedule our team accordingly so every turnover is covered without you having to manually request each clean.

Do you change linens and restock supplies?

Yes. Our vacation rental turnover service includes full linen changes — stripping used beds and making them fresh for the next guest. We can also restock guest supplies (toiletries, paper products, kitchen essentials) if you keep a supply kit at the property.
