Our Turnover Standard

The Bakerss Vacation Rental Cleaning Checklist

Every single turnover in North Myrtle Beach follows this checklist — no shortcuts, no skipped rooms, no rushed finishes.

🍳 Kitchen

  • Clean all countertops and backsplash
  • Wipe exterior of all appliances
  • Clean microwave inside and out
  • Check and clean refrigerator interior
  • Scrub sink and faucet
  • Wipe cabinet fronts and handles
  • Restock dish soap and paper towels
  • Run dishwasher with tablet if needed
  • Sweep and mop floor
  • Empty all trash

🚿 Bathrooms

  • Scrub toilet inside and out
  • Clean shower and tub thoroughly
  • Remove soap scum and hard water deposits
  • Wipe mirrors and vanity
  • Clean sink and faucet
  • Mop floor
  • Replace all towels — hotel fold
  • Restock toilet paper, soap, shampoo
  • Empty trash and replace liner

🛏️ Bedrooms

  • Strip and remake all beds with fresh linens
  • Check under beds for items and sand
  • Wipe all nightstands and surfaces
  • Check dresser drawers for left items
  • Vacuum carpet or sweep/mop hard floors
  • Fluff pillows and arrange décor
  • Dust ceiling fans and light fixtures
  • Check closets and clear hangers

🛋️ Living Areas

  • Vacuum all upholstered furniture
  • Wipe all hard surfaces and remotes
  • Clean TV screen
  • Check for and remove all guest items
  • Wipe light switches and door handles
  • Sweep, vacuum, or mop all floors
  • Remove sand from entryway and rugs
  • Confirm all amenities are working

🌿 Outdoor Areas

  • Sweep porch, deck, or balcony
  • Wipe outdoor furniture
  • Remove any trash or left items
  • Check grill if applicable — clean grates
  • Confirm outdoor lighting works
  • Rinse beach shower if present

✅ Final Check

  • Walk every room before marking complete
  • Confirm all fresh linens are on beds
  • Verify all supplies are fully restocked
  • Photograph property for confirmation
  • Text owner or property manager — complete
  • Lock up and secure per property protocol
