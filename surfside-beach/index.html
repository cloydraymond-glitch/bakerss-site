Raised Cottages Need Different Maintenance Than Standard Homes

The raised beach cottages that define Surfside Beach's oceanfront have specific maintenance needs that flat-slab homes don't. The elevated structure means weather exposure on all sides — including underneath, where wind-driven rain and humidity collect against structural framing and decking. Stairs and railings take daily wear from sandy feet and salt air. The ground-level open area under the house traps debris and creates the kind of moisture environment that accelerates wood rot and pest activity.

We pressure wash the entire exterior including under-structure areas, inspect and repair decking and stairs, apply exterior paint and stain that's formulated for high-humidity coastal environments, and clean gutters and downspouts that handle the intense rainfall patterns this exposed stretch of coast produces. If you own a raised cottage in Surfside Beach and haven't had a full exterior service in the last twelve months, call us.
