Gutter Cleaning Myrtle Beach

Gutter Cleaning Built for Coastal South Carolina

In Myrtle Beach and Horry County, gutters collect not just leaves and debris — but pine needles, salt deposits, Spanish moss, and storm debris that accumulate faster than in drier climates. Clogged gutters cause water to overflow, damaging fascia boards, soffits, foundations, and landscaping. Bakerss keeps your gutters clear and functioning properly year-round.

Regular gutter cleaning is one of the most cost-effective ways to prevent expensive water damage repairs. We clean and flush gutters thoroughly, clear downspouts, and inspect for any visible issues — giving you peace of mind that your drainage system is working.

  • Full gutter cleaning & debris removal
  • Downspout flushing & clearing
  • Gutter flushing to verify proper flow
  • Fascia & soffit visual inspection
  • Debris bagging & removal from property
  • Vacation rental & investment property service
