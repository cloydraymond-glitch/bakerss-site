DHEC compliance

South Carolina Requires Documented Pool Records for HOA Communities

HOA boards and community managers are responsible for maintaining DHEC-compliant pool records. Non-compliance can result in pool closure, HOA liability and resident complaints. Bakerss maintains all required documentation on every visit so your board can demonstrate compliance during inspections.

📋

Chemical Logs

Documented pH, chlorine, alkalinity, calcium hardness and cyanuric acid readings on every visit. Logs maintained in format compliant with DHEC requirements and available to your HOA management team.

🏊

Water Balance

All chemical parameters maintained within DHEC-acceptable ranges. Shock treatment, algaecide and specialty chemicals applied and documented as conditions require during Myrtle Beach's high-heat summer season.

🔨

Equipment Inspection

Pump, filter, skimmer baskets, heater and safety equipment inspected on every visit. Equipment issues reported immediately to your HOA management team or community manager with documented repair recommendations.

Frequently asked questions

HOA Pool Maintenance Myrtle Beach SC FAQ

Do you provide DHEC-compliant pool maintenance for HOA communities?

Yes. Bakerss provides DHEC-compliant pool maintenance for HOA community pools throughout Myrtle Beach and Horry County. Every visit includes chemical testing, pH and chlorine adjustment, alkalinity management, skimming, vacuuming, filter service and required DHEC pool log documentation. South Carolina DHEC regulations require HOA pools to maintain documented records, and Bakerss handles that compliance on every visit.

How often does an HOA pool need to be serviced?

Most HOA pools in Myrtle Beach require weekly service during the off-season and twice-weekly service from May through September due to peak bather load, high temperatures and humidity. Bakerss adjusts service frequency seasonally and recommends twice-weekly service for summer to maintain DHEC-compliant water chemistry.

What happens if our HOA pool fails a DHEC inspection?

Bakerss works proactively to prevent inspection failures by maintaining accurate chemical logs and ensuring water chemistry stays within required ranges on every visit. If your pool receives an inspection notice, contact us immediately at 843-467-7136. We can provide emergency chemical treatment and documentation support to address deficiencies before a closure order is issued.

Do you provide pool opening and closing services for HOA pools?

Yes. Seasonal pool opening — including chemical startup, equipment inspection and initial chemical balancing — and seasonal pool closing are available for HOA communities. We coordinate opening and closing timing with your HOA management team and community calendar.
