Maintenance planning

Coastal HOA Preventive Maintenance Checklist

Salt air, humidity, heavy seasonal use and storm exposure make a recurring common-area inspection schedule especially valuable along the Grand Strand.

Monthly / Routine

Check entrances, landscape beds, walking surfaces, lighting, signage, trash areas, clubhouse cleanliness and visible safety concerns.

Quarterly / Seasonal

Review exterior mildew, drainage concerns, irrigation coverage, caulking, gate hardware, filters and amenity condition.

Before / After Storms

Inspect drains, loose furnishings, fencing, branches and access routes; document post-storm damage and prioritize urgent hazards.

This planning checklist is illustrative. Reserve studies, engineering assessments, pool regulations and specialized inspections remain separate from routine maintenance.

Frequently asked questions

HOA Maintenance FAQs for Boards & Community Managers

What does an HOA maintenance contract include?

An HOA maintenance contract can include grounds care, common-area cleaning, pool and amenity service, pressure washing, routine repairs, preventive inspections and service reporting. The exact scope, frequency, exclusions and pricing are defined in the proposal.

How much does HOA maintenance cost in Myrtle Beach?

Costs depend on acreage, common-area assets, pool and clubhouse requirements, visit frequency, labor needs and response expectations. Bakerss prepares property-specific pricing after reviewing the scope and, when appropriate, conducting a walkthrough.

Can Bakerss prepare a bid for our HOA board or RFP?

Yes. Share the RFP, service specifications, community map, bid deadline and desired contract terms. Bakerss can prepare a written proposal for board or community-manager review.

Do you work with community association managers and self-managed HOAs?

Yes. Bakerss can coordinate with community association managers, management companies, board representatives and self-managed associations, with designated contacts and approval procedures.

Can an HOA combine landscaping, pool care and repairs under one vendor?

Yes. Services can be bundled into a recurring agreement when they fit the property and available qualified personnel. Specialized or regulated trade work is scoped and assigned according to applicable requirements.

How can our association switch maintenance vendors?

Start with your current contract notice period, asset inventory, maintenance calendar and unresolved work orders. A transition proposal can define the new scope, start date, contacts and reporting expectations.

What insurance and technician qualifications can Bakerss provide?

Bakerss can provide relevant insurance documentation for vendor review. Its team includes trained, certified, licensed and experienced technicians; the credentials applicable to a particular trade or assignment should be confirmed during onboarding.

What is the difference between an HOA maintenance budget and a reserve study?

Routine operating budgets cover recurring services such as cleaning, landscaping and minor maintenance. Reserve studies generally plan for major future repair or replacement of association assets; they are separate from routine vendor maintenance proposals.

Does Bakerss offer preventive maintenance for coastal HOA communities?

Yes. Plans can address common-area wear, humidity and mildew, drainage observations, exterior fixtures, seasonal grounds needs, HVAC filter scheduling and storm preparation within the agreed scope.

Can HOA boards receive photos and maintenance reports?

For managed accounts, Bakerss can provide work-order records, technician notes, photos, status updates and service history according to the agreed reporting process.

Does Bakerss handle emergency or after-hours HOA maintenance?

Urgent requests can be reviewed based on technician availability, site access and the approved service agreement. Any after-hours coverage or response-time commitment must be specified in writing.

Which Grand Strand communities can request HOA maintenance?

Bakerss accepts inquiries from associations in Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Conway, Carolina Forest, Socastee, Little River, Surfside Beach, Garden City, Murrells Inlet, Pawleys Island, Litchfield Beach and Georgetown, subject to scope and scheduling.
