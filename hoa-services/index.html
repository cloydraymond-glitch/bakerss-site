Frequently asked questions

HOA Maintenance Myrtle Beach SC FAQ

What HOA maintenance services does Bakerss provide?

Bakerss provides HOA maintenance services including grounds and lawn maintenance, landscaping, pool maintenance and DHEC compliance, common area cleaning, clubhouse cleaning, pressure washing, painting, handyman work orders and full-service maintenance retainer contracts for HOA communities throughout Myrtle Beach and Horry County, SC.

Do you carry insurance for HOA maintenance work?

Yes. Bakerss is fully licensed and insured in South Carolina. General liability and worker's compensation documentation is available on request. We issue certificates of insurance naming your HOA management company or association as additionally insured for vendor onboarding compliance.

Do you work directly with HOA management companies?

Yes. Bakerss works with HOA management companies, community managers and HOA boards directly. We support work order response, provide monthly invoicing with itemized billing, and maintain consistent communication with your management team and on-site community manager.

Can you maintain multiple HOA communities?

Yes. Bakerss maintains multiple HOA communities across Myrtle Beach and Horry County with coordinated scheduling, consistent service standards and consolidated or per-community invoicing depending on your management company's preference.

Do you provide HOA pool maintenance that meets DHEC requirements?

Bakerss can provide HOA pool maintenance support including chemical testing, water-balance observations, service logs and equipment-condition reporting according to the approved scope and applicable South Carolina requirements.

How do HOA maintenance contracts work?

Bakerss provides a written scope of work outlining all included services, service frequency, pricing and exclusions. Monthly flat-rate invoicing is available for predictable HOA budgeting. Contracts can be structured as annual agreements or month-to-month depending on your HOA's preference. Commercial payment terms are defined in the written agreement for approved accounts.
