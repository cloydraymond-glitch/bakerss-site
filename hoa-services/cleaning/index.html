Frequently asked questions

HOA Cleaning Myrtle Beach SC FAQ

What does HOA cleaning include?

HOA cleaning with Bakerss covers clubhouse cleaning, common restrooms, fitness center and gym, mailrooms, management offices, meeting rooms, kitchen areas, lounge spaces and other shared amenity facilities. Services include vacuuming, mopping, surface sanitizing, restroom cleaning, window cleaning and trash removal on a recurring schedule.

How often should HOA clubhouses be cleaned?

Most HOA clubhouses in Myrtle Beach benefit from two to three cleanings per week during peak resident activity and once per week during offseason. Communities with active event schedules, fitness centers or pool-adjacent restrooms typically require more frequent service. Bakerss builds a schedule around your community's activity calendar.

Do you clean HOA restrooms and pool area bathrooms?

Yes. Pool area restrooms and common restrooms are some of the highest-traffic spaces in any HOA community. Bakerss provides thorough restroom cleaning including toilet sanitation, sink and mirror cleaning, floor mopping, supply restocking and odor control on scheduled visits.
