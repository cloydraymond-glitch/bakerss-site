Conway Property Maintenance — Inland Doesn't Mean Less Work

Conway sits 15 miles inland from the beach, but don't mistake distance from the ocean for distance from coastal property challenges. The Waccamaw River corridor, the tree canopy throughout the historic district, and the heavy rainfall that funnels through the Carolina Bays all create maintenance demands that are unique to this part of Horry County.

Homes along the historic Avenues area and the Riverwalk corridor have mature hardwood canopy — beautiful oak and magnolia trees that dump leaves, pine straw, and debris into gutters constantly. The older craftsman and bungalow homes in the historic district have painted wood siding, wraparound porches, and exterior woodwork that need regular cleaning, painting, and repair to stay in good condition. Near CCU, the neighborhoods see higher tenant turnover and investment properties that owners need to maintain between occupants. And in Conway's newer upscale communities — Barons Bluff, Castlewood, Aquila Estates — the expectations match those of any luxury neighborhood in Myrtle Beach.

We serve Conway homeowners, landlords, and investment property owners who want a reliable, local team that knows this city and doesn't need to explain where things are.

Conway Properties: What We See Every Day

The hardwood canopy in Conway's historic neighborhoods is spectacular — and relentless on gutters. Homes with oak and magnolia overhead need gutter cleaning two to three times per year to prevent the overflow damage that leads to fascia rot and foundation erosion. The older painted wood siding on historic district homes needs soft washing, not pressure washing — high pressure strips paint and drives moisture into wood that's already decades old.

Conway is also Horry County's most affordable residential market, which means more investment properties, more rentals, and more landlords who need a dependable maintenance partner they can call without having to manage each job themselves. We work with several Conway property managers and investment owners who have us on recurring schedules — cleaning, maintaining, and keeping their portfolios in rent-ready condition year-round.
