Myrtle Beach Property Maintenance — We Know This City

Myrtle Beach is not one neighborhood — it's a city of distinct communities, each with its own property demands. Grande Dunes is 2,200 acres of Mediterranean-style luxury homes with tile roofs, stucco exteriors, gated entrances, and irrigated landscaping that requires expert care. Market Common is a walkable urban village of townhomes and condos where manicured shared streetscapes set the standard. Carolina Forest is the family hub — large single-family homes on established lots with the yards and landscaping to match. And the beachside corridors from Arcadian Shores to the downtown oceanfront carry some of the densest concentrations of vacation rentals anywhere on the East Coast.

We've maintained properties in all of them. We know that Grande Dunes exteriors need soft-wash technique to protect stucco. We know that Carolina Forest lawns are predominantly Zoysia and Bermuda that go dormant brown in winter and need specific spring fertilization to green up right. We know that Airbnb owners along Ocean Boulevard need a team that can turn a unit between a 10am checkout and a 3pm check-in and send photo confirmation before guests arrive.

What Makes Myrtle Beach Properties Different

Salt air off the Atlantic accelerates corrosion on gutters, light fixtures, and exterior hardware. Stucco and painted siding accumulates black algae streaks faster here than anywhere inland — annual soft washing is not optional, it's maintenance. Grande Dunes homes with tile roofs need careful low-pressure cleaning that preserves the grout. Vacation rental properties along the beachfront cycle through hundreds of guests per year, and the sand, sunscreen, and salt they bring in demands a cleaning team that knows what guest-ready actually means.
