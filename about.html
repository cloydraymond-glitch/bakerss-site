About Bakerss Property Services LLC

Local Property Maintenance Built Around Accountability, Documentation & One-Vendor Convenience.

Bakerss Property Services LLC provides recurring and project-based property maintenance for homeowners, vacation rentals, HOAs, apartment communities, commercial properties and managed portfolios throughout Myrtle Beach, Horry County and surrounding Grand Strand communities.

Who We Are

A Property Service Company Designed for Owners Who Need Reliable Follow-Through.

Bakerss was built around a simple operating principle: property owners and managers should not have to chase multiple vendors, wonder whether a technician arrived, or wait days for vague updates.

Our model combines field service with documented communication. Depending on the account and scope, clients can receive technician notes, arrival and departure times, job photos, service documentation and ongoing maintenance coordination through the Bakerss client portal.

That makes Bakerss especially well suited for second-home owners, property managers, multifamily communities, HOAs, vacation rentals and commercial clients that value visibility and accountability as much as the repair, cleaning, landscaping or maintenance work itself.

Our Operating Standard

More Than “We Offer Many Services.”

Our goal is to make property maintenance easier to manage. We organize service around clear scopes, dependable communication, documented visits and a practical one-vendor approach across multiple property needs.
