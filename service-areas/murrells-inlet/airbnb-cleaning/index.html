Questions About Vacation Rental Cleaning

Does Bakerss provide Airbnb cleaning in Murrells Inlet SC?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance provides Airbnb cleaning, VRBO cleaning and vacation rental turnover cleaning in Murrells Inlet for short-term rentals, condos, beach homes, second homes and managed properties.

What is included in a vacation rental turnover cleaning?

Turnover cleaning can include kitchen cleaning, bathroom cleaning, bedroom reset, floor cleaning, trash removal, surface cleaning, guest-ready presentation, visible damage notes, restock observations and photo updates depending on the agreed scope.

Can Bakerss support same-day Airbnb turnovers?

Same-day turnover availability depends on checkout time, check-in time, property size, cleaning condition, travel schedule and existing bookings. Bakerss can quote recurring turnover schedules for active short-term rentals.

Do you handle linens for vacation rentals?

Linen service depends on the property setup and agreed scope. Bakerss can support bed resets with owner-supplied linens or coordinate linen expectations as part of the cleaning checklist.

Can you report damage or maintenance issues found during cleaning?

Yes. Visible damage, missing items, maintenance concerns, leaks, clogged drains, broken fixtures, HVAC concerns, exterior concerns or guest-related issues can be noted and routed for quote approval.

Do you clean outside areas for Airbnb guests?

Exterior guest-facing areas such as entries, patios, balconies, pool deck areas or outdoor furniture can be included when defined in the turnover checklist. Pressure washing and exterior maintenance can be quoted separately.

Can Airbnb cleaning be bundled with lawn care, pool maintenance or handyman service?

Yes. Airbnb cleaning can be bundled with lawn care, pool maintenance, handyman services, pressure washing, vacation home watch, window cleaning, gutter cleaning and recurring property maintenance plans.

How is Airbnb cleaning priced?

Pricing depends on property size, bedroom and bathroom count, checkout condition, same-day timing, linen scope, restocking requirements, frequency, access, travel and any bundled maintenance services.

Do you work with property managers?

Yes. Bakerss can support property managers, owner-managed rentals, second-home owners and individual Airbnb or VRBO hosts with recurring cleaning and property maintenance support.
