Murrells Inlet Property Conditions

Built for Live Oak Roots, Marsh Humidity and HOA Standards

Murrells Inlet properties face a different maintenance profile than oceanfront Myrtle Beach or inland Conway. Mature live oaks across Wachesaw Plantation, Prince Creek, Collins Creek and surrounding neighborhoods create shade, debris loading and root pressure that affect decks, fences, pavers, walkways, gutters, irrigation heads and outdoor structures. Tidal marsh humidity accelerates wear on hinges, locks, gate hardware, exterior fixtures and moving parts.

That is why this page is not limited to basic handyman work. The real Murrells Inlet customer often needs one company that can handle the repair list, the exterior issue, the HOA notice, the post-storm cleanup, the STR turnover add-on and the recurring maintenance item without making the owner coordinate five vendors.

Bakerss is positioned for that exact need: practical repairs, property maintenance, cleaning, exterior care and add-on services for homeowners, second-home owners, rental investors, HOAs, builders and commercial sites.

FAQ

Murrells Inlet Handyman & Added Services FAQ

What services did Bakerss add for Murrells Inlet?

Added services include holiday and seasonal décor, fence installation and repair, irrigation and sprinkler systems, window cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, HVAC filter replacement, storm prep and post-storm services, new construction punch list and clean, deck building and repairs, concrete and asphalt services, carpet cleaning and broader home repair support.

Do you offer storm prep before hurricanes?

Yes. Pre-storm work can include patio furniture tie-down, drain clearing, shutter installation, exterior checks and generator readiness checks. Post-storm support can include debris removal, tarping coordination, photo documentation and cleanup.

Do you service irrigation systems after sod installation?

Yes. Irrigation is a natural follow-up to lawn care and sod work. We can help identify zone issues, adjust heads, coordinate winterization, check coverage and recommend repairs when new sod needs proper watering coverage.

Can you replace HVAC filters for out-of-state owners?

Yes. Bakerss can provide scheduled HVAC filter replacement on monthly or quarterly visits for STRs, second homes and managed properties. This is filter replacement only, not HVAC repair.

Do you handle builder punch list and final clean work?

Yes. Builders and remodelers can use Bakerss for minor punch list items, final cleaning, touch-up painting, pressure washing, sod coordination and closing-ready presentation.

Can these services be bundled into a maintenance plan?

Yes. Many services fit naturally into a monthly or quarterly property maintenance plan, especially filter replacement, window cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, irrigation checks, storm prep, STR repairs and seasonal property checks.
