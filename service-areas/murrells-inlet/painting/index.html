Why Murrells Inlet painting is different

Paint Has to Fight Humidity, Oak Tannins, Salt Air and Shade

A Murrells Inlet paint job is not the same job as a typical inland repaint or a standard Myrtle Beach condo refresh. Homes near the inlet and along the Prince Creek corridor deal with heavy live oak canopy, high humidity, marsh moisture, wind-driven rain, pollen, and tannin deposits from oak leaves and acorns. Those conditions affect how paint bonds, how long exterior coatings last, and how much preparation is required before finish coats are applied.

That is why Bakerss approaches Murrells Inlet painting as a preparation-first service. We inspect caulking, siding, trim, fascia, soffits, decks, doors, shutters, and interior moisture-prone areas before quoting. When needed, we coordinate soft washing, mildew treatment, scraping, sanding, priming, drywall repair, and caulking before paint is applied.

Preparation standard

The Murrells Inlet Paint Prep Process

1

Surface and moisture inspection

We inspect siding, trim, drywall, decks, cabinets, caulk joints, wood rot risk, mildew, tannin staining, and previous paint failure before quoting.

2

Cleaning and mildew treatment

Exterior surfaces are cleaned before painting. Soft washing is recommended where oak tannins, marsh mildew, pollen, and salt film are present.

3

Repairs, sanding, caulking and primer

Loose paint is removed, glossy surfaces are deglossed, cracks are caulked, repairs are completed, and primer is applied where needed for adhesion and stain blocking.

4

Finish coats and cleanup

We apply appropriate interior or exterior coatings, protect floors and landscaping, clean the work area, and complete a final walkthrough.

FAQ

Painting FAQ — Murrells Inlet, SC

How much does painting cost in Murrells Inlet?+
Single rooms usually start around $300–$650. Exterior trim and door projects often start around $650–$2,500. Full exterior painting commonly starts around $3,500 for smaller single-story homes and increases based on size, height, prep, repairs, and coating system.
Do you soft wash before exterior painting?+
Yes, when needed. In Murrells Inlet, soft washing is often important before exterior painting because live oak tannins, mildew, pollen, salt film, and marsh moisture can interfere with adhesion and finish quality.
How long does exterior paint last in Murrells Inlet?+
With proper prep and quality exterior coatings, many Murrells Inlet homes can expect roughly 6–9 years depending on exposure, shade, moisture, product quality, and maintenance. Homes under heavy live oak canopy or near marsh conditions may need earlier touch-ups.
Do you paint Prince Creek and Wachesaw Plantation homes?+
Yes. We serve Prince Creek, Wachesaw Plantation, TideWater, Collins Creek Landing, MarshWalk-area properties, and surrounding Murrells Inlet neighborhoods.
Do you handle drywall repairs before painting?+
Yes. We can patch holes, repair small drywall damage, sand, prime, and prepare surfaces before paint. Larger damage or water issues are quoted separately.
Do you paint vacation rentals between guests?+
Yes. We offer rental refresh painting, scuff repair, touch-ups, accent walls, and quick-turn interior updates for Airbnb and vacation rental owners when scheduling allows.

Local service guidance · Murrells Inlet

Surface preparation matters more than a generic price table

Before painting, inspect peeling coatings, damp areas, loose caulk, damaged drywall and the substrate. Correct surface preparation and compatible products matter to finish quality.

An estimate should specify rooms or elevations, prep work, primer, number of coats, paint product and exclusions. Rental touch-up painting differs from full-interior or exterior repainting.

Exterior schedules depend on weather, dry time, access, HOA requirements and the property condition. Pricing should be based on inspected scope rather than outdated fixed ranges.
