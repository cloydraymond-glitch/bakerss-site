Murrells Inlet neighborhood cleaning breakdown

Murrells Inlet Has Three Completely Different Property Markets — Each With Different Cleaning Needs

Murrells Inlet is the Seafood Capital of South Carolina and one of the most eclectic property markets on the Grand Strand — ranging from upscale golf club communities like Wachesaw Plantation to working waterfront neighborhoods to newer master-planned subdivisions in Prince Creek. Each requires a distinct cleaning approach.

🏌️ Prince Creek & Wachesaw Plantation

Prince Creek is the largest planned community in Murrells Inlet — thousands of homes spread across sub-communities including Blackmoor, TideWater at Plantation Bay, Collins Creek Landing, Belle Isle Yacht Club and others. Properties here range from townhomes and condos to large single-family homes on golf course lots, and the HOA standards across these communities are high. Many Prince Creek homeowners are retirees or second-home buyers who moved down from the Mid-Atlantic or Northeast — they want cleaning that matches the standard of the neighborhood, not a budget contractor who services beach rentals. Wachesaw Plantation is a private gated golf club community with even higher presentation expectations for its member homeowners.

🦞 MarshWalk & Waterfront Corridor

The Murrells Inlet MarshWalk is a half-mile boardwalk lined with restaurants, bars, seafood markets and retail shops that draws hundreds of thousands of visitors annually. Commercial properties in and around the MarshWalk corridor face the same cleaning demands as any high-traffic hospitality district — restrooms, floors, kitchens, customer-facing areas and employee spaces all need consistent cleaning to match the reputation of a destination dining market. Offices and professional services near the waterfront serve a professional clientele who judge the space accordingly.

🏡 Second Homes & Inlet Neighborhoods

Much of the residential real estate around Murrells Inlet proper — the neighborhoods near the water, on the inlet side, away from the planned communities — is second-home and vacation rental inventory owned by families who have held properties for decades. These homes sit empty for months at a time between visits, accumulating dust, developing humidity issues in closets and bathrooms, and occasionally showing the effects of deferred maintenance. Seasonal deep cleaning before owner visits, recurring light cleaning to maintain properties during off-season and move-out support when longtime owners sell are the dominant cleaning needs in these neighborhoods.

Garden City Beach & South Strand Coverage

Bakerss covers not just Murrells Inlet proper but the Garden City Beach neck to the north and the South Strand corridor toward Pawleys Island. Properties between Murrells Inlet and Garden City Beach share the inlet and marsh geography — with similar humidity and moisture challenges — and are served as part of the same Murrells Inlet service area without a separate zone charge.

Second homes and seasonal cleaning

Murrells Inlet Has More Second Homes Per Capita Than Almost Any Horry County Community

A second home that sits empty from October through March accumulates problems that the owner won't discover until they walk in the door for spring. Dust settles on every surface. Humidity infiltrates closets and bathroom grout. Insects find their way in through imperfect seals. The refrigerator may have been left with something inside. A property that was clean when the owners locked up in September is not clean in April.

Bakerss provides pre-arrival cleaning for Murrells Inlet second-home owners — a thorough cleaning scheduled to be complete a day or two before the owner arrives, so the first experience is a property that feels like it was maintained, not abandoned. We also provide light recurring cleaning on agreed schedules during vacant periods to prevent the buildup that makes pre-arrival cleaning a bigger job than it needs to be.
