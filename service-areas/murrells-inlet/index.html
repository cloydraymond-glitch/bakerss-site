Property Maintenance in Murrells Inlet, SC

Murrells Inlet's property landscape is defined by its waterway access, established golf communities, and the kind of property values that demand consistent, professional maintenance. Prince Creek — the community's largest master-planned development — encompasses multiple neighborhoods, a golf course, and active HOA structures with specific maintenance standards. Wachesaw Plantation and Collins Creek Landing add luxury waterway and golf community segments where maintenance expectations are among the highest in Horry County.

The marsh and tidal creek environment in Murrells Inlet creates humidity levels that accelerate mildew and algae growth on all exterior surfaces, particularly on properties adjacent to the waterway. Epoxy garage floors are increasingly in demand in the community's larger homes and golf-community properties. Pool maintenance is consistent given the warm-season length and the growing STR market near the MarshWalk corridor. Sod installation is active in Murrells Inlet's newer subdivisions and on established properties replacing salt-damaged or thin turf.
