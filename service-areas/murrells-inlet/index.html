Local Knowledge

Maintaining Property in the Seafood Capital

Murrells Inlet property care is shaped by two things: the marsh and the HOA. Salt-laden humidity off the inlet works on every exterior surface year-round — corroding pool equipment and rails, feeding mildew on north-facing siding, and dulling paint faster than inland neighborhoods see. And much of the community lives in planned developments where amenity centers, common areas, and architectural standards mean property care comes with documentation requirements, not just a handshake. Bakerss runs both playbooks: marsh-aware exterior maintenance for waterfront and bluff homes, and board-ready service records for the HOA communities that make up most of the inlet's growth.

From golf-course homes along the TPC corridor to creek-front properties off the Marsh Walk, one team handles the lawn, the pool, the pressure washing, the epoxy garage floor, and the punch list — with the same standard the inlet expects from its restaurants applied to its contractors.
