📍 Murrells Inlet: Seafood Capital & Pirate Lore

Murrells Inlet traces its history to the 17th century and is said to have been a favorite hideout of the pirate Blackbeard — historical accounts place him sheltering in the inlet's protected waterways. The area developed around rice cultivation and maritime commerce before becoming the destination it is today.

The Murrells Inlet Marsh Walk is a half-mile boardwalk flanked by acclaimed seafood restaurants, and the town's 37-structure historic district represents one of the most intact 19th-century coastal communities in South Carolina. Property here carries real heritage value.

For property owners in Murrells Inlet, that heritage value means every improvement must match the standard the community has held for centuries. Our solid commercial epoxy system is the only specification that holds long-term in Murrells Inlet’s saltwater-adjacent climate.

Murrells Inlet SC Epoxy Floor FAQ

Does Murrells Inlet's saltwater environment affect garage floor coatings?+
Significantly. Properties within a few miles of the inlet experience elevated salt air exposure that accelerates concrete surface degradation and creates adhesion challenges for water-based coatings. Diamond grinding removes the compromised surface layer, and our moisture barrier primer addresses the vapor transmission common in low-elevation coastal properties throughout Murrells Inlet.

How long does epoxy last?+
With 100% solids commercial epoxy installed correctly on a diamond-ground, moisture-primed surface, 20–30 years is a realistic lifespan for residential garage use. Big-box water-based products typically peel within 1–3 years. The difference is entirely in the solids content, surface preparation, and system design.
