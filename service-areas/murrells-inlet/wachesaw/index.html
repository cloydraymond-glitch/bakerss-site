Specific to Wachesaw Plantation East

Care for homes in a gated golf-course community

Wachesaw Plantation East is a gated residential golf community in Murrells Inlet. It is separate from Wachesaw Plantation along the Waccamaw River. The distinction matters when arranging entrance instructions, quoting work or determining which property rules apply.

Access and scheduling

Before a visit, confirm the current gate procedure, approved work hours, equipment access and any parking restrictions supplied by the homeowner or authorized manager. We do not assume access arrangements are identical across gated communities.

Golf-course-adjacent lots

Some properties face the golf course while others do not. Lawn and landscape scopes should reflect the actual lot, irrigation zones, shade, slopes and boundaries—not an assumed course-facing layout.

Tree cover and roof drainage

Gutter service frequency depends on the trees near your roof, gutter guards and observed debris. A gutter inspection can identify accessible downspouts or areas needing separate repairs; no universal quarterly schedule is appropriate for every home.

How a Wachesaw Plantation East property quote works

We price actual work, not an assumed neighborhood-wide rate. For lawn service, that means the accessible turf and trim areas; for gutter cleaning, the roofline and safe access; for repairs, the task and materials. Written scope helps prevent misunderstandings.

Questions from local homeowners

Wachesaw Plantation East property-care FAQs

Is Wachesaw Plantation East the same as Wachesaw Plantation?

No. They are separate Murrells Inlet communities with different golf courses and access arrangements. This page specifically addresses Wachesaw Plantation East.

Can Bakerss enter through the gate for scheduled maintenance?

We coordinate using the valid access instructions provided by the property owner or authorized manager. Entry remains subject to the community's current requirements.

How often should gutters be cleaned under large trees?

Frequency depends on actual overhanging branches, debris buildup, guards and drainage performance. We can discuss inspections and a tailored recurring schedule rather than guarantee that one interval fits every property.

Can lawn care and exterior washing be combined?

Yes, where scheduling, access and the approved service scope allow. Each task and its frequency should be defined in the quote.

Do you maintain the golf course or association common areas?

Not without a separate agreement with the authorized property operator. This page offers homeowner property services; it does not imply an association or club contract.

How are prices determined?

Prices depend on the actual property, work requested, access, frequency, labor and materials. Request a current quote rather than relying on historical pricing ranges.
