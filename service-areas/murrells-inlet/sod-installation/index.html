Murrells Inlet Lawn Replacement

Sod Installation Built for Inlet Soil, Shade and Drainage

Murrells Inlet sod jobs are not all the same. A full-sun front lawn in Collins Creek Landing does not need the same grass as a shaded Wachesaw property under live oak canopy. A Prince Creek home with HOA expectations does not need the same preparation as a low, marsh-adjacent yard that stays wet after heavy rain. The best sod installation starts with site conditions, not a generic grass recommendation.

Bakerss evaluates sunlight, drainage, grade, soil condition, irrigation access, traffic and the property’s visual standard before recommending sod. That matters in Murrells Inlet because lawn failure usually comes from one of four causes: wrong grass type, poor soil prep, low spots that hold water, or watering mistakes during the first few weeks after installation.

We install sod for homeowners, vacation rental owners, real estate listings, HOA properties, apartment and commercial grounds, and second-home owners who need a local team that can restore curb appeal without constant follow-up.

Grass selection is the difference between a lawn that establishes and a lawn that fights the property. We match sod to the actual Murrells Inlet site: sun, shade, moisture, traffic, HOA standard and maintenance expectations.

🌿
Low Maintenance

Centipede Sod

Popular across Horry County for lower-maintenance residential lawns. Works best in sunny to lightly shaded areas with proper soil preparation. Good fit for many standard Murrells Inlet homes where the owner wants clean curb appeal without premium maintenance intensity.

A dense, premium lawn option with excellent appearance when maintained properly. Often a strong choice for higher-value properties, HOA communities and owners who want a finished, upscale lawn look.

Murrells Inlet-Specific Conditions

Why Sod Fails in Murrells Inlet — and How We Prevent It

Live Oak Shade

Wachesaw and Mature-Canopy Lots

Heavy oak canopy creates shade, leaf drop, acorn debris and root competition. Installing full-sun turf under deep shade is a failure path. We evaluate actual light exposure and recommend the right grass or landscape alternative.

Drainage

Marsh-Adjacent Moisture

Some Murrells Inlet properties hold water after storms or irrigation. Sod cannot establish well over soft, poorly draining soil. Low spots and grade issues should be corrected before sod is installed.

Soil Prep

Sandy Coastal Soil

Sandy soil can drain fast and lack nutrients. We prepare the surface, remove debris, correct grade, loosen compacted areas and set the lawn up for root contact instead of simply laying sod over a weak base.

HOA Presentation

Prince Creek Curb Appeal

Prince Creek and nearby HOA communities demand clean edges, uniform coverage and consistent presentation. We install with straight seams, tight cuts, proper edges and cleanup suited for visible community standards.

Rental Wear

Vacation Rental Foot Traffic

Rental properties can see concentrated traffic near driveways, patios, pools and walkways. Sod selection and layout should account for where guests walk, unload, grill and gather.

Watering

First Weeks Matter Most

The installation is only the first half. New sod needs correct watering during establishment. We provide clear post-install guidance so the lawn is not under-watered, over-watered or cut too early.

Sod Installation Questions — Murrells Inlet

What type of sod is best for Murrells Inlet?

The best sod depends on sun, shade, irrigation, use and maintenance expectations. Centipede is common for lower maintenance, Bermuda is strong for full sun and traffic, St. Augustine can help in partial shade, and Zoysia is a dense premium option.

How much does sod installation cost in Murrells Inlet?

Pricing is based on square footage, grass type, removal, prep, grading, delivery access and drainage conditions. We measure the job and provide a clear quote before work begins.

Do you install sod in Prince Creek and Wachesaw?

Yes. We install sod throughout Prince Creek, Wachesaw Plantation, Wachesaw East, Collins Creek Landing, Tidewater Plantation and surrounding Murrells Inlet neighborhoods.

Can you remove the old lawn before installing new sod?

Yes. Old grass, weeds, dead turf and debris can be removed before grading, soil prep and new sod installation.

Do you fix low spots before installing sod?

Yes. Low spots, poor grade and standing water should be addressed before sod is installed. We identify these issues during the estimate and quote prep accordingly.

Do you maintain the lawn after sod installation?

Yes. We offer recurring lawn care, edging, trimming, blowing and property maintenance after the new sod establishes.
