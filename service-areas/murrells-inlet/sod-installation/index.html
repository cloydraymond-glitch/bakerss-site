Local sod strategy

Choosing the Right Sod for Murrells Inlet Conditions

There is no one-size-fits-all sod choice for every Murrells Inlet property. Centipede is common because it can work well for many lower-maintenance coastal lawns. Bermuda can perform well in full sun and higher-use areas, but it demands stronger maintenance. Zoysia offers a denser premium look but should be matched carefully to sunlight and expectations. St. Augustine may be used where a broader-blade turf and certain shade conditions make sense.

The right decision depends on sunlight, irrigation coverage, soil conditions, drainage, property use, mowing expectations, owner preference and budget. Bakerss can help review the project and quote the installation scope around the actual lawn conditions rather than forcing every property into the same turf recommendation.

Murrells Inlet sod installation FAQ

Questions About Sod Installation

Does Bakerss install sod in Murrells Inlet SC?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance installs sod in Murrells Inlet for residential homes, rental properties, HOA communities, commercial properties, new construction lawns and lawn replacement projects.

What sod types can be installed?

Common sod options for Murrells Inlet include centipede, Bermuda, Zoysia and St. Augustine. The best choice depends on sunlight, irrigation, soil conditions, maintenance expectations and property use.

Do you remove old grass before installing sod?

Yes. If the existing lawn needs to be removed, the quote can include removal, disposal, grading, leveling and soil preparation before new sod is installed.

Do you grade or level the yard first?

Yes. Yard grading and leveling can be included when needed. Proper preparation helps with the finished appearance, drainage and sod-to-soil contact.

Can you help with irrigation before sod is installed?

Yes. Bakerss can inspect sprinkler coverage, identify obvious irrigation concerns and quote irrigation or sprinkler repair before sod installation when needed.

How much does sod installation cost?

Pricing depends on square footage, sod type, existing lawn removal, grading, soil preparation, access, irrigation condition, delivery logistics, disposal and cleanup requirements.

How soon can new sod be walked on?

New sod should be protected during establishment. Foot traffic should be limited until the sod roots into the soil and the lawn is stable enough for normal use.

How often does new sod need water?

New sod needs consistent watering during establishment. The exact schedule depends on sod type, weather, soil, sun exposure and irrigation coverage.

Can sod installation be paired with lawn care?

Yes. Sod installation can be paired with recurring lawn care, landscaping, irrigation service, pressure washing, property maintenance plans and other Bakerss services.
