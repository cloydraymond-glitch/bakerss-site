Questions About Irrigation & Sprinkler Repair

Does Bakerss provide irrigation and sprinkler repair in Murrells Inlet SC?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance provides irrigation and sprinkler repair in Murrells Inlet for homes, lawns, sod projects, rental properties, HOA common areas, commercial properties and property maintenance clients.

What sprinkler problems can you repair?

Sprinkler repair can include broken heads, poor coverage, damaged nozzles, zone issues, controller or timer settings, visible leaks, valve concerns, low-pressure observations and watering problems depending on the system and quoted scope.

Can you check irrigation before sod installation?

Yes. Bakerss can review sprinkler coverage and obvious irrigation concerns before sod installation so the new lawn has a better chance of establishing correctly.

Do you replace broken sprinkler heads?

Broken sprinkler heads and damaged nozzles can be quoted for replacement when parts and system conditions allow.

Do you work on irrigation timers and controllers?

Yes. Timer and controller settings can be reviewed and adjusted when accessible and within the agreed scope. Replacement or advanced electrical issues may be quoted separately.

Can you fix a sprinkler zone that will not come on?

Zone issues can be evaluated through troubleshooting. The repair may involve settings, valves, wiring, pressure, heads or other system components depending on the actual cause.

Why is part of my lawn dry while another area is soaked?

Uneven watering can come from broken heads, clogged nozzles, incorrect head spacing, poor pressure, bad timing, stuck valves, leaks or coverage design issues.

How is sprinkler repair priced?

Pricing depends on the issue, labor time, parts needed, system access, number of zones, broken heads, valves, controller concerns, pipe breaks, troubleshooting needs and bundled maintenance services.

Can irrigation repair be part of a property maintenance plan?
