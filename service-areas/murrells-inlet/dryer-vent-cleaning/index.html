What does dryer vent cleaning include?

Dryer vent cleaning can include lint removal from the dryer vent path, exterior termination checks, visible airflow observations, vent connection observations and cleanup depending on the quoted scope and access.

Why should dryer vents be cleaned?

Dryer vents should be cleaned because lint buildup can reduce airflow, increase drying time, strain the dryer and create a maintenance concern for homes, rentals and managed properties.

What are signs a dryer vent may need cleaning?

Common signs include longer drying times, damp towels after a full cycle, excess heat near the dryer, lint around the laundry area, a musty smell, or visible lint buildup at the vent termination.

Can you clean dryer vents for vacation rentals and Airbnbs?

Yes. Dryer vent cleaning can be scheduled for vacation rentals, Airbnbs, condos and second homes as a one-time service or part of a recurring property maintenance plan.
