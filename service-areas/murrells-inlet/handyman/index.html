Local Property Conditions

Why Murrells Inlet Homes Need Different Maintenance Priorities

Live Oak & Shade Issues

Many Murrells Inlet neighborhoods have mature live oak coverage. Beautiful canopy also means shade, debris, root pressure and longer moisture retention after rain.

  • Lifted pavers and walkway trip hazards
  • Shaded deck and stair deterioration
  • Organic debris against trim and exterior surfaces
  • Loose railings and exterior wood damage

Marsh Humidity & Hardware

Inlet-area humidity and coastal exposure accelerate wear on moving parts and exterior hardware. These issues are especially common near the marsh, waterway and shaded properties.

  • Corroded hinges, latches and locksets
  • Sticking exterior doors
  • Failed caulk around penetrations
  • Fixture and fastener corrosion
