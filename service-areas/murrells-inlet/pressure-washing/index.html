Murrells Inlet Exterior Cleaning

Built for Inlet Homes, Not Generic Pressure Washing

Murrells Inlet is different from a standard inland subdivision or a beachfront condo corridor. Mature live oaks, tidal marsh humidity, shaded driveways, screened porch areas, pool decks and outdoor entertaining spaces all create faster exterior buildup. A driveway under live oak canopy in Wachesaw or Prince Creek does not age like a sun-exposed driveway in another market. A home near the marsh does not carry mildew the same way a dry inland property does.

That is why this service is structured as exterior surface restoration, not just “blast it with pressure.” Bakerss separates each surface by method: soft washing for siding and painted surfaces, controlled pressure for concrete, material-sensitive cleaning for wood and composite decks, and targeted treatment for organic staining.

Local Surface Problems

What Makes Murrells Inlet Exteriors Get Dirty Faster

Live Oak Canopy

Tannins, Acorns and Leaf Staining

Wachesaw, Prince Creek and older inlet neighborhoods have mature oak coverage that drops organic material year-round. Rain pulls tannins from leaves, bark and acorns onto concrete. Over time, that creates brown and black staining that basic rinsing will not correct.

Marsh Humidity

Mildew and Algae Return Faster

Humidity near the inlet and shaded areas around homes can keep siding, fences, patios and pool decks damp longer. Soft washing with the right cleaning solution treats the biological growth instead of only moving it around.

Outdoor Living

Pool Decks and Screened Porches

Murrells Inlet homes often rely heavily on outdoor space. Pool decks, screened porches, rear patios and walkways need consistent cleaning because they affect guest experience, safety and homeowner use.

HOA Presentation

Prince Creek and Wachesaw Standards

In high-standard communities, exterior staining stands out quickly. Driveways, sidewalks, entryways and siding should stay clean enough to meet community expectations and protect property value.

Pressure Washing Questions — Murrells Inlet

How much does pressure washing cost in Murrells Inlet?

Small exterior cleaning scopes usually start around $125. Driveways, house washing, pool decks, patios, decks, fences, full exterior packages, commercial properties and HOA scopes are quoted based on the actual property.

Should my house be pressure washed or soft washed?

Most siding and painted surfaces should be soft washed, not pressure blasted. Soft washing uses low pressure and cleaning solution to treat mildew, algae and organic growth without risking water intrusion or surface damage.

Do you remove live oak stains from driveways?

Yes. Many Murrells Inlet driveways stain from live oak tannins, acorns, leaf debris and shade. We evaluate the staining and use the appropriate concrete cleaning process for the surface.

How often should Murrells Inlet homes be washed?

Most homes should be washed annually. Properties near marsh areas, under heavy tree canopy, used as rentals, or held to strict HOA appearance standards may benefit from cleaning every six months.

Can you clean pool decks and screened porches?

Yes. We clean pool decks, patios, walkways, outdoor kitchens, screened porches, lanais and other exterior living areas.

Do you provide HOA or commercial pressure washing?

Yes. Bakerss provides exterior cleaning for HOAs, apartment communities, restaurants, retail spaces, clubhouses, pool buildings and managed commercial properties.
