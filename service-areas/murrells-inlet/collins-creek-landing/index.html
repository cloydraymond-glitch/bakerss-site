Neighborhood Profile

Collins Creek Landing — Creek-Front Living in Prince Creek

Collins Creek Landing developed as part of the broader Prince Creek master development — one of Horry County's most ambitious planned residential communities stretching along Highway 707 through Murrells Inlet. The Prince Creek corridor encompasses multiple distinct communities, each with their own HOA but sharing the corridor's overall upscale residential character and the live oak canopy that defines this stretch of Murrells Inlet.

Collins Creek Landing's defining feature is its creek-front positioning — lots along the creek offer direct water access, marsh views, and the authentic South Carolina Lowcountry waterway character that attracts buyers specifically seeking this environment over the more manicured golf course communities nearby. Creek-front lots have been among the community's most consistently valued properties, with prices reflecting both the water access and the natural beauty of the creek-side setting.

The community's HOA maintains active standards consistent with the Prince Creek corridor's upscale positioning. Lawn care, landscape maintenance, and exterior appearance are all subject to oversight and enforcement. Weekly professional lawn care during growing season is effectively required for HOA compliance. The live oak canopy throughout Collins Creek Landing — along with the creek-side vegetation — creates meaningful gutter maintenance demands, with most properties needing 3 annual cleanings.

Development

Centex & Prince Creek

Developed 2000s within the Prince Creek master corridor. Builders include Centex Homes and others. Single-family homes $250K–$600K+. Creek-front premium lots with water views and access. Active HOA with standards consistent with Prince Creek corridor.

Maintenance

Creek Environment Demands

Live oak canopy creates 3 annual gutter cleanings as standard. Creek proximity elevates humidity — mildew conditions more aggressive than upland properties. Annual exterior washing recommended. HOA enforcement active.
