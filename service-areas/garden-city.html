Oceanfront & Canal Property Maintenance in Garden City

Garden City sits at the southern end of the Grand Strand — a community of oceanfront homes, canal-front properties, and vacation rentals that face some of the most demanding coastal maintenance conditions in Horry County. Directly on the Atlantic, Garden City properties experience maximum salt air exposure and the highest levels of moisture, mold, and surface deterioration.

The Garden City Pier anchors a community with a strong vacation rental identity. Many properties here are investment rentals whose owners need a reliable maintenance partner — someone to handle the lawn, the cleaning, the pressure washing, and the minor repairs without the owner having to coordinate five different vendors.

Bakerss provides comprehensive all-in-one property maintenance for Garden City — from oceanfront homes to canal-side rentals. We understand the specific demands of this coastal community and deliver consistent, professional service for every property type.
