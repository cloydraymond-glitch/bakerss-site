Lawn and irrigation strategy

Confirm What the HOA Maintains Before Quoting Duplicate Service

Public listings describe low HOA fees, but current documents determine whether the owner or association maintains turf, irrigation, landscape beds or shared grounds.

Where the owner is responsible, Bakerss checks turf type, irrigation coverage, sun, sandy soil and drainage before recommending sod or watering changes.

Cost-control principle: verify responsibility first, then fix the reason turf failed before installing replacement sod.
