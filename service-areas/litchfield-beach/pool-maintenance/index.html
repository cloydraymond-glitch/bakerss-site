Coastal maintenance challenges

Common Pool Problems Around Litchfield Beach

Storm debris: Coastal storms and windy afternoons can push leaves, pine needles, palm debris and sand into pools quickly. Properties with mature trees or open exposure may need more frequent attention during certain seasons.

Pollen and organic load: Spring pollen, live oak debris and pine needles can affect water appearance, baskets, coping and deck areas. A pool can look neglected even when the main issue is heavy debris load between visits.

Guest use: Short-term rental pools see sunscreen, sand, towels, toys, heavy use and unpredictable schedules. A plan that ignores guest turnover will not perform well in a rental market.

Pool-area presentation: Many complaints are about the space around the pool. Dirty deck surfaces, algae-looking concrete, scattered debris, weeds in cracks, unclean furniture and nearby landscaping issues all affect the guest or resident experience.
