Method matters

Pressure Washing vs. Soft Washing in Litchfield Beach

Not every exterior surface should be blasted with high pressure. Concrete driveways, sidewalks and certain hardscapes may need pressure to remove buildup, but siding, painted surfaces, trim, screens, doors, older wood, pavers, pool areas and delicate exterior details often require lower pressure, controlled rinsing or a surface-specific approach.

Bakerss reviews the job by surface type, age, condition, soil level, water access, drainage and surrounding materials. This matters in Litchfield Beach because many properties combine concrete, pavers, painted trim, vinyl, stucco, wood decks, screen enclosures, pool equipment, landscaping and irrigation components in tight exterior areas.

We clean for the property, not just the stain.

The goal is to improve presentation without creating avoidable damage. That means choosing the right cleaning method, protecting adjacent areas where practical, avoiding unnecessary pressure on vulnerable materials and identifying visible issues before work begins.
