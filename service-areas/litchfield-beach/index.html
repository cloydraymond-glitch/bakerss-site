How can I protect an oceanfront condo from salt air?

Regularly inspect exposed hardware, window seals and accessible exterior surfaces, and use material-appropriate cleaning. Coordinate association-controlled building work with the condo manager.

How should I prepare a Litchfield Beach home for a hurricane?

Follow official evacuation guidance, secure approved outdoor items when safe, document the property condition and arrange authorized pre-storm checks. Do not enter unsafe areas during or immediately after a storm.

What should I do after a storm in Litchfield?

Follow local safety instructions first. When access is safe, document visible damage, contact your insurer as needed and request qualified repairs for electrical, structural, roof or flood-related issues.

How do I reduce moisture and mold risk in a vacant coastal home?

Maintain suitable indoor temperature and humidity, service the AC, look for leaks and schedule checks during extended absences. Suspected mold should be assessed and addressed by appropriate professionals.
