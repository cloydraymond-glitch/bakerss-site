Care designed for Compass Point’s detached homes

Compass Point is a residential section of Litchfield by the Sea, known for individual Lowcountry-style homes, porches, mature landscaping and a pond-centered setting. That is a different maintenance profile from a condominium building where an association may handle much of the exterior. Litchfield by the Sea’s community directory identifies Compass Point as a sub-community.

For an owner, the useful starting question is not simply “How often should we mow?” It is which parts of the home are owner-maintained, which are subject to association rules, and which exterior tasks can be scheduled together.

What we verify before work

Owner authorization; entrance instructions; association approval where applicable; equipment access; the property’s actual lawn, pond exposure, irrigation and exterior condition. No affiliation with the community or its management is implied.

A seasonal checklist for a Compass Point home

Spring: Inspect irrigation coverage, landscape beds, exterior trim and shaded walkways before peak growing season.

Summer: Monitor turf and drainage after heavy rain, review HVAC performance, and coordinate upkeep around owner arrivals or guest stays.

Fall and winter: Clear accessible gutter debris, check door and window sealing, inspect porches and schedule any approved preventive repairs.

Compass Point homeowner questions

What maintenance matters most for Compass Point detached homes?

Landscape beds, pond-facing yard conditions where applicable, irrigation coverage, porch surfaces, gutter drainage and seasonal exterior observations are useful priorities. The actual scope depends on the home and any association requirements.

Can Bakerss check a Compass Point second home when the owner is away?

Bakerss offers vacation-home watch visits subject to agreement. A plan can include exterior observations, visible leak or moisture checks, HVAC status observations, photo reporting and coordination of approved repairs; it is not a security patrol.

Can you service an individual home within the gated community?

Service depends on approved gate entry, parking instructions, homeowner authorization and applicable Litchfield by the Sea or sub-association rules. Bakerss does not represent itself as the community’s contracted vendor.

Can you help with porches, trim and exterior paint?

Yes. We can quote porch and trim repairs, cleaning and painting where within scope. Material selection, surface conditions and approval requirements are reviewed before work begins.

How should pond-facing landscaping be maintained?

We inspect drainage and irrigation rather than assume every pond-side lot has identical needs. Work near a pond should respect property boundaries, vegetation and any community restrictions.

Do you offer bundled lawn and home maintenance?

A recurring proposal can combine approved lawn, irrigation, exterior cleaning and home-watch tasks, with separate allowances for repairs and materials.
