Before you schedule service in a gated or managed community

Identify whether the requested task is for a privately owned interior, an owner-maintained exterior area, or shared property. Confirm authorization and community rules before booking. For vacation stays, include contact details, planned arrival dates, cleaning deadlines and the scope of linen handling.

Coastal conditions such as high humidity, salt exposure, storms and seasonal occupancy can affect what needs attention, but conditions vary by building and lot.
