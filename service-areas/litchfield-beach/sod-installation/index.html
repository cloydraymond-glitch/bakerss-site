Sod installation can include old grass removal, site cleanup, soil preparation, grading support, sod delivery, turf installation, rolling, watering guidance and coordination with irrigation repair depending on the approved scope.

Can Bakerss help repair damaged lawns?

Yes. New sod needs consistent watering during establishment. Irrigation and sprinkler issues should be reviewed before or during sod work so the new lawn has a better chance of establishing correctly.
