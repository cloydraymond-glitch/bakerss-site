Brick and exterior cleaning strategy

Maximum Pressure Is Not the Same as Professional Cleaning

Brick, mortar, painted trim, pavers, concrete and windows require different methods. Coastal organic growth and irrigation marks may be visible, but aggressive pressure can damage mortar or force water into vulnerable areas.

Bakerss evaluates material condition, drainage, nearby landscaping and HOA requirements before cleaning.

Protection principle: use the lowest effective pressure and the correct process for the surface.

Conditions worth checking

✓Dark organic staining on brick
✓Irrigation marks on walls and drives
✓Loose or deteriorated mortar
✓Salt film on windows and fixtures
✓Algae near lake-facing patios
