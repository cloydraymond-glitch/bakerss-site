Service timing strategy

The Right Work at the Wrong Time Can Still Disrupt the Property

Pressure washing during lunch, landscaping during a concert or repairs during guest check-in can create unnecessary conflict. Ocean Drive service should be planned around business hours, loading zones, event calendars and rental schedules.

Operating principle: define the service window before dispatching the crew, especially near Main Street and oceanfront condo buildings.

Information needed before service

✓Business hours or guest schedule
✓Parking and loading instructions
✓Event dates and restricted times
✓Association or building requirements
✓Noise and water-use limitations
