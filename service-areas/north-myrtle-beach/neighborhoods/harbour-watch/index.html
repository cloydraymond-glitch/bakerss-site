Harbour Watch lawn strategy

Do Not Treat the Entire Lot as One Turf Environment

Centipede, Bermuda, Zoysia or St. Augustine may be present. Open front lawns may perform differently from shaded side or rear areas.

Bakerss evaluates the actual grass, mowing height, irrigation, shade, soil firmness and drainage before recommending sod replacement.

Better sequence: identify the cause of thin turf before charging for replacement that may fail again.

Conditions worth checking

✓Dry arcs from poor sprinkler coverage
✓Soft areas receiving roof runoff
✓Shade too dense for reliable turf
✓Root damage and uneven mowing
✓Pine debris holding moisture
✓Overspray staining garage approaches
