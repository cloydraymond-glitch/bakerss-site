Lawn strategy

Sandy Soil Does Not Mean Water Everything More

Sandy soil can dry rapidly while shaded side yards remain damp. Bakerss checks turf type, sunlight, irrigation coverage and drainage before recommending watering changes or sod.

Better sequence: identify drought, shade, compaction, drainage or mowing problems before replacing turf.

Conditions worth checking

✓Dry turf beside hot concrete
✓Wet side yards receiving roof runoff
✓Overspray on fences and siding
✓Scalping on compact lawns
✓Shade beneath structures or trees
