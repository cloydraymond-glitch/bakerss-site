North Myrtle Beach property maintenance FAQ

Questions From Homeowners, Rental Owners and Property Managers

What property maintenance services does Bakerss provide in North Myrtle Beach?

Bakerss provides lawn care, landscaping, vacation-rental cleaning, interior cleaning, pressure washing, window cleaning, pool maintenance, gutter cleaning, handyman work, irrigation repair, dryer vent cleaning, home watch and recurring property maintenance plans.

Does Bakerss serve vacation rentals and second homes?

Yes. We support vacation rentals, second homes and absentee owners with turnover cleaning, off-site laundry, guest consumable restocking, inspections, photo documentation, storm checks and maintenance coordination.

Can several services be combined into one maintenance plan?

Yes. Approved cleaning, lawn, landscape, exterior, pool, home-watch and handyman services can be combined into a documented recurring plan based on the property’s actual needs.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is based on the address, property type, size, condition, access, service frequency, labor requirements and approved scope. Photos and an onsite review may be required before final pricing.
