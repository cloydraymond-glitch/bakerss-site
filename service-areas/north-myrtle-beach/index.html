North Myrtle Beach property maintenance FAQ

Questions From Homeowners, Rental Owners and Property Managers

What property maintenance services does Bakerss provide in North Myrtle Beach?

Bakerss provides White Glove estate care, grounds maintenance, interior cleaning, HVAC preventive maintenance, pool service, vacation-rental support, pressure washing, dryer-vent cleaning, home watch and priority work-order coordination.

Does Bakerss serve vacation rentals and second homes?

Yes. We support vacation rentals, second homes and absentee owners with turnover cleaning, off-site laundry, guest consumable restocking, inspections, photo documentation, storm checks and maintenance coordination.

Can several services be combined into one maintenance plan?

Yes. Approved cleaning, grounds, HVAC preventive maintenance, pool, home-watch, exterior and work-order services can be combined into a documented recurring plan based on the property’s actual needs.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is based on the address, property type, size, condition, access, service frequency, labor requirements and approved scope. Photos and an onsite review may be required before final pricing.


Can clients see technician notes and service photos?

Yes. The Bakerss Client Portal can provide technician arrival and departure times, field notes, photographs and work-order updates for documented property visibility.

Does Bakerss provide HVAC preventive maintenance?

Yes. Bakerss can provide HVAC preventive-maintenance and service support through experienced HVAC professionals, subject to the approved property scope and service availability.
