Built for North Myrtle Beach's Rental Geography

North Myrtle Beach vacation rentals cluster into very different operating environments. Cherry Grove and Ocean Drive rentals are beach-first properties where the cleaning load is driven by sand, sunscreen, wet linens, balcony debris, and fast Saturday turns. Barefoot Resort and Windy Hill rentals serve families, golfers, and seasonal visitors who expect resort presentation without hotel housekeeping. Tidewater Plantation and ICW-adjacent homes attract guests who notice finer details: glass doors, stainless appliances, bathrooms, entry floors, porch furniture, and outdoor living areas.

That is why this page cannot be a copy of a Myrtle Beach or Conway cleaning page. North Myrtle Beach STR cleaning is operationally different. The drive times between communities, same-day peak-season compression, elevator/condo access, parking constraints, linen volume, and guest expectations all affect the turnover. Bakerss treats each property as an operating asset, not just another house to clean.

Turnover Scope

Every NMB Turnover Includes a Guest-Ready Reset

  • Kitchen sanitation: counters, sink, stovetop, microwave interior, appliance exteriors, dishes checked, refrigerator reviewed.
  • Bathrooms: toilets, tubs, showers, mirrors, vanities, fixtures, floors, and folded towel presentation.
  • Bedrooms: beds stripped and remade, floors cleaned, surfaces dusted, closets checked, forgotten items reported.
  • Living areas: floors vacuumed/mopped, high-touch surfaces sanitized, trash removed, furniture reset.
  • Beach-load areas: sand tracked through entries, balconies, laundry areas, patio doors, and outdoor seating addressed.
  • Photo report and damage notes sent after the turnover so remote owners know the condition before the next guest does.
North Myrtle Beach difference: Beach rentals are judged hard on floors, bathrooms, linens, and smell. Golf villas are judged hard on entry presentation, kitchen condition, and sleeping-area reset. Tidewater-style rentals are judged hard on detail. The checklist changes with the property type.
