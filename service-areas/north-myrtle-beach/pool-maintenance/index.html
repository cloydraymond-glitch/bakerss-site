North Myrtle Beach Salt-Air Factor

Salt Air Is an Equipment Problem, Not Just a Beach Problem

In Cherry Grove, Ocean Drive, Crescent Beach, and Windy Hill, pools live in a salt-air environment even when they are not saltwater pools. Metal handrails, ladder cups, heater cabinets, pump housings, bonding connections, anchors, fasteners, light rings, and hardware can age faster than they do inland. A good North Myrtle Beach pool service routine has to include eyes on the equipment pad and visible hardware every visit.

Bakerss corrosion-watch protocol: On beachside North Myrtle Beach pools, we check exposed metal, pump-area fittings, visible leaks, filter pressure, corrosion around hardware, and signs of premature equipment wear. Catching an issue early can keep a small repair from becoming a peak-season pool shutdown.

  • Pump and filter fittings
