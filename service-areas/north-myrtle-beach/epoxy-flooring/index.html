North Myrtle Beach Context

Four Beach Communities. One Tough Garage Environment.

North Myrtle Beach was built from four distinct beach communities: Cherry Grove, Crescent Beach, Ocean Drive, and Windy Hill. That history matters because every section has a different garage-floor demand profile. Cherry Grove channel homes and raised beach houses see constant salt, sand, fishing gear, beach carts, and storage use. Ocean Drive and Crescent Beach properties often need a finished garage that supports second-home value and vacation-rental presentation. Windy Hill homes near Barefoot Landing combine beach traffic with entertainment-district guest activity. Barefoot Resort and Tidewater Plantation add golf-cart storage, HOA curb appeal, and premium property expectations.

A North Myrtle Beach epoxy floor cannot be treated like a generic inland garage. The slab needs to be mechanically profiled, moisture evaluated, cleaned of salt and surface contaminants, and coated with a system that can handle tire heat, wet beach equipment, humidity, and seasonal occupancy gaps. That is the difference between a floor that looks good for a season and a floor that becomes a long-term property upgrade.

Installation System

Our North Myrtle Beach Epoxy Floor Installation Process

1

Site Walk + Moisture Review

We inspect slab condition, cracks, oil staining, prior coatings, salt contamination, humidity risk, drainage, and garage use: cars, carts, beach gear, storage, rental turnover, or showroom-style finish.

2

Diamond Grinding

Mechanical grinding profiles the concrete so the coating can bond properly. This is the step most cheap installs skip and the reason many garage coatings peel.

3

Crack + Surface Repair

We fill cracks, divots, small spalls, and surface imperfections before coating so the finished floor looks clean and performs as one continuous system.

4

Epoxy Base + Flake or Metallic Finish

Choose from durable full-flake, partial-flake, marble-flake, or metallic-style finishes. Popular NMB selections include coastal grays, sandy neutrals, ocean blues, and earth-tone blends.

5

Polyaspartic Topcoat

The topcoat gives abrasion resistance, chemical resistance, UV stability, and cleanability for beach gear, hot tires, golf carts, and everyday use.

FAQ

Epoxy Flooring FAQ — North Myrtle Beach

How much does an epoxy garage floor cost in North Myrtle Beach?+
A 1-car garage starts around $1,800, a 2-car garage starts around $3,000, and a 3-car garage starts around $4,500. Final pricing depends on slab condition, square footage, moisture needs, crack repair, and finish selection.
Why does salt air matter for garage floor coating?+
Salt air, sand, and humidity can contaminate and weaken the concrete surface. That is why North Myrtle Beach slabs should be cleaned, mechanically profiled by diamond grinding, and evaluated for moisture before coating.
Do you install epoxy floors in Barefoot Resort and Tidewater Plantation?+
Yes. We serve Barefoot Resort, Tidewater Plantation, Cherry Grove, Ocean Drive, Crescent Beach, Windy Hill, and surrounding North Myrtle Beach communities.
Can you work around vacation rental calendars?+
Yes. For STR and second-home owners, we can schedule around owner blocks, rental vacancies, pre-season refresh windows, and property-manager access requirements.
Is epoxy slippery when wet?+
It can be if the wrong finish is used. We can add slip-resistant texture to help with wet beach gear, rainwater, pool areas, and garage entries.
Do you offer metallic epoxy in North Myrtle Beach?+
Yes. Metallic finishes are available, including coastal blue, gray, bronze, copper, and custom blends. We also install full-flake and marble-flake systems.
