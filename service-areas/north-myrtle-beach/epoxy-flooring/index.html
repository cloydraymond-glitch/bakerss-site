Epoxy Floor FAQ — North Myrtle Beach

Does salt air from the ocean affect epoxy floors?

Yes — salt air contributes to concrete surface micro-degradation over time, creating a porous surface layer that must be removed before coating. Diamond grinding removes this layer completely. Acid etching, used in most DIY kits, leaves it in place and creates adhesion failures within 1–2 years.

What color options are available for North Myrtle Beach installs?
