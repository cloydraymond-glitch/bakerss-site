📍 North Myrtle Beach History & Epoxy Context

North Myrtle Beach was created in 1968 through the consolidation of four distinct beach communities: Cherry Grove Beach, Crescent Beach, Ocean Drive Beach, and Windy Hill Beach. Each had developed independently since the 1940s — Ocean Drive being particularly notable as the birthplace of the shag dance, which became South Carolina's official state dance.

Today, the city is home to Barefoot Landing and some of the most valuable beachfront and waterway property in the state. Second homes, vacation rentals, and primary residences with high-value construction are the norm here. Property owners in North Myrtle Beach don't settle for hardware store solutions — and neither do we.

Salt Air & Coastal Concrete

North Myrtle Beach's oceanfront and waterway-adjacent properties experience accelerated concrete surface degradation from salt air. This creates micro-porosity in the concrete surface that big-box acid-etch prep doesn't address. Diamond grinding removes the compromised surface layer entirely, giving commercial epoxy a clean, sound substrate to bond to.

The Pharmaceutical Industry Connection

FAQ

North Myrtle Beach Epoxy FAQ

Does salt air from the ocean affect epoxy floors?+
Yes — salt air contributes to concrete surface micro-degradation over time, creating a porous surface layer that must be removed before any coating is applied. Diamond grinding removes this layer completely. Acid etching, used in most DIY kits, leaves it in place and creates adhesion failures within 1–2 years. Our process is specifically designed for oceanfront and near-ocean properties like those in North Myrtle Beach.

What industries rely on epoxy flooring most heavily?+
The industries with the most demanding floor requirements — aerospace, automotive manufacturing, pharmaceutical production, food processing, and healthcare — all standardized on epoxy. These sectors have floors exposed to chemicals, heavy machinery, continuous moisture, and hygiene requirements that no other coating system met at commercial scale. The same system they use is what we install in your garage.
