Sunset Harbour has its own maintenance considerations

Sunset Harbour is a North Myrtle Beach neighborhood of single-family homes with a community clubhouse and swimming pool. Published information from its association manager also identifies forms for exterior changes and other homeowner requests. Any proposed improvements should be checked against the current governing documents; association-controlled amenities are not part of an individual homeowner's maintenance scope.

Properties near the waterway may require extra attention to exposed exterior hardware, painted trim, deck and patio surfaces, and drainage. Those are site-assessment considerations, not claims that every home has the same conditions.

How Bakerss scopes a Sunset Harbour maintenance plan

  1. Assess the property: Review exterior condition, photos, site access and maintenance priorities.
  2. Confirm responsibilities: Distinguish private homeowner work from association-controlled facilities and obtain the right approvals.
  3. Quote the work: Separate recurring visits from repair projects, labor and materials.
  4. Schedule and report: Agree on visit frequency, documentation and the process for follow-up work orders.

Request a proposal based on the actual property instead of a community-wide price assumption. We do not claim to be the contracted maintenance provider for Sunset Harbour's association.

Sunset Harbour homeowner FAQs

Does Bakerss provide maintenance within Sunset Harbour?

Bakerss can quote owner-authorized property maintenance in the Sunset Harbour area, subject to scheduling, access and agreed scope.

Who approves work affecting HOA common areas?

The association or its authorized manager must approve common-area or amenity work. An individual homeowner may not authorize work on association-controlled facilities.

Can I combine lawn care and pressure washing?

Yes. Separate tasks and visit frequency can be included in one written scope once access and property conditions are reviewed.

Do you maintain private docks or boat ramps?

Marine structures and shared amenities require separate permissions, inspections and any appropriate specialist contractors. They are not automatically included in a residential maintenance plan.

What if I am away from my Sunset Harbour home?

A defined home-watch visit can document visible conditions and flag follow-up items; it is not a guarantee against hidden defects.

How do I get a quote?

Provide the service address, desired work, photos when available, access instructions and the preferred service frequency.
