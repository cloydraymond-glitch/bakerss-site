North Myrtle Beach-Specific Cleaning

Built for Sand, Salt Film, Humidity, and Second-Home Ownership

North Myrtle Beach is a narrow coastal city shaped by four historic beach communities — Cherry Grove, Ocean Drive, Crescent Beach, and Windy Hill — plus resort communities like Barefoot Resort and gated properties like Tidewater Plantation. That mix creates a different interior cleaning market than central Myrtle Beach, Conway, or Murrells Inlet. Many properties are part-time residences, golf villas, beach condos, or investment homes where the owner may not be present for months at a time.

The cleaning challenge is physical and operational. Fine beach sand collects in entryways, under furniture, along baseboards, and inside sliding-door tracks. Salt air leaves a light film on glass, stainless appliances, fixtures, floors, and window frames. NMB's humidity makes bathrooms, laundry closets, and HVAC returns more vulnerable to mildew odor. A basic wipe-down misses the details that make a property feel truly clean.

NMB cleaning priority: We treat entry zones, bathrooms, sliding doors, kitchen surfaces, ceiling fans, vents, baseboards, and flooring as the areas most likely to show coastal buildup. This page is written for interior cleaning, not STR-only turnover cleaning, so it targets homeowners, second-home owners, condos, villas, and move-in/move-out projects.

What Is Included

Standard Clean Checklist

  • Kitchen counters, sink, stovetop exterior, appliance exteriors, cabinet faces, microwave interior, and floor cleaning.
  • Bathrooms cleaned and sanitized: toilets, tubs, showers, vanities, mirrors, fixtures, floors, and visible mildew-prone areas.
  • Bedrooms reset: dusting, floors, trash, beds made if linens are available, and closet/floor check.
  • Living areas dusted and vacuumed or mopped, including high-touch surfaces, tables, fans when reachable, and entry zones.
  • North Myrtle Beach sand protocol: extra attention to entryways, under furniture edges, sliding-door tracks, and corners where fine sand collects.
  • Salt-film wipe-down on glass-adjacent surfaces, stainless appliances, fixtures, and frequently touched coastal surfaces.
  • Photo or text confirmation available for remote owners after service is complete.

FAQ

Interior Cleaning FAQ — North Myrtle Beach

How much does interior cleaning cost in North Myrtle Beach?+
Most standard cleans start at $100. A typical 2BR/2BA condo runs $135–$195, while a 3BR home or villa usually runs $165–$255 depending on condition and frequency. Deep cleans cost more because they include baseboards, buildup, vents, tracks, and detail work.
Do you clean second homes when the owner is not there?+
Yes. Many North Myrtle Beach clients are second-home owners who use lockbox or door-code access. We can clean before arrival, after departure, monthly while vacant, or on a recurring schedule.
Do you clean Barefoot Resort condos and villas?+
Yes. Barefoot Resort is one of the most important North Myrtle Beach cleaning areas. We clean villas, condos, townhomes, and second homes with recurring or pre-arrival service.
What makes NMB interior cleaning different from inland cleaning?+
Fine beach sand, salt film, higher humidity, HVAC dust, sliding-door tracks, and closed-up second-home odor make NMB cleaning more detail-sensitive than inland cleaning. Entryways, bathrooms, floors, fans, vents, and glass-adjacent surfaces need consistent attention.
Do you offer deep cleaning before listing or selling a property?+
Yes. Deep cleaning is available before real estate photos, showings, closings, owner arrivals, new leases, or seasonal openings. This is especially useful for Cherry Grove, Ocean Drive, Windy Hill, Barefoot Resort, and Tidewater properties.
Do you bring cleaning supplies?+
Yes. Standard supplies and equipment are included unless you prefer specific products for stone, stainless, flooring, or allergy-sensitive areas.
