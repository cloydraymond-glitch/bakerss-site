North Myrtle Beach Service Zones

Built Around the Communities That Define NMB Lawn Care

Barefoot Resort

Golf-Community Lawn Detail

Barefoot Resort lawns require clean edges, consistent cut height, and presentation that fits a resort-golf environment. We focus on sidewalks, driveways, bed lines, patio edges, and visible frontage so the property does not fall behind HOA expectations.

Tidewater Plantation

Gated HOA Standards

Tidewater properties need precise, predictable service. Overgrowth, uneven trimming, and debris left on hardscapes stand out quickly in a gated golf community. Our schedule is designed to prevent those issues before they become notice-worthy.

Cherry Grove

Salt, Sand & Storm Debris

Cherry Grove lawns face sandy soils, salt exposure, storm debris, and wind-driven yard mess. We keep turf trimmed, edges clean, and hard surfaces blown clear so homes look cared for even after heavy coastal weather.

Ocean Drive

Ocean Drive properties sit in one of the highest-visibility sections of North Myrtle Beach. Lawn care here must support curb appeal for guests, neighbors, and passersby, especially during peak rental weeks.

Windy Hill

Rental-Ready Lawn Service

Crescent Beach

Turf & Coastal Conditions

Grass Types, Salt Exposure, and North Myrtle Beach Soil

Bermuda & Zoysia

Golf-Community Turf

Common in higher-visibility communities, Bermuda and Zoysia need consistent summer mowing and clean edging to avoid a ragged look. We avoid scalping during heat stress and adjust the cut to keep turf healthy.

Centipede

Low-Maintenance Coastal Lawns

Centipede performs well in sandy soils but does not tolerate aggressive cutting. Correct height matters. Overcutting leads to thinning, weeds, and brown patches.

St. Augustine

Shade & Salt Tolerance

St. Augustine is common where shade and coastal exposure exist. It needs a higher cut, sharper blades, and a more careful approach than Bermuda.

Storm Season

Debris & Drainage Watch

After strong coastal weather, yards accumulate palm debris, pine straw, leaves, sticks, and sand. Lawn visits can be paired with cleanup, gutter cleaning, pressure washing, or property checks.

FAQ

North Myrtle Beach Lawn Care FAQ

How much does lawn care cost in North Myrtle Beach?+
Lawn care starts at $50 per visit. Most standard North Myrtle Beach homes run $65–$95 per visit. Barefoot Resort, Tidewater, larger lots, and HOA-detail properties may run $80–$130 depending on size, edging, access, and service frequency.
Do you service Barefoot Resort lawn-care accounts?+
Yes. Barefoot Resort is a strong fit for recurring lawn care because the community requires consistent curb appeal, clean edging, and reliable scheduling.
Do you service Tidewater Plantation?+
Yes. Tidewater Plantation lawns are handled with gated-community standards in mind: clean cut, clean edges, hardscape blowdown, and proactive communication.
Can you maintain a vacation rental lawn around guest check-ins?+
Yes. For STR properties, we can coordinate lawn care around guest arrivals, owner stays, or recurring weekly route schedules. Photo confirmation is available for out-of-town owners.
How often should NMB lawns be mowed in summer?+
Most Bermuda and Zoysia lawns need mowing every 7–10 days in peak growing season. Centipede and St. Augustine often run 10–14 days depending on rain, fertilizer, and shade.
