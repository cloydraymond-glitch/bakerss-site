The NMB Deck Challenge

Why Deck Construction Is Different in North Myrtle Beach's Dual Salt Air

Every deck built on the Grand Strand faces coastal salt air. North Myrtle Beach faces it from two directions simultaneously — the Atlantic Ocean to the east and the Intracoastal Waterway to the west. For deck construction, this dual exposure creates the most demanding fastener corrosion environment on the Grand Strand. Zinc-plated screws and hardware — the standard specification in most residential construction — begin showing visible surface corrosion in NMB within 18–24 months. By year four or five, the rust has migrated into surrounding wood, creating rot pathways at every fastener location. Individual board replacement becomes necessary well before the decking material has reached its wear threshold.

Stainless steel fasteners perform for the full lifespan of the decking material in NMB's dual salt air environment — 25+ years for composite decking, 15–20 years for properly maintained pressure-treated wood. The hardware cost difference between stainless and zinc-plated on a standard NMB deck is $250–$500. The cost of replacing a deck eight to ten years early because zinc fasteners failed the structure is $8,000–$25,000. The specification decision is not close.

For North Myrtle Beach vacation rental investors — concentrated in Barefoot Resort, Cherry Grove, and the Ocean Drive corridor — a well-built, well-furnished deck is a direct revenue investment. Outdoor living space photographs as one of the most compelling STR listing amenity categories, and NMB's 10-month outdoor living season makes the deck useful for the vast majority of the year. Properties with appealing composite decks with comfortable seating and grilling capability command measurably higher nightly rates than comparable properties without outdoor amenity space.

For Barefoot Resort homeowners, a deck adds the functional outdoor living space that the golf community lifestyle supports — morning coffee with fairway views, evening entertaining in the space that interior rooms cannot replicate. For Tidewater Plantation residents, a deck adds the ICW-view outdoor living that many Tidewater lots' ICW positioning makes possible. The investment in a correctly built deck adds both functional value and structural value to NMB properties that benefit from it for the full lifespan of the installation.

Composite Decking — NMB First Choice

Capped Composite with Stainless Hardware

Capped composite decking (Trex, TimberTech, Fiberon) with stainless steel fasteners is our primary recommendation for North Myrtle Beach. No staining, no sealing, no annual refinishing in NMB's demanding environment. Handles salt air, moisture, and beach sand without degradation. 25–30 year lifespan. $38–$68/sq ft installed — highest upfront cost, lowest lifetime cost by a significant margin in NMB's conditions.

Pressure-Treated with Stainless

More Affordable, Still Correctly Specified

Pressure-treated pine with stainless steel fasteners — the non-negotiable specification change — is viable and more affordable at $22–$38/sq ft installed. Requires restaining every 2–3 years in NMB's conditions to maintain appearance and extend lifespan. With proper maintenance commitment, PT wood decks last 15–18 years in NMB. Without it, lifespan shortens significantly.

FAQ

Deck Building FAQ — North Myrtle Beach

Why are stainless fasteners so important in North Myrtle Beach?+
NMB's dual salt air from the Atlantic and ICW creates corrosion timelines for zinc-plated hardware that are 2–3 times faster than inland markets. Zinc fasteners in NMB show visible corrosion in 18–24 months and migrate rust into surrounding wood by year 4–5, requiring premature board replacement. Stainless steel performs for the full 25+ year lifespan of composite decking in these conditions.
How much does a new deck cost in North Myrtle Beach?+
Composite deck construction starts around $7,600 for a small deck (200 sq ft) and runs $11,400–$34,000 for standard sizes. Pressure-treated wood starts at $4,400. All prices include stainless fasteners, permits, and inspections. Free estimates.
Can you repair my existing NMB deck instead of rebuilding it?+
Yes. If the deck structure is sound, individual board replacement and fastener upgrading from zinc to stainless can significantly extend the deck's life at a fraction of full replacement cost. We assess honestly and recommend repair when it is the better financial decision.
Do you pull Horry County deck permits?+
Yes — all deck construction requires Horry County permits, and we handle the entire process: application, engineering documentation where required, submission, and all required inspections.
Do you build decks in Barefoot Resort and Tidewater Plantation?+
Yes — both are established NMB service areas. We are familiar with access procedures and community standards in both. Material and design consultation available for properties in these communities.
