Define which areas the association actually controls

North Myrtle Beach associations vary in the services they maintain. Some communities manage landscaping in front yards, while others maintain only common areas or shared amenities.

A useful HOA proposal identifies parcels, service frequency, work order authorization thresholds, access and reporting. Services for privately owned homes should not be represented as association responsibilities without confirmation.

For gated or resort-style communities, establish manager contacts and procedures before visiting restricted or amenity areas.
