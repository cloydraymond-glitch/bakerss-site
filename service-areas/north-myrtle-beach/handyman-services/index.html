Unique North Myrtle Beach Positioning

Handyman Service Built Around the Way North Myrtle Beach Properties Actually Wear Out

North Myrtle Beach is a collection of separate coastal property environments. Cherry Grove faces canal, marsh and inlet conditions. Ocean Drive and Crescent Beach carry intense rental turnover and oceanfront salt exposure. Windy Hill has condos, beach homes and STRs near Barefoot Landing. Barefoot Resort adds golf-course neighborhoods, HOA standards, irrigation systems and second-home owners who may not be local when problems appear.

That mix creates a broader maintenance requirement than basic handyman work. Owners need someone who can repair a running toilet, adjust a door, replace a lock, clean windows, inspect a dryer vent, swap HVAC filters, secure furniture before a storm, repair a fence panel, check irrigation heads after a sod install, and document everything with photos when the owner is out of town.

Bakerss is structured for exactly that: practical handyman service plus broader property maintenance for North Myrtle Beach homeowners, STR owners, condo units, HOAs, multi-family properties and investor-owned homes.
