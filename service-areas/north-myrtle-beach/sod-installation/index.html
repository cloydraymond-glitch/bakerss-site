Why sod fails

Successful Sod Requires Preparation, Water & Maintenance

Sod can look great on day one and still fail if soil contact is poor, watering is inconsistent, drainage is wrong, weeds were not addressed or mowing starts too aggressively. Bakerss approaches sod as a complete lawn installation process, not just a drop-and-go material job.

🧱

Ground prep matters

Old turf, roots, debris, uneven soil and poor contact can prevent sod from rooting properly.

💧

Water plan matters

New sod requires consistent watering and practical irrigation coverage during establishment.

🌞

Sunlight matters

Shade, traffic and turf type affect which sod makes sense for a specific yard.

📋

Aftercare matters

Mowing timing, watering, traffic limits and ongoing lawn care affect long-term performance.

Questions About New Sod, Turf Replacement & Lawn Installation

Does Bakerss install sod in North Myrtle Beach SC?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance provides sod installation, new lawn installation, turf replacement, yard repair, soil preparation and lawn support throughout North Myrtle Beach.

What types of sod can be installed?

Common warm-season sod options may include centipede, Bermuda, zoysia and St. Augustine depending on sunlight, soil, irrigation, traffic and property goals.

Do you remove old grass before installing sod?

Yes. When needed, sod installation can include old turf removal, weed cleanup, site prep, grading, soil work and debris cleanup before new sod is installed.

Can you grade the yard before sod installation?

Yes. Yard grading, leveling, low-area correction and topsoil work can be included depending on drainage, slope, access and existing property conditions.

Do I need irrigation for new sod?

New sod needs consistent water during establishment. Irrigation is strongly helpful, but the exact watering plan depends on turf type, weather, soil, shade and site conditions.

How is sod installation priced?

Sod installation is priced by square footage, turf type, delivery, site access, prep work, old grass removal, grading, soil amendments, irrigation concerns and cleanup requirements.

Do you install sod for HOAs and commercial properties?

Yes. Bakerss installs sod for homes, vacation rentals, HOAs, condo communities, apartment properties, commercial properties and managed landscapes.

Can sod installation be combined with landscaping?

Yes. Sod installation can be combined with landscaping, mulch, pine straw, shrubs, plants, palm trimming, pressure washing and recurring lawn care.

Do you provide lawn care after sod installation?

Yes. Bakerss can provide mowing, lawn care, landscaping and recurring property maintenance after the sod has established enough for service.
