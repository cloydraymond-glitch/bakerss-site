The Pier Community Handyman Profile

How Maximum Dual Exposure and Hurricane History Shape Garden City Beach Repairs

Garden City Beach's geographic position creates the most demanding exterior hardware maintenance environment among the communities we serve south of Myrtle Beach. The dual exposure — Atlantic salt air from the east, marsh humidity from the south — concentrates two corrosive influences simultaneously on every property. The community's unincorporated character means there are no municipal maintenance standards creating a baseline, which makes the contrast between maintained and unmaintained properties more visually stark than in code-enforced communities. And the hurricane history that the Garden City Pier embodies — destroyed and rebuilt multiple times through the community's history — is reflected in residential construction that includes hurricane-repair patchwork across many properties' original fabric.

This hurricane repair heritage creates a specific handyman expertise requirement: the ability to work on construction that is not uniform. A Garden City Beach cottage that was damaged in a significant storm and repaired in sections may have 1960s original framing, 1980s replacement siding, 2005 window replacement, and 2018 roof work — four different eras of construction in one structure. Hardware, fixtures, and building systems from these different eras require different approaches, different parts sourcing, and different installation methods. Our experience with Garden City Beach's mixed-era construction is what enables effective repairs on the property stock this community contains.
