FAQ

Garden City Beach Expanded Services FAQ

Do you install and remove Christmas lights in Garden City Beach?

Yes. Bakerss installs and removes outdoor Christmas lights, wreaths and seasonal décor for homes, vacation rentals, HOAs and managed properties. Many clients supply their own décor and use Bakerss for safe setup and removal.

Can you handle storm prep before hurricanes or major storms?

Yes. Storm prep includes outdoor furniture tie-down, drain clearing, shutter assistance, generator checks and exterior readiness items. Post-storm services include debris removal, temporary tarping support and photo documentation.

Do you offer HVAC filter replacement without doing HVAC repair?

Yes. HVAC filter replacement is a maintenance service only. We can replace owner-supplied or approved filters monthly or quarterly for STR owners, second-home owners and property managers.

Do you repair fences and decks near the beach?

Yes. Fence and deck repairs are core coastal property services. We handle storm-damaged panels, gates, loose railings, deck boards, stairs, fasteners and repair scopes tied to salt air and moisture exposure.

Do you provide window cleaning, dryer vent cleaning and carpet cleaning?

Yes. These are strong add-ons for vacation rentals, move-out cleans, seasonal refreshes and HOA properties. Window cleaning pairs well with pressure washing; dryer vents and carpet cleaning pair well with interior cleaning and STR turnover work.

Can you support builders with punch list and final cleaning?

Yes. New construction punch list and clean can include final cleaning, touch-up painting, pressure washing, fixture corrections, sod support and closeout help before owner walkthrough or closing.
