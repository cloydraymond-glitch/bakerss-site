Questions

Garden City Pool Maintenance FAQ

How much is pool service in Garden City SC?

Weekly residential service starts at $150–$250/month, beach rental pools at $200–$350/month with turnover scheduling. Canal-front pools with heavy salt exposure get a free on-site quote.

What happens to my pool after a tropical storm?

Wind-blown debris, salt spray, and rain dilution wreck chemistry fast. We prioritize Garden City pools for post-storm visits — debris removal, full rebalance, and an equipment check, typically within 48 hours.

Do you service pools on the canals?

Yes — canal-front pools are some of our most exposure-prone work, so every visit includes rail, ladder, and pump-fitting corrosion checks alongside standard cleaning.

Can you keep our rental pool guest-ready all summer?

That's the job — we sync to your booking calendar, service before check-ins, and send photo reports so you see the pool exactly as your guests will.
