Why Garden City Is Different

Pool Care Built for Salt, Sand, Guests and Storms

Garden City Beach is a narrow coastal market with a high concentration of vacation rentals, second homes, canal-front homes, and beach-access properties. Pool service here is not just “brush and balance.” The environment pushes pools out of range quickly: salt spray accelerates rail and ladder corrosion, summer humidity supports algae pressure, stormwater dilutes sanitizer, and guests bring sunscreen, sand, and organic load into the water every turnover week.

A Garden City pool also has a visibility problem: the pool is often one of the first amenities a guest checks after arrival. Cloudy water, leaf debris, algae on steps, low chlorine, or a noisy pump can trigger a complaint before the vacation even starts. Our service is designed around that reality — clean water, documented visits, quick reporting, and a maintenance routine that protects rental income as much as it protects the equipment.

Canal Homes

Creek & Canal Exposure

Homes along Garden City canals and inlet-side corridors need extra corrosion attention. We check rails, ladders, pump fittings, baskets, pressure readings, and exposed hardware for salt-air wear before small issues become expensive failures.

Beach Rentals

Guest-Ready Scheduling

Rental pools need service that works with check-in windows. We help Garden City STR owners keep pools ready for arrival day, peak weekends, family groups, and back-to-back summer bookings.

Storm Season

Fast Recovery After Weather

After tropical rain, wind, and debris, pools need more than skimming. We remove debris, rebalance chemistry, check equipment, and report anything that needs repair or owner approval.

What Every Garden City Pool Visit Includes

  • Water testing for pH, chlorine, alkalinity, stabilizer and calcium
  • Chemical balancing based on actual test readings

Pool Maintenance FAQ — Garden City Beach

How much is pool maintenance in Garden City Beach SC?+
Weekly residential pool maintenance typically starts at $150–$250/month. Vacation rental pools commonly run $200–$350/month depending on size, debris load, rental schedule, chemical demand and exposure. Canal-front and commercial-style pools receive a free quote.
Do you service Garden City vacation rental pools?+
Yes. Vacation rental pool service is a core Garden City need. We can coordinate around check-in windows, provide photo reports, and flag issues that could affect guest experience before they become complaints.
Do you clean pools after storms?+
Yes. Storm cleanup may include debris removal, skimming, brushing, vacuuming, shock treatment, chemistry rebalance, basket clearing and equipment checks. Pricing depends on severity and chemical requirements.
Are chemicals included in the monthly price?+
Monthly service pricing covers labor and routine service unless a custom plan says otherwise. Chemicals, specialty treatments, parts and repair materials are normally billed separately and itemized.
Can you service pools when the owner is out of state?+
Yes. Many Garden City owners are remote. We can use gate codes, lockboxes or manager access, then send photo documentation and service notes after each visit.
Do you handle pool repairs?+
We perform basic checks and can identify repair needs. Small maintenance items may be handled in-house when appropriate; larger equipment repairs or replacements are quoted separately before work begins.
