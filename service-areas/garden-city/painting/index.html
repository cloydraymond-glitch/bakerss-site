Painting Built for Garden City Beach Conditions

Garden City Beach sits between Surfside Beach and Murrells Inlet, with direct Atlantic exposure on one side and tidal marsh humidity nearby. That geography creates two paint enemies at the same time: salt contamination that interferes with adhesion, and mildew pressure that stains new paint if surfaces are not treated before coating. A basic rinse and repaint may look good for a few months, but it does not create a long-lasting coastal finish.

Many Garden City Beach properties also have mixed-era construction. A cottage may include original siding, repaired storm sections, replaced fascia, patched trim, newer windows, and older porch framing. Those different substrates do not all accept paint the same way. We inspect the surface, identify failed caulk, soft trim, chalking, bare wood, mildew staining, previous coating failure, and storm-repaired sections before pricing the job.

Our Painting Process

The Bakerss Painting Process for Garden City Beach

1

On-site inspection

We inspect siding, trim, fascia, caulk, previous coatings, mildew, chalking, and repair history before quoting.

2

Soft wash and surface cleaning

Exterior projects begin with soft washing and chemistry designed for salt film and biological growth. High pressure is not used on siding.

Dry time and prep

We allow proper dry time, scrape loose paint, sand edges, replace failed caulk, spot-prime bare wood, and treat problem areas.

Cleanup and walkthrough

Final inspection, cleanup, touch-ups, and photo documentation available for out-of-town owners.

FAQ

Painting FAQ — Garden City Beach

How long does exterior paint last in Garden City Beach?+
Near-ocean and pier-area properties often see 5–7 years on older construction, 7–9 years on newer construction, and 8–10 years for more inland Garden City West properties when properly prepped and painted with premium coastal-grade products.
Why do you soft wash before exterior painting?+
Salt film, mildew, chalking, and organic growth prevent paint adhesion. Soft washing with surfactant and mildewcide removes contamination without damaging siding or forcing water behind older cottage materials.
How much does painting cost in Garden City Beach?+
Interior 3BR painting typically starts around $1,800. Exterior single-story painting typically starts around $2,300. The exact price depends on size, prep, repairs, height, substrate condition, and paint system.
When should I repaint a Garden City Beach vacation rental?+
Interior STR repainting is best from November through January so updated listing photos are ready before February and spring booking activity. Exterior work is best in mild, dry weather windows.
Do you paint older Garden City Beach cottages?+
Yes. Older cottages are a major part of the Garden City Beach property mix. We inspect mixed substrates, repaired sections, older trim, fascia, caulk failure, and previous coating issues before quoting.
Do you handle small painting jobs like trim, doors, shutters and touch-ups?+
Yes. Trim refreshes, shutters, doors, accent colors, fascia sections, rental touch-ups, drywall patches and room-by-room interior painting are all available.
