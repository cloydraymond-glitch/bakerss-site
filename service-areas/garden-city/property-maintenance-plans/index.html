Questions About Property Maintenance Plans

Does Bakerss offer property maintenance plans in Garden City SC?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance offers recurring property maintenance plans in Garden City for homes, vacation rentals, second homes, HOAs, apartments, commercial properties and managed properties.

What can a property maintenance plan include?

A property maintenance plan can include lawn care, landscaping, cleaning, handyman repairs, pressure washing, pool maintenance, vacation home watch, gutter cleaning, window cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, fence repair, irrigation support and work order service depending on scope.

Can plans be customized?

Yes. Property maintenance plans are customized by property type, service frequency, owner needs, rental activity, exterior condition, cleaning needs, work order volume and budget.

Do you offer plans for vacation rentals and second homes?

Yes. Bakerss can build maintenance plans for Garden City vacation rentals, Airbnbs, VRBOs, second homes and absentee-owner properties.

Can commercial, apartment and HOA properties use maintenance plans?

Yes. Maintenance plans can be built for commercial properties, apartments, HOAs, community common areas, business properties and managed rental properties.

Can lawn care and landscaping be included?

Yes. Lawn care, landscaping, mulch, bed maintenance, trimming, blowing and curb appeal work can be included in a plan.

Can handyman repairs be included?

Yes. Handyman repairs, work orders, punch lists and maintenance tasks can be included or quoted separately depending on the plan structure.

How are property maintenance plans priced?

Pricing depends on property size, service frequency, included services, work order volume, cleaning scope, lawn and exterior needs, pool requirements, reporting expectations and materials.

Can you provide storm checks?

Yes. Storm checks, visible property observations and post-storm maintenance notes can be included depending on the property and plan scope.

How do I request a property maintenance plan quote?
