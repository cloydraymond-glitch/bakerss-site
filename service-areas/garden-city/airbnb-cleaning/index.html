Why Garden City Beach Is a Different STR Market

Cleaning for the pier-town guest, not a generic beach rental

Garden City Beach sits between Surfside Beach and Murrells Inlet, with its own STR identity: more residential than central Myrtle Beach, more beach-cottage driven than resort-tower driven, and more casual than the premium Lowcountry experience south of the marsh. Guests who book here usually want direct beach access, the pier-community feel, outdoor showers, decks, golf carts, family cookouts, and a property that feels cared for without feeling over-commercialized.

That guest profile changes the cleaning standard. A Garden City turnover is not just “wipe, vacuum, leave.” Sand control, sliding-door tracks, outdoor shower cleanliness, deck/entry presentation, bathroom mildew prevention, kitchen reset, linen presentation, and maintenance reporting all affect whether the next guest feels like the property is ready for them.

Seasonal Deep Cleans

Protect reviews before peak booking season

Garden City Beach rentals should be deep cleaned before the heavy spring/summer rental season and again after the season if the property has heavy occupancy. This is where baseboards, inside appliances, cabinets, furniture edges, vents, grout, ceiling fans, blinds, and hidden sand buildup get addressed.
