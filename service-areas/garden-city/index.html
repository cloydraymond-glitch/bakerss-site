Garden City Beach FAQ

Garden City Beach property maintenance questions.

How much does property maintenance cost in Garden City Beach?

Pricing depends on property size, access, the number of services, visit frequency and the condition of the home. Request a walkthrough for a written scope and estimate.

Who looks after vacation rentals in Garden City Beach?

Bakerss Property Services can coordinate rental turnover cleaning, minor repairs, preventive maintenance and exterior upkeep for owners and property managers, subject to scheduling.

Can Bakerss watch my Garden City Beach second home while I am away?

Yes. Ask about scheduled visual home-watch visits, documented observations and repair coordination. The scope and response procedures are agreed in advance.

How do I protect an oceanfront home from salt air?

Rinse or clean appropriate exterior surfaces, inspect exposed metal and hardware, maintain protective finishes and address corrosion early. Use methods appropriate to each surface.

How should I prepare a Garden City Beach home for a hurricane?

Follow official evacuation guidance, secure loose exterior items when safe, document property condition and arrange an appropriate pre-storm inspection. Never enter during unsafe conditions.

What should I do after a storm in Garden City Beach?

Wait for authorities to confirm access is safe, document visible damage, avoid downed power lines and unstable structures, and arrange qualified assessments and repairs.

How often should I service AC near the ocean?

A common starting point is professional preventive service at least annually; coastal exposure, rental occupancy and manufacturer guidance may justify more frequent inspections and filter changes.

How can I reduce mold risk in a vacant beach house?

Control indoor humidity, maintain working HVAC and drainage, inspect for leaks and arrange periodic property checks. Active mold problems may require specialist assessment.

Do you maintain raised beach houses, decks and stairs?

We can assess maintenance needs involving stairs, railings, deck surfaces and other accessible exterior components. Structural or regulated work may require appropriately qualified contractors.

Do I need a permit for a deck or fence in Garden City Beach?

Requirements depend on the property jurisdiction, scope, flood zone and any HOA rules. Verify requirements with Horry County and any applicable authorities before starting work.

Can Bakerss support Garden City Beach condo associations and HOAs?

Yes. We can discuss recurring cleaning, grounds, exterior maintenance, common-area work orders and documentation for condominium and HOA properties.

Can you provide a free estimate or property walkthrough?

Yes. Contact Bakerss with the property location, photos and requested services to discuss a quote or walkthrough. Availability and scheduling are confirmed directly.
