Garden City Beach sits at the boundary of Horry and Georgetown counties and has long been known as a quieter, more laid-back alternative to the more commercial Grand Strand towns to the north. ⚓ Epoxy’s Marine Origins — Relevant Here

Epoxy's earliest commercial use was in marine applications — Russian chemists first used it as a primer to improve paint adhesion on ship hulls. The reason: saltwater and humidity caused conventional coatings to fail rapidly in marine environments. Garden City's proximity to the ocean creates the same conditions on concrete that ships face on steel. The marine-grade chemistry that solved those problems in the early 20th century is the foundation of what we install in your garage today.

Do older Garden City beach cottages have concrete slabs that can be epoxy coated?+
Yes — most mid-century beach cottages in Garden City have concrete slab garage and storage areas that are excellent candidates for epoxy coating, though they often require additional prep work. Scaled surfaces, previous coating failures, and surface moisture issues are common in older construction. We assess all of this during the free estimate and factor it into the quote. How long does epoxy last?+
With 100% solids commercial epoxy installed correctly on a diamond-ground, moisture-primed surface, 20–30 years is a realistic lifespan for residential garage use. Big-box water-based products typically peel within 1–3 years. The difference is entirely in the solids content, surface preparation, and system design.
