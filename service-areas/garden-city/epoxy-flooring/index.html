Garden City Beach Epoxy Flooring

Beach Cottage Concrete Needs More Than Paint

A Garden City Beach garage floor is not a decorative afterthought. For many properties, it is a working beach-storage zone: sandy chairs, saltwater fishing equipment, coolers, pool chemicals, bicycles, golf-cart accessories, owner supplies, and turnover materials all live on that slab. Bare concrete absorbs stains, holds dust, and looks unfinished in listing photos or owner walk-throughs. A properly installed epoxy system turns that concrete into a sealed, easy-clean, high-presentation surface.

The challenge is the slab underneath. Garden City Beach includes original 1950s and 1960s cottages, raised coastal homes, newer infill properties, and garages near both the Horry and Georgetown County sides of the community. Older concrete may have scaling, hairline cracks, previous coatings, moisture vapor issues, and salt-air surface contamination. That is why we do not install over a quick acid etch. We mechanically diamond grind the slab, assess moisture concerns, repair cracks where appropriate, and build the system from the concrete up.

FAQ

Epoxy Flooring FAQ — Garden City Beach

Can you epoxy older Garden City Beach cottage concrete?+
Yes. Older cottage slabs are common in Garden City Beach. They often require more grinding, crack repair, coating removal, or moisture evaluation, but they can usually be coated successfully when the prep is done correctly.
How much does an epoxy garage floor cost in Garden City Beach?+
A 1-car garage starts around $1,800, a 2-car garage starts around $3,000, and larger garages start around $4,500. Final price depends on square footage, slab condition, finish selection, and whether old coating removal or moisture-primer work is needed.
Why is diamond grinding better than acid etching?+
Diamond grinding mechanically opens the concrete surface and removes weak material, giving the epoxy a stronger bond. Acid etching is inconsistent on older or contaminated coastal concrete and is a major reason DIY floors peel.
Do you install epoxy on both the Horry and Georgetown County sides of Garden City?+
Yes. We service the full Garden City Beach area, including properties near the Horry-Georgetown county line, with the same prep standard and installation system.
What colors are best for a beach cottage garage?+
Sandy neutrals, driftwood blends, stone gray, oyster-shell tones, tan flake, and earth-inspired finishes are strong choices for Garden City cottage style. Ocean blues and metallics also work well in newer coastal homes.
How soon can I use the garage after installation?+
Timing depends on the system and weather conditions. We provide specific cure-time instructions after installation, including when the floor is safe for foot traffic, storage, and vehicle parking.
