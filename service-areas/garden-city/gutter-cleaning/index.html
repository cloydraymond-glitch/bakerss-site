Storm-season planning

When Garden City Properties Should Consider Gutter Cleaning

Before storm season: gutters should be reviewed before repeated heavy rain exposes clogs, downspout issues or overflow points. Pre-season cleaning is especially useful for second homes, rentals and HOA buildings that are not watched daily.

After major wind or rain: tropical systems, thunderstorms and wind events can load gutters with leaves, needles, roof grit and branches. Post-storm service can help catch fresh blockages before the next rain.

After leaf and pollen buildup: even coastal properties without heavy tree cover can collect pollen, sand, shingle grit and wind-driven debris. Neighborhoods with pine trees often need more frequent attention.

Before owner arrival or peak rental season: clean gutters reduce visible overflow risk and support a more polished exterior presentation for homeowners, guests and property managers.
