Garden City pressure washing FAQ

Questions About Pressure Washing

Does Bakerss provide pressure washing in Garden City SC?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance provides pressure washing in Garden City for homes, vacation rentals, Airbnbs, second homes, HOAs, apartments, commercial properties and property maintenance clients.

What surfaces can you pressure wash?

Pressure washing can include driveways, sidewalks, patios, pool decks, porches, entries, walkways, exterior hard surfaces, dumpster pads, storefront areas and select exterior surfaces depending on material and condition.

Can you pressure wash vacation rentals and Airbnbs?

Yes. Bakerss can quote pressure washing for Garden City vacation rentals, Airbnbs and second homes to improve guest-ready curb appeal and exterior presentation.

Do you pressure wash pool decks?

Yes. Pool deck pressure washing can be quoted for residential pools, rental pools, HOA amenities and commercial pool areas depending on surface type, staining and access.

Can you clean commercial sidewalks and entries?

Yes. Commercial sidewalks, entries, storefront areas, patios, dumpster pads and exterior hard surfaces can be quoted for one-time or recurring cleaning.

Do you use soft washing?

Some exterior surfaces may need a lower-pressure or chemical-assisted approach rather than high pressure. The method depends on surface material, staining, age and risk of damage.

Can pressure washing remove rust or oil stains?

Some stains may improve, but rust, oil and deep stains may require specialty treatment and may not fully disappear. Expectations should be set before the work is approved.

How often should Garden City properties be pressure washed?

Frequency depends on shade, humidity, traffic, tree cover, pool use, rental schedule and proximity to salt air. Many properties benefit from seasonal or annual exterior cleaning, while high-traffic rentals and commercial areas may need more frequent service.

Can pressure washing damage surfaces?

Improper pressure can damage sensitive materials. That is why surface type, age, finish, staining and condition should be reviewed before work is performed.

Can pressure washing be scheduled before guest arrival?

Yes. For vacation rentals, pressure washing can be scheduled before busy periods, owner visits, listing photos or guest arrival windows when scheduling allows.

Do you need water access?

Most pressure washing jobs require reliable water access at the property. Water availability should be confirmed during quoting.

How is pressure washing priced?

Pricing depends on surface type, square footage, staining level, access, water availability, height, chemical needs, property type and whether service is bundled into a maintenance plan.

Can pressure washing be part of a property maintenance plan?

Yes. Pressure washing can be bundled with lawn care, landscaping, pool maintenance, Airbnb cleaning, HOA maintenance, commercial maintenance, gutter cleaning and recurring property maintenance plans.

How do I request a pressure washing quote?

You can request a quote online, call or text 843-467-7136, or email info@bakerss.com with the property address, photos, surfaces to clean and any staining concerns.
