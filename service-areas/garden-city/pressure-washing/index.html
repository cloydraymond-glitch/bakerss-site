Why Garden City Beach is different

Salt from the ocean. Humidity from the inlet. Sand from every guest arrival.

Garden City Beach sits between Surfside Beach and Murrells Inlet, with the Atlantic Ocean on one side and marsh influence nearby. That positioning creates a surface-cleaning problem most inland properties do not experience: salt film sticks to siding and windows, marsh humidity feeds mildew, and rental traffic carries sand across driveways, stairs, decks, pool areas, and entry walks. Pressure washing here is not cosmetic only. It protects paint, reduces slippery organic growth, improves listing-photo quality, and keeps guest arrival areas looking managed.

For Garden City Beach vacation rentals, exterior presentation is part of the guest experience. A clean driveway, bright entryway, washed deck, and mildew-free siding support better first impressions before the guest ever unlocks the door. For homeowners, routine soft washing and concrete cleaning keep salt and mildew from becoming a permanent layer that shortens paint life and stains porous concrete.

Garden City Beach service standard: siding gets soft washed, not blasted; concrete gets appropriate pressure; decks get material-specific treatment; and post-storm salt spray gets removed fast before it bonds into paint and trim.

Soft wash vs pressure wash

The right method matters on older Garden City cottages

Soft washing

For siding, trim, soffits and painted surfaces

  • Low pressure under controlled application
  • Surfactant to release salt film
  • Mildewcide to kill organic growth
  • Safer around siding seams, windows and older construction
  • Longer-lasting results than water pressure alone
Pressure washing

For concrete, masonry and hard surfaces

  • Driveways and walkways
  • Concrete patios and pool decks
  • Brick or masonry surfaces when appropriate
  • Surface cleaner use where feasible
  • Controlled pressure to reduce striping and etching risk

FAQ

Garden City Beach pressure washing FAQ

How much does pressure washing cost in Garden City Beach?

Driveway cleaning starts around $125. House soft washing typically ranges from $230 to $590. Full exterior packages commonly run $575 to $1,050 depending on size, buildup, height and access.

Should Garden City Beach siding be pressure washed?

No. Siding should be soft washed with low pressure and the correct cleaning solution. High pressure can force water behind siding, damage seals, disturb paint, and create issues on older beach cottages.

How often should oceanfront Garden City Beach homes be washed?

Usually twice per year: pre-season and post-season. Oceanfront homes and pier-area STRs accumulate salt and mildew faster than inland properties. Garden City West homes often do well with annual washing.

Do you offer post-storm washing?

Yes. Post-storm washing removes salt spray, organic debris, dirty runoff, and storm film from siding, concrete, decks and entry areas. The best window is usually within 48–72 hours after the storm passes.

Can you wash a vacation rental before guest arrival?

Yes. We work with STR owners and property managers to schedule exterior cleaning around openings in the calendar, pre-season photos, mid-season touch-ups, and post-storm resets.

Do you clean decks and patios?

Yes. We clean wood decks, composite decks, concrete patios, pool decks, entry steps, balconies and outdoor living areas using the method appropriate for the surface.
