Why Murrells Inlet Properties Need Specialized Maintenance

Murrells Inlet is known as the Seafood Capital of South Carolina — a tight-knit waterfront community with marshfront homes, waterway-adjacent properties, and a growing vacation rental market. The proximity to tidal marshes and the Murrells Inlet waterway creates elevated humidity and salt air exposure that accelerates deterioration on all property surfaces.

Waterfront and marsh-adjacent properties in Murrells Inlet deal with mold and mildew growth that exceeds even standard coastal norms. Paint peels faster. Gutters fill with organic debris from the surrounding wetland vegetation. Lawns deal with drainage challenges from the low-elevation waterfront terrain. Bakerss understands the specific demands of maintaining property in this environment.

Whether you own a marshfront second home, a vacation rental in the Murrells Inlet community, or an investment property in the surrounding area, Bakerss delivers all-in-one property maintenance with one phone call.
