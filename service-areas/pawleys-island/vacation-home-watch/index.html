Does Bakerss offer vacation home watch in Pawleys Island SC?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance offers vacation home watch in Pawleys Island for absentee owners, second homes, vacation rentals, managed properties and seasonal properties.

What can a vacation home watch visit include?

A vacation home watch visit can include exterior observations, visible issue checks, storm checks, yard and curb appeal observations, basic interior checks when approved, photo notes and maintenance coordination by agreed scope.

Can Bakerss check the property after storms?

Yes. Storm checks can be included or requested depending on availability and approved scope. We can look for visible exterior issues, debris, gutter concerns, fence damage, water intrusion signs and maintenance needs.

Can home watch be paired with cleaning, lawn care or handyman services?

Yes. Vacation home watch can be paired with Airbnb cleaning, interior cleaning, lawn care, landscaping, handyman services, pressure washing, gutter cleaning and property maintenance plans.

How is vacation home watch priced?

Pricing depends on visit frequency, property size, interior access, reporting expectations, storm checks, photos, travel, rental schedule and whether additional maintenance services are included.

How do I request a quote?

You can request a quote online, call or text 843-467-7136, or email info@bakerss.com with the property address, how often you want checks, whether interior access is needed, and what maintenance services should be included.

Is vacation home watch the same as property management?

No. Vacation home watch is focused on approved property checks, visible observations, reporting and maintenance coordination. It can support owners and property managers, but it does not replace legal property management, leasing, rent collection or tenant-placement services.
