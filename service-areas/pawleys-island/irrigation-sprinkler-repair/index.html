Bakerss can help with broken sprinkler heads, damaged risers, clogged nozzles, poor spray coverage, dry spots, leaking zones, stuck zones, timer issues, controller settings, valve concerns, overspray and irrigation problems affecting lawn or landscape health.

Should irrigation be checked before sod installation?

Yes. Irrigation should be checked before sod installation whenever possible because new sod needs consistent coverage while roots establish. Weak zones, broken heads or poor timer settings can cause new turf to fail.

Yes. Irrigation repair is often paired with lawn care, landscaping and sod installation because watering affects turf health, plant beds, curb appeal and long-term property maintenance.
