A Strong Turnover Is More Than “Clean the House”

Vacation rental cleaning should reset the property for the next guest while also catching visible issues that could become complaints. Bakerss builds the scope around the property’s checklist, timing and maintenance needs.

Common turnover focus areas

  • Kitchens, counters, sinks and visible appliance surfaces
  • Bathrooms, showers, toilets, mirrors and fixtures
  • Floors, sand, dust, trash and general reset details
  • Bedrooms and bed reset by approved scope
  • Entry areas, patios or visible guest-facing spots by scope

Maintenance observations can include

  • Guest damage or obvious broken items
  • Stains, missing items or visible repair needs
  • Pool, lawn, pressure washing or exterior concerns
  • Doors, locks, fixtures, drywall or hardware issues
  • Follow-up services that should be quoted separately
