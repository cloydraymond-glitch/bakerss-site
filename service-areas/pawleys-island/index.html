Pawleys Island FAQ

Pawleys Island property maintenance FAQs.

How much does property maintenance cost in Pawleys Island?

Pricing depends on property size, visit frequency, access, service mix and the condition of the home. Request a property walkthrough for a written scope and estimate.

Who can look after my Pawleys Island second home while I am away?

Bakerss offers scheduled home-watch visits and can coordinate approved cleaning, grounds, HVAC and repair services. Visit frequency and reporting are agreed in advance.

Do you provide White Glove estate care in Pawleys Island?

Yes. We can develop a tailored recurring maintenance scope for larger or higher-maintenance properties, including coordination of multiple approved services.

How do I protect an oceanfront or creekfront home from salt air?

Inspect exposed metal, fasteners, finishes, doors and HVAC components routinely; clean surfaces using appropriate methods and address corrosion or water intrusion promptly.

How should I prepare a Pawleys Island home for a hurricane?

Follow local emergency guidance, secure loose outdoor items when safe, review drainage and gutters, document property conditions and arrange any permitted pre-storm work in advance.

What should I do after a coastal storm?

Follow official re-entry and electrical safety guidance first. Once access is safe, document visible damage, prevent additional water intrusion where feasible and arrange qualified inspections and repairs.

How can I reduce mildew or moisture in a vacant coastal home?

Keep HVAC and humidity control functioning, check for leaks, maintain ventilation as appropriate and schedule property observations. Suspected significant mold contamination needs qualified assessment.

Do you maintain homes in Litchfield and DeBordieu?

Service requests are considered for the wider Pawleys Island and Litchfield area, including gated communities where access is authorized. Confirm the exact property address and scope with Bakerss.

Can you coordinate HOA or condo common-area maintenance?

Yes. We can discuss grounds, cleaning, minor repairs and scheduled work orders for associations and property managers, subject to approval and access.

How often should coastal HVAC systems be maintained?

Many coastal homeowners plan professional preventive HVAC service at least annually, often before heavy cooling season. The appropriate schedule depends on equipment, usage and exposure.

Can you clean decks, docks and boardwalk surfaces?

We can assess suitable exterior cleaning and maintenance scopes. Dock or waterfront work may involve access restrictions, environmental requirements or specialist contractors.

Do I need a permit for a deck or fence in Pawleys Island?

Permit and design requirements vary by whether the property is within the Town of Pawleys Island or unincorporated Georgetown County, and by HOA or coastal restrictions. Confirm with the relevant authority before work begins.
