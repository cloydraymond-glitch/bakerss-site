Frequently asked questions

Pawleys Island Neighborhood Maintenance — Common Questions

What Pawleys Island neighborhoods does Bakerss serve?

Bakerss provides property maintenance across all Pawleys Island neighborhoods including Pawleys Plantation, Heritage Plantation, True Blue, Historic Pawleys, Oceanfront Pawleys and every community in between.

Do oceanfront and creekfront Pawleys Island properties need different maintenance than golf-community homes?

Yes. Oceanfront and creekfront properties in areas like Oceanfront Pawleys and Creekfront Pawleys often need salt-air-aware exterior care and home watch support for second homes, while golf communities like Pawleys Plantation, True Blue and Willbrook Plantation have course-facing landscaping standards.

Does Bakerss maintain historic and older cottage-style homes in Pawleys Island?

Yes. Historic Pawleys is known for older beach cottages that often need exterior care suited to established coastal homes rather than a generic new-construction approach, and Bakerss tailors maintenance accordingly.
