Pawleys Island window strategy

Window Cleaning That Fits Coastal Property Conditions

Pawleys Island window cleaning is different from basic inland glass cleaning. Coastal air, humidity, storms, sand, pollen, irrigation overspray and rental activity all affect how quickly windows become cloudy or streaked. The property may be clean, but dirty glass can still make it look unfinished.

Bakerss Property Maintenance can structure window cleaning around the actual property. That may include exterior glass only, inside-and-out cleaning, screens, tracks, sills, patio doors, storefront glass, vacation rental windows, post-project glass cleanup or recurring maintenance. The goal is clear glass and better property presentation without making the manager or homeowner coordinate several vendors.
