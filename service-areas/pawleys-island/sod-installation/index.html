Sod Installation That Fits Coastal Lawn Conditions

Pawleys Island sod installation requires more planning than a basic inland lawn project. Sandy soil, summer heat, salt air, storm runoff, shaded lots, rental traffic and irrigation coverage can all affect turf performance. The right sod project starts with measuring, evaluating the existing lawn, reviewing water access, identifying grade issues and choosing a turf type that fits the property.

Bakerss Property Maintenance can structure sod installation around the property’s real needs. That may include old turf removal, grading, soil preparation, topsoil, sod installation, rolling, cleanup, irrigation repair coordination, lawn care and ongoing property maintenance. The goal is a clean finished lawn with better long-term success, not just a quick green cover.
