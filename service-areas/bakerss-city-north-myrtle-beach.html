About North Myrtle Beach

Serving the North Myrtle Beach Community

North Myrtle Beach was incorporated in 1968 through the consolidation of four beach communities: Crescent Beach, Cherry Grove Beach, Ocean Drive Beach, and Windy Hill Beach. Today it's a vibrant coastal city of approximately 17,000 year-round residents that hosts over 5 million visitors annually. The city is famous as the birthplace of the shag dance — South Carolina's official state dance — and has evolved into one of the Grand Strand's most sought-after markets for vacation rental investment. Communities like Barefoot Resort, Tidewater Plantation, and Barefoot Landing have established North Myrtle Beach as a destination for both tourism and high-end residential living.

North Myrtle Beach properties face all the challenges of coastal South Carolina maintenance — salt air, humidity, mildew, sand intrusion, and aggressive summer lawn growth — with the added complexity of high STR turnover rates. A property that hosts back-to-back guests throughout the summer generates far more wear on surfaces, appliances, and grounds than a typical residential home. Owners who don't maintain a regular cleaning and maintenance schedule find their properties deteriorating faster, their reviews declining, and their revenue dropping. The oceanfront exposure also accelerates corrosion and paint degradation on exterior surfaces.

North Myrtle Beach has an exceptionally high concentration of short-term rental properties — from oceanfront condos in Cherry Grove to golf villas in Barefoot Resort to luxury single-family homes in Tidewater Plantation. Bakerss serves all of these property types with specialized services matched to each community's standards and guest expectations. We're one of the most requested maintenance providers in the North Myrtle Beach STR market because owners know we show up, do the job right, and communicate clearly.
