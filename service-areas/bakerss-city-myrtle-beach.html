About Myrtle Beach

Serving the Myrtle Beach Community

Myrtle Beach was incorporated in 1938 and grew from a quiet beach town into South Carolina's most visited tourist destination. Today it draws over 15 million visitors annually, making short-term rentals one of the most active property segments in the country. With a population of approximately 35,000 year-round residents — swelling to hundreds of thousands during peak season — Myrtle Beach is a city of homeowners, investors, and property managers who all share one common need: reliable, professional property maintenance they can count on.

The coastal environment creates real challenges for property owners in Myrtle Beach. Salt air accelerates rust, corrosion, and paint degradation on exterior surfaces. High summer humidity promotes mildew and algae growth on siding, driveways, and decks. Sand and heavy rainfall fill gutters quickly, and the year-round warm climate means lawns grow aggressively for 9 or 10 months of the year. Properties that aren't maintained consistently show wear much faster than inland homes — and in a competitive STR market, that means lost bookings and lower nightly rates.

Myrtle Beach has one of the most diverse property mixes of any coastal market: oceanfront condos, resort communities, golf course neighborhoods, inland residential subdivisions, commercial properties, and everything in between. Bakerss serves all of them — whether you need turnover cleaning for your Airbnb condo, weekly lawn care for your HOA community, or a full exterior refresh before listing your home for sale.
