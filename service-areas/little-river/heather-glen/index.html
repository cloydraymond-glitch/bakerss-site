Neighborhood Profile

Heather Glen — Little River's Premier Golf Community

Heather Glen Golf Links developed in the late 1980s and early 1990s, establishing the 27-hole golf facility (Blue, Red, and White courses) that has anchored the community's identity and driven its residential development for three decades. The courses are designed with the Grand Strand's characteristic flat coastal terrain and feature the tree-lined, water-hazard design that has made Heather Glen consistently popular among both resort golfers visiting the Grand Strand and the permanent and semi-permanent residents who play the course regularly.

Residential development followed the course opening, with communities of patio homes and single-family homes built through the 1990s and 2000s in configurations that place many properties adjacent to or with views of the golf fairways. The product was developed with the active adult buyer specifically in mind — patio homes with lower exterior maintenance burden, community amenities appropriate for social and recreational activity, and the quality-of-life positioning that makes Little River competitive with Myrtle Beach for the retirement relocation buyer.

Heather Glen's active adult and retirement demographic creates specific maintenance service preferences that differ from the primary residence or STR investor markets. This demographic values reliability and consistency — a maintenance relationship that functions without requiring constant owner coordination. Many Heather Glen homeowners split time between their Little River property and a northern home, making a trusted local maintenance team that manages the property professionally during their absence an absolute requirement, not an option.
