Little River SC + Epoxy Flooring

Blue Crab Capital of the Grand Strand. Marine-Grade Protection for Waterfront Concrete.

Little River is an unincorporated waterfront community at the northernmost point of Horry County, adjacent to the North Carolina border, on the Little River Inlet and the Intracoastal Waterway. The Blue Crab Festival has drawn visitors every May since 1975. Evidence of Native American settlement at Waites Island near Little River has been documented — including burial mounds and pottery shards placing human habitation here for thousands of years. Today, Little River features a marina district, waterfront dining, sportfishing, and a mix of waterfront estates and residential neighborhoods. The proximity to water is everything here — and it's why moisture preparation is critical for every concrete surface in the community.

Epoxy's Original Marine Application

Epoxy was first developed for marine use: protecting ship hulls from saltwater and humidity — the exact same exposure Little River's concrete faces daily. The chemistry has been refined over 90 years, but the fundamental application is unchanged. Your garage sits in the same environment those early marine chemists were solving.

💧

Waterfront Moisture Testing

Little River's position on the Intracoastal Waterway means properties here experience some of the highest salt air and moisture exposure in Horry County. Concrete in waterfront properties requires additional moisture vapor transmission testing and a robust moisture barrier primer.

Diamond Grinding — Marine-Grade Prep

Salt air at Little River accelerates surface degradation more aggressively than inland areas. Diamond grinding removes the full compromised surface layer. Acid etching — used in most DIY kits — leaves it in place. This distinction is the difference between a 20-year floor and an 18-month failure.
