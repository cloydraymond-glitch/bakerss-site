About Little River, SC

Little River — Authentic Working Waterfront at the NC Border

Little River sits at the northern tip of Horry County — just across the state line from Brunswick County, North Carolina — at the confluence of the Intracoastal Waterway and the Little River Inlet. Its history as an 18th century commercial fishing port defines its identity in ways that no amount of resort development pressure has erased. The deep-sea fishing charter fleet, the seafood restaurants and marina along Mineola Avenue, and the Blue Crab Festival held every May since 1981 — drawing over 100,000 visitors annually — are the ongoing expressions of a working waterfront community that has chosen authenticity over commercialization.

This authentic character has attracted a specific type of new resident: retirees and active adults from the Mid-Atlantic and Midwest who specifically chose Little River over Myrtle Beach's resort corridor because they wanted the coastal lifestyle without the resort-commercial intensity. Heather Glen's 27-hole golf community (Blue, Red, and White courses), Bridgewater's active adult development, and Cypress Village's retirement community represent the residential growth that this demographic has driven. The resulting Little River community is a genuine blend of working waterfront heritage and active adult lifestyle — a combination unique on the Grand Strand.

Little River's active adult and retirement community growth — Heather Glen, Bridgewater, Cypress Village, and Sunset Harbour have all attracted significant retirement relocation activity — creates a junk removal demand profile that peaks during moving-in and transition periods. Marine and Waterfront Equipment — The Fishing Village Legacy

Little River's working waterfront heritage creates a marine equipment removal category that purely residential communities don't generate. Private dock owners, boating enthusiasts, and the community's connection to the charter fishing fleet create accumulated marine equipment — worn dock hardware, old outboard motors, dated marine electronics, mooring equipment past its service life, and the general inventory of waterfront property ownership — that requires professional removal. We handle marine and dock equipment removal for Little River waterfront properties with the volume capacity and appropriate disposal pathways that marine equipment requires.
