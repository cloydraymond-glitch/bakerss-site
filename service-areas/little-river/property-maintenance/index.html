About Little River, SC

Little River — Authentic Working Waterfront at the NC Border

Little River sits at the northern tip of Horry County — just across the state line from Brunswick County, North Carolina — at the confluence of the Intracoastal Waterway and the Little River Inlet. Its history as an 18th century commercial fishing port defines its identity in ways that no amount of resort development pressure has erased. The deep-sea fishing charter fleet, the seafood restaurants and marina along Mineola Avenue, and the Blue Crab Festival held every May since 1981 — drawing over 100,000 visitors annually — are the ongoing expressions of a working waterfront community that has chosen authenticity over commercialization.

This authentic character has attracted a specific type of new resident: retirees and active adults from the Mid-Atlantic and Midwest who specifically chose Little River over Myrtle Beach's resort corridor because they wanted the coastal lifestyle without the resort-commercial intensity. Heather Glen's 27-hole golf community (Blue, Red, and White courses), Bridgewater's active adult development, and Cypress Village's retirement community represent the residential growth that this demographic has driven. The resulting Little River community is a genuine blend of working waterfront heritage and active adult lifestyle — a combination unique on the Grand Strand.

The Retirement Community Case for Professional Property Maintenance Plans

The Active Adult Homeowner

The Heather Glen or Bridgewater resident who moved to Little River for golf, the marina district's character, and the Blue Crab Festival lifestyle did not choose Little River to spend time managing contractors. They have the disposable income to invest in professional maintenance relationships — and they have a specific preference for maintenance that runs without requiring their attention. The Seasonal and Part-Time Resident

A significant portion of Little River's retirement community residents are seasonal — present in Little River for spring and fall, traveling or spending time at a northern property in the summer heat and winter cold. During their absence, the property doesn't maintain itself. Lawn growth continues through the 9-month growing season. Gutters load. The marine environment accumulates on exterior surfaces. A property maintenance plan that executes on schedule regardless of the owner's presence is the only solution that prevents the catch-up maintenance that returning seasonal residents otherwise face after every extended absence. We service, report, and flag any developing issues immediately — the owner returns to a maintained property, not to a list of deferred work.

The Vacation Rental + Primary Residence Owner

Little River's growing STR market includes properties that serve dual roles — part-time primary residences and part-time vacation rentals. Managing the maintenance calendar around this dual use is complex when coordinated with individual contractors. A maintenance plan that includes lawn care, gutter cleaning, pressure washing, STR turnover cleaning, and handyman hours — all calendar-synced and managed by one team — eliminates this complexity. One contact manages everything. The owner manages bookings and guests. The property is maintained throughout.
