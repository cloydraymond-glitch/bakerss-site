Georgetown County questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Bakerss currently serve all of Georgetown County?

Not yet. Georgetown County is an active expansion area, and service availability currently depends on the property location, requested work, route capacity and project scope.

Can I request service in Georgetown County now?

Yes. Send the property address and requested services. We will confirm whether we can currently accept the work, place the property in our expansion pipeline or recommend the next practical step.

Which Georgetown County areas are part of the planned buildout?

The planned county hub is structured to support Georgetown, Pawleys Island, Litchfield Beach, Waccamaw Neck, DeBordieu, Prince George, Belle Isle, Andrews, Sampit and additional communities as route coverage expands.

What services may become available across Georgetown County?

The planned service mix includes White Glove estate care, grounds maintenance, interior cleaning, HVAC preventive maintenance, home watch, pool support, exterior care and recurring property-maintenance plans, subject to location and availability.

Can Georgetown County clients use the Bakerss Client Portal?

Where service is approved and portal access is included, clients can review technician arrival and departure times, service notes, photographs and work-order updates.

Can one plan include cleaning, grounds and HVAC maintenance?

Yes, where route capacity permits. Approved White Glove plans can combine recurring interior cleaning, grounds care, HVAC preventive maintenance, home watch and priority work-order support.

Have a Georgetown County Property That Needs Complete Care?

Send the address, property type, service priorities and requested frequency. Bakerss will confirm current availability without overstating coverage.
