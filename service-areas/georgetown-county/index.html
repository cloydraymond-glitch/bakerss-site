Local maintenance priorities

Plan Around Water, Weather & Building Age

Georgetown County properties can face different risks than newer inland subdivisions or high-turnover beach rentals.

Flooding & Drainage

Check grading, downspouts, standing water and post-storm conditions. Active flooding, contamination and structural damage may require emergency specialists.

Humidity & Mildew

Moisture-prone siding, shaded surfaces, ventilation and HVAC upkeep benefit from regular inspection and appropriate cleaning methods.

Historic Materials

Brick, masonry, older siding and painted wood may need gentle methods and preservation review rather than aggressive washing.

Hurricane Preparation

Document existing conditions, secure loose exterior items, check drainage and arrange property checks as weather and staffing allow.

Bakerss does not represent itself as a flood-restoration contractor, licensed historic preservation specialist or emergency responder for every location. Permits, preservation approvals and regulated trades are handled according to the project requirements.
