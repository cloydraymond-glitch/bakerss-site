What a Deep Clean Actually Covers

The Difference Between a Standard Clean and a True Deep Clean

A standard recurring clean covers every regularly accessible surface — all floors, bathrooms, kitchen surfaces, dusting, trash, beds made. It maintains the baseline cleanliness that keeps a home feeling well-kept between deep cleans. What it does not cover is the layer beneath the layer — the inside of kitchen cabinets that accumulate six months of crumb and spill debris, the interior oven that develops baked-on residue that no one addresses on a weekly visit, the grout lines that a standard scrub brush passes over but doesn't penetrate deeply enough to remove embedded buildup, the window tracks that collect the combined efforts of months of Myrtle Beach's coastal environment.

A deep clean goes to all of these areas systematically. It takes 2–3 times longer than a standard clean — because 2–3 times as much is actually addressed. For first cleans of a new property, first cleans for new recurring clients, seasonal vacation rental preparation, and post-vacancy restoration, a deep clean is the correct service. It is the reset that makes everything that follows a standard clean genuinely effective.

What Every Myrtle Beach Deep Clean Includes

  • Inside all kitchen cabinets and drawers — complete interior cleaning
  • Full oven interior including hidden glass layer between oven door panes
  • Refrigerator interior — all shelves, drawers, and door compartments
  • Behind and under all appliances
  • Baseboards throughout — wiped, not just dusted
  • Window sills and full window tracks (the area standard cleaning skips)
  • Ceiling fans — blades and motor housing wiped down
  • Bathroom grout scrubbing — shower, tub surround, and floor grout lines
  • Tub and shower caulk lines — scrubbed and inspected for mildew penetration
  • Under-sink cabinet interiors
  • Wall spot-cleaning — the handprints and scuff marks that accumulate over time
  • All light switch plates and outlet covers — removed and cleaned
  • All doors and door frames — top surfaces and frame edges
  • Blind and window covering cleaning

Coastal Considerations

Why Myrtle Beach Homes Need More Frequent Deep Cleaning

The same subtropical coastal conditions that create outdoor maintenance demands in Myrtle Beach also affect indoor cleaning requirements. Year-round humidity sustains the bathroom mildew conditions that standard cleaning manages but deep cleaning fully resets — including grout and caulk cleaning that requires more than a weekly scrub. Salt air infiltrates every home near the Atlantic and creates the surface film on glass, appliances, and window frames that deep cleaning systematically removes. The result is that Myrtle Beach homes benefit from deep cleaning on a 2–4 times per year schedule rather than the 1–2 times that drier inland markets might require.

Deep Cleaning FAQ — Myrtle Beach

How often should a Myrtle Beach home get a deep clean?+
Primary residences: twice yearly is typical — spring and fall. Vacation rentals: 2–4 times per year depending on occupancy volume. High-occupancy STRs with 40+ stays annually benefit from quarterly deep cleaning. Properties with pets or smokers may need more frequent deep cleaning.
What's the difference between deep cleaning and regular cleaning?+
A regular clean maintains every accessible surface on a standard schedule. A deep clean adds: inside all kitchen cabinets and appliances, behind furniture and appliances, baseboards throughout, window tracks, ceiling fans, grout scrubbing, and wall spot-cleaning. Deep cleans take 2–3× longer and cost proportionally more — because 2–3× more area is actually being cleaned.
