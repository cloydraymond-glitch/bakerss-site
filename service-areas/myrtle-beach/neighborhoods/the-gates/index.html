Everyday curb appeal

A Residential Plan Built Around Consistency

In an established neighborhood, curb appeal is shaped by regular details: clean edges, controlled weeds, trimmed shrubs, clear walks and prompt attention to loose gates, damaged fence sections or irrigation problems. The goal is not an oversized estate program; it is dependable maintenance that keeps the property from slipping between visits.

Local questions

The Gates Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss offer recurring lawn care in The Gates?

Yes. Recurring service may include mowing, edging, trimming and blowing based on lawn size, growth, access and the approved schedule.

Can you address wet side yards or drainage concerns?

Bakerss can document visible drainage symptoms and quote approved cleanup, grading-support or irrigation-related work. Complex drainage design may require a specialized contractor.

Do you repair fences and gates?

Yes. Bakerss can quote minor fence, gate, post and panel repairs after reviewing photos or the property.

Can pressure washing be scheduled with lawn service?

Yes. Driveways, walkways, patios, entries and other approved surfaces can be coordinated with recurring exterior maintenance.

Will you hand-pull weeds from beds?

Hand-pulling can be added when specifically included in the approved scope. It is different from routine string trimming or mowing.

What do you need to quote a home in The Gates?

Send the property address, lawn photos, approximate service frequency, bed or shrub needs, repair concerns and any access instructions.
