Two premium Carolina Forest communities

Maintenance for Gated Custom Homes Near the Waterway

Waterway Palms centers on a major clubhouse and amenity complex with a pool, tennis courts, social spaces, boat storage and direct Intracoastal Waterway launch access. The Bluffs is another established gated waterway community with large homes and boating-oriented amenities.

Waterway exposure increases humidity, wind and storm debris. Shaded pavers, pool decks, fences and siding may develop organic buildup faster than protected inland surfaces. Custom homes often include multiple irrigation zones, palms, ornamental beds, broad driveways and outdoor entertainment areas.

Bakerss scopes each property around lot size, landscape design, turf type, irrigation, drainage, water exposure, exterior materials, access rules and private versus association-controlled areas.

Frequently asked questions

Waterway Palms Plantation & The Bluffs Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss provide property maintenance in Waterway Palms and The Bluffs?

Yes. Bakerss Property Services LLC provides approved lawn, landscape, irrigation, washing, pool, home-watch and handyman services in both gated communities.

What makes these communities different?

Both communities combine gated access, custom homes, premium landscaping and Intracoastal Waterway amenities. Moisture, storm exposure, irrigation and high presentation standards require coordinated service.

Can Bakerss maintain waterfront or waterway-facing homes?

Yes. Bakerss can maintain owner-controlled lawns, landscapes, hardscapes and outdoor living areas. Structural dock, seawall or bulkhead work may require specialized contractors.

What amenities are associated with Waterway Palms?

Public community information describes a large clubhouse, swimming pool, tennis courts, playground, social areas, boat storage and a private boat launch to the Intracoastal Waterway.

Can Bakerss coordinate gated access?

Yes. Owners or managers should provide current vendor-entry, parking, service-hour and insurance instructions.

Does Bakerss provide second-home watch?

Yes. Scheduled observations, storm checks, photo reporting and authorized maintenance coordination are available.

Can lawn, pool and handyman services be combined?

Yes. Approved services can be combined into one recurring property-maintenance plan.

How do I receive an accurate quote?

Provide the address, lot size, photos, requested services, access information and any association requirements.
