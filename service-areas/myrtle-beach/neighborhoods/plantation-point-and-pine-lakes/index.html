Maintenance for Mature Golf-Community Properties

Pine Lakes Country Club opened in 1927 as Myrtle Beach’s first golf course. Plantation Point and Pine Lakes developed around an established central Myrtle Beach corridor where custom homes, mature trees and highly visible landscapes require more than basic mowing.

Plantation Point properties often include larger lawns, broad driveways, pools, ornamental beds and executive-home landscaping. Pine Lakes includes a wider mix of home ages and landscape conditions, with mature tree canopy, established shrubs and irrigation systems modified over time.

Bakerss scopes each property around lot size, turf type, tree canopy, irrigation, drainage, exterior materials, outdoor living areas, ownership schedule and current community requirements.

Does Bakerss provide property maintenance in Plantation Point and Pine Lakes?

Yes. Bakerss Property Services LLC provides approved lawn care, landscaping, irrigation repair, pressure washing, pool support, home watch and handyman services in both communities.

What makes these neighborhoods different from newer developments?

Plantation Point and Pine Lakes include established homes, mature trees, larger landscapes, older irrigation components and high golf-community visibility.

Can Bakerss maintain estate-sized lawns and landscapes?

Yes. Mowing, edging, shrub care, bed maintenance, palm care and debris removal can be scoped according to the actual lot size and landscape complexity.

Why is Pine Lakes significant to Myrtle Beach?

Pine Lakes Country Club opened in 1927 as Myrtle Beach's first golf course, giving the surrounding area a long-established golf and residential identity.

Can Bakerss help with older irrigation systems?

Yes. Approved work may include heads, leaks, timers, valves, coverage problems and overspray.

Does Bakerss provide home-watch service?

Can lawn, pool and handyman services be combined?

Yes. Approved services can be combined into one recurring property-maintenance plan.
