Golf and coastal presentation

Maintenance Where Every Edge Is Visible

Properties near fairways, broad streets and coastal approaches often have open sight lines, formal beds and mature plantings. Small issues—uneven edging, irrigation overspray, driveway staining or wind-blown debris—stand out quickly, so the maintenance plan must emphasize detail, timing and communication.

Property-specific planning

What drives maintenance in The Dunes area

The service plan should protect presentation while accounting for sun exposure, irrigation and coastal weather.

1

Golf-front visibility

Lawns, beds and borders may be visible from streets, neighboring homes and course-facing elevations.

2

Irrigation precision

3

Coastal residue

4

Estate coordination

Turf, irrigation and exterior conditions

Match turf care to exposure and irrigation

Open Bermuda or Zoysia areas may respond differently from shaded side yards or ornamental zones. Bakerss evaluates visible growth, mowing height, sprinkler coverage and drainage before recommending corrective work.

Access and community coordination

Respect community and owner instructions

Service is coordinated around current owner, manager or association instructions for entry, parking, work hours and landscape approvals. Bakerss does not publish gate information or assume blanket authorization.

Local questions

The Dunes Club & Dunes Cove Property Maintenance FAQ

Can Bakerss maintain golf-front or highly visible lawns?

Yes. The scope can emphasize edge definition, mowing consistency, bed presentation, debris removal and irrigation observations appropriate to the property.

Do you repair irrigation dry zones and overspray?

Bakerss can inspect visible symptoms and repair approved sprinkler heads, zones, leaks, timers and coverage issues within the agreed scope.

Can you coordinate lawn, pool and home watch services?

Yes. Multiple approved services can be scheduled through one Bakerss relationship to reduce vendor coordination for the owner or manager.

How do you handle salt and pollen buildup?

Periodic pressure washing and window cleaning can address visible buildup on driveways, entries, patios, siding and glass, with methods selected for the surface.

Will you follow association landscape requirements?

Bakerss follows the current instructions supplied by the owner, manager or association. Required approvals remain the property owner’s responsibility unless otherwise agreed.

What information produces the best quote?

Provide the address, photos, property use, lawn and landscape priorities, irrigation concerns, desired frequency and any access or service-window requirements.
