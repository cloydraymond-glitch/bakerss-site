Mixed-use maintenance

Different Properties, Different Operating Pressures

Market Common combines residences, rentals, offices, retail, restaurants and heavily used public-facing spaces. A residential lawn schedule cannot simply be copied onto a storefront or guest turnover. Service must reflect pedestrian traffic, parking access, business hours, HOA instructions and the visibility of every entry point.

Property-specific planning

Market Common maintenance pressure points

The property type—not just the address—determines the best scope and service window.

1

Customer-facing appearance

Entries, walks, glass, landscaping and litter affect the first impression of a business.

2

Tighter access

Townhomes and commercial spaces may have restricted parking, loading or service windows.

3

Guest and tenant turnover

Rentals and managed units need cleaning, repair notes and rapid follow-up between occupants.

4

High-use hardscape

Sidewalks, patios, curbs and entries accumulate wear and organic staining faster than low-traffic areas.

Turf, irrigation and exterior conditions

Landscape detail matters even where turf is limited

Compact lawns, tree wells, courtyards and ornamental beds are highly visible. Precise edging, litter control, irrigation checks and clean transitions between landscape and hardscape can matter more than raw mowing acreage.

Access and community coordination

Schedule around residents, guests and businesses

Bakerss uses owner- or manager-provided instructions for parking, loading, keys, service windows and association requirements. Commercial work can be planned to reduce disruption when the approved scope allows.

Local questions

Market Common Property Maintenance FAQ

Can Bakerss work around Market Common business hours?

Yes, when scheduling and access allow. Provide operating hours, parking or loading restrictions and any preferred service window with the quote request.

Do you maintain both homes and commercial properties?

Yes. Bakerss serves residential, rental, office, retail and managed properties, with a scope designed for the property’s actual use.

Can you clean storefront approaches and sidewalks?

Yes. Pressure washing and exterior cleaning can be quoted for approved entries, walks, patios, curbs and other suitable surfaces.

Do you support short-term rentals in Market Common?

Yes. Services may include turnover cleaning, deep cleaning, repair notes, handyman support and recurring exterior maintenance.

Can several services appear on one maintenance plan?

Yes. Approved cleaning, landscape, pressure washing, lawn and handyman needs can be coordinated through one recurring relationship.

What information should a business or manager provide?

Send the address, property type, operating hours, access restrictions, photos, desired frequency and the exact surfaces or maintenance categories to be quoted.
