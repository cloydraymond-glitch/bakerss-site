Grande Dunes estate strategy

Maintenance for Luxury Homes, Golf Properties and Waterway Estates

Grande Dunes is not one uniform neighborhood. It includes multiple residential districts, golf-course settings, Intracoastal Waterway and marina properties, custom homes, townhomes and second residences. The correct maintenance plan must be tailored to the individual property and its association requirements.

Luxury properties often contain multiple irrigation zones, specimen palms, ornamental beds, paver driveways, pools, spas, outdoor kitchens, decorative lighting and large glass areas. Every component contributes to the arrival experience and must remain coordinated rather than serviced by disconnected vendors.

Golf-facing lots require precise turf edges and controlled beds. Waterway and marina-area properties may experience greater humidity, wind and organic buildup. Seasonal owners need dependable inspections, storm checks, photos and authorized repair coordination. Bakerss scopes each property around its actual architecture, landscape, exterior materials, access and ownership pattern.

Frequently asked questions

Grande Dunes Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss provide property maintenance in Grande Dunes?

Yes. Bakerss Property Services LLC provides approved one-time and recurring estate lawn care, landscaping, irrigation repair, pressure washing, pool support, home watch and handyman services for Grande Dunes properties.

What makes Grande Dunes property maintenance different?

Grande Dunes includes luxury custom homes, golf-course properties, waterway and marina settings, premium outdoor living spaces and high community presentation standards. These properties benefit from coordinated estate-level maintenance rather than basic mow-and-blow service.

Can Bakerss maintain second homes and seasonal residences?

Yes. An approved executive home-watch plan may include scheduled observations, storm checks, photo documentation and coordination of authorized maintenance while the owner is away.

Can lawn, pool, irrigation and handyman services be combined?

Yes. Approved lawn, landscape, irrigation, exterior, pool, home-watch and minor repair services can be combined into one documented property-maintenance plan.

Does Bakerss work around gated-community and association requirements?

Yes. Owners or managers should provide current access, parking, service-hour, insurance and landscape requirements. Bakerss follows property-specific instructions and does not claim blanket association authorization.

Can Bakerss maintain outdoor kitchens, patios and pool decks?

Yes. Authorized services may include pressure or soft washing, condition reporting, fixture observations and coordination of minor repairs for owner-controlled outdoor living areas.

How often should Grande Dunes exterior surfaces be cleaned?

Frequency depends on shade, irrigation overspray, humidity, surface material, proximity to water and the owner's presentation standard. Some pavers, patios, pool decks and shaded elevations require more frequent service.

How do I receive an accurate Grande Dunes quote?

Provide the property address, photos, lot size, requested services, access information, desired frequency and any association requirements. An onsite assessment may be required before final pricing.
