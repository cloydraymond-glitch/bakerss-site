Salt Air Repair Reality

What Myrtle Beach's Environment Does to Your Property Hardware

Properties in Myrtle Beach experience exterior and near-exterior hardware degradation on timelines that inland homeowners never encounter. Salt particles deposit continuously on every surface within miles of the Atlantic. Combined with the high humidity that keeps moisture against metal surfaces, this creates corrosion at 3–5 times the rate of comparable inland properties. The practical result is a specific set of repairs that repeat on predictable cycles in Myrtle Beach:

  • Exterior door lock replacement every 3–5 years as salt infiltrates mechanical components
  • Window hardware inspection and replacement every 5–7 years as cranks and latches corrode
  • Sliding door track cleaning and roller replacement every 3–4 years
  • Exterior light fixture replacement every 5–8 years as housings corrode
  • Screen door hardware replacement every 3–5 years
  • Exterior faucet and hose bib replacement every 7–10 years
The proactive approach: Identifying and replacing these components on schedule — before they fail — costs significantly less than emergency replacement when failure occurs at an inconvenient moment. A $125 door lock replacement scheduled proactively costs $125. An emergency lock replacement at 6 PM when a guest is locked out costs $125 plus the 4-star review from a guest who waited 90 minutes.
