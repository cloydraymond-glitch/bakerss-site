Myrtle Beach Concrete Coatings

Epoxy Flooring Built for Coastal Garage Conditions

Myrtle Beach garages deal with humidity, moisture vapor, sand, salt residue, hot tires, storm-season dampness, lawn equipment, beach gear, golf carts, bikes, tools and daily vehicle traffic. A garage floor coating has to be selected and installed around the actual slab condition, not just the color the owner wants.

Professional epoxy flooring starts with prep. Concrete must be cleaned, profiled and assessed before coating. Existing coatings, weak surface material, oil contamination, cracks, moisture concerns and slab defects can all affect adhesion. A coating that is installed over poorly prepared concrete may look good at first and fail later.

Bakerss installs epoxy flooring as part of a complete property improvement system. That means we can also help with garage painting, pressure washing, handyman repairs, exterior improvements, rental property maintenance and commercial maintenance.

Why Prep Matters

Epoxy Failure Usually Starts Before the Coating Goes Down

The coating system is only as good as the slab preparation underneath it. Myrtle Beach humidity, slab moisture and garage contamination make prep especially important.

Bonding

Concrete Profile

Epoxy needs a properly profiled concrete surface. Smooth or sealed concrete may not provide enough bite for a long-term bond.

Moisture

Moisture Vapor Awareness

Coastal humidity and slab moisture can create coating problems when moisture is ignored during planning.

Contamination

Oil, Salt & Tire Residue

Garages often carry vehicle residue, salt, sand, cleaners and old coatings that must be addressed before installation.

Myrtle Beach Epoxy Flooring Questions

How much does epoxy flooring cost in Myrtle Beach?

Epoxy garage flooring in Myrtle Beach starts at $1,800 for a 1-car garage, $3,000 for many 2-car garage projects and $4,500 for larger 3-car garages. Final pricing depends on measured square footage, concrete condition, crack repair, moisture concerns, coating system, flake coverage and topcoat selection.

Why does concrete preparation matter for epoxy floors in Myrtle Beach?

Concrete preparation is critical because Myrtle Beach garages face humidity, moisture vapor, sand, salts, tire contamination and surface wear. Diamond grinding creates a mechanical profile that helps the coating bond properly. Poor prep is one of the main reasons cheaper coatings peel or fail.

Do you use big-box epoxy kits?

No. Bakerss does not position big-box paint kits as professional epoxy flooring. Professional epoxy flooring requires proper concrete prep, compatible coating systems, correct mixing, application timing and a durable topcoat.

Can you add a non-slip additive?

Yes. Non-slip additive can be included for garages, pool-adjacent areas, commercial floors and high-traffic spaces where traction is important.

How long does epoxy flooring take to install?

Many garage epoxy projects can be completed within a short project window, but timing depends on prep needs, moisture conditions, coating system, cure time, temperature and humidity. Bakerss provides a project schedule during the estimate process.

Can epoxy flooring be bundled with other property improvements?

Yes. Epoxy flooring can be bundled with painting, pressure washing, handyman repairs, garage refresh work and full property maintenance plans for Myrtle Beach homes, rentals and commercial properties.
