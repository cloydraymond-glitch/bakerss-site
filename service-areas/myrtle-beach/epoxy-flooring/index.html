Why Myrtle Beach Is Different

What Coastal Humidity Does to a Bad Garage Floor

Myrtle Beach records an average annual relative humidity of 85% at 4am — one of the highest overnight humidity levels on the East Coast. Concrete is porous. Moisture vapor transmits through it continuously. Water-based epoxy products — the type sold at every hardware store on US-17 — cannot withstand sustained moisture vapor transmission. They lift, bubble, and peel within 12–24 months. 100% solids commercial epoxy applied over a diamond-ground surface with a moisture barrier primer is the only system that holds in this climate.

What We Install

The Only Correct Spec for Coastal SC

🏆

100% Solids Commercial Epoxy

Myrtle Beach's subtropical humidity is the reason 100% solids is non-negotiable. Water-based products (30–50% solids) leave gaps that moisture vapor exploits. 100% solids fills those gaps — the same spec used in NASCAR facilities and aircraft maintenance hangars.

💧

Moisture Testing — Required Here

We test every slab before we coat. Myrtle Beach's proximity to the water table makes moisture vapor transmission unpredictable. Slabs from the 1990s–2000s are particularly susceptible — the era of most Grand Strand residential construction.

🔬

Diamond Grinding — Non-Negotiable

We grind every slab with industrial diamond tooling. Acid etching doesn't create adequate mechanical profile on Myrtle Beach concrete. Salt air contributes to surface mineral deposits that block chemical bonding. Grinding removes all of it.

🚀

NASA Used This Chemistry

The same epoxy chemistry that protects astronaut capsule floors was selected for non-slip performance, chemical resistance, and extreme durability — the exact properties that make it right for your Myrtle Beach garage.
