How We're Different

Donation Sorting on Every Myrtle Beach Job

Before anything leaves a Myrtle Beach property, we sort. Furniture in good condition, clothing, housewares, and other usable items are separated from disposal items. We work with Myrtle Beach area charitable organizations to donate usable goods — keeping quality items out of the waste stream and providing value to the community simultaneously. If you want specific items designated for donation, we document them and provide drop-off receipts where available for tax documentation purposes.

This sorting process also benefits you directly: many items in a cleanout have resale value that a simple haul-away approach destroys. Antique furniture, collectibles, working electronics, and quality housewares can be worth more than the cost of the removal job when sold through the appropriate channel. We will tell you honestly when we see items that should be considered for sale rather than disposal.
