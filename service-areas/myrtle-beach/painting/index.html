Coastal Preparation

Why Myrtle Beach Painting Requires Proper Prep

Many paint failures begin before the first finish coat is applied. Prep work is what separates a quick paint job from a lasting property improvement.

Preparation

Our Prep Process May Include

  • Pressure washing or soft washing
  • Surface cleaning and mildew removal
  • Scraping loose paint
  • Sanding rough surfaces
  • Caulking failed joints and gaps
  • Spot priming bare or repaired areas
  • Minor drywall or surface repairs
Protection

Skipping Prep Can Lead To

  • Peeling paint
  • Blistering
  • Premature fading
  • Moisture intrusion
  • Mildew staining
  • Poor adhesion
  • Shortened paint lifespan
