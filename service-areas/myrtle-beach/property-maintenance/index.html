Choose the right scope

What can a recurring maintenance plan cover?

Different buildings need different service schedules. We start with the property's condition, access instructions, owner priorities and recurring tasks—not a one-size-fits-all monthly package.

Homeowners and second homes

Scheduled exterior observations, gutters, lawn and landscape care, seasonal preparations, minor approved repairs and coordination when the owner is away.

Vacation rentals and Airbnb properties

Turnover-related maintenance, visible issue reports, cleaning coordination, exterior upkeep and planned repairs between guest stays. Cleaning and linen services require an explicit scope.

HOAs and apartment communities

Common-area walk-throughs, work-order support, grounds, trash and debris tasks, pool-area observations and reporting tailored to manager approval limits.

Commercial buildings

Exterior presentation, planned inspections, vendor coordination and approved corrective maintenance for offices, storefronts and multi-tenant properties.

Transparent responsibilities

What's included—and what requires separate authorization?

Scheduled recurring work

Your agreement specifies the property areas, tasks, visit frequency, reporting format and service contact. Only listed tasks are included in the recurring price.

Extra work orders

Parts, unexpected repairs, extensive cleaning, storm cleanup and larger improvement projects are separately approved unless expressly included in the contract.

Qualified trade services

HVAC, plumbing, electrical or other regulated work is assigned to appropriately credentialed personnel under applicable requirements; eligibility and pricing depend on the requested scope.

Urgent issues

Identify an escalation contact, response expectations and any emergency fees in the signed agreement. Availability and response times are confirmed for each client rather than assumed to be continuous.

Grand Strand property planning

A seasonal maintenance calendar for Myrtle Beach

Coastal weather, seasonal rentals and heavy property use can influence when preventive work is most useful. Scheduling should follow your building's actual needs and booked dates—not an assumed universal booking season.

Before peak occupancy

Review visible leaks, HVAC concerns, paint touch-ups, door hardware, exterior cleanliness, lawn condition and guest-facing repairs.

During busy periods

Use a defined process for documented issues and approvals between cleanings, guest stays or tenant work orders.

After storms

Check accessible areas when conditions are safe; document visible impacts and quote any necessary mitigation or repairs separately.

Off-season planning

Schedule projects needing more access time, such as painting, pressure washing, landscaping refreshes and larger repairs.

How pricing works

Request a property-specific maintenance proposal

We base pricing on property size, work frequency, required labor, travel and access, materials, reporting and the number of services combined. A flat monthly amount without a detailed scope can create confusion about what's included.

For a useful quote, send your address, building type, recurring needs, estimated monthly work orders, requested visit schedule, photos and the name of the person authorized to approve changes.

Common questions

Myrtle Beach property maintenance FAQs

Can I combine lawn care, cleaning and repairs?

Yes. A proposal can combine approved recurring services. Each property's schedule, service limits and charges are documented before work begins.

Does a monthly plan include unlimited handyman labor?

No. Included tasks, work-order allowances and any extra labor charges depend on the written agreement. Larger repairs are quoted or authorized separately.

Can Bakerss support short-term rental properties?

Yes. Plans can account for scheduled cleaning coordination, guest-facing repairs, exterior upkeep and owner-approved work between bookings.

Do you work with HOAs and apartment property managers?

Yes. We can develop proposals for recurring common-area tasks, approved maintenance work orders and reporting requirements.

Are emergency services available around the clock?

Availability and response commitments depend on the signed agreement, staff availability, access and the nature of the request. We do not promise universal 24/7 service for every plan.

How are materials and unexpected repairs billed?

The proposal states the labor, material markup or handling terms, spending authorization thresholds and invoicing rules. Work outside the agreed scope requires approval.

Can you inspect a property after a tropical storm?

Visual follow-up can be scoped after conditions and access are safe. It is not a guarantee of storm response, a formal inspection or a substitute for insurance-related assessments.

How do I get started?
