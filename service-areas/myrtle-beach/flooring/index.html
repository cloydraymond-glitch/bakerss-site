Why Flooring Matters in Myrtle Beach

How Flooring Improves Your Property — and Your Life in Myrtle Beach

Carpet in an active Myrtle Beach vacation rental is a declining asset. Beach sand acts as abrasive grit, accelerating fiber degradation every time guests walk across it. The coastal humidity creates the persistent moisture conditions that generate odors in carpet that professional cleaning cannot fully reverse after years of use. Carpet that looked acceptable when installed looks worn and dingy within four to five years of active STR use — and guests notice. Carpet generates the cleanliness concerns in reviews that are difficult to prevent because carpet's appearance degrades continuously regardless of cleaning frequency.

LVP — Luxury Vinyl Plank with a waterproof SPC core and 12–20 mil wear layer — eliminates all of these problems simultaneously. It is 100% waterproof. Beach sand cannot abrade its wear layer noticeably within 15–20 years. It photographs as beautiful as real hardwood in listing images. It cleans completely with a damp mop. And it eliminates the replacement cycles that carpet demands in active rental properties. For primary residence owners, LVP delivers the same benefits: beautiful hardwood appearance without the hardwood's vulnerability to Myrtle Beach's coastal humidity.

Carpet

Bedrooms Only — With Realistic Expectations

If carpet is specified anywhere in a Myrtle Beach property, bedrooms only — and with the understanding that active STR use requires 3–5 year replacement cycles. We install carpet and are honest about where it makes sense. Main living areas and entries should always be hard surface in Myrtle Beach.

What NOT to Use

Laminate — Never in Myrtle Beach

Laminate looks like LVP but is NOT waterproof. It swells and delaminates at seams from STR moisture exposure. We see laminate failures in Myrtle Beach rentals 2–4 years after installation. We will not install laminate in a Myrtle Beach property and will tell you exactly why when asked.

FAQ

Flooring FAQ — Myrtle Beach

What flooring is best for a Myrtle Beach vacation rental?+
Waterproof LVP with a 12–20 mil wear layer and SPC (stone plastic composite) core for main living areas and bedrooms. Porcelain tile for all bathrooms and kitchens — always. Never laminate (not waterproof). Carpet only in bedrooms, if at all, with 3–5 year replacement expectation in active STRs.
How much does LVP flooring cost in Myrtle Beach?+
$3.50–$7.50 per square foot installed, depending on product tier. A standard 3BR Myrtle Beach home (approx 1,200 sq ft of flooring) runs $4,200–$9,000. Free on-site estimates with material samples shown.
Why can't I use laminate in my Myrtle Beach rental?+
Laminate is not waterproof. In Myrtle Beach's active rental environment — guests arriving with wet beach feet, drinks spilled, bathroom moisture — laminate swells and delaminates at seams within 2–4 years. It looks like LVP but fails under the conditions that LVP handles without damage. We do not install laminate in Myrtle Beach properties.
How long does a flooring project take in Myrtle Beach?+
A typical 3BR Myrtle Beach home takes 3–5 days — 1 day for removal, 1 day for subfloor preparation, 2–3 days for installation. Larger homes run 5–7 days. STR owners should schedule in the off-season (November–February) to minimize revenue impact.
Do you install epoxy garage flooring in Myrtle Beach?+
Yes — polyaspartic epoxy flooring for garages, utility rooms, and laundry rooms. UV-stable (important for Myrtle Beach's year-round sun), moisture-resistant, available in solid colors, chip patterns, and metallic finishes. $3–$7/sq ft installed. Free estimates.
