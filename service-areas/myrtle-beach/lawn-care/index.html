Turf and site conditions

Grass Types and Lawn Issues We Commonly See in Myrtle Beach

Each lawn type has different maintenance needs. Bakerss watches for visible turf stress, low spots, irrigation symptoms, thin areas and damage that may require landscaping or sod repair.

Centipede grassCommon on residential lawns; prefers lower maintenance but can thin when stressed by poor soil, shade or improper mowing.
Bermuda grassHandles heat and traffic well, but needs consistent cutting and can look rough when edges and runners are not controlled.
Zoysia grassDense, premium appearance when maintained properly; can scalp or brown if cut incorrectly during stress periods.
St. Augustine grassCoarse-bladed and shade tolerant in the right conditions; benefits from careful mowing and moisture management.
Sandy soilMany Myrtle Beach lawns drain fast and show dry spots quickly when irrigation coverage is uneven.
Storm and salt exposureCoastal weather leaves debris and can stress turf around ocean-facing, low-lying and high-wind properties.
