Myrtle Beach Lawn Service

Reliable Lawn Care for Myrtle Beach Properties

Myrtle Beach lawns grow hard during warm, humid weather. Bermuda, Zoysia, Centipede and St. Augustine all respond differently to heat, rain, shade, salt exposure, sandy soil and irrigation. A lawn near Ocean Boulevard does not behave exactly like a lawn in Carolina Forest, Forestbrook or Market Common.

Bakerss focuses on consistent presentation: proper mowing height, clean edging, string trimming around obstacles, and blowing clippings off driveways, sidewalks, patios and walkways. For homeowners, that means better curb appeal. For vacation rental owners, that means a cleaner arrival experience. For HOAs and managed properties, it means fewer appearance complaints and a more dependable service schedule.

When lawn care needs more than mowing, Bakerss can also handle pressure washing, gutter cleaning, sod installation, mulch, shrub trimming, landscaping, handyman repairs, painting and full property maintenance support.

Grass Types

Myrtle Beach Grass Types Need Different Lawn Care

Full Sun

Bermuda

Common in sunny Myrtle Beach lawns. It usually wants consistent mowing during peak growth and clean edging to stay sharp.

Premium Look

Zoysia

Dense, attractive turf that benefits from correct mowing height and routine maintenance to avoid scalp marks and stress.

Low Maintenance

Centipede

Often used in sandy acidic soil. It should not be overcut or pushed like Bermuda.

Shade Tolerance

St. Augustine

Useful in some shade-heavy areas, but it needs appropriate mowing height and water management.
