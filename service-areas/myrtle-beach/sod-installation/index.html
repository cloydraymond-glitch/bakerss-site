Myrtle Beach Lawn Replacement

Sod Installation Built for Coastal South Carolina Lawns

Myrtle Beach lawns fight heat, humidity, sandy soil, salt exposure, irrigation issues, shade pockets, foot traffic, pet damage, rental use and HOA appearance standards. When a lawn is thin, dead, patchy or full of weeds, installing sod can be the fastest path to a clean, finished yard.

A successful sod installation is more than laying rolls of grass. The old lawn may need to be removed. Soil may need grading. Low spots may need correction. The right sod has to match sun exposure, irrigation, maintenance expectations and neighborhood requirements. A shaded Forestbrook yard does not need the same sod decision as a sunny Carolina Forest front lawn or a rental property near Ocean Boulevard.

Bakerss installs sod as part of a full property maintenance system, which means we can also handle lawn care, landscaping, mulch, rock beds, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, pool maintenance and ongoing property care after the sod is installed.

Grass Types

Best Sod Types for Myrtle Beach Properties

The best sod is not always the most expensive sod. It is the grass type that fits sun, shade, irrigation, soil, traffic and maintenance expectations.

Common Choice

Centipede Sod

Popular in Myrtle Beach because it can work well in sandy, acidic soil and is often lower maintenance when conditions are right.

Full Sun

Bermuda Sod

Strong full-sun turf with good traffic tolerance. It needs routine mowing and performs best with good sunlight and care.

Premium Look

Zoysia Sod

Dense and attractive with a premium appearance. It requires proper mowing, sunlight and establishment care.

Shade Tolerance

St. Augustine Sod

Can be useful in some shade-tolerant applications, but it needs correct watering, mowing height and site conditions.
