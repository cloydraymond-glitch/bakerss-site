Why Property Maintenance Plans Exist

The Problem a Maintenance Plan Solves for Myrtle Beach Property Owners

Managing a Myrtle Beach property without a maintenance plan means managing the coordination overhead of multiple contractor relationships. Finding lawn care. Scheduling gutter cleaning. Booking cleaning before each rental. Calling someone for handyman work when something breaks. Tracking which service was done when. For a property owner who lives in Myrtle Beach and can personally manage this coordination, the overhead is manageable if inconvenient. For the out-of-state property owner — the investor in Ohio whose Myrtle Beach condo generates $45,000 annually, or the family with a second home in Carolina Forest who visits four times a year — individual contractor management from 800 miles away is genuinely difficult and creates the deferred maintenance that accelerates property condition decline.

A Bakerss property maintenance plan eliminates this problem. One contact — Ray — manages the entire service calendar for your Myrtle Beach property. Lawn care happens on schedule. Gutters are cleaned before they overflow. Exterior washing is scheduled at the right time. Cleaning happens before each guest arrival. When something breaks, the call goes to one number and it gets resolved. Post-visit reports document every service performed. You know your Myrtle Beach property is maintained without managing the details yourself.

Property Maintenance Plan FAQ — Myrtle Beach

How much does a property maintenance plan cost in Myrtle Beach?+
Plans are custom-built for each property. STR plans with cleaning included run higher based on occupancy volume. Free consultation to design and price your specific plan.
Can you manage my property if I live out of state?+
Yes — out-of-state Myrtle Beach property owners are our most active maintenance plan clients. We establish access, execute services on schedule, and report after every visit. Your involvement is reviewing reports and approving any decisions that require it. Most out-of-state clients check in monthly for 5 minutes — that is the full management overhead the plan requires from them.
Do you include STR vacation rental cleaning in property maintenance plans?+
Yes — vacation rental cleaning can be included in property maintenance plans with calendar sync for automatic dispatch on checkout day. Plans for STR properties typically include turnover cleaning, seasonal deep cleans, lawn care, gutter cleaning, and handyman hours.
Is there a contract for property maintenance plans?+
We work month-to-month — no long-term contract required. The best maintenance relationship is one that earns continued trust through consistent results. If the plan is not delivering what it should, you should be free to adjust or cancel. That said, most of our maintenance plan clients have been with us for multiple years because the relationship works.
What happens if something needs emergency repair outside of scheduled services?+
Emergency repairs are covered on the plan through your included handyman hours for minor items, or quoted separately for larger scopes. Emergency response is 24/7 regardless of time or day. For STR properties, guest-facing emergencies are the highest priority — resolved the same day the call comes in.
