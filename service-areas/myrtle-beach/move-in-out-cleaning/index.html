The Security Deposit Equation

How Move-Out Cleaning Protects Your Security Deposit

The financial math of professional move-out cleaning is simple and consistently favorable. A Myrtle Beach landlord's cleaning deduction from a security deposit typically runs $200–$600 for anything beyond a spotless property. Professional move-out cleaning for a standard 3BR Myrtle Beach home costs $275–$450. In most cases, professional cleaning costs less than the landlord's cleaning deduction — and in virtually all cases, it costs significantly less than a dispute over the full deposit that ultimately requires documentation, small claims court, and the landlord's discretionary judgment about what "clean enough" means.

For landlords and property managers on the other side of the transaction: professional move-out cleaning before a property is returned to market reduces the time between tenants, ensures the property photographs cleanly for new listing images, and produces a documented cleaned condition at move-in that protects your ability to make cleaning deductions at the end of the next tenancy. The $350 move-out cleaning investment consistently produces better financial outcomes than the alternative.
