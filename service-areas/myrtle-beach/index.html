Why Myrtle Beach Properties Need More

Coastal property maintenance is a system—not a single service call.

The strongest maintenance plan addresses the conditions that repeatedly create work orders, property deterioration and presentation issues.

Heat & Humidity

HVAC and moisture demand

Long cooling seasons and coastal humidity make air-conditioning performance, filters, drainage, ventilation and interior-condition monitoring especially important.

Storm Exposure

Wind, rain and exterior wear

Routine exterior checks, debris cleanup, drainage attention and post-storm property reviews help identify issues before they become larger repairs.

Landscape Growth

Fast recurring exterior maintenance

Warm-season growth means lawns, shrubs, irrigation and exterior presentation can change quickly without consistent service.

Vacation Traffic

Turnovers and guest-related wear

Short-term rentals need cleaning, resets, repairs and fast response when guest issues appear between stays.

Pools

High-use seasonal systems

Residential and managed pools require consistent chemistry, cleanliness, equipment attention and documented service.

Multiple Vendors

Coordination becomes the hidden cost

Bakerss is structured to reduce vendor fragmentation by coordinating multiple property-service categories through one relationship.
