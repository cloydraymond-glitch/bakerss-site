Property Maintenance in
Myrtle Beach, SC

Myrtle Beach is the heart of the Grand Strand — a 60-square-mile coastal city incorporated in 1938 that welcomes 15 million annual visitors, hosts roughly 30,000 active vacation rental listings, and is home to approximately 35,000 permanent residents. Named for the native wax myrtle shrub that grows abundantly along South Carolina's coast, Myrtle Beach developed from a small beach retreat into the 13th most-visited tourist destination in the United States. The city spans from the Atlantic Ocean westward along Highway 501 and is bordered by North Myrtle Beach to the north and Surfside Beach to the south.

City Profile

Myrtle Beach at a Glance

Population: ~35,000 permanent residents; city population swells to 450,000+ at peak summer season with visitors and seasonal residents combined. The visitor-to-resident ratio of more than 12-to-1 makes Myrtle Beach unlike any similarly-sized American city in terms of infrastructure and service demand.

Schools: Myrtle Beach is served by Horry County Schools, South Carolina's fourth-largest school district. Local schools include Myrtle Beach High School — home of the Sea Hawks — along with Myrtle Beach Middle School, Myrtle Beach Elementary, Ocean Bay Middle School, and Seaside Elementary. Coastal Carolina University is located in nearby Conway (14 miles).

Notable facts: Myrtle Beach hosts more than 100 golf courses within a 30-mile radius — the highest concentration in the world — earning the area its "Golf Capital of the World" designation. Broadway at the Beach draws over 10 million visitors annually. The Myrtle Beach International Airport serves over 3.7 million passengers per year. The city was the site of Myrtle Beach Air Force Base from 1940 until the base's closure in 1993, with the former base redeveloped into the Market Common district.
