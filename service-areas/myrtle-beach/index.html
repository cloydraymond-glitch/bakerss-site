About the Market

Property Maintenance in Myrtle Beach

The Grand Strand's subtropical coastal climate creates specific property maintenance demands that set Myrtle Beach apart from any inland market.

Short-Term Rentals

One of the largest STR markets in the eastern US. Constant demand for professional turnover cleaning, between-stay repairs, epoxy floor upgrades, and ongoing property maintenance from owners who often don't live nearby.

Salt Air & Humidity

Direct coastal environment means accelerated exterior degradation — paint, metal, concrete, and exterior surfaces all need more frequent attention than inland properties. Our concrete moisture prep protocol is critical for any epoxy installation within 10 miles of the ocean.

HOA Communities

Grande Dunes, Carolina Forest, Market Common, and dozens of other HOA communities with active enforcement and specific maintenance standards we understand and consistently satisfy.

Year-Round Season

Warm-season grasses grow actively for 9–10 months. Combined with a 5+ month high-humidity season and annual hurricane prep requirements, professional maintenance here is year-round.
