About the Market

Property Maintenance in Myrtle Beach, SC

The Grand Strand's subtropical coastal climate creates specific property maintenance demands that set Myrtle Beach apart from any inland market. Salt air accelerates paint degradation, metal corrosion, and exterior surface deterioration. High summer humidity creates mildew and algae growth on every exterior surface within weeks. Fifty-plus inches of annual rainfall fill gutters and stress every drainage system on the property.

The city's enormous vacation rental market — roughly 30,000 active short-term rental listings — creates intense demand for professional cleaning, turnover management, and ongoing property maintenance from owners who often don't live nearby. And with Myrtle Beach's long season, warm-season grasses grow actively for 9–10 months of the year, making consistent lawn care essential rather than optional for any property expected to look maintained.

We understand every dimension of this market because we operate in it every day — from Carolina Forest HOA standards to Grande Dunes luxury expectations, Ocean Lakes turnover volume, and the specific demands of coastal concrete prep for epoxy garage floors.

🏖️ STR Capital of the East

🏘️ Active HOA Communities

🌊 Salt Air & Humidity
