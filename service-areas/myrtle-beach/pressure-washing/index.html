Surface Safety

Pressure Washing vs. Soft Washing

The right method depends on the surface. Too much pressure can damage siding, paint, wood, stucco and older materials. Too little cleaning action can leave algae and mildew behind.

High Pressure

Best for Durable Hard Surfaces

Concrete driveways, sidewalks and some masonry surfaces can often handle pressure washing when the surface condition supports it.

Low Pressure

Best for Siding & Delicate Surfaces

Soft washing uses lower pressure and cleaning solution for vinyl siding, painted surfaces, stucco and certain coastal exterior materials.

Assessment

Surface First, Method Second

Bakerss assesses the material, buildup, age, condition and risk level before choosing the cleaning method.

How often should I pressure wash my Myrtle Beach property?

Most Myrtle Beach properties benefit from exterior washing at least once per year. Oceanfront, rental, shaded, tree-covered or high-humidity properties may need service twice per year because algae, mildew, pollen and salt residue build up faster.

What is soft washing?

Soft washing uses lower pressure and cleaning solution to clean more delicate surfaces such as vinyl siding, painted surfaces, stucco and certain exterior materials. High pressure is better suited for harder surfaces such as concrete when appropriate.

Can you remove green mildew from siding?

Yes. Green mildew and algae on Myrtle Beach siding are common because of humidity, shade, rain and coastal conditions. Bakerss uses the correct cleaning approach for the surface so the siding can be cleaned without unnecessary damage.
