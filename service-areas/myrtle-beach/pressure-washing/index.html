Surface-specific cleaning strategy

Pressure Washing vs. Soft Washing in Myrtle Beach Conditions

The right result comes from matching the cleaning method to the material. Too much pressure can scar wood, force water behind siding, disturb paint or damage delicate finishes. Too little cleaning power leaves mildew, algae and black streaking behind.

Coastal exterior cleaning risks

Why Myrtle Beach Properties Need More Frequent Exterior Cleaning

The combination of warm moisture, shaded areas, irrigation overspray, sand, pine pollen, salt exposure and heavy foot traffic creates fast exterior buildup. A property can look clean in March and visibly neglected by midsummer if surfaces are not maintained.

What We Watch Before Cleaning

  • Surface type and risk of damage
  • Oxidized siding or delicate paint
  • Nearby plants, mulch, pine straw and landscape beds
  • Water access, drainage and runoff direction
  • Tenant, guest, resident or customer traffic
  • Rust staining, algae, mildew, grease or gum concerns
