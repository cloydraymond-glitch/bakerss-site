Handyman process

How Bakerss Handles Myrtle Beach Repair Requests

Clear handyman work starts with defining the issue, access, photos, material needs, authorization limits and what should be quoted separately.

1

Request

You send the repair need, location, access notes, photos if available and any timing requirement.

2

Scope Review

We determine whether the item fits handyman scope, needs materials or should be quoted as a larger project.

3

Schedule

We schedule access, coordinate with management or owner contact and confirm service expectations.

4

Repair

The work is completed when practical, with notes if additional parts, materials or trade-specific work is needed.

5

Report

Completed work, photos and next-step recommendations can be provided for property managers and owners.
