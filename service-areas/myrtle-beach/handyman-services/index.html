One Local Team for Myrtle Beach Home Repairs, Exterior Work, Cleaning & Maintenance

Myrtle Beach properties need a broader maintenance plan than a typical inland home. Salt air coats windows, corrodes exterior hardware and accelerates wear. Vacation rental turnover creates high-frequency repairs and cleaning needs. HOAs enforce curb appeal. Hurricane season creates urgent storm-prep and post-storm work. New construction and investor properties need punch lists, final cleans and exterior presentation before closing or listing.

Bakerss is positioned as a full-service property maintenance company instead of a single-trade contractor. That matters because the most profitable and stressful property issues are rarely isolated. A new sod installation often exposes irrigation problems. A move-out clean reveals carpet stains, drywall damage and dryer vent buildup. A pressure washing visit naturally pairs with window cleaning. A property check is the ideal time to swap HVAC filters, inspect fence gates, document storm damage and identify small handyman items before they become guest complaints.
