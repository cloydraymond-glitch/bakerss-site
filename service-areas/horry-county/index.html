Common Questions

Horry County Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss serve communities throughout Horry County?
Yes. Bakerss serves communities throughout Horry County, including Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Conway, Surfside Beach, Murrells Inlet, Garden City, Socastee and Carolina Forest. Call 843-467-7136 to confirm coverage for the specific property.
What services does Bakerss offer across Horry County?
Bakerss offers White Glove estate care, grounds maintenance, interior cleaning, HVAC preventive maintenance, pool service, vacation-home support, HOA maintenance, apartment maintenance, commercial property support and priority work-order coordination.
How does Bakerss document completed service?
The Bakerss Client Portal can provide technician arrival and departure times, field notes, service photographs and work-order updates for documented property visibility.
Do you offer retainer contracts for HOA and apartment communities?
Yes. Bakerss offers monthly retainer contracts for HOA communities and apartment operators across Horry County. Services include grounds maintenance, DHEC-compliant pool care, cleaning, and handyman work orders. Net-30 invoicing available for approved accounts. COI on request.
Can one plan include cleaning, grounds and HVAC maintenance?
Yes. Approved White Glove and customized property-care plans can combine recurring interior cleaning, grounds care, HVAC preventive maintenance, pool service, home watch and priority work-order support.
How do I request a Horry County property assessment?
Submit the property address, property type, service priorities and photos through the online form, or call 843-467-7136 to confirm service availability and next steps.
