Frequently Asked Questions

Horry County Property Maintenance FAQs

Answers for homeowners, community boards, property managers and commercial owners.

How much does property maintenance cost in Horry County?

Pricing depends on the property type, size, frequency, access, required trades and whether work is recurring or project-based. Request a property walkthrough and itemized proposal for the specific scope.

Does Bakerss provide HOA and condo association maintenance in Horry County?

Yes. Bakerss can propose common-area grounds, cleaning, pool and work-order services for HOA and condominium communities, subject to site requirements and an agreed scope.

Can Bakerss handle apartment make-ready and turnover services?

Yes. Apartment and rental-property support can include cleaning, painting, minor repairs, inspection notes and coordinated work orders. Turnaround targets are agreed for each account.

Do you offer commercial property maintenance contracts?

Yes. Commercial owners and property managers can request recurring maintenance proposals covering agreed exterior, cleaning, grounds and repair-coordination needs.

Which Horry County communities do you serve?

Core service-area pages cover Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Garden City Beach, Conway, Carolina Forest, Socastee and the Horry County portion of Murrells Inlet. Contact us to confirm other addresses.

Can one maintenance plan cover HVAC, cleaning, grounds and pool care?

Depending on the property and available qualified personnel, a customized plan may coordinate these services under one service relationship. Regulated work is handled only by appropriately authorized providers.

How do you document work for property managers?

The Bakerss Client Portal can provide technician notes, service photographs, arrival and departure information and work-order updates, depending on the account setup.

What should Horry County property owners do before a hurricane?

Follow official emergency guidance, secure movable outdoor items when safe, document property condition and arrange permitted preparation work in advance. Never enter unsafe or restricted areas.

Do you provide post-hurricane cleanup or flood restoration?

Contact Bakerss for an assessment of accessible, safe cleanup or repairs. Major flooding, contaminated water, structural damage and other hazardous conditions may require specialized licensed contractors.

Do I need a permit for exterior property work in Horry County?

Permit requirements depend on the work and whether the property is within a municipality or unincorporated Horry County. Confirm with the appropriate building department before beginning regulated work.

How often should coastal property maintenance be scheduled?

Frequency depends on occupancy, ocean exposure, landscaping, equipment and HOA requirements. Many properties benefit from routine scheduled checks plus seasonal storm-readiness reviews.

How do I request an HOA vendor bid or property walkthrough?

Use the online quote form or call 843-467-7136 with the property location, type, approximate size, service needs and preferred schedule. Bakerss can discuss the appropriate next steps.
