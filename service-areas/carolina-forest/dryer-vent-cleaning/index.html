Why dryer vent cleaning matters

Dryer Vent Cleaning Is a Preventive Maintenance Task, Not Just a Cleaning Add-On

When lint builds up inside dryer vent lines, the dryer has to work harder to move air. That can mean longer dry times, extra heat, appliance strain and more owner complaints on rental or apartment properties. For Carolina Forest property owners and managers, it is a smart item to schedule before it becomes a tenant, guest or appliance problem.
