Why home watch matters

A Vacant or Seasonal Home Needs Regular Eyes on It

Small problems can sit unnoticed when a homeowner is out of town: storm debris, irrigation problems, fence damage, gutter overflow, exterior staining, pest activity, lawn issues, HVAC concerns, leaks or signs that the property needs service. Home watch is not a substitute for insurance, security or licensed inspections, but it is a practical layer of visibility for owners who cannot be there.
