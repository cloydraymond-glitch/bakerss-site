Questions from property owners

Bellegrove Preserve maintenance FAQ

Final recommendations depend on the property, current conditions, access and approved scope.

Does Bakerss provide recurring service in Bellegrove Preserve?

Yes. Recurring lawn, exterior and maintenance plans are available subject to route capacity, scope and access.

Can you build a seasonal maintenance schedule?

Yes. The scope can separate weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual tasks.

Do you pull weeds by hand?

Hand-pulling can be included as a separately approved labor item when requested.

Can you repair irrigation before installing sod?

Yes. Visible coverage issues should be evaluated before replacing turf so the underlying problem is not repeated.

How do I request a quote?

Provide the property address, photos, desired services, frequency and any current community instructions.
