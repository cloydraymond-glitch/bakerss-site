Questions from property owners

Black Creek Plantation maintenance FAQ

Final recommendations depend on the property, current conditions, access and approved scope.

Do you maintain large residential lots?

Yes. The quote is based on measured or visibly reviewed service areas, access, obstacles, frequency and equipment requirements.

Can you divide an estate into service zones?

Yes. Primary lawn, secondary acreage, wooded edges, beds, hardscape and structures can be scoped separately.

Do you provide storm cleanup?

Approved debris removal, exterior cleanup and minor repair work can be quoted after conditions are reviewed.

Can one plan include lawn, gutters and repairs?

Yes. Services can be combined when responsibilities, timing and pricing are clearly defined.

Do you provide condition reporting?

Documented observations may be included for recurring or second-home maintenance scopes.
