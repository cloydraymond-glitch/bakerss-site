Annual planning

A seasonal maintenance checklist for Carolina Forest homes

Spring

Check sprinkler coverage before turf grows rapidly; review lawn density, landscape-bed weeds and accumulated winter debris.

Summer

Adjust mowing and trimming to active growth; monitor irrigation leaks, shaded damp areas and organic staining on concrete.

Fall

Evaluate gutters and drainage paths after storms; remove leaves and pine needles and schedule approved cleanup.

Winter

Review repairs, exterior surfaces and upcoming landscape changes while turf growth is slower; avoid unnecessary heavy pruning.

Common questions

Bellegrove Preserve maintenance FAQs

These answers apply to our services in the Carolina Forest area, subject to the property, timing and approved scope.

Does Bakerss serve Bellegrove Preserve?

Bakerss Property Services offers lawn care, landscaping, exterior cleaning, handyman repairs and maintenance planning for property owners in the Carolina Forest area, including Bellegrove Preserve. Scheduling and the final scope depend on access, route capacity and the requested work.

Can you handle lawn mowing and landscape-bed cleanup together?

Yes. A quote can combine mowing, edging, trimming, debris removal and landscape-bed upkeep. We review the property and separate recurring work from seasonal or one-time cleanup so pricing and expectations stay clear.

What should I do if my sprinkler system creates dry patches?

Check whether the affected zone runs, look for damaged or blocked heads and note any overspray or puddles. Bakerss can assess visible irrigation problems before you spend money replacing stressed grass or installing new sod.

Do you offer pressure washing for driveways and walkways?

Yes. Bakerss can quote pressure washing for suitable exterior surfaces. The cleaning method depends on the concrete or other material, existing staining, nearby plants and any community restrictions.

Can you prepare a home before hurricane season?

Bakerss can quote exterior maintenance such as gutter cleaning, loose-debris cleanup and visible property checks. These tasks do not make a house hurricane-proof; homeowners should also follow official storm-preparation guidance.

Do you offer one-time jobs or recurring plans?

Both options can be quoted. One-time projects are scoped separately, while recurring plans can combine agreed weekly, monthly or seasonal work. We confirm the schedule and what is excluded before service begins.

Can HOA rules affect which work you perform?

Yes. Owners should provide any current architectural guidelines, access restrictions and approved work hours. Bakerss cannot approve HOA alterations on the association’s behalf, so approval remains the owner’s or association’s responsibility.

How do I get an estimate?

Send your address, photos, the task list and preferred timing through the Bakerss quote form. You can also call or text 843-467-7136 or email info@bakerss.com. We will review the scope and explain next steps.
