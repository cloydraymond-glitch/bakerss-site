Questions from property owners

Avalon maintenance FAQ

Final recommendations depend on the property, current conditions, access and approved scope.

How often should Avalon lawns be serviced?

Frequency depends on the season, turf growth, lot condition and owner expectations. Weekly or recurring schedules are typically evaluated during the property review.

Can lawn and pressure washing be scheduled together?

Yes. Approved recurring lawn service and periodic exterior cleaning can be coordinated to reduce vendor handoffs.

Do you handle small exterior repairs?

Yes. Minor handyman work can be quoted separately or incorporated into a broader property-maintenance plan.

Will you follow current community instructions?

Yes. Owners or managers should provide current parking, access, work-hour and exterior-change requirements.

Can you maintain rental or second-home properties?

Yes. Recurring exterior service and documented condition observations can be structured around the approved scope.
