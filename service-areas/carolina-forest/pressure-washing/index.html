Local service strategy

Carolina Forest Pressure Washing Should Match the Surface, Not Just Blast Everything

Not every exterior surface should be cleaned the same way. Concrete driveways and sidewalks may tolerate higher pressure, while siding, trim, screens, painted surfaces and some older materials may need a softer approach. Bakerss evaluates the cleaning area, surface type, buildup, access and property use before quoting the job.

That matters in Carolina Forest because properties often combine concrete driveways, vinyl siding, patios, fenced yards, screened porches, HOA sidewalks, pool decks and landscaped bed edges. A careless approach can leave streaking, overspray issues or surface damage. The goal is clean presentation with the right method for the surface.

What surfaces can be pressure washed or soft washed?

Services may include driveways, sidewalks, walkways, patios, porches, pool decks, fences, exterior siding, entry areas, dumpster pads, common areas and commercial storefront surfaces depending on material and condition.
