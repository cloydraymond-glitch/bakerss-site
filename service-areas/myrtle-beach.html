Why Myrtle Beach Properties Need Specialized Maintenance

Myrtle Beach is one of the most visited destinations in the United States — and one of the most demanding environments for property maintenance. Salt air from the Atlantic corrodes surfaces, fades paint, and damages exterior finishes faster than any inland climate. High humidity promotes mold, mildew, and algae growth on driveways, siding, and decks. St. Augustine and Bermuda grass grows aggressively through the long, hot coastal season.

Myrtle Beach also has one of the most active short-term rental markets in the Southeast. Thousands of Airbnb and VRBO properties turn over guests weekly — every lawn, every cleaning, every maintenance item directly affects your reviews and revenue.

Bakerss Property Maintenance is built specifically for Myrtle Beach property owners. We understand salt-air deterioration, coastal turf, vacation rental timelines, and the needs of second-home owners who need a trusted team when they can't be there. One vendor. Everything handled.
