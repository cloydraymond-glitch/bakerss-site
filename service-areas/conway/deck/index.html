How a Deck Improves Life in Conway, SC

Conway, established in 1734 and named Horry County's seat of government, sits along the Waccamaw River — and the river is central to the community's identity in ways that directly shape how outdoor living space is valued here. Properties along the Waccamaw River corridor, near the historic Riverwalk, and within Wild Wing Plantation's golf community setting all benefit from decks that connect the indoor living environment to the outdoor experience that makes Conway's location special.

Wild Wing Plantation's 27-hole golf course creates the most compelling deck application in Conway — a properly positioned deck on a golf course-adjacent lot provides fairway views, morning coffee in the setting that drew the buyer to Wild Wing, and the outdoor entertainment space that golf community socializing gravitates toward. For Wild Wing vacation rental properties, a deck differentiates the listing from comparable interiors-only rentals and supports higher nightly rates.

Conway's inland position creates a deck construction context that differs meaningfully from coastal Myrtle Beach. Reduced salt air means standard fastener specifications perform closer to their rated lifespans than in the oceanfront environment — though we still specify higher-quality hardware than the entry-level options. The heavy tree canopy that defines Conway's residential character creates shade benefits for decks (more comfortable in summer than fully exposed coastal installations) while also creating the debris load that requires periodic deck cleaning to manage tannin staining. Composite decking handles this cleaning requirement with nothing more than a seasonal rinse.

Material Guide

Best Decking Materials for Conway's Inland Environment

Conway's inland position creates a different material selection context than coastal Myrtle Beach. The reduced salt air extends the lifespan of all decking materials relative to their oceanfront counterparts — pressure-treated wood performs closer to its rated 15–20 year lifespan, and composite decking achieves the full 25–30 year specification. The primary material concern in Conway is the tree canopy — tannin deposits from the pines and oaks overhead stain light-colored composite decking and require periodic pressure washing. Mid-tone gray composites minimize visible tannin staining. Dark composites hide it effectively.

We discuss material options, show physical samples, and recommend based on your specific lot conditions, tree coverage, HOA requirements, and budget. Free consultations before any commitment.
