Conway Market Positioning

STR Cleaning for a City That Does Not Behave Like Myrtle Beach

The Conway vacation rental market is driven by different intent than oceanfront Myrtle Beach. Guests are not usually booking for boardwalk nightlife or high-rise resort amenities. They are booking Wild Wing golf trips, parent weekends at Coastal Carolina University, graduation visits, tournament stays, historic downtown weekends, Waccamaw River access, or a quieter inland base with a short drive to the beach. That changes the cleaning priorities.

A Conway guest is more likely to use the kitchen, parking area, entryway, laundry space, and storage closets than a typical resort-condo guest. Golf groups bring shoes, bags, carts, and outdoor debris. CCU family stays often involve multiple guests using bathrooms and common areas hard for two or three days. Riverwalk and historic-district visitors expect a clean, comfortable home that feels local rather than sterile. Our turnover process is built around those actual use patterns.

Turnover Scope

What Every Conway Vacation Rental Turnover Includes

  • Kitchen sanitation: counters, sink, stovetop, microwave interior, appliance fronts, refrigerator check, dishes reset.
  • Bathroom reset: toilets, tubs, showers, sinks, mirrors, floors, towels, high-touch surfaces, and guest-ready presentation.
  • Bedroom reset: beds stripped and remade, linens handled, closets checked, floors vacuumed, surfaces dusted.
  • Living areas: floors, tables, remotes, high-touch points, trash removal, guest-item check, and presentation reset.
  • Golf and entry protocol: entry floors, mudroom areas, gear corners, shoe debris, and high-traffic transition areas.
  • Final walkthrough with photo reporting for damage, missing items, supply needs, and maintenance concerns.
  • Same-day turnover support for checkout-to-check-in windows when scheduling allows.
  • Owner communication before small issues become guest complaints.
Conway difference: Wild Wing and CCU-area rentals often get heavier kitchen, bathroom, laundry, and entryway use than beach-condo guests. The turnover cannot be a light wipe-down. It must reset the property for the next guest group with zero visible evidence of the previous stay.
