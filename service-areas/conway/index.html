Lawn & Turf

Conway Lawn Care: Soil, Shade & Grass Type Matter

Conway lawns are shaped by inland soil, shade, irrigation, tree canopy and river proximity. The correct approach for a Wild Wing fairway-facing lawn may be wrong for a shaded Waccamaw River property or a Downtown Conway lot with older soil and mature trees.

Bakerss evaluates the property before recommending lawn care, sod installation or turf replacement. Zoysia may work well in partial shade. Bermuda is strongest in full sun and golf-community style lots. Centipede can be practical for lower-maintenance acidic sandy soils. St. Augustine may fit heavier-shade conditions when water and site conditions support it.

Partial Shade

Full Sun
