Conway Neighborhood Maintenance — Common Questions

What Conway neighborhoods does Bakerss serve?

Bakerss provides property maintenance across all Conway neighborhoods including Wild Wing Plantation, Rivers Edge Plantation, Shaftesbury Glen, Historic Downtown Conway, Kingston Oaks, Midtown Village and every community in between.

Do riverfront and golf-community properties need different maintenance in Conway?

Yes. Waterfront neighborhoods along the Waccamaw River often need storm, drainage and home-watch support, while golf communities like Wild Wing Plantation and Shaftesbury Glen have course-adjacent lawn and irrigation presentation standards.

Does Bakerss maintain historic and older homes in downtown Conway?

Yes. Historic Downtown Conway properties in Conwayborough often need exterior care suited to older homes and established landscaping, and Bakerss tailors service accordingly rather than applying a generic new-construction approach.
