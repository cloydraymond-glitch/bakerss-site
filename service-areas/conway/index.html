Local Knowledge

Maintaining Property in the County Seat

Conway is the oldest community on the Grand Strand by two centuries — chartered in 1732, nearly a century before Myrtle Beach existed — and its property care reflects that age and character. The live oaks that arch over Main Street and the Riverwalk are the city's signature and the homeowner's workload: ten months a year of leaf litter, tannin staining, and pollen across roofs, gutters, driveways, and pools. Much of Conway also runs on well water, where dissolved iron stains concrete, fixtures, and pool finishes orange-brown unless fills are treated. And with Coastal Carolina University's campus on the east side, Conway carries one of the county's biggest landlord markets — student rentals that turn over every May and August like clockwork.

Bakerss runs all three playbooks: canopy-aware exterior care (gutters, washing, and pool debris on tighter rotations), well-water treatment built into pool and pressure-washing service, and turnover packages for CCU-area landlords — cleaning, paint touch-ups, junk removal, and lawn resets between leases. From Riverfest weekend crowds downtown to quiet acreage off Highway 905, one local team covers the whole county seat.
