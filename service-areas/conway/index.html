Property Maintenance in Conway, SC

Conway is Horry County's seat of government and one of South Carolina's most historically significant coastal communities. Founded in 1734 as Kingston Township and renamed Conway in 1802, the city sits along the Waccamaw River about 14 miles inland from the Atlantic. Home to Coastal Carolina University, the county courthouse, and the region's principal medical center, Conway's historic downtown along Main Street and the Waccamaw Riverwalk is one of South Carolina's most walkable small-city cores.

City Profile

Conway at a Glance

Population: ~24,000 permanent residents, Horry County's second-largest city by population, and one of South Carolina's fastest-growing cities for over a decade — driven by small-city character, Myrtle Beach proximity (14 miles), and more accessible housing prices than the beachfront communities.

Schools: Conway High School (the Tigers), Conway Middle School, and multiple elementary schools operate within Horry County Schools. Coastal Carolina University, established in 1954 and elevated to university status in 1993, enrolls approximately 12,000 students — a major driver of the local economy and rental housing market.

Notable facts: Conway's historic downtown centers on the Waccamaw Riverwalk, a mile-long boardwalk recognized as one of South Carolina's premier small-city attractions. Conway Cemetery contains graves from the Revolutionary War era, and much of downtown is a National Register of Historic Places district.

About the Market

Property Maintenance in Conway, SC

Conway's position inland from the coast creates a distinctly different property maintenance environment from the oceanfront communities of the Grand Strand. Reduced direct salt air exposure extends paint and exterior surface cycles compared to oceanfront Myrtle Beach. However, Conway's dense tree canopy — dominated by longleaf pines, live oaks, and a rich hardwood mix along the Waccamaw corridor — creates one of Horry County's most demanding gutter maintenance environments. Wild Wing Plantation, Coastal Heights, and Conway's established neighborhoods all sit beneath tree coverage that fills gutters in six to eight weeks at peak debris periods, requiring two to four annual cleanings depending on canopy density. Conway's rapid population growth — driven partly by buyers seeking Myrtle Beach proximity at more accessible price points — makes it one of Horry County's most active residential maintenance markets.

🎓 Coastal Carolina University

Conway is home to CCU (~12,000 students) — driving a significant rental property market and consistent demand for move-in/out cleaning and turnover services.

🏌️ Golf Communities

Wild Wing Plantation's 27-hole course and Burning Ridge Golf Links create the golf-community maintenance standard, where weekly lawn care matches the fairway-adjacent environment.

🌳 Dense Tree Coverage

Conway's mature canopy creates the heaviest gutter demand in Horry County — many properties need 3-4 cleanings annually rather than the standard twice-yearly schedule.

🏠 Rapid Growth Market

One of SC's fastest-growing cities. New construction creates lawn establishment demands that differ from established neighborhood maintenance.

How often do Conway gutters need cleaning?

Most Conway properties need gutter cleaning 2-3 times annually. Properties in Wild Wing Plantation and under heavy pine or oak coverage may need 3-4 cleanings per year due to the continuous debris load from mature canopy trees. Call for a property-specific recommendation.

Yes — Wild Wing Plantation is one of our most active Conway service areas. We maintain properties throughout the golf community with HOA-compliant lawn care, the gutter frequency the community's tree coverage demands, and epoxy garage floor installs.
