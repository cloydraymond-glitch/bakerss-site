Conway sprinkler repair FAQ

Questions About Irrigation & Sprinkler Repair

Does Bakerss repair irrigation and sprinkler systems in Conway SC?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance provides irrigation and sprinkler repair in Conway for homes, lawns, rentals, HOAs, apartments, commercial properties and managed properties.

What sprinkler problems can you help with?

Common issues include broken sprinkler heads, poor coverage, dry spots, stuck zones, leaking lines, damaged valves, timer issues, low pressure and overspray onto sidewalks or driveways.

Can you repair irrigation before or after sod installation?

Yes. Irrigation checks and repairs can be coordinated before sod installation, lawn replacement, landscape refreshes or recurring lawn care so the new turf has better watering coverage.

Do you service rental properties, HOAs and commercial irrigation systems?

Yes. Bakerss can quote sprinkler and irrigation repairs for rental properties, HOAs, apartment communities, commercial sites and property managers in Conway.

Can you fix sprinkler zones that will not turn on?

Zone issues can be evaluated and quoted based on the controller, wiring, valves, heads, pressure and system condition.

Can you help with sprinkler overspray?

Yes. Sprinkler heads may need adjustment, replacement, relocation or nozzle changes depending on the cause of the overspray.

How is irrigation repair priced?

Pricing depends on the issue, labor time, parts, number of zones, accessibility, system condition, leak location, valves, heads, controller needs and approved scope.

How do I request irrigation repair?

You can request a quote online, call or text 843-467-7136, or email info@bakerss.com with the property address, photos if available and a description of the sprinkler issue.
