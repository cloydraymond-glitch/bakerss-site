Conway’s Dual Property Market

CCU Rental Repairs, Wild Wing HOA Maintenance & Historic Conway Homes

Conway is not one simple handyman market. It has three different service patterns: CCU-area rental properties that need quick make-ready repairs, premium HOA communities like Wild Wing Plantation and Burning Ridge that need exterior compliance support, and older Conway homes near the Waccamaw River and historic corridors that require careful repair judgment.

That is why Bakerss does more than single-item handyman calls. We group practical repairs with property maintenance services that Conway owners already need: fence repairs after storms, deck repairs before season, irrigation checks after sod installation, window cleaning after pollen and weather, dryer vent cleaning for rental safety, HVAC filter replacement for absentee owners, and full punch list work before tenant move-in or builder closing.
