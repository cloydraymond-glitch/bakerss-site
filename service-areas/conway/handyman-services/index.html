Conway's Dual Handyman Market

How CCU and Wild Wing Create Conway's Two Distinct Handyman Needs

Conway's handyman market reflects the city's position as both a university town and an established golf community — two demographics with fundamentally different maintenance needs and service expectations.

The CCU Rental Property Market

Coastal Carolina University's 12,000 students create a rental property market in Conway — landlords with single-family homes, townhomes, and condominiums within the CCU radius who experience the intensive between-tenant repair cycle that student occupancy generates. Students use properties intensively — door handles pulled with force through nine months of move-in rush, drywall impacted by furniture moves, faucets and fixtures operated continuously by multiple occupants simultaneously, garbage disposals abused in ways the manufacturer didn't anticipate. The move-out in May and December reveals this accumulated wear in a compressed window when the landlord needs repairs completed before the next tenant moves in.

The CCU landlord's handyman relationship is not about the sophistication of individual repairs — it is about the reliability and speed of execution at the May and December transition windows. Drywall patches that need to be done before painting. Door handle and lock sets that need replacement before key distribution. Faucets that drip audibly and need to be addressed before a new tenant's first complaint. Our familiarity with this cycle — its timing, its specific repair categories, and its compressed execution window — makes us the right handyman partner for Conway's CCU rental market.

Wild Wing Plantation and Burning Ridge

Wild Wing Plantation's 27-hole golf community and active HOA create Conway's premium residential handyman market. Properties in Wild Wing are assessed against the community standard — and the HOA's enforcement ensures that visible exterior deterioration, failed fixtures, and appearance-reducing maintenance deferrals generate violation correspondence that the property owner must address. Wild Wing homeowners who proactively maintain their properties to HOA standard never receive these letters. Those who defer maintenance until something is visibly obvious to an inspector face the administrative and potentially financial consequences of HOA enforcement. Proactive handyman maintenance that addresses developing issues before they become visible failures is the most cost-effective approach in any active HOA community.
