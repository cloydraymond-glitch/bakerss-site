The Conway lawn problem

New Construction, Larger Lots & Inland Heat Create Real Yard Issues

Conway is growing fast, and many new neighborhoods have builder-installed turf that looks good at closing but struggles after one summer if mowing and watering are inconsistent. Thin sod, compacted construction soil, poor drainage around low areas, and fast weed invasion are common. For larger Conway lots, the issue is often not just mowing; it is maintaining a property on a schedule that keeps the lawn from getting out of hand during hot, wet weeks.

  • New sod and thin turf need consistent cut height
  • Large lots require route discipline and proper time allocation
  • Drainage pockets near river-area soils can stay soft after rain
  • HOA neighborhoods need predictable curb appeal
  • Rental lawns need service before tenant or owner complaints
  • Summer growth can require 7-day service, not 14-day service
Conway service recommendation: weekly mowing from April through October for most Bermuda, Zoysia, and St. Augustine lawns. Biweekly can work for slower-growing Centipede or cooler-month maintenance, but it should not be forced during peak growth if the lawn is overgrowing between visits.

Conway lawn care starts at $50 per visit for smaller lots. Many Conway homes fall between $65 and $120 per visit because lots are often larger than beachside properties and require more trimming, edging, and mowing time.

During peak growth, most Conway lawns should be mowed every 7 to 10 days. Biweekly can work in cooler months or on slower-growing turf, but it can fall behind during hot, wet summer weeks.
