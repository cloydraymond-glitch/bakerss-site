Why Conway is Different

Pressure Washing Built for Inland Conway Conditions

Conway does not have the same pressure washing profile as Myrtle Beach or Murrells Inlet. The work here is less about direct ocean salt spray and more about inland humidity, shaded tree coverage, river-area moisture, clay-sand soil runoff, and the heavy organic staining produced by pine straw, oak leaves, and pollen. Driveways in Conway can turn dark fast because shaded concrete stays wet longer after storms, and once mildew takes hold, simple rinsing will not correct it.

Conway’s growth also creates another need: new construction and recently developed neighborhoods often deal with red-brown dirt tracking, irrigation stains, construction dust, and unfinished landscape runoff. Bakerss cleans these surfaces with the right method for each material: higher pressure for concrete, soft washing for siding, and controlled cleaning chemistry for mildew, algae, and organic staining.

FAQ

Pressure Washing FAQ — Conway SC

How much does pressure washing cost in Conway, SC?+
Driveway cleaning usually starts at $125. House soft washing commonly ranges from $225–$650 depending on size, height, access, and staining. Commercial properties are quoted after reviewing the surface area and schedule requirements.
Do you pressure wash homes in Wild Wing Plantation?+
Yes. Wild Wing Plantation is a core Conway service area for driveway cleaning, house washing, sidewalk cleaning, patio cleaning, and HOA curb appeal work.
Do Conway homes need soft washing or pressure washing?+
Most Conway homes need both. Concrete driveways and walkways are pressure washed. Siding, trim, soffits, painted surfaces, and older materials should be soft washed to avoid damage.
How often should a Conway house be washed?+
Most Conway homes benefit from annual house washing. Properties under heavy pine or oak cover may need driveway cleaning twice per year because tannin stains and mildew build up faster in shaded areas.
Can you clean Conway commercial properties after hours?+
Yes. Commercial pressure washing can be scheduled before opening, after closing, or during lower-traffic windows for storefronts, offices, and managed properties.
Will pressure washing damage siding?+
High pressure can damage siding. Bakerss soft washes siding and painted surfaces using lower pressure and cleaning solution, then rinses safely.
