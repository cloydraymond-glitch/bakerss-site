About This Service in Conway

Pressure Washing in Conway, SC

Conway's inland position actually creates some specific pressure washing challenges not found at the beach. The dense tree canopy in communities like Wild Wing Plantation and Coastal Heights means driveways and walkways accumulate heavy pine straw staining, tannin deposits from oak leaves, and algae growth in shaded areas. Conway's humid summers and heavy spring rainfall create ideal conditions for green algae and black mildew on siding. We use the right technique for each surface — high pressure on concrete, soft washing on siding and painted surfaces.

We serve all Conway property types: single-family homes in Wild Wing Plantation, Coastal Heights, Burning Ridge, and all surrounding communities; commercial properties along the Highway 501 and SC-544 corridors; historic downtown residential properties; and investment rental properties.

FAQs — Pressure Washing in Conway

How much does pressure washing cost in Conway, SC?+
Pressure washing in Conway starts at $125 for driveways and walkways. House washing for a standard Conway home runs $225–$450 depending on size. We provide free estimates based on your specific property.
Do you pressure wash Wild Wing Plantation homes?+
Yes. Wild Wing Plantation is one of our most active Conway pressure washing areas. We wash driveways, siding, and decks throughout the community.
How often should Conway homes be pressure washed?+
Most Conway homes benefit from exterior washing once per year. Homes under heavy pine or oak coverage — common in Conway — may benefit from twice-yearly driveway cleaning as tree debris staining accumulates faster.
Do you soft wash Conway siding?+
Yes. Vinyl siding and painted surfaces on Conway homes are always soft washed — not high-pressure blasted. High pressure on these surfaces causes damage. We use the correct technique for every surface.
Can you pressure wash a Conway commercial property?+
Yes. We serve commercial properties in Conway including storefronts, office buildings, and parking areas on a schedule around your business hours.
