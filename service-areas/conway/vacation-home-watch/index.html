Questions About Home Watch Service

Does Bakerss offer vacation home watch service in Conway SC?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance offers vacation home watch and second-home property checks in Conway for absentee owners, seasonal homeowners, rentals, Airbnbs and managed properties.

What is included in a vacation home watch check?

A home watch check can include exterior observations, interior walkthrough items, visible leak checks, HVAC thermostat checks, door and window observations, storm-related observations, photo notes and maintenance recommendations depending on the approved scope.

Can you check a property after storms or heavy rain?

Yes. Storm and heavy-rain checks can be discussed and quoted based on availability, location, urgency and the approved service plan.

Can vacation home watch include lawn care, cleaning or repairs?

Yes. Bakerss can combine vacation home watch with lawn care, interior cleaning, Airbnb cleaning, handyman services, pressure washing, pool maintenance, gutter cleaning, dryer vent cleaning and other property maintenance services.

Do you provide photos or notes after a check?

Photo notes and visible issue documentation can be included depending on the service plan and approved scope.

Can you check the property before I arrive?

Yes. Pre-arrival checks can be quoted for owners who want the property reviewed before they come back into town or before guests arrive.

How often should a vacation home be checked?

Frequency depends on the property, owner preference, season, rental use, insurance requirements and risk level. Common options include weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, pre-arrival, post-departure and storm-related checks.

How is vacation home watch pricing determined?

Pricing depends on property size, location, check frequency, interior and exterior scope, photo notes, storm checks, repair coordination, rental support and any included maintenance services.

Can Bakerss repair issues found during a home watch check?

Yes. Bakerss can quote approved repair and maintenance work through handyman services, property maintenance plans, lawn care, cleaning, pressure washing, pool maintenance and exterior services.

How do I request a Conway vacation home watch quote?

You can request a quote online, call or text 843-467-7136, or email info@bakerss.com with the property address, property type, desired check frequency, access details and any specific concerns.
