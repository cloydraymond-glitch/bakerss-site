CCU Landlord Context

How Professional Cleaning Protects Conway Rental Property Investments

Coastal Carolina University's annual student population cycle creates predictable, high-intensity move-out cleaning demand every May and December. Properties occupied by students for eight to twelve months accumulate cleaning needs that standard tenant-performed cleaning almost never fully addresses. Professional move-out cleaning that covers inside all appliances, bathroom grout, baseboards, and all the surfaces that standard tenant cleaning misses is the difference between a security deposit dispute and a property returned to the rental market cleanly and quickly.

The financial math favors professional cleaning consistently. A Conway landlord's cleaning deduction from a security deposit typically runs $200–$500 for anything beyond a spotless property. Professional move-out cleaning for a standard 3BR Conway home costs $275–$450 — often less than the deduction, and always less than the cost of a disputed deposit that requires documentation and small claims court involvement. We provide move-out cleaning throughout Conway's CCU rental market on the accelerated timeline that lease transitions demand.
