CCU Rental Turnovers

Move-Out Cleaning for Conway Landlords and Student Rentals

Coastal Carolina University creates a predictable cleaning rush in Conway. When semester leases end, landlords often need properties cleaned quickly enough to photograph, show, repair, repaint, and re-lease. Waiting too long can cost more than the cleaning itself.

Our move-out cleaning is designed for property condition recovery: kitchens, bathrooms, floors, baseboards, high-touch areas, trash left behind, appliance surfaces, cabinet fronts, and general reset work. For heavily used student rentals, Bakerss can also coordinate related services like handyman repairs, touch-up painting, and pressure washing before the next tenant cycle.

Conway landlord note: The highest-demand transition windows are typically late spring/early summer and winter break. For multiple units, schedule early so cleaning, repairs, and relisting do not stack on the same week.
