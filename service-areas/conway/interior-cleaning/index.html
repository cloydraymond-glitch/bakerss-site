Conway neighborhood cleaning breakdown

Conway Has Four Distinct Property Markets — Each With Different Cleaning Demands

Conway is Horry County's oldest city and its seat of government — a community with a historic downtown, a major university, established residential neighborhoods and commercial corridors that each generate distinct cleaning needs entirely separate from the beach rental market 20 miles to the east.

🏛️ Downtown Conway Historic District

Downtown Conway is one of the most intact historic commercial districts in South Carolina — a riverfront Main Street lined with 19th and early 20th century buildings housing law offices, medical practices, retail shops, restaurants, government offices and professional services. Properties here need cleaning that respects older construction — wood floors, plaster walls, historic fixtures and surfaces that require care rather than industrial cleaning methods. Commercial cleaning for downtown Conway offices means consistent, professional service that matches the character of a historic district where the Horry County Courthouse, Conway Medical Center offices and established professional firms set the standard. Residential properties in and near the historic district include Victorian-era homes that have been converted to offices or maintained as residences — each with its own particular surface and material requirements.

🎓 Coastal Carolina University Area

Coastal Carolina University sits just outside Conway proper on Highway 501, and the surrounding neighborhoods are dense with student rental housing — houses, duplexes and small apartment buildings that house CCU's nearly 12,000 students. This market generates one of the most consistent and predictable cleaning cycles in all of Horry County: end-of-lease move-outs every May and December, with landlords needing thorough cleaning before re-renting or showing properties. Student rental move-outs require more thorough cleaning than standard residential move-outs — the properties see hard use, and the margin between what students leave behind and what new tenants expect is wide. Bakerss provides move-out cleaning for CCU area landlords scoped around the actual condition, not a flat rate that gets the surface and calls it done.

🏘️ Kingston, Highway 501 & Conway Neighborhoods

Kingston is Conway's established residential neighborhood closest to downtown — a mix of long-term homeowners, rental properties and historic homes that sit outside the immediate downtown commercial district but share its character. The Highway 501 corridor from Conway toward Myrtle Beach is lined with commercial properties — offices, retail, services — that need reliable commercial cleaning separated from beach-market demand. Beyond Kingston, Conway has a collection of residential subdivisions and neighborhoods extending toward the Waccamaw Neck that need recurring residential cleaning for owner-occupied homes, landlord-ready move-out cleaning for active rental portfolios and deep cleaning for properties that have been heavily occupied.

Waccamaw Neck & Rural Conway Properties

West of downtown Conway along the Waccamaw River, and north toward Aynor, Conway's rural periphery includes farm-adjacent residential properties, larger homes on acreage and rural rental homes that need cleaning without beach-market pricing or beach-market assumptions about the scope. Bakerss serves these properties as part of Conway coverage — scoped around the actual property size, condition and needs, with no rural-area premium.

The CCU cleaning cycle

May and December Mean Move-Out Cleaning Season for Conway Landlords

Coastal Carolina University runs on a traditional academic calendar — fall semester ends in December, spring semester ends in May. Those two months are move-out season for the hundreds of student rental properties surrounding the CCU campus. Landlords have a narrow window between lease end and new lease start to clean, repair and show properties.

Student rental move-outs are genuinely different from standard residential move-outs. Properties see harder use — kitchens, bathrooms and common areas all show the evidence of heavy occupancy by people for whom cleaning is not a priority. Bakerss quotes CCU area move-outs based on what the property actually needs, and completes a thorough clean that gets the property ready for the next showing, not just the next tenant move-in.
