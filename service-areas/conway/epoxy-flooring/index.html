Waccamaw River Proximity & Moisture

Conway sits on the Waccamaw River — historically the city's commercial artery. Riverside and low-lying areas have elevated moisture vapor transmission through concrete. Our moisture barrier primer is essential here, not optional.

Historic Homes — Older Concrete

Conway's older neighborhoods feature concrete slabs from the 1950s–1980s with surface scaling, moisture issues, and previous failed coatings. We assess all of this during the free estimate and address it before coating begins.

FAQ

Conway Epoxy Floor FAQ

Do you serve all of Conway including older neighborhoods near downtown?

Yes — we service all of Conway including the historic neighborhoods along the Waccamaw River, the Coastal Carolina University area, and all new construction in surrounding Horry County zip codes. Older slabs from the mid-20th century are assessed carefully during our free estimate — they often have surface scaling or previous coating failures that require additional prep work, which we factor into the quote.

What color options are available for Conway installations?

130+ colors across four systems: Earth Inspired flakes, Team Inspired (including Chanticleer teal and green combinations), Marble Flakes, and 43 metallic colors. Warm earthy tones like Bamboo, Driftwood, Hammock, and Shipwreck are especially popular with Conway homeowners. Browse all color samples →

How much does epoxy floor coating cost in Conway?

Starting at $1,800 for a 1-car garage, $3,000 for 2-car, and $4,500 for 3-car — all-inclusive covering diamond grinding, moisture primer, full solid epoxy system, and polyaspartic topcoat. Free estimates for all Conway properties. 843-467-7136.

Why does the Waccamaw River proximity affect my garage floor?

Conway's position along the Waccamaw River and its historically low elevation contribute to elevated soil moisture throughout the area. Concrete slabs absorb and transmit this moisture upward continuously. Without a moisture barrier primer, even 100% solids epoxy can delaminate in high moisture-vapor-transmission environments. We test every slab before coating.

How long does installation take?

Most 1 and 2-car garages are complete in 1–2 days. 3-car garages typically take 2 days. Light foot traffic in 24 hours, vehicle traffic in 72 hours.
