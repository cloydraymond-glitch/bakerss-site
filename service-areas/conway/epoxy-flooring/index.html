📍 Conway: Founded 1732, Still the Heart of Horry County

Conway was founded in 1732 as "Kingston" — one of eleven townships created in a royal plan to colonize South Carolina. The town was renamed Conwayborough in 1801 to honor Revolutionary War veteran Colonel Robert Conway, then shortened to Conway in 1883.

The Conway Downtown Historic District includes City Hall built in 1825 — designed by Robert Mills, the same architect who designed the Washington Monument. Many buildings in downtown Conway are listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Today, Conway is home to Coastal Carolina University and sits at the gateway to the Grand Strand's 60 miles of beaches. With Horry County growing by an estimated 427,000 residents by 2025, Conway is experiencing the fastest growth it has seen in its nearly 300-year history.

That growth means new construction, new garages, and new homeowners who want the best — not the cheapest. We've been installing solid commercial epoxy throughout Conway and Horry County since day one.

Why does the Waccamaw River proximity affect my garage floor?+
Conway's position along the Waccamaw River and its historically low elevation contribute to elevated soil moisture throughout the area. Concrete slabs absorb and transmit this moisture upward continuously — a process called moisture vapor transmission. Without a moisture barrier primer, even 100% solids epoxy can delaminate in high-MVT environments. We test every slab before coating and apply the appropriate primer system.
How long does installation take?+
Most 1 and 2-car garages are complete in 1–2 days. 3-car garages typically take 2 days. Light foot traffic in 24 hours, vehicle traffic in 72 hours. We work around your schedule.
