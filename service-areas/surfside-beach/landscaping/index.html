Landscaping Needs Drainage Awareness, Plant Selection, Clean Bed Lines & Maintenance Planning

The best Surfside Beach landscapes are designed around exposure and upkeep. A rental near Ocean Boulevard may need low-maintenance curb appeal that photographs well. A home near Deerfield may need cleaner beds, trimmed shrubs and better irrigation awareness. A commercial property along Business 17 may need crisp entrances that look professional every day. Bakerss scopes landscaping around the property’s actual use, site conditions and long-term maintenance expectations.

Exposure

Salt, Sand & Heat

Coastal properties need tougher material and plant choices that can handle humidity, wind, sand movement, heat and seasonal weather.

Materials

Mulch, Pine Straw & Rock

Each bed material performs differently. The right choice depends on drainage, slope, HOA standards, rental traffic and maintenance tolerance.

Water

Irrigation & Drainage

Plants, palms, shrubs and sod fail quickly when irrigation coverage, standing water, sandy soil or runoff problems are ignored.

Presentation

Guest & Customer Curb Appeal

Vacation rentals, HOAs and commercial properties need entrances, walkways, beds and exterior photos that look clean and intentional.

A landscape refresh should solve the reason the old landscape stopped working.

If shrubs failed because of shade, salt exposure, poor irrigation, bad drainage, overgrowth, rental damage or neglected maintenance, simply replacing plants may repeat the same failure. Bakerss reviews the reason the area declined before recommending materials, plants and maintenance.
