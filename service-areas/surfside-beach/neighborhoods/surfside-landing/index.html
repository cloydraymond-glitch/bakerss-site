Surfside Landing Condo Maintenance FAQs

Does Bakerss offer maintenance for Surfside Landing condos?

Bakerss can quote condo cleaning, rental turnover support, home watch, HVAC support and approved repairs at Surfside Landing, subject to access, scope and scheduling.

Can you work between vacation rental stays?

Yes. We review guest departure and arrival times, unit access and the approved service checklist before confirming the visit.

Can you maintain my condo while I am away?

Authorized property checks, photo notes, cleaning and maintenance follow-up can be included in a recurring plan. The agreement defines access, frequency and reporting.

Can Bakerss work on shared areas or building systems?

Shared-area work requires authorization from the appropriate owner, manager or association. We confirm responsibility and access before quoting or scheduling.

Can you provide HVAC and repair support?

Yes. Requests are reviewed for equipment, access and scope. Work requiring a particular trade license, permit or specialty qualification is assigned or coordinated accordingly.

Are materials and additional repairs included?

The written quote defines labor, materials and any other applicable charges. Approval requirements for additional work are agreed before service.

How do I request a condo maintenance quote?

Provide your unit number, service needs, photos if available, preferred timing and the authorized access contact. Request a quote online, call 843-467-7136 or text us.
