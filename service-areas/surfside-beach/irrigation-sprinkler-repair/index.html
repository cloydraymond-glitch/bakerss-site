Questions About Irrigation and Sprinkler Repair

Does Bakerss provide irrigation and sprinkler repair in Surfside Beach SC?

Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance provides irrigation and sprinkler repair in Surfside Beach for homes, vacation rentals, HOAs, apartment communities, commercial properties, lawns, sod and landscapes.

What irrigation repairs can be included?

Irrigation repair can include sprinkler head replacement, broken zone troubleshooting, timer and controller review, valve issues, leaks, low-pressure concerns, coverage problems, clogged heads and drip or spray-area observations.

Do you repair sprinkler heads?

Yes. Bakerss can replace or adjust damaged, clogged, misaligned or broken sprinkler heads depending on parts, access and system condition.

Can you fix zones that are not working?

Yes. Broken, weak, stuck or non-working zones can be reviewed to determine likely controller, valve, wiring, head or line issues.

Can you help before or after sod installation?

Yes. Irrigation coverage should be reviewed before or after sod installation, and sprinkler repair can be paired with sod installation, lawn care and landscaping.

Do you service HOA and commercial irrigation systems?

Yes. Bakerss can review irrigation and sprinkler repair for HOA common areas, entrances, apartment communities, commercial properties, offices and managed landscapes.

Can irrigation repair be part of a property maintenance plan?

Yes. Irrigation repair can be bundled with lawn care, landscaping, sod installation, HOA maintenance, commercial maintenance and recurring property maintenance plans.

What are signs irrigation needs repair?

Common signs include dry spots, soaked areas, broken heads, overspray, low pressure, zones not running, zones stuck on, leaks, uneven coverage or a controller that is not operating correctly.

How is irrigation repair priced?

Pricing depends on the issue, number of zones, sprinkler heads, valves, controller condition, leaks, access, parts, labor, system age and whether service is one-time or part of a maintenance plan.
