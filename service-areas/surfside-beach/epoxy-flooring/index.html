📍 Surfside Beach: The Family Beach of the Grand Strand

Originally known as Roach's Beach, then briefly as "Floral Beach," the area became Surfside Beach in 1952. When Hurricane Hazel destroyed nearly a third of its buildings in 1954, developers saw opportunity rather than ruin. They rebuilt Surfside as the Grand Strand's family destination — and that identity has held for 70 years.

Surfside's beaches are cleaned daily, supervised by lifeguards, and feature accessible ramps and facilities. In 2016, the town signed a proclamation making it the nation's first autism-friendly travel destination. This is a community that thinks carefully about the long term — which is exactly how we approach every epoxy installation we do here.

Today, Surfside Beach homeowners include permanent residents, retirees, and vacation property owners who expect quality from every contractor they hire. We meet that expectation with 100% solids commercial epoxy and a prep process that leaves nothing to chance.

💡 The Food Service Industry Connection

The same non-porous, seamless epoxy surface we install in Surfside garages is what commercial kitchens, food processing facilities, and USDA-inspected plants install on their floors. The FDA requires non-porous, cleanable surfaces in food manufacturing — epoxy's seamless surface means no grout lines, no crevices, no bacteria harborage.

For your garage, that means: oil drips, dirt, and debris sit on top of the coating and wipe up completely. Sand from the beach doesn't work into pores. Chemical spills don't stain. The same property that satisfies the FDA satisfies every Surfside Beach homeowner.

100% Solids Epoxy — Non-Negotiable

Surfside's coastal climate is identical to the rest of the Grand Strand — high humidity, salt air, and moisture vapor transmission through concrete. 100% solids is the only system that holds long-term here.

Surfside Beach Epoxy FAQ

What is the difference between the epoxy used in commercial kitchens and garage epoxy?+
The chemistry is the same — 100% solids commercial epoxy applied as a seamless, non-porous surface coating. Commercial kitchens use it because the FDA requires non-porous, cleanable floors that don't harbor bacteria. Garages benefit from exactly the same properties: oil doesn't soak in, spills sit on top, and the surface cleans completely. The specification we install meets or exceeds commercial food service requirements.
