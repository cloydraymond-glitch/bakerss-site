What We Install

100% Solids — Non-Negotiable

Surfside's coastal climate is identical to the rest of the Grand Strand — high humidity, salt air, and moisture vapor transmission. 100% solids is the only system that holds long-term here.

🍽️

The Food Service Industry Connection

The same non-porous, seamless epoxy surface we install in garages is what commercial kitchens and USDA-inspected plants use. The FDA requires non-porous surfaces — epoxy's seamless finish means no grout lines, no crevices, no bacteria harborage. Oil drips wipe up completely. Sand from the beach doesn't work into pores.

🔬

Diamond Grinding — No Shortcuts

We grind every slab before coating. Without a mechanical surface profile, any epoxy coating — regardless of quality — will eventually delaminate. We don't cut this step.

Epoxy Floor FAQ — Surfside Beach

What is the difference between the epoxy used in commercial kitchens and garage epoxy?

The chemistry is the same — 100% solids commercial epoxy applied as a seamless, non-porous coating. Commercial kitchens use it because the FDA requires non-porous, cleanable floors. For your garage: oil doesn't soak in, spills sit on top, sand wipes clean. The specification we install meets or exceeds commercial food service requirements.

What color options are available for Surfside Beach installs?

130+ colors across four systems. Earth Inspired and Marble Flakes complement Surfside's family aesthetic. 3D Metallics in warm sands and ocean tones are also popular. Browse all 130+ color samples →
