About the Market

Property Maintenance in Surfside Beach, SC

Surfside Beach's residential character sets it apart from the commercial intensity of Myrtle Beach to the north. The town's permanent-resident base — larger proportionally than most Grand Strand communities — creates strong demand for consistent, year-round lawn care, landscaping, and home maintenance from owners who live here full time and take pride in their properties. Oceanside Village, the gated community along the southern end of Surfside, has one of the most active HOA maintenance standards on the South Grand Strand.

Salt air exposure in Surfside Beach is significant — the town sits directly on the Atlantic, and properties within a half mile of the oceanfront face the same accelerated surface degradation as any Myrtle Beach oceanfront property. Epoxy garage floors have been growing in demand in Surfside's larger residential properties, where the finished aesthetic and coastal concrete protection adds both appeal and durability. Pool maintenance is a consistent need given the town's mix of residential pools and STR properties with guest-facing amenities.
