Local Knowledge

Maintaining Property in the Family Beach

Surfside Beach packs an entire beach-town economy into about two square miles — which shapes how property care works here. Cottage and duplex rentals between Ocean Boulevard and Highway 17 flip guests weekly all summer, so turnover timing matters as much as quality. Golf-cart neighborhoods like Oceanside Village run on community standards and shared amenities. And every structure in town sits close enough to the Atlantic that salt air works on siding, fixtures, and pool equipment year-round. Bakerss runs Surfside on a rhythm built for all three: rental-calendar scheduling, community-standard documentation, and coastal-grade exterior care.

For the families who live here full-time — Deerfield, Caropines, Lakewood — the value is simpler: one local team for the lawn, the pool, the pressure washing, and the punch list, with the same standard the Family Beach expects from everyone who works in it.
