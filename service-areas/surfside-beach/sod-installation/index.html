Sod Types

Centipede, Bermuda, St. Augustine and Zoysia Sod Options

Centipede Sod

Popular Low-Maintenance Choice

Centipede is common in coastal South Carolina yards and can be a good fit for owners who want moderate maintenance and a practical warm-season lawn.

Bermuda Sod

Best for Sunny, High-Use Areas

Bermuda performs best in full sun and can handle traffic well, but it is not ideal for heavily shaded yards. It requires regular mowing and maintenance.

St. Augustine Sod

Thicker Coastal Look

St. Augustine offers a broader blade and can work well in certain coastal yards. It needs proper watering, care and the right site conditions.

Zoysia Sod

Dense Premium Turf

Zoysia can provide a dense, attractive lawn with good wear tolerance, but cost, maintenance and establishment expectations should be reviewed first.

New Sod Aftercare

How to Protect New Sod After Installation

  • Water consistently during the establishment period
  • Avoid heavy foot traffic until the sod begins rooting
  • Do not mow until the sod has rooted enough that it does not lift
  • Use sharp mower blades and avoid cutting too low on the first mow
  • Watch edges, corners and sunny areas for drying faster than shaded areas
  • Repair irrigation problems quickly before the sod dries out
  • Schedule ongoing lawn care so the investment is protected after rooting
