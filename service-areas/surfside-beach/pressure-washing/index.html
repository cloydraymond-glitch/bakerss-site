Surfside Beach Exterior Cleaning

Soft Washing Built for Older Beach Cottages and Coastal Surfaces

Surfside Beach is not a blank-slate subdivision. Many properties are older family cottages, multigenerational beach homes, short-term rentals, HOA homes and compact lots near the ocean. That matters because pressure choice matters. High pressure on older siding can drive water behind vinyl, damage painted wood, scar trim, disturb caulk lines and create moisture problems behind the surface.

Bakerss uses a surface-specific method. Siding, stucco, painted wood, vinyl and fiber cement are soft washed with chemistry that kills mildew and removes salt film. Concrete, driveways, walkways and some masonry receive higher pressure. Decks, outdoor showers and pool areas are cleaned based on material, condition and age.

The result is clean exterior presentation without the damage risk that comes from treating every surface the same.

Surfside-Specific Maintenance

Where Surfside Beach Pressure Washing Matters Most

Oceanfront and Near-Ocean

Salt Film Builds Fast

Homes within a few blocks of the Atlantic collect salt film faster than inland properties. Salt holds moisture against paint, siding, trim and hardware. Twice-yearly washing is the correct standard for oceanfront and near-ocean Surfside Beach homes.

Oceanside Village

HOA-Aware Curb Appeal

Oceanside Village homes need clean driveways, siding, patios and exterior areas that match community standards. Bakerss services homes, rentals and second homes with organized scheduling and clean communication.

Vacation Rentals

Listing Photos and Guest Arrivals

Exterior washing before listing-photo season and before peak summer guest traffic helps rentals look maintained online and on arrival. Outdoor showers, patios, decks and driveways are high-impact cleaning areas for STR guests.

Older Cottages

Protection Over Aggression

Older Surfside cottages need soft washing, not aggressive blasting. The goal is to remove mildew and salt without damaging siding, trim, caulk, paint or older exterior materials.

How much does pressure washing cost in Surfside Beach?

Driveway pressure washing starts around $125. Single-story house soft washing usually starts around $225. Full exterior packages often range from $550 to $1,000+ depending on surfaces, buildup and property size.

Should Surfside Beach siding be soft washed?

Yes. Siding should be soft washed, especially on older cottages, painted wood, vinyl, stucco and fiber cement. High pressure can force water behind siding and damage exterior materials.

Do you service Oceanside Village?

Yes. Bakerss provides pressure washing and soft washing for Oceanside Village homes, patios, driveways, pool decks, sidewalks, siding and rental properties.

How often should oceanfront Surfside Beach homes be washed?

Oceanfront and near-ocean homes should usually be washed twice per year due to salt film, humidity and mildew. Inland Surfside properties are usually annual unless they have heavy shade, tree debris or storm exposure.

Do you clean outdoor showers?

Yes. Outdoor shower cleaning is available and strongly recommended for Surfside Beach rental homes and cottages because these areas collect sand, soap residue, mildew and organic buildup quickly.

Do you offer post-storm washing?
