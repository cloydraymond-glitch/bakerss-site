About Surfside Beach — The Family Beach

Surfside Beach, SC — Understanding the Community We Serve

Surfside Beach incorporated in 1951 — one of the last Grand Strand communities to formalize — and has governed itself since that founding with a deliberate, specific goal: preserving the residential, family-oriented character that distinguishes it from the resort-scale development corridor to the north. With approximately 4,500 permanent residents in roughly one square mile, Surfside Beach is the smallest municipality on the Grand Strand. The Town Council's consistent land use decisions have kept the commercial scale modest and the residential identity intact for more than seven decades.

This governance philosophy creates a distinctive property market. Many Surfside Beach properties have been in the same family for two and three generations — a grandparent bought the 1960s beach cottage, the children grew up vacationing in it, and now the grandchildren manage it as a vacation rental. This multigenerational ownership pattern means many Surfside properties carry 50 and 60 years of use, renovation, and personal history. It also means the guest base for Surfside vacation rentals is among the most loyal on the Grand Strand — the same family returning to the same property every summer for a decade or more. That loyalty is worth protecting through consistent professional maintenance.

Surfside Deck Construction Context

What Makes Deck Building Different in Surfside Beach's Older Properties

A deck added to a Surfside Beach cottage built in 1972 presents structural integration challenges that adding a deck to a 2015 construction does not. The ledger attachment — where the deck framing connects to the house structure — must be made into a building system that was not built with deck attachment in mind. Older dimensional lumber, potential rot at the connection point, and the absence of modern moisture protection at the house-to-deck junction all need to be assessed and addressed before any decking material is selected. Getting this wrong creates the water infiltration that damages both the deck and the house structure it connects to.

The direct Atlantic salt air at Surfside Beach creates the fastener corrosion imperative that applies to all Grand Strand deck construction. Zinc-plated screws and structural connectors corrode visibly in 18–24 months at direct oceanfront Surfside properties — faster than anywhere inland on the Grand Strand. By year three or four, zinc corrosion is migrating into surrounding wood and compromising the structural connections that hold the deck together. Stainless steel throughout — from the decking fasteners to the joist hangers to the post bases — is the baseline specification for a Surfside Beach deck expected to achieve its full lifespan.

Repair Services

Deck Repair for Surfside Beach's Existing Structures

Many Surfside Beach cottages have existing decks from the 1990s and 2000s that were built with the zinc hardware that was standard at the time. After 20+ years of Surfside's salt air, these fasteners have corroded, the corrosion has migrated into surrounding wood, and individual board replacement is becoming necessary with increasing frequency. Fastener upgrade to stainless on an existing Surfside deck — replacing all visible fasteners and structural connectors — extends the deck's effective lifespan significantly at a fraction of full replacement cost. We assess every existing deck honestly: repair if viable, replace if the structure has been compromised beyond economic repair.
