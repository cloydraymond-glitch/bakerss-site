Grass Types

Bermuda, Zoysia, Centipede and St. Augustine in Socastee

The best Socastee lawn care plan depends on grass type. Bermuda needs sunlight and frequent mowing during active growth. Zoysia can provide a dense, attractive lawn and may tolerate some partial shade depending on variety and site conditions. Centipede can work for lower-maintenance lawns in sandy acidic soil. St. Augustine may be considered for certain shade conditions, but it needs water and proper care.

Two properties on the same street can require different management. One yard may have full sun, irrigation and fast Bermuda growth. Another may have pine canopy, thin turf and areas where sod replacement or shade-tolerant planning is more realistic than aggressive mowing.

Maintenance Calendar

Recommended Socastee Lawn Care Schedule

  • March–April: bi-weekly mowing as turf wakes up, spring cleanup, pine debris control
  • May–September: weekly mowing for most active lawns, especially Bermuda and irrigated properties
