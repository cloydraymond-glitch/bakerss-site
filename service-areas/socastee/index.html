Socastee Neighborhood Maintenance — Common Questions

What Socastee neighborhoods does Bakerss serve?

Bakerss provides property maintenance across all Socastee neighborhoods including Forestbrook, Deerfield, Windsor Plantation, Island Green, River Oaks, Socastee Landing and every community in between.

Do waterway and pond-front Socastee properties need different maintenance?

Yes. Communities near the Intracoastal Waterway, Osprey Cove and Waterway Palms South often need drainage-aware lawn care and moisture management, while golf-adjacent neighborhoods like Island Green and River Oaks have course-facing presentation standards.

Does Bakerss serve newer growth-corridor neighborhoods in Socastee?

Yes. Newer planned communities along the Highway 707 and Highway 544 corridors, including The Farm at Timberlake, The Gates, Windsor Plantation and Brighton Woods, are all within the Socastee service area.
