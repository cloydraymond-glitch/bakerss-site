Socastee, SC · Horry County's Wooded Suburb · Forestbrook Country

Property Maintenance in
Socastee, SC

Socastee is Horry County's most densely wooded unincorporated community — a sprawling suburban area west of Myrtle Beach along the Intracoastal Waterway that has grown from rural farmland into one of the county's largest residential markets. The community takes its name from the Siouan-speaking people who inhabited the area before European settlement, and the name is believed to derive from words meaning 'sweet bay tree' — appropriate for an area whose defining characteristic remains its extraordinary tree coverage. Home to approximately 25,000 residents, Socastee encompasses the Forestbrook neighborhood, the Burning Ridge Golf Club area, the Highway 544 corridor communities, and extends along the ICW to the Myrtle Beach city limits.

City Profile

Socastee at a Glance

Population: ~25,000 residents — making Socastee one of Horry County's largest unincorporated communities by population. Socastee has absorbed significant residential growth as buyers seeking proximity to Myrtle Beach at lower prices have moved into its expanding neighborhoods along the Highway 544 and Highway 501 corridors.

Schools: Socastee High School — home of the Braves — is one of Horry County's largest secondary schools. Socastee Middle School, Forestbrook Middle School, Socastee Elementary, and Forestbrook Elementary serve the community's younger students. Socastee High School has a distinguished athletic tradition and is recognized for its STEM programming within the Horry County Schools district.

Notable facts: The Socastee Swing Bridge — listed on the National Register of Historic Places — crosses the Intracoastal Waterway at Socastee and is one of the last operating swing bridges in South Carolina. The bridge has been a community landmark since 1934. The Socastee Swamp Fox Chapter of the Sons of the American Revolution reflects the community's deep roots in the Lowcountry's Revolutionary War history. Burning Ridge Golf Links has hosted multiple professional and amateur tournaments and is considered one of the Grand Strand's most challenging public golf courses.

About the Market

Property Maintenance in Socastee, SC

Socastee's mature tree canopy is its most distinctive physical feature and its most significant property maintenance driver. The community's dense mix of longleaf pines, loblolly pines, and hardwood species creates a canopy coverage so complete in neighborhoods like Forestbrook that many properties receive dappled light at best. Gutters in heavily canopied Forestbrook properties fill in six to eight weeks year-round — not seasonally — requiring three to four annual cleanings that stand in sharp contrast to the twice-yearly standard that applies to coastal Myrtle Beach without heavy tree coverage. Socastee High School, part of Horry County Schools, serves the community alongside several elementary schools including Socastee Elementary and Forestbrook Elementary. The community is also home to the Socastee Swamp Fox Chapter of the Sons of the American Revolution, reflecting its deep historical roots in the Lowcountry of South Carolina.

Questions About Socastee Property Maintenance

How often do Forestbrook gutters need cleaning?+
Forestbrook properties under heavy pine and oak coverage need gutter cleaning 3–4 times annually. The continuous debris load from the canopy fills gutters in six to eight weeks at peak periods, making quarterly service appropriate for most Forestbrook properties.
Do you do tannin pressure washing in Socastee?+
Yes — pine needle and oak tannin staining on driveways and walkways is one of the most common Socastee pressure washing requests. High-pressure washing effectively removes the dark brown staining that the tree canopy creates.
How much does lawn care cost in Socastee?+
Lawn care in Socastee starts at $50 per visit. Most residential properties run $60–$90 per visit depending on lot size. Free estimates.
Do you manage shade lawns in Forestbrook?+
Yes. Shaded Forestbrook turf requires specific management — appropriate grass species for shade, different mowing heights, and fertilization timing that differs from sun-exposed coastal properties. We apply the correct approach for your specific site conditions.
Is Socastee in your service area?+
Yes — Socastee is part of our core Horry County service area including Forestbrook, Burning Ridge, and all corridor communities.
