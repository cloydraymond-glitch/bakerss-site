Automotive Industry Proven It First

The automotive industry has used 100% solids epoxy in service bays and showrooms since the 1960s. Dealerships demand floors that resist motor oil, transmission fluid, battery acid, and hydraulic fluid while supporting the weight of hydraulic lifts. The specification that meets those demands is the same system we install in Socastee garages.

🏘️

New Construction Socastee Slabs

Socastee's newer construction (many homes from the 1990s–2010s) features slabs in generally better condition than older beach cottage construction. Moisture testing is still required before coating — Horry County's subtropical humidity means vapor transmission exists countywide.

🔬

Diamond Grinding — Same Standard Everywhere

Whether a Socastee home was built in 1990 or 2024, every slab gets diamond ground before coating. The same commercial standard that applies on the beach applies inland. No shortcuts regardless of location.

📍

Inland — But Same Humidity

Socastee doesn't have salt air exposure, but the subtropical humidity and concrete moisture vapor transmission challenges are identical throughout Horry County. The prep process and epoxy specification are the same regardless of location.
