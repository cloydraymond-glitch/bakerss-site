Sod Installation Built for Shade, Sandy Soil and HOA Standards

Socastee sod installation is not just rolling grass over bare dirt. Sod Types

Centipede, Bermuda, Zoysia and St. Augustine Sod in Socastee

Centipede Sod

Lower-Maintenance Warm-Season Turf

Centipede can work well for sandy acidic soils and owners who want a moderate-maintenance lawn when sunlight and drainage are appropriate.

Bermuda Sod

Full-Sun Performance

Bermuda is strong for sunny, high-use areas and newer open lots. It needs frequent mowing and does not perform well in heavy shade.

Zoysia Sod

Dense, Attractive Lawn Option

Zoysia can be a premium option for maintained lawns and some partial-shade areas, but site conditions and cost should be reviewed first.

St. Augustine Sod

Shade-Considered Option

St. Augustine may be considered for certain shade conditions, but water, disease pressure and maintenance expectations must be discussed.

New Sod Aftercare

How to Protect New Sod in Socastee

  • Water consistently during establishment, especially in full-sun areas
  • Avoid heavy foot traffic until the sod has started rooting
  • Do not mow until the sod no longer lifts easily
  • Use sharp mower blades and avoid cutting too low
  • Watch edges, corners and sunny slopes for faster drying
  • Repair irrigation issues quickly before sod dries out
  • Continue professional lawn care after rooting to protect the investment
