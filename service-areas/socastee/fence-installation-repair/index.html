Common fence problems

Fence Problems We Quote in Socastee SC

Fence issues often start small and become more expensive when ignored. Gates begin dragging, posts start moving, panels loosen, boards split, rails fail and storm-damaged sections pull the rest of the fence out of line.

Leaning Fence Sections

Leaning sections are often caused by post movement, rot, wet soil, storm pressure or poor original installation. We identify whether the post, rail, panel or entire section needs attention.

Gate Alignment Issues

A dragging or sticking gate can come from hinge failure, settling posts, warped lumber, latch misalignment or ground clearance issues. Gate repairs are common for rentals and fenced backyards.

Storm & Wind Damage

Socastee properties can see fence damage from wind, saturated ground, falling limbs and storms. We can quote section repair, panel replacement, post replacement and cleanup by approved scope.

Privacy & Rental Property Fences

Privacy fences protect yards, pets, tenants, pools, equipment and outdoor spaces. Rental and HOA properties often need fast visible repairs to keep the property presentable.
