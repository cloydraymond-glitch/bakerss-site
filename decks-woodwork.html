Why It Matters Here

Coastal Conditions Are Hard on Wood

Myrtle Beach's salt air, high humidity, strong UV, and heavy summer rain accelerate wood deterioration faster than almost anywhere else in the country. Regular maintenance and proper sealing aren't optional — they're what keeps a deck safe and structurally sound.

  • 🌊
    Salt Air Penetration

    Salt from the ocean air accelerates corrosion of fasteners and oxidation of wood surfaces, causing graying, cracking, and structural loosening.

  • 💧
    High Humidity & Rain

    Horry County averages over 50 inches of rain per year. Without proper sealing, wood absorbs moisture continuously, leading to rot, warping, and mold growth.

  • ☀️
    Intense UV Exposure

    South Carolina's direct sun breaks down wood fibers and fades stain faster than cooler climates — annual or biannual resealing extends deck life significantly.

  • 🪲
    Insects & Wood-Boring Pests

    Termites, carpenter bees, and other insects are active year-round in coastal SC. Proper sealing and regular inspections are the first line of defense.
