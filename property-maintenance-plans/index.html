Why Maintenance Plans Work

Stop Managing Property Problems One Emergency at a Time

Property maintenance in the Myrtle Beach market is not one service. It is a chain of recurring tasks that all affect the same outcome: whether the property stays clean, safe, functional, presentable and ready for owners, guests, residents or tenants. A vacation rental owner may need a filter changed, a toilet checked, a loose handle fixed, windows cleaned, a dryer vent cleared and patio furniture secured before a storm. A HOA board may need common-area repairs, storm cleanup, pressure washing, pool checks, parking lot touchups and seasonal décor installed. A homeowner may need monthly eyes on the property because they are not local.

A maintenance plan turns that chaos into a managed schedule. Instead of waiting until something fails, Bakerss inspects, documents, coordinates and repairs recurring issues before they become high-cost emergencies. That matters in Horry County because properties face salt air, humidity, high seasonal use, storm exposure, heavy rental turnover, HOA enforcement, irrigation dependence, pool and amenity needs, and a fast-growing construction market where punch-list work is constant.

Property Maintenance Plan Questions

What is included in a Bakerss property maintenance plan?

Plans can include scheduled property checks, handyman repairs, lawn care, pool service, cleaning coordination, HVAC filter replacement, dryer vent cleaning, window cleaning, pressure washing, storm prep, post-storm documentation, irrigation checks, fence repairs, deck repairs, seasonal décor, concrete/asphalt service and other recurring property tasks. The final scope is written before service begins.

How much do property maintenance plans cost in Myrtle Beach?

Basic property check plans may start around $149–$299/month. More complete homeowner and STR plans commonly range higher depending on included services, frequency and property size. HOA, apartment and commercial retainers are custom quoted after a property review.

Do you work with out-of-state property owners?

Yes. Maintenance plans are ideal for out-of-state homeowners and STR owners who need local property checks, photos, repairs, filters, storm prep, cleaning coordination and general property oversight.

Can I include HVAC filter replacement only?

Yes. HVAC filter replacement can be set monthly, bi-monthly or quarterly depending on occupancy, pets, STR use and owner preference. Filter service can also be paired with dryer vent checks, property checks and minor repair reporting.

Do you offer storm prep and post-storm service?

Yes. Storm service can include outdoor furniture securement, drain clearing, shutter assistance, exterior checks, post-storm photos, debris removal, tarping when safe, damage documentation and repair coordination.

Do you offer maintenance plans for HOAs and apartment communities?

Yes. Bakerss builds custom retainers for HOAs, condo associations, apartments, multi-family properties, commercial sites and property managers across Horry County.
