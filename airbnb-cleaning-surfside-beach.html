The Family Beach Difference

Cleaning Surfside Beach Rentals Requires a Different Approach

Surfside Beach bills itself as "The Family Beach" — and its rental market reflects that completely. Unlike Myrtle Beach's high-rise condo towers, Surfside's vacation rentals are overwhelmingly single-family beach houses, raised cottages, duplexes, and residential homes spread across golf-cart-friendly streets running from the oceanfront inland to Highway 17.

That means cleaning here looks fundamentally different. A family of eight in a 4-bedroom raised beach house generates more laundry, more kitchen use, more sand tracked across multi-level floors, more outdoor gear to clean around, and more surfaces to cover than a 2-bedroom condo with an elevator. Surfside's trademark — wide, shallow beaches ideal for kids, golf cart accessibility, and a community-centered atmosphere — brings a guest type who uses every inch of a property.

Surfside is also now home to one of the Grand Strand's most exciting new attractions: a brand-new $20 million all-concrete fishing pier that opened in March 2024 on the town's 60th birthday. The pier — the only structure of its kind on the South Carolina coast — is becoming the centerpiece of Surfside's identity and driving increased interest in nearby vacation rentals.

Garden City Beach

Directly south of Surfside, Garden City Beach runs to the mouth of Murrells Inlet. Properties here draw guests who want beach access plus the Murrells Inlet seafood scene.
