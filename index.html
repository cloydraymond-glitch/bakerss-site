Frequently asked questions

Bakerss Property Services — Common Questions

What areas does Bakerss Property Services serve?

Bakerss serves Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Murrells Inlet, Garden City, Socastee, Conway, Carolina Forest, Pawleys Island, Litchfield Beach, Horry County and Georgetown County.

What property maintenance services does Bakerss provide?

Bakerss provides White Glove estate care, HVAC repair and preventive maintenance, weekly interior cleaning, lawn and grounds care, pool maintenance, plumbing and electrical work-order support, handyman repairs, vacation-home care, apartment maintenance, HOA maintenance and commercial property maintenance.

Does Bakerss offer HOA and apartment maintenance?

Yes. Bakerss supports HOA communities, apartment communities, condo communities and managed properties with common-area cleaning, grounds maintenance, pool-area support, work orders, repairs, dryer vent cleaning, pressure washing and recurring maintenance plans.

Does Bakerss help vacation rental and second-home owners?

Yes. Bakerss supports vacation rentals, Airbnb properties and second homes with turnover cleaning, home watch, storm checks, lawn care, pool maintenance, handyman repairs, pressure washing, window cleaning and exterior maintenance.

Does Bakerss provide HVAC repair and preventive maintenance?

Yes. Bakerss provides HVAC diagnostics, repair, preventive maintenance, monthly system checks and qualifying emergency-service availability through experienced HVAC technicians. Service scope depends on the system, property and licensing requirements.

What can clients see in the Bakerss Client Portal?

Recurring clients can review documented technician arrival and departure times, field notes, property photographs, work-order activity and service history.

Does Bakerss offer a complete care plan for luxury homes?

Yes. White Glove Estate Care is designed for premium homes and second homes needing weekly interior cleaning, full grounds maintenance, HVAC preventive maintenance, property inspections and priority access to repair technicians.

How do I request a quote from Bakerss Property Services?

You can request a quote online at bakerss.com/get-quote/, call or text 843-467-7136, or email info@bakerss.com with the property address, service needs, photos if available and whether you need one-time or recurring maintenance.

How much does property maintenance cost in Myrtle Beach?

Costs vary by service type and property size. Bakerss provides free quotes for all services including lawn care, cleaning, pool maintenance, HOA maintenance, apartment maintenance and commercial maintenance across Horry County SC. Call or text 843-467-7136 for a no-obligation quote.

Does Bakerss serve Conway and Carolina Forest?

Yes. Bakerss Property Services serves Conway and Carolina Forest with lawn care, landscaping, cleaning, pool maintenance, handyman services, HOA maintenance, apartment maintenance and commercial property maintenance. Visit the Conway or Carolina Forest city pages for full details.
