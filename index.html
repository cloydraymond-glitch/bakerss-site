What is the difference between a maintenance plan and a one-time service?

A one-time service is a single visit for a specific task such as a deep clean, sod installation or pressure washing job. A recurring maintenance plan is a scheduled contract that bundles multiple services — such as lawn care, pool maintenance and cleaning — on a weekly, biweekly or monthly basis at a set rate. Maintenance plans are available for homeowners, HOAs, vacation rental owners and commercial property managers.
