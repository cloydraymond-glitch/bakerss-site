Premium full-service property care

One Property. One Call. Complete Care.

Bakerss Property Services LLC is a veteran- and family-owned, Myrtle Beach Area Chamber of Commerce member serving discerning homeowners, second-home owners and managed properties across the Grand Strand.

Coastal property ownership creates overlapping maintenance demands: air-conditioning load, humidity, storm exposure, salt and sand, fast landscape growth, pool care, cleaning, tenant or guest issues and ongoing repair work. Bakerss is structured to coordinate those needs through one local property-services relationship.

Our field capability includes HVAC technicians, Certified Pool Operators, plumbers, electricians, cleaning teams, grounds crews and property-maintenance technicians. Where a scope requires a particular license, permit or specialty credential, the work is assigned or coordinated accordingly.

The operational difference is visibility: recurring clients can submit work orders, follow status from scheduled → in progress → proposal submitted → completed, review live job photos and technician notes, and retain service history for owner, HOA, management or reporting needs.
