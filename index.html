FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Bakerss Property Maintenance and our services in Horry County, SC.

What cities does Bakerss Property Maintenance serve?
+
We serve all of Horry County, SC — including Myrtle Beach, Murrells Inlet, Conway, Surfside Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Garden City, Socastee, Carolina Forest, Forestbrook, Little River, and surrounding areas. If you're in Horry County, give us a call.
How much does lawn care cost in Myrtle Beach, SC?
+
Lawn care starts at $50 for standard residential yards in Myrtle Beach and surrounding areas. The final price depends on yard size, terrain, frequency (weekly vs bi-weekly), and add-ons like edging, trimming, and blowing. We offer free estimates — just call or text (843) 467-7136.
How much does pressure washing cost in Myrtle Beach?
+
Pressure washing services start at $125 in the Myrtle Beach and Horry County area. Driveways, siding, decks, patios, roofs, and full house washes all vary in price. Soft washing is also available for delicate surfaces. We'll give you a clear quote before any work begins.
Do you clean Airbnb and vacation rental properties?
+
Absolutely. Vacation rental turnover cleaning is one of our most popular services. We handle same-day turnovers, linen changes, restocking, and can coordinate directly with your rental management platform. Many Myrtle Beach and Grand Strand rental owners rely on us for the entire season.
Are you available for emergency or same-day service?
+
Yes — we answer our phone 24/7. Emergency cleaning, last-minute job prep, and same-day availability are all part of how we operate. Call or text us at (843) 467-7136 any time.
Are you licensed and insured in South Carolina?
+
Yes. Bakerss Property Maintenance is fully licensed and insured for all services offered in South Carolina. Your property and peace of mind are protected on every job.
Can you handle multiple services at once — like lawn care AND pressure washing?
+
That's our specialty. Many of our clients use us for 3–5+ services on their property. We coordinate everything together so you're not juggling multiple contractors. One call, one team, one relationship.
How often should I pressure wash my home in Myrtle Beach?
+
Most Myrtle Beach and coastal SC homeowners should pressure wash their home exterior at least once a year — ideally in spring before tourist season. The combination of humidity, salt air, pollen, and mildew means homes here build up grime significantly faster than inland properties.
