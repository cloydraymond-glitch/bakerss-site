About Bakerss

Coastal Property Care Built on Reliability & Trust

Coastal properties in Myrtle Beach and Horry County face challenges that inland properties don't — salt air, fast-growing landscaping, heavy guest turnover, humidity, storms, and sand. Bakerss Property Maintenance is built specifically to help property owners stay ahead of those challenges with consistent, reliable local service.

We serve homeowners who want more than a quick mow or a basic clean. Our clients value appearance, responsiveness, and peace of mind — and that's exactly what we deliver, whether you're full-time, part-time, or managing rentals from out of state.

  • Veteran & family owned — licensed, bonded, and insured

  • Strong fit for second homes & remote property owners
