Who We Are

One Local Team for Complete Property Maintenance

Bakerss Property Maintenance helps property owners reduce the frustration of managing multiple vendors. Instead of hiring one contractor for lawn care, another for pressure washing, another for cleaning, another for pool maintenance, and another for general repairs, our clients can work with one local team that understands the property and the standard expected.

That matters in Myrtle Beach and Horry County. Coastal properties deal with humidity, salt exposure, mildew growth, storm debris, seasonal rental traffic, fast-growing lawns, pool usage, guest turnover, and property presentation demands. Bakerss is structured to support those real local conditions.

Our clients include local homeowners, out-of-state investors, short-term rental owners, property managers, HOA boards, condominium associations, apartment communities and commercial property owners.
