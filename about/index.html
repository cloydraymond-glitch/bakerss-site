Our Story

Meet Ray & Courtney Baker

Bakerss Property Maintenance was founded by Ray and Courtney Baker with one simple mission: "We take care of your property so you can enjoy life."

As Horry County locals, Ray and Courtney understand the unique demands of coastal South Carolina properties — the salt air, the humidity, the pine pollen, the hurricane prep, and the joy of a beautifully maintained coastal home.

What started as a commitment to quality has grown into a full-service property maintenance company trusted by hundreds of homeowners, vacation rental owners, and businesses across the Grand Strand.

When you call Bakerss, you're not calling a call center or a franchise. You're calling Ray and Courtney directly — and they answer 24/7.
