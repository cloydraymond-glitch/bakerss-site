Who We Are

More than a vendor. A property-services partner.

Bakerss Property Services LLC is a veteran- and family-owned property maintenance company based in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. We serve homeowners, second-home owners, rental properties, HOAs, apartment communities, commercial properties and property-management teams throughout the Grand Strand.

Coastal properties rarely have just one maintenance need. Air-conditioning demand, humidity, storms, landscaping, pools, cleaning, tenant or guest issues and everyday repairs often overlap. Bakerss is structured to coordinate those needs through one dependable local relationship.

Our goal is straightforward: make property maintenance easier to manage, easier to document and easier to trust. That means clear communication, professional field service, documented work and a team that understands the property—not just the individual work order.
