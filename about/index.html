Our Story

Built From the Ground Up in Horry County

Ray and Courtney Cloyd started Bakerss Property Maintenance with a simple belief: the Grand Strand deserves a property maintenance company that actually shows up, does the job right, and treats every property like it's their own. Growing up in and around Horry County, they knew the market — the vacation rental owners who can't afford a missed turnover, the homeowners fighting coastal mildew season after season, the out-of-state investors who need a local team they can trust without being on-site.

What started as a lawn care and cleaning operation quickly grew as clients kept asking "can you do this too?" — epoxy garage floors, painting, pressure washing, handyman work, flooring, landscaping, pool maintenance, sod installation, junk removal. The answer was always yes. Today, Bakerss offers 18+ services and is one of Horry County's most versatile property maintenance teams, with the crew, equipment, and systems to handle nearly anything a property needs.

The name says it all: Bakerss — that extra S is intentional. It's not just one Baker. It's a team, a family, and a commitment to the communities they grew up in and still call home.
