Our Mission

We Take Care of Your Property So You Can Enjoy Life

Ray and Courtney Cloyd started Bakerss Property Maintenance with one simple mission: make property ownership easier for Horry County homeowners, vacation rental owners, and commercial property managers who want consistent, professional results without the hassle of managing multiple vendors.

As Horry County locals, Ray and Courtney understand the unique demands of coastal South Carolina properties — from salt air and hurricane prep to the rapid growth rates of coastal lawns and the turnover demands of vacation rentals in one of the country's most active short-term rental markets.

Whether you own a beach cottage in Surfside, a waterfront home in Murrells Inlet, or a commercial property in Conway — every Bakerss client receives the same level of care and attention that Ray and Courtney bring to their own property.
