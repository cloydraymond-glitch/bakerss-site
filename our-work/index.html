Working with Bakerss

Planning your next service visit

Do you serve residential and commercial properties?

Yes. Bakerss supports homeowners, vacation rentals, apartments, HOAs and commercial properties. Share your property type and the work needed so we can review the scope.

Can I include several maintenance needs in one request?

Yes. Include the HVAC, plumbing, repairs, cleaning or recurring property-maintenance needs you want reviewed. Our team will discuss a coordinated scope and the next steps.

What should I send with a quote request?

Include the property location, photos, the issue or work needed, access details and your preferred timing. Call or text 843-467-7136 or request service online.

Are these photos from actual Bakerss projects?

The gallery is presented as jobsite documentation from Bakerss service work. Individual project descriptions explain what the photographs show, including when a result cannot be established from the images alone.

Can you help with several units or a commercial property?

Yes. Share the property type, number of units, work-order needs and access requirements. We can discuss a coordinated maintenance scope for apartments, HOAs and commercial properties.

Do you provide before-and-after photos for every job?

Photo documentation may be available depending on the service agreement and the work performed. Ask about photo reporting when requesting a quote.

Can I request work similar to a gallery project?

Yes. Use the related service links or send the project name, your property location, photos and a description of the issue through the quote form.
