When a family in Conway calls because their lawn hasn't been cut in three weeks and the HOA left a notice, we show up the same day. When a Myrtle Beach STR investor calls at 9 PM because guests arrive tomorrow and the kitchen hasn't been cleaned since the last checkout, Ray answers personally. When a Surfside Beach homeowner has watched their deck rot for two summers and finally decides to replace it, we give them a free estimate and a start date they can count on.

The photos above are the record of those calls. Every project happened in Horry County — on real streets in real communities, for real homeowners who trusted us with their properties. We don't use stock photography on this page. What you see is what we've built, cleaned, painted, and maintained.

Move-Out Cleaning in Murrells Inlet — Links Brook

Residential move-out cleaning is one of the most detail-intensive services we provide. The June 2026 Links Brook job in Murrells Inlet required a full top-to-bottom clean of every surface a landlord or property manager will inspect — refrigerator interior, crisper drawers, kitchen tile floors, upstairs loft carpet, bathroom toilet and tub, and the loft landing carpet. Landlord-ready means exactly that: clean enough that a tenant inspection passes without dispute and a new occupant can move in without doing any cleaning themselves.

Lawn Care in Myrtle Beach — The Standard Our Clients Set

Myrtle Beach's 15 million annual visitors and 30,000-plus active vacation rental listings create the highest-stakes lawn care market on the South Carolina Grand Strand. A lawn that looks neglected in a listing photo costs bookings. A yard that generates an HOA violation notice in Carolina Forest or Market Common creates headaches that the out-of-state property owner has to manage from across the country. We mow to a consistent height, edge every visit, and blow every hard surface clean before we leave. The stripes in the photos above aren't accidental — they're trained technique applied consistently on every single visit.

In Conway, the demands are different. The Waccamaw River corridor, Wild Wing Plantation's fairway-adjacent properties, and the CCU rental market all create distinct lawn care requirements. We've served them long enough to know which properties have live oak roots under the surface, which HOAs require specific mowing heights, and which rental properties need same-day service before a tenant transition.

Pressure Washing the Grand Strand

Salt air is the dominant variable in every pressure washing decision along the Grand Strand. A driveway in Surfside Beach that hasn't been washed in two years has two years of salt deposit, biological growth, and UV weathering — visible as the gray-green staining that makes a property look a decade older than it is. We use surface cleaners for flat concrete and pavers and we adjust pressure and temperature for each surface type. The transformation on a Myrtle Beach paver driveway is one of the most immediate visual improvements we produce in any service category.

Deck Building in Conway and Murrells Inlet

The before-and-after deck photos represent some of the most dramatic improvements we've completed. The Conway before photos — lumber staged on a bare yard, the old rotted structure removed — and the after photos showing the completed PT wood deck with dual staircases tell the complete story of what a well-executed deck replacement delivers. In Murrells Inlet, the transformation from a deteriorated painted entry structure to a full-width front deck represents exactly the kind of work that Linksbrook and Prince Creek generate — older construction that has reached the end of its useful life and needs skilled replacement.

Cleaning — The Airbnb Economy and the Family Beach Standard

The Bakerss cleaning team has cleaned properties from Myrtle Beach oceanfront condos to Surfside Beach Family Beach cottages to Murrells Inlet rental homes near the Marshwalk. The STR cleaning market along the Grand Strand operates at a pace and standard that residential cleaning services built for primary residences aren't designed to handle. The family returning to a Surfside cottage they've rented for nine consecutive summers will notice if the outdoor shower hasn't been properly cleaned or if the kitchen doesn't match the listing standard.
