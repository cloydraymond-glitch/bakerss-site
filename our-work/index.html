Thermostat connectivity observations

Myrtle Beach, South Carolina

These jobsite photographs show thermostat displays reporting a failed connection and an offline status. They document the symptoms visible at the time of the photos. A connectivity message alone does not establish the underlying cause or confirm that the heating and cooling equipment has failed.

Thermostat concerns can be included in an HVAC service request so the technician can review the controls, equipment response and appropriate next steps.
