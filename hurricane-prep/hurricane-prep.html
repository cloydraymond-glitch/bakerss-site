The Problem

Horry County Gets Hit. Are You Prepared?

The Grand Strand averages a significant tropical system every 2–3 years, and near-miss events cause property damage every single hurricane season. Coastal salt air, high humidity, and storm surge from the Atlantic create year-round property maintenance challenges — and a scramble every June when hurricane season opens. Bakerss is your one call for everything from pre-season prep to post-storm restoration.
