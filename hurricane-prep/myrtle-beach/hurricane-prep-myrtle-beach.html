Pre-Storm Checklist

Hurricane Prep Checklist for Myrtle Beach Properties

What every Myrtle Beach homeowner and property manager should do before a named storm. Bakerss can handle every item on this list — call us before the weather window closes.

1

Inspect & Clear Gutters

Clogged gutters cause overflow and fascia damage during heavy rain. Clear all debris before storm season.

2

Secure Outdoor Furniture

Unsecured chairs, tables, and planters become projectiles in high winds. Store or anchor everything.

3

Trim Trees & Overhanging Limbs

Dead branches and overhanging limbs are the leading cause of storm damage to roofs in Horry County.

4

Check Roof & Flashing

Loose shingles and compromised flashing fail fast in high winds. Identify and repair before a storm hits.

5

Install Storm Shutters or Board Windows

Physical protection from wind-driven debris is the most critical step for coastal SC properties.

6

Clear Downspouts & Drainage

Blocked drainage systems turn minor rain into major flooding. Clear all paths before storm season.

7

Document Property Condition

Photo and video documentation before a storm is essential for insurance claims after.

8

Identify Post-Storm Vendors

Know who you're calling for debris cleanup, roof tarping, and pressure washing before the storm hits.
