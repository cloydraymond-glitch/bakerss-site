Built around your property

More complete than basic lawn service.

Covington Lake and Covington Lake East homes often combine high-visibility streetscapes with lake-influenced moisture patterns. Bakerss builds service around the actual lot—front elevation, side-yard drainage, rear lawn exposure, irrigation performance and outdoor living areas—rather than using a generic neighborhood checklist.

Local maintenance priorities

What should shape the service plan

01

Front-versus-rear conditions

The front lawn may dry quickly while the rear stays soft near lower areas.

02

Irrigation balance

Runtime and coverage should reflect sun, turf type and moisture.

03

Outdoor living upkeep

Patios, pool decks and fences need recurring cleaning and observation.

04

Curb appeal expectations

Edges, beds and concrete remain highly visible in planned communities.

Lot-by-lot planning

Front lawn and rear lawn may need different decisions

Sun exposure, irrigation coverage and proximity to lower areas can create different maintenance conditions on the same property.

Review before replacing turf

  • Confirm sprinkler coverage
  • Document standing water
  • Identify shade and traffic
  • Match sod to site conditions
