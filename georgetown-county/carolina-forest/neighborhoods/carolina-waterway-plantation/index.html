Built around your property

More complete than basic lawn service.

Homes near the Intracoastal Waterway can look immaculate one week and collect mildew, windblown debris or drainage issues after a single stretch of humid weather. Bakerss coordinates the visible details—turf, beds, hardscapes, gutters, exterior surfaces and minor repairs—under one recurring property-care relationship.

Local maintenance priorities

What should shape the service plan

01

Humidity and organic growth

Siding, pavers, fences and shaded entries can discolor quickly.

02

Wind and debris

Fronds, branches and loose material require fast cleanup after storms.

03

Drainage pressure

Low sections and compacted soil can hold water after heavy rain.

04

Owner coordination

Second homes and gated properties benefit from clear access and reporting.

Waterfront maintenance sequence

Inspect, clean, protect and report

Waterway exposure creates fast-changing conditions. Recurring visits should address both presentation and developing maintenance concerns.

  • Check storm and wind debris
  • Observe low or saturated turf
  • Monitor mildew-prone surfaces
  • Report visible exterior concerns
