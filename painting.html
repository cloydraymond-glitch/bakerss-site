Painting Myrtle Beach SC

Painting Built for Coastal Properties

The coastal climate in Myrtle Beach and Horry County is demanding on painted surfaces. Salt air, UV exposure, and high humidity cause paint to fade, peel, and blister faster than inland areas — especially on exterior surfaces. Interior surfaces in vacation rentals take constant abuse from heavy guest turnover. Bakerss Property Maintenance delivers painting that holds up, looks sharp, and is done with the right materials for coastal conditions.

Whether you need a single room refreshed before the next rental guest, a full exterior repaint before listing, or a commercial space updated for business — we handle it with professional standards every time.

  • Interior painting — walls, ceilings, trim & baseboards
  • Exterior painting — siding, trim, fascia & doors
  • Touch-up & refresh painting for vacation rentals
  • Pre-listing & pre-sale property painting
  • Commercial interior & exterior painting
  • Coastal-grade materials rated for salt air & humidity
