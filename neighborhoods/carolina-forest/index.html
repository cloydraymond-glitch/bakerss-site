Turf science in Carolina Forest

Zoysia, Bermuda & St. Augustine — The Three Turf Types You Will Find in Carolina Forest

Carolina Forest was developed largely in the early 2000s through the 2010s, and the sod choices made during those phases still dictate how lawns need to be managed today. Zoysia was the dominant installation choice for many communities — particularly The Farm, Berkshire Forest and Waterbridge. It offers a dense, attractive stand but needs specific management: mowing heights between 1–2 inches, lighter fertilizer applications than Bermuda and a slightly later pre-emergent window because of its slower spring green-up compared to coastal Bermuda.

Bermuda appears in sunnier lots and newer construction phases throughout Plantation Lakes and surrounding communities. It responds well to the warm Horry County growing season but requires timely pre-emergent application — targeting February 1–20 for inland Carolina Forest areas — and more frequent mowing during summer peak growth.

St. Augustine shows up in shadier lots where Bermuda struggles to thrive. It tolerates partial shade better but is sensitive to scalping and needs to be kept at 3–4 inches to maintain root depth. Misidentifying turf type and mowing too short causes thinning that recovery from takes a full growing season.

Bakerss Carolina Forest lawn care programs are built around identifying what is actually growing on your lot and managing it correctly — not applying a generic Grand Strand schedule to every property we touch.

Pre-Emergent Timing for Carolina Forest

✓Inland CF communities: first pre-emergent Feb 1–20 (slightly later than coastal)
✓Second application 6–8 weeks later for full season coverage
✓Bermuda: target soil temp above 50–55°F consistently
✓Zoysia: slightly delayed green-up; do not rush first mow
✓St. Augustine: avoid pre-emergents labeled unsafe for SA

Carolina Forest Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss serve all Carolina Forest subdivisions?

Yes. Bakerss serves all major Carolina Forest communities including The Farm, Berkshire Forest, Waterbridge, Plantation Lakes, Covington Lake, The Bluffs on the Waterway, Stonegate, Emmens Preserve, Myrtle Trace and surrounding subdivisions throughout the Carolina Forest area.

What grass types are common in Carolina Forest?

Carolina Forest lawns are predominantly Zoysia in communities established during the 2000s development boom. Bermuda appears in newer construction and sunnier lots. St. Augustine is found in shadier properties. Bakerss manages all three turf types with species-specific schedules.

Do you do HOA common area maintenance in Carolina Forest?

Yes. Bakerss provides recurring HOA common area maintenance including grounds mowing, entry landscaping, bed maintenance, sidewalk pressure washing and seasonal mulching for Carolina Forest community associations.

Can Bakerss install sod in Carolina Forest?

Yes. Bakerss installs Bermuda, Zoysia, St. Augustine and Centipede sod throughout Carolina Forest. Sod installation quotes start at $3,500 depending on square footage, grass type selection, soil prep requirements and whether existing turf removal is needed.

How is pressure washing different for Carolina Forest vs. coastal neighborhoods?

Carolina Forest properties are inland, so salt air is less of a factor. However, heavy tree canopy across many older CF sections produces significant pollen, organic staining, algae on north-facing surfaces and moss on driveways that pressure washing addresses effectively — particularly in spring after pollen season and after fall leaf drop.
