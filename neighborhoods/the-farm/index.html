The Farm community context

What Sets The Farm Apart From Other Carolina Forest HOA Communities

The Farm at Carolina Forest is one of the largest single HOA communities within the broader Carolina Forest area. It has its own pool, clubhouse, walking trails and internal street network — and a homeowner base that is primarily year-round families, not seasonal visitors or investment buyers.

Zoysia Dominant TurfThe Farm was sodded primarily with Zoysia during its development phase. Zoysia requires mowing at 1.5 to 2 inches, lighter fertilization than Bermuda and a slightly later pre-emergent window that Bakerss accounts for in all Carolina Forest service schedules.
Year-Round Family DemographicsThe Farm skews heavily toward year-round families who use their yards actively. Maintenance needs to keep up between visits and during busy school and work seasons — not fall behind when owners are distracted.
HOA Monitoring Is ConsistentThe Farm HOA monitors lawn height, bed condition, landscape upkeep and exterior appearance on a consistent schedule. Notices come in writing with correction deadlines.
Walking Trail Rear VisibilityThe Farm's walking trail system passes behind and alongside many residential lots. Back-yard landscape beds, rear patios and side-yard conditions are visible to neighbors and walkers — not just to street-facing inspection.
Spring Pollen SeasonCarolina Forest's inland tree canopy dumps heavy pollen on driveways, patios and walkways every spring. Pressure washing after pollen season is one of the most consistent service requests from The Farm homeowners.
Storm Season Gutter LoadingThe Farm's interior tree coverage creates significant gutter debris after fall leaf drop and tropical weather events. Pre-storm and post-storm gutter cleaning is a seasonal priority for homes under tree canopy.

Common questions

The Farm at Carolina Forest Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss serve The Farm at Carolina Forest?

Yes. Bakerss provides lawn care, landscaping, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, pool maintenance and HOA maintenance for The Farm at Carolina Forest homeowners. The Farm is within our Carolina Forest service area and is a community we maintain on recurring schedules.

What grass is at The Farm?

The Farm was developed primarily with Zoysia sod. Zoysia is dense, attractive and handles The Farm's mix of sun and partial shade well — but it requires mowing at 1.5 to 2 inches and lighter fertilization inputs than Bermuda. Over-mowing or over-fertilizing Zoysia causes it to thin and open to weed pressure.

How does The Farm HOA enforcement work?

The Farm has an active HOA that issues written notices for lawn height violations, overgrown landscape beds, unkempt shrubs and exterior appearance issues. Notices include correction deadlines. Bakerss prevents those notices by maintaining properties on a schedule that stays ahead of inspection rather than reacting after a notice arrives.

Do you handle driveway pressure washing after pollen season at The Farm?

Yes. Spring pollen season in Carolina Forest deposits significant yellow coating on driveways, patios, sidewalks and pool decks throughout The Farm. Pressure washing after peak pollen — typically April through early May — is one of the most common service requests. Bakerss can schedule this as a one-time service or include it in a recurring seasonal plan.

Can Bakerss provide a quote for The Farm lawn care and landscaping together?

Yes. Bakerss quotes lawn care and landscaping together for The Farm homeowners and can bundle them with pressure washing and gutter cleaning into one monthly maintenance plan. Request a quote at bakerss.com/get-quote/ or call 843-467-7136.
