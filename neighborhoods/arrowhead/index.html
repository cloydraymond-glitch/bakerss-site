Arrowhead community context

Lawn Care & Landscaping Built for Arrowhead Golf Community Standards

Arrowhead is a golf course community in Myrtle Beach with active HOA enforcement. Properties sit along and around a golf course environment where lawn condition, bed definition and exterior cleanliness carry more visual weight than in standard subdivisions. Bakerss understands that context.

HOA Compliance Is the Baseline, Not the Goal

In Arrowhead, meeting HOA standards is the floor — not the ceiling. Properties here benefit from lawn and landscape maintenance that consistently exceeds minimum requirements. Sharp edges, defined bed borders, clean driveways and trimmed shrubs all contribute to the overall community appearance that keeps property values stable and HOA boards satisfied. Bakerss Myrtle Beach lawn care targets that standard on every visit.

Bermuda Grass Needs Precise Management

Most Arrowhead lawns are Bermuda grass, which thrives in the full-sun conditions that golf course-adjacent lots typically provide. Bermuda responds well to the Myrtle Beach growing season but requires proper pre-emergent timing — first application targeting February 1–15 for coastal Horry County — and consistent mowing at the right height (1–1.5 inches) during peak season to maintain density and prevent weed pressure from overtaking dormant areas.

Driveways and Entries Get More Scrutiny Here

In a golf course community, the driveway, front walkway and entry area are visible from the street and from neighboring properties with far more frequency than in typical subdivisions. Pollen staining, organic debris and algae on concrete surfaces stand out in this environment. Seasonal pressure washing keeps those high-visibility surfaces looking maintained throughout the year.

Gutter Issues After Storms and Fall

Arrowhead's mature tree canopy — a feature of established golf course community landscaping — produces significant gutter debris in fall and after tropical weather events. Clogged gutters cause fascia damage, foundation erosion and exterior staining on Arrowhead homes that are otherwise well-maintained. Seasonal gutter cleaning prevents that damage from occurring quietly between owner visits or contractor inspections.

Most Arrowhead driveways benefit from pressure washing one to two times per year — typically after spring pollen season and before winter.
