Why Prince Creek is different

The Largest Master-Planned Community in Southern Horry County Needs More Than One Service Approach

Prince Creek spans multiple phases of development built across nearly two decades, which means the turf types, tree maturity and soil conditions vary significantly between sub-neighborhoods. TideWater lots planted in the early 2000s may carry mature Bermuda stands that need different management than a Longwood or Prince Creek West lot with newer Zoysia sod installed in the 2010s development phase.

Centipede grass also appears throughout some Murrells Inlet sections of Prince Creek — particularly in shadier lots where Bermuda cannot maintain density. Centipede is low-fertilizer-need turf that declines rapidly when treated with high-nitrogen programs intended for Bermuda. Identifying what is actually growing on the lot before applying a chemical program is the difference between a healthy lawn and a thinning stand that takes a full growing season to recover.

Prince Creek's pond system and mature tree canopy also create specific maintenance challenges. Pond banks accumulate debris that affects HOA common areas. Tree roots stress driveways and sidewalks. Leaf drop after fall — particularly from the hardwood species common in established Prince Creek lots — fills gutters faster than many homeowners anticipate. Bakerss Murrells Inlet lawn care and gutter cleaning programs account for these community-specific conditions rather than applying a generic coastal schedule across a 3,000-acre master plan.

Prince Creek Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss serve all Prince Creek sub-communities?

Yes. Bakerss serves TideWater at Prince Creek, Harmony at Prince Creek, Bellwood Landing, Hunters Ridge, Highwood, Longwood, Cameron Village, Prince Creek West and surrounding sub-neighborhoods throughout the Prince Creek master-planned community in Murrells Inlet.

Do you provide HOA common area maintenance in Prince Creek?

Yes. Prince Creek has multiple active HOA sub-associations each managing their own common areas, entry corridors, amenity pools and community grounds. Bakerss provides recurring common area maintenance contracts for Prince Creek community associations including grounds mowing, landscaping, pool service and exterior cleaning.

What grass types are in Prince Creek?

Prince Creek lawns include Bermuda in older full-sun phases, Zoysia in many mid-2000s to 2010s installations, Centipede in shadier lots — particularly near pond edges — and occasional St. Augustine in high-shade areas. Each requires a different management approach. Bakerss identifies what is growing before applying chemical programs.

Can Bakerss install sod in Prince Creek?

Yes. Bakerss installs Bermuda, Zoysia, St. Augustine and Centipede sod in Prince Creek. New construction phases in Prince Creek West and bare-area replacements throughout the community are common installation scenarios. Sod quotes start at $3,500.

How does tree canopy affect maintenance in Prince Creek?

Prince Creek's mature tree canopy — particularly in older phases like TideWater and Harmony — creates significant gutter debris in fall, organic staining on driveways and patio surfaces, shade stress on Bermuda lawns and pond bank debris accumulation in HOA common areas. A maintenance schedule that accounts for these factors prevents problems from compounding between service visits.
