Neighborhood SEO standards

Keep Local Pages Useful, Distinct & Hierarchical

The neighborhood directory is valuable only when the pages underneath it are meaningfully different. Avoid publishing near-duplicate city × service × neighborhood pages that exist primarily to swap place names.

Parent City Ownership

Every neighborhood page should clearly link to and support its parent city/service-area hub.

Real Community Context

Use verifiable community characteristics, property patterns, maintenance considerations and relevant service needs rather than generic keyword paragraphs.

Commercial Bridge

Every local page should lead into an actual service, proof page, review page, completed-work page or quote path.
