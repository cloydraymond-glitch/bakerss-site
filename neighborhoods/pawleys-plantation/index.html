What Makes Pawleys Plantation Different From Other Grand Strand Communities

Pawleys Plantation sits in Georgetown County — not Horry County — on a Jack Nicklaus Signature Golf Course. That geography, prestige and HOA structure creates specific maintenance demands that generic Myrtle Beach service companies often underserve.

Georgetown County Turf ConditionsGeorgetown County's slightly different humidity and soil profile — heavier clay content than parts of Horry County — affects how Bermuda and Centipede turf respond to fertilization and pre-emergent applications.
Jack Nicklaus Golf EnvironmentProperties adjacent to championship golf maintain higher visual expectations from residents, guests and the golf course itself. Lawn condition and landscape definition face more scrutiny in this environment.
Active Gated HOA EnforcementPawleys Plantation HOA enforces lawn height, landscape condition, exterior appearance and property standards with real oversight. The gate protects values — and the HOA protects that investment actively.
Seasonal & Absentee OwnersA large portion of Pawleys Plantation homeowners are retirees or second-home owners visiting seasonally. Properties need year-round maintenance management without the owner present to direct it.
Tidal Marsh & Coastal ProximityPawleys Plantation's location near the Pawleys Island barrier island and adjacent marshes creates salt air and moisture exposure that affects exterior surfaces and accelerates algae growth on hard surfaces.
Underserved by Larger CompetitorsMost national and semi-national property maintenance companies focus on Myrtle Beach proper. Georgetown County communities like Pawleys Plantation are often underserved — which is where Bakerss fills the gap.

Common questions

Pawleys Plantation Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss serve Georgetown County including Pawleys Plantation?

Yes. Bakerss serves Georgetown County as a primary service area including Pawleys Plantation, Pawleys Island, Litchfield Beach and surrounding communities. Georgetown County is covered alongside our Horry County service territory.

What grass types are common at Pawleys Plantation?

Pawleys Plantation lawns are predominantly Bermuda and Centipede grass. Centipede is more common in Georgetown County than in northern Horry County — it is a low-maintenance warm-season grass that performs well in the area's soil conditions but requires very specific fertilization care. Over-fertilizing Centipede with high-nitrogen products causes decline. Bakerss applies Centipede-safe programs rather than a generic fertilization schedule.

Can Bakerss manage my Pawleys Plantation property while I am away?

Yes. Bundled monthly maintenance plans for Pawleys Plantation absentee and seasonal owners are one of the most common requests Bakerss receives from Georgetown County property owners. One contract covers lawn care, landscaping, pool maintenance and pressure washing on a schedule the owner does not have to manage actively.

Do you work with the Pawleys Plantation HOA board?

Bakerss builds all service schedules around Pawleys Plantation HOA compliance windows and architectural standards. We can also provide direct contracts to the community association for common area grounds maintenance, entry corridor landscaping, amenity pool service and exterior cleaning.

How is Pawleys Plantation different from Myrtle Beach communities for maintenance purposes?

Georgetown County has slightly heavier clay soil profiles than much of Horry County, which affects drainage, fertilization uptake and turf recovery after stress. The presence of Centipede grass — less common in Myrtle Beach proper — requires different chemical programs. Coastal marsh proximity creates a different salt and moisture exposure profile. And the community's distance from Myrtle Beach means it is underserved by many companies that focus their routes and schedules on higher-volume Horry County markets.
