Plantation Lakes community context

One of Carolina Forest's Largest Communities With Multiple Phases and Varying Maintenance Needs

Plantation Lakes stretches across a large footprint in the Carolina Forest area, developed in multiple phases that introduced different home sizes, lot configurations and turf types over time. What is true for a 2005 Plantation Lakes lot may not be true for a 2018 construction phase in the same community.

Multi-Phase Turf VariationOlder Plantation Lakes phases tend to have established Zoysia. Newer phases may carry Bermuda sod with aggressive summer growth that needs more frequent mowing during peak season. Bakerss identifies which species is on each lot before applying a management program.
Lake System ThroughoutPlantation Lakes is named for its internal lake network. Lake-adjacent lots experience elevated moisture, faster algae growth on hard surfaces and rear-yard visibility considerations.
Active HOA Across All PhasesPlantation Lakes HOA monitors compliance across all phases — older established sections and newer construction phases alike. Entry corridors, common area landscaping, pond banks and lot-level appearance all fall under HOA oversight.
New Construction Sod NeedsActive new construction phases create consistent demand for sod installation, new landscape establishment and first-year turf care programs. New homeowners frequently need lawn establishment support before transitioning to standard recurring maintenance.
Large Common Area FootprintPlantation Lakes has a significant common area footprint — entry monuments, internal road corridors, lake banks, walking paths and amenity areas — that the community association manages.
Spring Pollen and Pressure WashingPlantation Lakes driveways, patios and hard surfaces accumulate significant pollen staining each spring. Seasonal pressure washing after peak pollen is a consistent service request across all phases of the community.

Plantation Lakes Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss serve all phases of Plantation Lakes?

Yes. Bakerss provides lawn care, landscaping, HOA maintenance, pressure washing, sod installation and pool maintenance throughout all Plantation Lakes phases in the Carolina Forest area — from established older sections to active new construction phases.

What grass types are in Plantation Lakes?

Plantation Lakes has both Bermuda and Zoysia across its phases, depending on when each section was developed. Older phases tend toward established Zoysia. Newer construction often carries Bermuda sod. Bakerss identifies the species on each lot before applying a maintenance program.

Can Bakerss install sod in new Plantation Lakes construction lots?

Yes. New construction lots in active Plantation Lakes development phases frequently need sod installation, soil preparation and first-year turf care programs. Bakerss provides Bermuda, Zoysia, St. Augustine and Centipede sod installation starting at $3,500.

Does Bakerss provide HOA common area maintenance for Plantation Lakes?

Yes. Bakerss can provide recurring common area maintenance for the Plantation Lakes community association including entry corridor landscaping, internal road grounds mowing, lake bank edge care and common sidewalk cleaning on HOA-specified schedules.

How does lake proximity affect maintenance in Plantation Lakes?

Lake-adjacent lots experience higher ambient moisture and faster algae establishment on hard surfaces than interior lots. Rear patio and driveway staining develops faster near the lake bank. Bakerss pressure washing schedules and turf management programs account for these moisture differences.
