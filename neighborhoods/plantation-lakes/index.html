Why Plantation Lakes is different

What Makes Plantation Lakes Property Maintenance Unique

Plantation Lakes in Little River is a large-scale master-planned community with extensive waterfront lots, pond-front homes and HOA-maintained common areas. The community's waterfront lots mean many properties have pond bank turf areas requiring specialized mowing approaches on slopes and near water features. Bermuda and Centipede turf are common in Plantation Lakes and require different fertilization schedules than the more acidic pine-heavy lots found in inland Carolina Forest communities. The resort-style amenity pool at Plantation Lakes sees heavy seasonal use, making consistent DHEC-compliant pool maintenance critical for HOA compliance and resident satisfaction.
