Cherry Grove Maintenance Challenges Are Different From Every Other NMB Neighborhood

Cherry Grove sits at the northern tip of North Myrtle Beach, surrounded by the Atlantic Ocean on one side and a network of tidal canals on the other. That geography creates specific property maintenance demands you do not see even two miles inland.

Oceanfront & Near-Ocean Salt Air Exposure

Properties within two blocks of the ocean in Cherry Grove experience accelerated corrosion on metal fixtures, faster paint degradation on exterior wood surfaces and dramatically higher algae and mildew growth on decks, railings, driveways and exterior walls. Pressure washing schedules for Cherry Grove oceanfront properties need to account for that — quarterly washing is often appropriate for high-exposure surfaces that would be fine with annual cleaning two miles inland.

Canal-Front Humidity Compounds Everything

Cherry Grove's canal system creates a second humidity exposure layer on top of the ocean proximity. Canal-front properties see moisture from two directions — tidal air from the canal side and salt air from the ocean. This accelerates algae growth on docks, bulkheads, decks and pool surrounds faster than standard oceanfront properties, and creates a persistent moisture environment that affects exterior surfaces year-round, not just in summer.

Raised Beach House Architecture

Most Cherry Grove vacation rentals are raised beach homes — elevated structures with ground-level parking areas, exterior stairs, underhouse storage and exposed structural pilings. Pressure washing these properties requires working on multiple elevations and surfaces: the deck, the stairs, the ground-level driveway, the underhouse concrete and the exterior siding approach. Bakerss quotes Cherry Grove properties based on actual structure type, not a flat per-square-foot rate.

STR Turnover Density in Peak Season

Cherry Grove runs some of the highest STR occupancy rates in North Myrtle Beach during June, July and August. That means Bakerss Airbnb cleaning schedules in Cherry Grove during peak season require coordination across multiple properties on the same day — and the same Saturday turnover window. We build that capacity into our NMB schedule so Cherry Grove properties are not competing with each other for availability on high-demand turnover days.

How often should Cherry Grove decks and driveways be pressure washed?

Oceanfront and near-ocean properties in Cherry Grove typically need pressure washing two to four times per year on decks and high-exposure surfaces, and at minimum annually on driveways and lower-exposure concrete. Canal-front properties may need more frequent deck washing due to the double humidity exposure from ocean and canal air.
