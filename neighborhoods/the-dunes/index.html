The Dunes community context

Myrtle Beach's Oldest Prestige Community Requires a Different Maintenance Standard

The Dunes Club was founded in 1949 on the oceanfront north of Myrtle Beach proper. The residential community that developed around it contains some of the largest lots, oldest established landscaping and highest-value properties in the entire Grand Strand. Maintenance here is not a commodity service.

Estate Lots with Mature Landscaping

Many Dunes Club area properties were developed in the 1950s through 1980s — meaning the trees are mature, the landscape beds are established and the turf has decades of history. Managing mature Bermuda stands requires different decisions than managing a newly sodded lot. Root depth, thatch accumulation, irrigation uniformity and shade stress from mature canopy all influence how the lawn responds to chemical and mechanical treatments. Bakerss Myrtle Beach lawn care for Dunes area properties accounts for that maturity.

Club Community Appearance Standards

Living adjacent to one of Myrtle Beach's oldest private golf clubs creates a mutual standard of appearance that extends beyond the club fence onto member properties. Overgrown edges, neglected landscape beds, stained driveways and unkempt pool areas stand out in The Dunes in a way they would not in a standard residential subdivision. Bakerss landscaping and lawn care programs for this community are calibrated to that visibility.

High-Value Absentee Owner Population

A significant portion of Dunes Club area homeowners are business owners, retirees and high-net-worth individuals who split time between Myrtle Beach and other residences. For these owners, a bundled property maintenance plan that handles lawn care, landscaping, pool service and exterior cleaning without requiring the owner to manage individual vendor schedules is not a luxury — it is the only practical way to maintain a property at this level while living elsewhere part of the year.

Oceanfront-Adjacent Salt Air Exposure

Properties in The Dunes sit close to the Atlantic — many have direct ocean or Intracoastal views. That proximity means salt air is a real factor in exterior maintenance. Pool equipment corrodes faster, exterior metal fixtures degrade more quickly and hard surfaces accumulate algae and mineral deposits at an accelerated rate compared to inland properties. Pressure washing and pool maintenance schedules for Dunes properties need to account for that exposure level.

The Dunes Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss provide lawn care in The Dunes and Dunes Club area?

Yes. Bakerss provides lawn care for The Dunes and Dunes Club area homeowners in Myrtle Beach. We manage mature Bermuda estate lawns with appropriate thatch, fertilization and pre-emergent programs — and we schedule service to maintain the appearance standards that Dunes Club community properties are held to.

Can Bakerss manage my Dunes Club property while I am away?

Yes. Monthly bundled property maintenance plans for Dunes Club area absentee owners are a primary service offering for this community. One contract covers lawn care, landscaping, pool maintenance and pressure washing on a schedule that requires no owner involvement once established.

What makes lawn care in The Dunes different from standard Myrtle Beach properties?

The Dunes has mature estate lots with Bermuda turf that may have 40 or more years of growth history. Mature Bermuda stands accumulate thatch differently, have established root systems that respond to irrigation more predictably and grow through canopy shade from trees that were not present when the lawn was established. Managing that maturity correctly requires different inputs than managing a new-construction lawn with fresh sod.

Do you work with Dunes Club property owners who have Intracoastal or ocean views?

Yes. Properties in The Dunes with direct ocean, dune or Intracoastal proximity receive specific maintenance attention to the higher exterior exposure level those locations carry. Pressure washing frequency, pool equipment inspection intervals and exterior material maintenance are all adjusted for the elevated salt air and humidity exposure that oceanfront-adjacent Dunes properties experience.

Can you handle landscaping for mature Dunes Club property beds?

Yes. Bakerss landscaping programs for established Dunes Club properties include mature shrub and hedge management, established bed maintenance, seasonal mulching and annual color programs. We do not treat a 40-year-old azalea hedge the same way we would a newly installed landscape planting.
