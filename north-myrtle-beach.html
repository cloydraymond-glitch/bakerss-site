Conway, SC

Property Maintenance Built for Conway

Conway is the county seat of Horry County — a historic river town with established neighborhoods, growing residential development, and proximity to Coastal Carolina University. Unlike the beachfront communities, Conway's inland location brings different maintenance challenges: mature oak and pine canopy that fills gutters aggressively, the Waccamaw River's moisture influence, and shaded lots that promote mold and mildew on surfaces.

Conway's diverse property mix includes historic homes, established residential neighborhoods, student rental properties near CCU, new construction, and commercial properties. Bakerss provides all-in-one property maintenance for every property type in Conway — the same professional service we deliver across the Grand Strand, adapted for the county seat's unique character.
