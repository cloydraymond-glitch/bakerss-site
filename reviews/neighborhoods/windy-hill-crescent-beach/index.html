Windy Hill vs. Crescent Beach

Two Distinct Communities, Same Demand for Reliable Rental Property Maintenance

Windy Hill Beach occupies the southernmost section of North Myrtle Beach — one of the most expensive and in-demand beach neighborhoods in the area. Crescent Beach sits just to the north, a quieter but similarly STR-active stretch where families return annually and week-to-week occupancy drives the entire property economy.

Windy Hill Beach — High Value, High Demand

Windy Hill Beach properties command some of the highest nightly rates in North Myrtle Beach. That premium creates corresponding guest expectations — a pool that is not perfectly clear, a deck with visible staining or a driveway that has not been pressure washed recently produces the kind of review feedback that directly impacts a listing's ranking and future bookings. Bakerss manages Windy Hill properties on the kind of schedule that keeps those reviews positive. Cherry Grove and Windy Hill together represent the highest-value STR corridor in NMB.

Crescent Beach — Consistent Occupancy, Loyal Guests

Crescent Beach has a loyal repeat-guest base that returns year after year to the same properties. That dynamic cuts both ways — returning guests notice when a property has slipped in maintenance quality since their last visit. A driveway that was clean last July and has a season of staining this July is noticed. A pool that was pristine on their last stay and has cloudy water this time becomes a conversation in the review. Crescent Beach STR owners benefit from consistent maintenance that retains that returning guest quality expectation.

Salt Air Intensity at the Beachfront

Both communities sit directly on the Atlantic. Properties within one to three blocks of the oceanfront experience salt air exposure that accelerates surface degradation faster than anywhere else on the Grand Strand. Exterior wood, metal fixtures, deck surfaces, exterior stairs and hardscape all need more frequent maintenance cycles in direct ocean proximity. Pressure washing frequency that would be annual on an inland property should be seasonal on a Windy Hill oceanfront home.

Off-Season Property Watch

Both communities see significant vacancy in November through February. Properties sitting unoccupied through coastal winter storms, northeast wind events and cold rain accumulate exterior damage, roof debris and drainage issues that go undetected without regular inspection. Bakerss vacation home watch provides scheduled inspection visits so problems are identified and addressed before they compound — and before the spring booking season starts with a property that needs remediation instead of turnover cleaning.

Windy Hill & Crescent Beach Property Maintenance FAQ

Does Bakerss serve both Windy Hill Beach and Crescent Beach?

Yes. Bakerss serves both Windy Hill Beach and Crescent Beach as part of our North Myrtle Beach service territory. Both communities are covered for Airbnb cleaning, pool maintenance, pressure washing, lawn care, vacation home watch and property maintenance plans.

How often should oceanfront decks be pressure washed in Windy Hill and Crescent Beach?

Oceanfront and near-ocean decks in Windy Hill and Crescent Beach typically need pressure washing two to four times per year depending on direct exposure, shade and surface material. High-exposure wood decks in direct salt air may benefit from treatment after every heavy storm season event during summer and pre-season cleaning before spring booking ramps up.

Can Bakerss handle back-to-back Saturday turnovers during peak season?

Yes. We plan our North Myrtle Beach route to accommodate multiple Saturday turnovers across Windy Hill, Crescent Beach and Cherry Grove during peak weeks. If you are managing more than one property or have a high-volume booking calendar, contact us early in the season to establish your schedule.

Do you provide off-season services in Windy Hill and Crescent Beach?

Yes. Off-season vacation home watch, gutter cleaning, pressure washing and pre-season preparation are all available. Getting these services done during the quiet November through February window ensures your Windy Hill or Crescent Beach property is guest-ready when spring bookings activate.

Is North Myrtle Beach fully in your service area?

Yes. North Myrtle Beach — including Windy Hill Beach, Crescent Beach, Cherry Grove and Tidewater Plantation — is a primary service area for Bakerss. We maintain active routes throughout NMB for all property maintenance services.
