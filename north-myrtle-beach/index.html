North Myrtle Beach Property Maintenance — Four Communities, One Team

North Myrtle Beach was formed in 1968 when four existing communities merged into one city. Cherry Grove, Crescent Beach, Ocean Drive, and Windy Hill each have their own character, their own property stock, and their own maintenance needs — and we've worked in all of them.

Cherry Grove is the northernmost point — canal-front homes with private boat docks, classic raised beach houses, and the kind of scenic inlet views that attract generations of the same families back year after year. Crescent Beach is a mix of vintage beach cottages and newer construction closer to the shore. Ocean Drive is the historic heart of the city — the shag music scene, the restaurants, and some of the oldest vacation homes on the Grand Strand. Windy Hill sits at the southern end, transitioning into more contemporary construction and the Barefoot Resort corridor.

Properties across North Myrtle Beach share the same core challenge: nine miles of Atlantic exposure means constant salt air, high UV, and the kind of weathering that turns a well-maintained property into a maintenance problem in one or two seasons if the work gets skipped. We prevent that.

North Myrtle Beach Communities We Serve

Cherry Grove

The northernmost community and one of the most picturesque on the Grand Strand. Canal-front homes line the Cherry Grove waterway with private docks and boat access. Classic raised beach houses, mature landscaping, and a mix of vacation rentals and long-held family properties. Canal-front structures here need regular deck maintenance, staining, and pressure washing. Landscaping along the canal benefits from coastal-tolerant plant selection and consistent trimming.

Crescent Beach

A blend of older beach cottages and newer construction homes and condos closer to the ocean. Heavy vacation rental inventory with seasonal occupancy patterns that demand reliable turnover cleaning, exterior upkeep between seasons, and a maintenance partner who shows up when the calendar says they should. We handle Crescent Beach vacation rental cleaning and exterior maintenance on recurring schedules.

Ocean Drive

The cultural and commercial center of North Myrtle Beach — famous as the birthplace of shag dancing and home to some of the Grand Strand's oldest vacation homes. Properties here include mid-century and older homes with wood siding, painted exteriors, and the kind of weathering that comes from decades of oceanfront exposure. We provide careful soft washing, exterior painting, and full maintenance for Ocean Drive homes that need respectful treatment of older materials.

Windy Hill & Barefoot Resort

The southern section of North Myrtle Beach, where the character shifts toward newer construction and higher-end resort communities. Barefoot Resort brings golf course homes, larger single-family properties, and condominium complexes with elevated exterior standards. We provide lawn care, pressure washing, gutter cleaning, and full exterior maintenance for Windy Hill and Barefoot Resort properties.
