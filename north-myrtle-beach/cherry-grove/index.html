About Cherry Grove

Serving Cherry Grove

Cherry Grove Beach was one of the four independent beach communities — along with Ocean Drive, Crescent Beach, and Windy Hill — that merged in 1968 to form the City of North Myrtle Beach. Cherry Grove developed as a vacation community beginning in the 1940s and 1950s, when families from the Carolinas began building and buying beach cottages for summer use. The community developed its own distinct identity centered on the Cherry Grove Fishing Pier — one of the longest piers on the East Coast — and the Cherry Grove inlet at its northern tip. The inlet creates tidal channels and marsh areas that give the community its distinctive geography.

Cherry Grove today is a mix of older beach cottages that have been updated over generations, newer construction beach homes, and condominiums in smaller boutique buildings — a deliberate contrast to the high-rise towers further south. The community is bounded by the Atlantic Ocean to the east, the Cherry Grove inlet to the north, and Barefoot Resort to the west. Property values in Cherry Grove range from older cottages in the $300s to oceanfront homes valued at $2M+.

Cherry Grove has a very high STR participation rate — the vast majority of oceanfront and near-ocean properties are rented through Airbnb, VRBO, and vacation rental agencies during the beach season. Many properties are also rented for spring and fall shoulder seasons. This creates year-round demand for professional turnover cleaning, lawn maintenance, and exterior upkeep from owners who understand that their property's condition directly drives their rental income.

Cherry Grove's oceanfront position means maximum salt air exposure — one of the most demanding environments for exterior surfaces on the entire Grand Strand. Properties experience accelerated paint degradation, corrosion of metal fixtures, and intensive mildew growth on all exterior surfaces. The older housing stock means more frequent maintenance needs than newer construction. The high STR occupancy rate means properties experience continuous guest traffic and wear that requires consistent professional attention.
