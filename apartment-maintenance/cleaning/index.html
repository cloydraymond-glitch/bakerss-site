Frequently asked questions

Apartment Cleaning FAQ

What does apartment common area cleaning include?

Apartment common area cleaning with Bakerss covers clubhouses, leasing offices, fitness centers and gyms, mail rooms, laundry rooms, trash rooms, elevator interiors, breezeway surfaces and other shared spaces. Services include vacuuming and mopping floors, sanitizing surfaces, cleaning bathrooms and restrooms, dusting, trash removal and window cleaning on common area glass.

How often should apartment common areas be cleaned?

Most Myrtle Beach area apartment communities clean common areas two to three times per week during peak occupancy and reduce to weekly during off-peak periods. High-traffic spaces like fitness centers and mail rooms may require more frequent cleaning. Bakerss builds a custom schedule based on your occupancy patterns and budget.

Do you provide leasing office cleaning?

Yes. Leasing office cleaning is available as a standalone service or as part of a broader common area cleaning contract. Leasing office cleaning includes vacuuming, surface sanitizing, restroom cleaning, window cleaning and trash removal on a schedule that supports your leasing team's business hours.

Can you clean after construction or renovations?

Yes. Post-construction and renovation cleanup for common areas, clubhouses and apartment units is available. Construction cleanup includes dust removal, debris clearing, floor cleaning, window cleaning and detail cleaning to prepare the space for occupancy or leasing tours.
