Why DHEC compliance matters

South Carolina Requires Documented Pool Records for Apartment Communities

DHEC regulations require apartment community pools to maintain chemical testing logs, inspection records and proper signage. Non-compliance can result in pool closure orders during inspection — a serious liability for property management and ownership. Bakerss understands these requirements and maintains documentation on every service visit.

📋

Chemical Testing Logs

Every visit includes documented pH, chlorine, alkalinity and cyanuric acid readings. Logs are maintained per DHEC requirements and available to your management team on request.

Frequently asked questions

Apartment Pool Maintenance FAQ

Do you provide DHEC-compliant pool maintenance for apartments?

Yes. Bakerss provides DHEC-compliant pool maintenance for apartment communities including chemical testing and logging, water balance documentation, pH and chlorine management, skimming, vacuuming, filter service and all required inspection records. We understand South Carolina DHEC pool regulations and support your compliance documentation so your management team can demonstrate compliance during inspections.

How often should apartment pools be serviced in Myrtle Beach?

During peak summer season in Myrtle Beach, apartment community pools typically require twice-weekly service due to high bather load, heat and humidity. Standard residential pools may only need weekly service, but apartment pools serving dozens of residents daily require more frequent attention to maintain safe water chemistry and pass DHEC inspections. Bakerss offers weekly, twice-weekly and daily service schedules.

What chemicals are included in apartment pool maintenance?

Chemicals are billed at cost plus 15 percent. Every service visit includes chemical testing, pH adjustment, chlorine or bromine treatment, alkalinity adjustment and shock treatment as needed. Algaecide, clarifier, enzyme treatment and other specialty chemicals are applied as conditions require and documented in the pool log.

Can you maintain apartment pool equipment?

Bakerss inspects pool equipment on every visit including pump operation, filter pressure, skimmer and basket condition and heater status. Equipment repair and replacement is quoted separately and coordinated with your management team for approval before work is performed.

Do you provide pool maintenance for multiple apartment communities?

Yes. Bakerss can maintain pools across multiple apartment communities or properties in your portfolio with coordinated scheduling, consistent documentation and a single monthly invoice per property or a consolidated invoice for your entire portfolio.
