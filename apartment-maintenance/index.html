Built for Multi-Family

One Vendor for Turns, Common Areas and Work Orders

Apartment communities run on tight timelines. A unit needs to be turned before the next move-in date, a resident work order sits open, common areas need to look presentable for a tour, and the pool area needs a daily check during season. When those tasks are split across separate vendors, the property manager becomes the coordinator.

Bakerss combines unit turn support, common area cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, grounds care, pool-area checks and handyman-scope work orders into one written scope. That means fewer handoffs between vendors, clearer accountability when something is missed, and faster turnaround on the tasks that affect leasing timelines and resident satisfaction.

For property managers and regional teams, the value goes beyond the labor itself. It is consistent reporting, photo documentation of completed turns, predictable scheduling around move-out and move-in dates, and a local team that understands how coastal humidity, salt air and seasonal turnover volume affect a Myrtle Beach community.

FAQ

Apartment Maintenance Questions — Answered

Does Bakerss handle apartment unit turns?

Yes. Bakerss provides move-out turn service for apartment communities, including cleaning, minor repairs, punch-list items, dryer vent cleaning and make-ready coordination between move-out and move-in dates.

What common area services does Bakerss provide for apartments?

Bakerss provides common area cleaning for hallways, breezeways, leasing offices, clubhouses, fitness centers, mail kiosks and amenity spaces, along with grounds care, pool-area checks and pressure washing for shared areas.

Can Bakerss support work orders for apartment communities?

Yes. Bakerss can support resident work order volume with handyman-scope repairs such as fixture replacement, drywall patching, door hardware, caulking and minor plumbing or electrical fixes, dispatched on a schedule agreed with the property manager.

Do you offer recurring maintenance plans for apartment communities?

Yes. Bakerss builds recurring maintenance retainers scoped to the property, covering grounds, common area cleaning, pool checks, dryer vent cleaning and work order support on a daily, weekly or monthly cadence.

What size apartment communities does Bakerss work with?

Bakerss supports apartment communities of varying sizes across Myrtle Beach and Horry County, from smaller multi-family properties to larger communities that need daily or weekly onsite coverage.
