Multifamily Property Maintenance

One Vendor for Unit Turns, Resident Work Orders & Common Areas

Apartment communities run on tight timelines. A unit needs to be turned before the next move-in date, a resident work order sits open, common areas need to look presentable for a tour, and the pool area needs a daily check during season. When those tasks are split across separate vendors, the property manager becomes the coordinator.

Bakerss combines unit turn support, common area cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, grounds care, pool-area checks and handyman-scope work orders into one written scope. That means fewer handoffs between vendors, clearer accountability when something is missed, and faster turnaround on the tasks that affect leasing timelines and resident satisfaction.

For property managers and regional teams, the value goes beyond the labor itself. It is consistent reporting, photo documentation of completed turns, predictable scheduling around move-out and move-in dates, and a local team that understands how coastal humidity, salt air and seasonal turnover volume affect a Myrtle Beach community.

Grand Strand conditions

Preventive Maintenance for Coastal Apartment Communities

Myrtle Beach properties face humidity, salt exposure, seasonal occupancy and heavy use of shared spaces. Routine inspections and timely maintenance help identify issues before they disrupt residents or leasing.

Humidity, Mildew & Drainage

Watch for moisture-prone common areas, clogged drains, deteriorating caulk and recurring exterior staining; escalate suspected water intrusion for appropriate assessment.

Salt Air & Exterior Wear

Document corrosion on hardware, fasteners and exterior fixtures; schedule cleaning and minor repairs based on observed conditions.

Storm Readiness & Recovery

Prepare site checklists, inspect shared areas after storms when safe, document damage and coordinate authorized cleanup or repairs.

FAQ

Apartment Maintenance Questions — Answered

Does Bakerss handle apartment unit turns?

Yes. Bakerss provides move-out turn service for apartment communities, including cleaning, minor repairs, punch-list items, dryer vent cleaning and make-ready coordination between move-out and move-in dates.

What common area services does Bakerss provide for apartments?

Bakerss provides common area cleaning for hallways, breezeways, leasing offices, clubhouses, fitness centers, mail kiosks and amenity spaces, along with grounds care, pool-area checks and pressure washing for shared areas.

Can Bakerss support work orders for apartment communities?

Yes. Bakerss can support resident work-order volume with routine repairs, drywall and paint items, door and hardware issues, fixtures, plumbing and electrical maintenance within the approved scope, plus coordinated specialty-trade service when required.

Do you offer recurring maintenance plans for apartment communities?

Yes. Bakerss builds recurring maintenance retainers scoped to the property, covering grounds, common area cleaning, pool checks, dryer vent cleaning and work order support on a daily, weekly or monthly cadence.

What size apartment communities does Bakerss work with?

Bakerss supports apartment communities of varying sizes across Myrtle Beach and Horry County, from smaller multi-family properties to larger communities that need daily or weekly onsite coverage.

Can property managers see documentation for completed work orders?

Bakerss can provide documented service activity for managed accounts, including work-order status, technician notes, service photos, time records and completion history according to the reporting setup defined for the account.

How much does apartment maintenance cost per unit in Myrtle Beach?

Cost per unit depends on the community size, resident work-order volume, staffing model, amenity needs, preventive schedule and included repairs. Bakerss prepares property-specific proposals rather than promising a universal per-unit rate.

Can Bakerss supplement an existing apartment maintenance team?

Yes. Overflow work orders, seasonal turn volume, scheduled cleaning, grounds and other approved tasks can be scoped to complement an in-house maintenance team.

What is included in an apartment maintenance contract?

A written agreement can define covered services, scheduled visits, work-order allowances, response windows, approval limits, reporting, materials, overages and excluded specialty work.

How do you reduce apartment turnover delays?

A clear move-out inspection, early materials planning, defined cleaning and painting scopes, repair punch lists and documented completion can help avoid preventable handoff delays. Timing depends on condition and scope.

How often should apartment HVAC systems be serviced?

Many properties schedule seasonal HVAC checks and regular filter changes, but the appropriate frequency depends on equipment guidance, usage, operating conditions and the property maintenance plan.

Do you handle after-hours apartment maintenance emergencies?

After-hours coverage and response expectations depend on the service agreement and available qualified personnel. Emergency response is not guaranteed unless explicitly included in the contract.

Can you provide an apartment maintenance checklist or preventive schedule?

Yes. During a property walkthrough, Bakerss can outline a proposed recurring checklist covering shared areas, HVAC filters, dryer vents, grounds, exterior conditions and documented follow-up items.

Do you serve apartment communities outside Myrtle Beach?

Bakerss considers multifamily maintenance requests throughout the Grand Strand, including Conway, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Murrells Inlet, Little River, Pawleys Island and surrounding Horry and Georgetown County communities.

Are Bakerss technicians certified and licensed?

Bakerss has technicians with training, experience and relevant certifications or licenses. The specific credential and any required company authorization are verified against the work being proposed; not every technician is credentialed for every trade.

Can you manage apartment work orders and unit turns through one vendor?

Bakerss can combine approved resident work orders, make-ready services, common-area cleaning and recurring property care under a coordinated scope with documented service activity.
