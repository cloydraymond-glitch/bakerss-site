How to Pick the Right Property Maintenance Company in Myrtle Beach

Myrtle Beach and Horry County have no shortage of lawn care and property maintenance companies. Finding one you can actually trust — especially if you're an out-of-state property owner who can't be on-site to supervise — requires knowing what to look for and what questions to ask.

The Non-Negotiables

Licensed, Bonded, and Insured in South Carolina

This is the starting point, not a differentiator. Any legitimate property maintenance company operating in South Carolina should be able to provide proof of liability insurance and bonding on request. If a company hesitates to provide this, walk away. If something goes wrong on your property — a broken window, a damaged vehicle, an injury — insurance is the only thing protecting you.

Verifiable Reviews and References

Look for Google reviews specifically — they're the hardest to fake and the most relevant to local service quality. Be cautious of companies with only 2–customer reviews or reviews that all posted within a short window. Five or more detailed, varied reviews over time from real named reviewers is a meaningful signal.

Clear, Written Estimates

Any professional company should provide a written estimate before starting work — including the specific services, scope, pricing, and any conditions or exclusions. Verbal-only estimates are a red flag, especially for larger jobs like deck building, painting, or landscaping.

Out-of-State Owners

If you're managing a vacation rental or second home from out of state, communication reliability is as important as service quality. Ask directly: how will you confirm service completion? How do you handle issues found on the property? A company that can't answer these questions clearly isn't set up to serve remote owners.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring

  • Are you licensed and insured in South Carolina? Can you provide proof?
  • How long have you been operating in Horry County?
  • Do you have references from vacation rental or out-of-state owners specifically?
  • What's your communication process — how do I know when service is complete?
  • What happens if there's a problem or I'm not satisfied with the work?
  • Do you subcontract any services or is this handled by your own team?
  • What's your cancellation or rescheduling policy?

Red Flags to Watch For

  • No physical address or business registration in SC
  • Prices dramatically lower than competitors — usually signals cut corners, unlicensed workers, or no insurance
  • Reluctance to provide written estimates or contracts
  • No online presence, website, or verifiable reviews
  • Payment required fully upfront before any work is done
  • Pressure tactics or very limited-time offers

What Separates Good from Great

Most companies can mow a lawn or clean a house adequately. What separates the best property maintenance companies in Horry County is consistency, communication, and accountability — especially for vacation rental owners and out-of-state homeowners who need to trust that the property is being cared for without being able to check in person.

The best companies are reachable, communicate completion of work proactively, flag issues they notice on the property, and maintain the same quality standard on visit 50 as they did on visit 1.
