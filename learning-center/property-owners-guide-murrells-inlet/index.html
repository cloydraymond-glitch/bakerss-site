🦞 Property Owner's Guide to Murrells Inlet, SC — 2026

Murrells Inlet — South Carolina's Seafood Capital — is one of the most desirable communities in Horry County, combining waterway access, premier golf communities, and a vibrant dining scene along the MarshWalk. For property owners, it's also a market with specific maintenance demands driven by tidal marsh humidity, waterway exposure, and the premium property values that require professional upkeep to protect.

About Murrells Inlet

Prince Creek is Murrells Inlet's largest master-planned community — encompassing multiple neighborhoods, a golf course, and active HOA structures with specific maintenance standards. Wachesaw Plantation adds a luxury waterway and golf community segment with some of Horry County's highest per-property maintenance expectations. Collins Creek Landing and other waterway communities face the elevated humidity and biological growth conditions that come with tidal creek adjacency.

The MarshWalk corridor is Murrells Inlet's dining and lifestyle center — a mile-long elevated boardwalk overlooking the tidal marsh that draws visitors year-round and supports a growing vacation rental market in surrounding neighborhoods. The mix of permanent residents, second-home owners, and vacation rental investors creates consistent demand for professional property maintenance across the full service spectrum.

Property Maintenance in Murrells Inlet — What's Different Here

Murrells Inlet's marsh and tidal creek environment creates humidity levels that accelerate mildew and algae growth on all exterior surfaces — particularly on properties adjacent to the water. Properties within a quarter mile of the marsh face biological growth rates that require more frequent pressure washing than standard inland or even standard coastal properties.

Prince Creek's golf course adjacency sets a visual benchmark that HOA enforcement takes seriously. Lawn care to the golf course standard — consistent height, clean edges, well-maintained beds — is the expectation, not the exception, for properties in golf community locations. Gutter cleaning demand in Murrells Inlet is elevated by the area's established tree canopy, particularly in Prince Creek and Wachesaw where mature oaks and pines shed continuously.

Epoxy garage floors have been one of our fastest-growing services in Murrells Inlet — larger homes in Prince Creek and Wachesaw frequently have 2 and 3-car garages where a finished epoxy floor adds meaningful property value and rental appeal. Pool maintenance is consistent given the warm season length and the growing STR market near the MarshWalk.
