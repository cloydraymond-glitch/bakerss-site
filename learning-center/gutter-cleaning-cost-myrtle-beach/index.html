How Much Does Gutter Cleaning Cost in Myrtle Beach, SC?

Gutter cleaning is one of the most important and most overlooked maintenance tasks for Myrtle Beach homeowners. This guide covers what it actually costs in Horry County, what affects pricing, and why it's worth every penny.

What Determines Gutter Cleaning Prices in Myrtle Beach?

Gutter cleaning pricing in Horry County is primarily driven by four factors:

  • Linear footage of gutters — the total length of gutters on the home is the base pricing unit. Most single-story homes have 150–200 linear feet; two-story homes run 200–300+ feet.
  • Number of stories — working on second-story gutters requires different laddering, takes more time, and carries higher safety requirements — this is reflected in pricing.
  • Condition and debris load — gutters packed solid with pine needles, moss, or compacted leaf mats take significantly longer to clean than lightly debris-filled troughs. In Horry County, homes under heavy pine canopy often fall into this category.
  • Downspout condition — blocked downspouts require additional flushing and sometimes snaking, which adds to the service cost.

Why Gutter Cleaning Costs More in Coastal SC Than National Averages Suggest

National average gutter cleaning prices typically range from $75–$150. In Myrtle Beach and Horry County, expect to pay somewhat more for several reasons:

  • Longleaf and loblolly pine trees are everywhere in Horry County — pine needles clump when wet and create dense, heavy mats that take much longer to remove than standard leaf debris
  • Spanish moss blows into gutters during storms and wraps around downspout openings, requiring additional manual removal
  • Post-storm service calls are more common here than in most regions, requiring faster scheduling and sometimes emergency availability
  • The two-story raised beach cottages common in Surfside, Garden City, and Garden City Beach require additional ladder setups

How Often Should Gutters Be Cleaned in Myrtle Beach?

Twice a year is the recommended minimum for most Horry County homes — once in late spring after pollen season and once in late fall after the last leaf drop. Homes under heavy pine canopy or within range of Spanish moss-bearing oaks should consider quarterly service. After any tropical storm or significant weather event, an additional cleaning is worth scheduling regardless of your regular schedule.

Is Gutter Cleaning Worth the Cost?

Gutter cleaning is the most cost-effective preventive maintenance investment in any Horry County homeowner's budget. A $150–$200 annual cleaning prevents: foundation damage that can cost $5,000–$30,000+ to repair, fascia rot that costs $500–$2,000 to replace, and soffit damage that can reach $1,000–$3,500. The math is straightforward — it's not worth skipping.
