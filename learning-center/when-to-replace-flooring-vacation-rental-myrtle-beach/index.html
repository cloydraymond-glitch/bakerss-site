When to Replace Flooring in a Myrtle Beach Vacation Rental

4 min read Myrtle Beach & Horry County, SC By Bakerss Property Services

Flooring is one of the highest-impact, highest-cost maintenance decisions you'll make for a Myrtle Beach vacation rental. Replace it too early and you're leaving money on the table. Wait too long and you're losing bookings and collecting bad reviews. Here's how to time it right.

Signs Your Vacation Rental Flooring Needs Replacing Now

Guest Reviews Mention It

If a guest has mentioned flooring in a review — "the floors were stained" or "the carpet smelled" — you've already waited too long. Replace it before the next booking if at all possible. Flooring complaints in reviews are among the most damaging because they suggest the property hasn't been updated and signal neglect to future guests.

Carpet: Odor or Staining That Won't Clean Out

Professional carpet cleaning removes surface dirt and refreshes the appearance, but it doesn't fully eliminate embedded odors or set stains. Once Myrtle Beach beach rental carpet has developed a persistent "beach house smell" — a combination of salt air, sunscreen, and humidity in the fibers — replacement is the only effective solution. If your cleaning team regularly notes odor after cleaning, the carpet needs to go.

Visible Wear Paths

Heavy-traffic vacation rental use creates wear patterns in carpet and surface damage in wood and laminate that's visible in listing photos. If you can see the wear paths in your photos, guests can too. This signals a dated property regardless of how well everything else is presented.

Laminate Swelling or Edge Damage

Any swelling, separation at seams, or edge damage in laminate flooring means moisture has compromised the product. Laminate in a beach rental that's seen water exposure (and they all do) eventually shows this damage. Once started, it spreads — replace the affected area before it becomes the whole floor.

Grout Discoloration That Won't Clean

Tile itself rarely needs replacement, but grout that's permanently discolored and won't respond to professional cleaning is worth addressing — either through professional grout restoration or replacement of the worst areas. Dark grout lines photograph poorly and guests notice them.

Typical Flooring Lifespans in Myrtle Beach Vacation Rentals

  • Carpet: 3–7 years depending on quality and traffic. Replace aggressively.
  • Laminate: 5–10 years if no moisture damage. Coastal humidity shortens this.
  • LVP (quality product): 15–25 years. The right choice for coastal vacation rentals.
  • Hardwood: Not recommended for STR use in coastal SC environments.
  • Ceramic/porcelain tile: 20+ years. Replace grout as needed.

The Upgrade Opportunity

Flooring replacement is one of the best moments to upgrade materials. If you're replacing worn carpet, replacing with LVP rather than new carpet costs more upfront but saves money over the next decade — no recurring professional cleaning, longer replacement cycle, and better performance in the beach rental environment.

The listing photo improvement from replacing worn carpet with modern LVP is significant. New flooring photographs dramatically better and repositions the property visually in a competitive rental market.
