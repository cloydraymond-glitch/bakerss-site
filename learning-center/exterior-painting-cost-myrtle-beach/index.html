How Much Does Exterior Painting Cost in Myrtle Beach, SC?

Exterior painting in Myrtle Beach costs more than the national average — and should. Coastal SC's salt air, UV intensity, and humidity demand higher-quality paint, more thorough prep work, and more coats than most other regions. Here's what to expect and how to make sure you're getting real value.

What Affects Exterior Painting Prices in Myrtle Beach?

Exterior painting in coastal South Carolina is a more involved and more expensive undertaking than in most other parts of the country — and for good reason. Here are the primary factors that affect what you'll pay:

  • Square footage of paintable surface — the total area of siding, trim, shutters, and fascia being painted. This is calculated differently from home square footage.
  • Number of stories — two-story homes require scaffolding or extension equipment, additional safety measures, and more time. Expect 25–40% more than a comparable single-story home.
  • Surface condition and prep work — in coastal SC, prep is everything. Power washing, scraping, caulking, priming, and sanding dramatically affect how long the paint job takes and how long it lasts.
  • Paint quality — premium coastal-grade paints with built-in mildewcide and UV inhibitors cost more per gallon but last significantly longer in the Myrtle Beach environment. Cheap paint is almost always a false economy.
  • Number of coats — professional exterior painting in coastal SC should always include at least two finish coats. One-coat applications fade and fail faster in the UV and salt air environment.
  • Type of surface — vinyl siding, wood, fiber cement, stucco, and brick all require different prep approaches and primer types.

The Most Important Thing

In coastal SC, prep work determines how long your paint job lasts. A properly prepped surface with premium paint can last 7–10 years. A rushed job over dirty or mildewed surfaces may start peeling in 18 months. Always ask what prep work is included in any painting quote.

How Long Does Exterior Paint Last in Myrtle Beach?

Exterior paint in coastal SC typically lasts 5–8 years for wood siding, 6–10 years for fiber cement, and 5–7 years for vinyl — shorter lifespans than in drier inland climates. Oceanfront and waterway-adjacent properties see the shortest lifespans due to salt air intensity. Premium coastal-grade paints and thorough prep work are the two biggest factors in maximizing your investment.

Getting Multiple Quotes — What to Watch For

When comparing exterior painting quotes in Myrtle Beach, price alone is a poor guide. Ask every company:

  • What prep work is included? (Pressure wash, scrape, caulk, prime?)
  • How many coats of finish paint?
  • What paint brand and product line?
  • Is the quote for labor only or does it include materials?
  • What's the warranty on the work?

A quote that's $1,000 cheaper but skips prep work and uses one coat of builder-grade paint will cost you more in the long run than a thorough job done right the first time.
