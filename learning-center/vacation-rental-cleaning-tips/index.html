Vacation Rental Cleaning Tips for Horry County Property Owners

Myrtle Beach and Horry County host millions of visitors every year — making it one of the most active short-term rental markets in the country. With that volume comes intense competition for 5-star reviews, and nothing tanks a vacation rental rating faster than a cleaning complaint.

Here's what professional vacation rental cleaning actually involves, what guests notice most, and how to set up a system that keeps your property guest-ready every turnover.

What Guests Actually Notice First

Before anything else, guests evaluate three things within the first 60 seconds of entering a vacation rental:

  • Smell: Any musty, stale, or chemical odor immediately signals a problem — coastal properties are especially prone to humidity-driven mustiness
  • Visible surfaces: Kitchen counters, bathroom vanities, and the primary bed — if these look clean, the overall impression is clean
  • Floors: Sand on floors (inevitable in beach rentals) signals that cleaning was rushed
The Coastal SC Factor

Sand management is the unique challenge of every Myrtle Beach vacation rental. Sand tracks in from beach bags, towels, and feet and spreads throughout the property instantly. A thorough turnover cleaning must address sand in every corner, under furniture, and especially in bathrooms and entryways.

The Full Turnover Checklist

Kitchen

  • Clean inside and outside of all appliances — microwave, oven, refrigerator, dishwasher
  • Wipe all cabinet fronts and handles
  • Clean sink and faucet thoroughly — mineral deposits visible under beach lighting
  • Check and restock paper towels, dish soap, dishwasher pods
  • Run dishwasher with cleaning tablet if time allows

Bathrooms

  • Scrub toilet, including under rim and base
  • Clean shower and tub — soap scum is the most common guest complaint in beach rentals
  • Wipe mirrors and vanity completely
  • Restock toilet paper, soap, shampoo, conditioner
  • Replace towels with fresh sets — fold hotel-style for presentation

Bedrooms

  • Strip and remake all beds with fresh linens
  • Check under beds for lost items and sand accumulation
  • Wipe nightstands and dresser surfaces
  • Check inside dresser drawers for left items
  • Fluff and arrange all pillows and decorative elements

Living Areas

  • Vacuum all upholstered furniture — sand embeds deeply in sofas and chairs
  • Wipe all hard surfaces including TV remotes, light switches, and door handles
  • Check for and remove all guest belongings
  • Confirm all electronics and amenities are working

Setting Up a Reliable Turnover System

The biggest mistake vacation rental owners make is treating turnover cleaning as an ad hoc task rather than a system. For Horry County rental owners — especially those managing properties from out of state — a reliable local cleaning team with a consistent process is the only sustainable model at any scale.

What that system looks like:

  • Fixed cleaning team that knows your property — no new faces every turnover
  • Standardized checklist that covers every area every time
  • Reliable communication — confirmation when cleaning is complete and any issues flagged
  • Flexible scheduling that works around your booking calendar, not ours
