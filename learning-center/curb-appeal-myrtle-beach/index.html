7 Curb Appeal Upgrades That Pay Off in Myrtle Beach, SC

5 min read Myrtle Beach & Horry County, SC By Bakerss Property Maintenance

Curb appeal in Myrtle Beach isn't just about pride of ownership — it's a measurable driver of vacation rental booking rates, property sale prices, and guest first impressions. Properties that look great on the outside convert browsers to bookers and create the positive expectations that lead to 5-star reviews. Here are the upgrades that deliver the best return in this market.

1. Pressure Washing — Highest ROI, Period

No other single service does more for Myrtle Beach exterior appearance per dollar spent. A professional pressure washing of your driveway, siding, and walkways can restore a property that looks 10 years old to like-new condition in a few hours. Coastal mildew, algae, and organic staining accumulate fast — removing it with an annual or twice-yearly washing keeps the property looking maintained year-round.

Cost: $125–$650 depending on scope
Impact: Immediate, dramatic, before-and-after visible

2. Fresh Exterior Paint

Myrtle Beach's coastal environment degrades paint faster than inland markets — which means fresh paint has a bigger visual impact here than almost anywhere else. A freshly painted exterior with quality coastal-grade paint transforms the entire character of a property. For vacation rental listing photos, fresh exterior paint is worth more than almost any other single upgrade.

Cost: $2,500–$9,000 depending on home size
Impact: Transformative — property looks brand new

3. Concrete Landscaping Curbing

The gap between a well-maintained Myrtle Beach lawn and a truly polished property is often concrete curbing. Permanent curbing defines beds, keeps mulch contained, prevents grass from encroaching, and eliminates the soft, undefined edges that make even a well-maintained lawn look unfinished. It's a one-time installation that makes the property look professionally maintained for decades.

Cost: $200–$800 for most residential properties
Impact: Elevates the entire landscape — instantly more polished

4. Fresh Mulch in All Beds

Fresh mulch is one of the cheapest and most visually impactful things you can do before a listing photoshoot or a selling season. Dark mulch against green plants and clean curbing looks professionally maintained in photos and in person. It also helps plants retain moisture through Myrtle Beach's summer heat.

Cost: $150–$400 for most properties
Impact: Significant visual improvement, especially in photos

5. Front Door & Entry Refresh

The front door is the first thing guests touch and the main focal point of every exterior photo. A fresh coat of a bold, complementary color (navy, red, deep green, black) on the front door, fresh door hardware, and clean entry lighting makes a strong first impression for almost no cost.

Cost: $75–$400 (door paint + hardware + lighting)
Impact: Outsized for the investment — the focal point of every photo

For vacation rental listing photos: Pressure wash first. Then photograph. A clean property always photographs dramatically better than a stained one — even with better staging and landscaping. This is consistently the highest-ROI improvement for listing photo quality.

6. Professional Lawn Edging & Cleanup

A mowed lawn with soft, undefined edges looks informal. The same lawn with crisp edges along every driveway, walkway, and bed looks manicured. Professional edging is part of every Bakerss lawn care visit — but if a property has been neglected, a cleanup cut with hard edging along all borders makes an immediate difference in how maintained the property appears.

Cost: Included in professional lawn care ($50+)
Impact: Significant improvement to perceived maintenance quality

7. Gutter Cleaning & Downspout Paint Touch-Up

It sounds minor, but dirty, overflowing gutters and streaked fascia boards signal to buyers and guests that a property hasn't been maintained. Clean gutters, downspouts without the characteristic black streak staining, and touch-up paint on fascia complete the picture of a well-maintained property. This is particularly relevant for pre-sale prep and pre-season STR refresh.

Cost: $99–$225 for gutter cleaning; minor fascia paint touch-up additional
Impact: Subtle but signals maintenance quality to discerning buyers and guests

What Doesn't Pay Off as Well in Myrtle Beach

  • New sod in summer: Expensive and risky in Myrtle Beach's summer heat without intensive irrigation. Better to overseed in fall.
  • Elaborate water features: High maintenance in Myrtle Beach's climate. Hard to recoup in rental income.
  • Extensive hardscape additions: Expensive relative to curb appeal impact unless the property truly lacks outdoor living space.
