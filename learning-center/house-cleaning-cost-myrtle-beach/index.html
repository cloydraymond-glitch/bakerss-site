How Much Does House Cleaning Cost in Myrtle Beach, SC?

Whether you need recurring home cleaning, a one-time deep clean, or vacation rental turnover service in Myrtle Beach, this guide covers what professional interior cleaning actually costs in Horry County — with real pricing, not national averages.

What Affects House Cleaning Prices in Myrtle Beach?

Interior cleaning pricing in Horry County depends on several factors that cleaning companies assess before providing a firm quote:

  • Home size — number of bedrooms, bathrooms, and overall square footage is the primary pricing driver.
  • Type of cleaning — a standard recurring clean costs less than a deep clean or move-out clean, which requires significantly more time and detail work.
  • Frequency — weekly service is priced lower per visit than bi-weekly, which is lower per visit than one-time cleans.
  • Current condition — a home that hasn't been cleaned professionally in months takes more time to bring to standard than one on a regular schedule.
  • Special requirements — post-construction, post-renovation, and vacation rental turnovers have additional requirements and are priced accordingly.

Vacation Rental and Airbnb Cleaning in Myrtle Beach

Turnover cleaning for a 2-bedroom vacation rental in Myrtle Beach typically runs $110–$175 per turnover. Larger properties and homes that require linen service run higher. Most vacation rental owners in our client base have us on a recurring schedule that works around their booking calendar.

Weekly vs. Bi-Weekly Recurring Cleaning — Which Is Right for You?

For primary residences, bi-weekly cleaning is the most popular option — it keeps the home consistently clean without the cost of weekly service. Weekly cleaning is popular for vacation rentals with frequent turnover, households with young children or pets, and homeowners who want a consistently spotless environment.

The first cleaning of a recurring relationship is typically priced higher than subsequent visits because bringing a home to standard from scratch takes more time than maintaining it.

What's Included in a Standard Myrtle Beach House Cleaning?

  • Kitchen: counters, appliances exterior, sink, stovetop, cabinet fronts, floor
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower/tub, sink, mirror, vanity, floor
  • Bedrooms: dust surfaces, vacuum, make beds, empty trash
  • Living areas: dust all surfaces, vacuum upholstery, mop or vacuum floors
  • Throughout: empty all trash, wipe light switches and door handles, vacuum all floors

Deep cleans and move-out cleans include additional items: inside appliances, inside cabinets, baseboards, window sills, ceiling fans, and other areas not covered in standard recurring service.
