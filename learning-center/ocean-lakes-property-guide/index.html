🏖️ Property Owner's Guide to Ocean Lakes — Myrtle Beach, SC

Ocean Lakes Family Campground is one of the largest oceanfront campground and resort communities in the eastern United States — a gated, self-contained community with thousands of sites ranging from tent camping to fully built-out cottage-style vacation homes. For property owners with built sites in Ocean Lakes, it represents one of the Grand Strand's most active and distinctive vacation rental markets, with a loyal repeat guest base and a self-governing community structure that creates specific maintenance expectations.

Ocean Lakes Property Ownership

Ocean Lakes property owners who have built or purchased permanent structures within the campground own vacation rental assets in one of the most consistently booked STR environments on the Grand Strand. The community's enclosed, gated character, extensive amenities (pools, waterpark, beach access, activities), and decades-long reputation create strong demand that fills rentals throughout the season. That booking volume creates correspondingly high maintenance demand — properties cycling through guests at high frequency need reliable, consistent cleaning and upkeep to maintain guest satisfaction and the review scores that drive future bookings.

The density and proximity of structures within Ocean Lakes creates a specific aesthetic environment where property appearance relative to neighbors is immediately visible. Exterior maintenance that would be less noticeable on a standalone residential lot is conspicuous in Ocean Lakes's dense community setting — algae-stained siding, overgrown lots, or neglected decks stand out when surrounded by well-maintained neighbors.

Maintenance in Ocean Lakes — STR Intensity

Turnover cleaning is the highest-frequency maintenance demand for Ocean Lakes property owners. The gated community's check-out/check-in cycle creates tight turnaround windows that require a cleaning team that specifically understands beach property protocols — including the sand removal procedures that prevent guests from arriving to gritty floors and furniture.

Exterior pressure washing at Ocean Lakes properties benefits from the same coastal maintenance logic that applies to all Myrtle Beach oceanfront properties — salt air, UV intensity, and biological growth accumulate continuously. Annual exterior washing is the minimum; after any significant storm, post-storm cleaning removes the sand and organic debris that storms deposit on all exterior surfaces.

Deck and exterior surface maintenance is particularly important in Ocean Lakes given the density of the community and the heavy guest traffic that built structures experience during peak season. Deck cleaning before the season and inspection for any damage from the previous season's use are both worth scheduling as part of the annual maintenance calendar.
