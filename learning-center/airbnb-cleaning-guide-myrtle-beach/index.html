Airbnb & Vacation Rental Cleaning Guide — Myrtle Beach, SC 2026

Myrtle Beach is one of the most competitive short-term rental markets on the East Coast. More than 30,000 active listings compete for the same booking windows, the same guests, and the same five-star reviews. In a market this dense, the properties that consistently rank higher, book faster, and command better nightly rates share one characteristic above everything else: they are consistently, professionally clean.

This guide covers the complete Myrtle Beach Airbnb cleaning standard — what every turnover should include, what a beach-specific cleaning protocol requires, how to find and work with a reliable STR cleaning service, and what cleaning costs in 2026 across Horry County's vacation rental markets.

Why Cleaning Is the Most Important STR Variable in Myrtle Beach

Airbnb's algorithm rewards cleanliness scores above almost every other metric. Properties above 4.8 overall get meaningfully better search placement than properties below that threshold. And cleanliness is the single most-mentioned category in reviews, positive and negative — guests notice when a property is dirty before they notice almost anything else.

In Myrtle Beach specifically, the beach environment adds a layer of cleaning complexity that standard residential cleaners and inland STR operators haven't encountered: sand. Guests track beach sand into every room, every piece of furniture, every drawer and cabinet. A standard cleaning team without a beach-specific protocol leaves properties that smell clean but grit underfoot — and that generates "needs better cleaning" reviews that suppress your ranking for weeks.

The Myrtle Beach STR Cleaning Standard — What Every Turnover Includes

🏖️ Beach Sand Protocol (Every Turnover)

  • Entry mat and shoe area thoroughly cleared and vacuumed before anything else
  • All cushions removed from sofas, chairs, and outdoor furniture — vacuumed on all sides
  • Hard floors vacuumed before mopping — sand on wet floors scratches surfaces and creates residue
  • All drawers and storage areas checked for sand accumulation
  • All beach gear storage areas cleared and swept
  • Shower and bathroom drains checked and cleared of sand
  • Washer and dryer lint trap cleared — beach towels deposit sand in laundry systems

🧹 Standard Turnover — Every Room

  • All surfaces wiped — counters, tables, shelves, window sills, ceiling fan blades
  • All mirrors and glass surfaces streak-free
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout
  • All trash emptied and bins relined
  • All toiletries restocked to minimums (TP, paper towels, soap)
  • All dishes washed, dried, and returned to correct cabinets
  • Appliances wiped inside and out — microwave, oven, refrigerator
  • Beds made with fresh linens — hospital corners, pillow cases straight
  • Staging reset to listing photos — décor items, throw pillows, furniture arrangement
  • Final walk-through with photographic damage report before leaving

📸 Damage Reporting (Every Turnover)

  • Walk-through with dated photos of any new damage, staining, or missing items
  • Report sent to property owner before next guest check-in
  • Any safety or maintenance issues flagged immediately — not just at end of report

How to Work With an STR Cleaning Service in Myrtle Beach

Calendar Sync

Any professional STR cleaning service in Myrtle Beach should be able to accept access to your rental calendar via iCal link. This allows cleaning to be automatically dispatched on every checkout day without coordination from you — the system triggers the cleaning based on the calendar, not a phone call or text.

Same-Day Turnovers

Back-to-back same-day turnovers — checkout at 10 AM, new guest at 4 PM — are the standard operating model in Myrtle Beach's peak summer season. A cleaning service that can't reliably execute same-day turnovers is not actually equipped for the Myrtle Beach STR market. Confirm this capability before signing on.

Emergency Response

Guests check out early. Storms bring unexpected guests back. Previous guests leave properties in unusable condition. A professional STR cleaning service in Myrtle Beach needs to be reachable 24 hours a day and able to respond to emergency calls within a reasonable window.
