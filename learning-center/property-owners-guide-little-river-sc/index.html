🌅 Property Owner's Guide to Little River, SC — 2026

Little River sits at the northern tip of Horry County, bordered by North Carolina and the Intracoastal Waterway. Known for its working waterfront, seafood restaurants, casino boats, and waterway-adjacent residential communities, Little River offers a quieter, more authentic coastal SC experience than the Grand Strand's busier corridors — while sharing the same maintenance challenges that come with living adjacent to water in a subtropical climate.

About Little River

Little River's residential communities line the Intracoastal Waterway corridor, with a mix of established homes, newer construction, and waterfront properties at various price points. The working waterfront along the marina and restaurant district creates a distinct community character. As growth continues northward from North Myrtle Beach, Little River is seeing active new construction throughout its expansion corridors.

Property Maintenance in Little River — What's Different Here

Little River's Intracoastal Waterway adjacency creates elevated humidity conditions for all waterway-adjacent properties. The ICW's tidal influence means moisture levels near the water accelerate biological growth on exterior surfaces — pressure washing and exterior maintenance cycles are more frequent for waterway properties than for equivalent properties further inland.

Little River's position at the northern county border means slightly different tree species and canopy density from the pine-heavy south county communities. Gutter demand is significant for established properties under mature canopy. New construction demand for sod installation and landscaping is active throughout the growth corridors.
