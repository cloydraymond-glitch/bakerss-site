How Much Does Pressure Washing Cost in Myrtle Beach, SC?

Pressure washing prices in Myrtle Beach vary more than most homeowners expect — and national pricing guides consistently get it wrong for coastal South Carolina. Here's what professional pressure washing actually costs in Horry County, broken down by surface type and property size.

What Affects Pressure Washing Prices in Myrtle Beach?

Pressure washing pricing in Horry County varies based on several factors that any reputable company will assess before quoting:

  • Surface type — concrete driveways, vinyl siding, wood decks, and roofs all require different equipment, pressure settings, and cleaning solutions — and are priced accordingly.
  • Square footage — a small single-car driveway costs less than a large commercial parking area. A one-story ranch costs less to soft-wash than a two-story home.
  • Condition — heavily stained or algae-covered surfaces take more time and chemical treatment than lightly soiled ones.
  • Access difficulty — multi-story homes, steep roofs, and tight access areas add time and require additional equipment.
  • Number of services — bundling driveway cleaning with a house wash typically costs less per service than scheduling them separately.

Why Coastal SC Properties Cost More to Pressure Wash

Myrtle Beach and Horry County properties accumulate salt, algae, mildew, and pollen at a much faster rate than inland regions. The combination of salt air from the ocean, coastal humidity, and heavy pine pollen seasons means surfaces here need more chemical treatment and more thorough cleaning than a similar property in, say, Charlotte or Atlanta.

This is why national average pricing guides consistently underestimate what pressure washing actually costs in coastal SC — the work simply takes longer and requires more product.

Soft Wash vs. High-Pressure — Does It Affect Price?

Soft washing uses low pressure with a cleaning solution and is used on roofs, siding, and delicate surfaces. High-pressure washing is used on concrete, pavers, and masonry. Soft washing typically costs slightly more per square foot than high-pressure work because the cleaning solution has a higher material cost — but it's the right technique for protecting your home's surfaces from damage.

How Often Should You Pressure Wash in Myrtle Beach?

Most Horry County homeowners benefit from an annual house wash and driveway cleaning. After storm season — especially following any tropical weather event — an additional post-storm cleaning is worth scheduling to remove salt, algae, and storm staining before it sets in. Vacation rental properties often benefit from more frequent exterior cleaning to maintain guest-ready appearances.
