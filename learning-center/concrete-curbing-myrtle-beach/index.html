Concrete Landscape Curbing in Myrtle Beach — Is It Worth It?

If you've noticed the clean, continuous concrete borders framing landscape beds on well-maintained properties around Horry County, that's concrete landscape curbing — and it's one of the most practical landscaping upgrades a Myrtle Beach homeowner can make.

What Is Concrete Landscape Curbing?

Concrete landscape curbing is a continuous, poured-in-place concrete border installed between your lawn and your landscape beds. Unlike plastic edging that shifts, fades, and breaks down in coastal heat and UV, concrete curbing is permanent. It's installed by a machine that extrudes a continuous ribbon of concrete in virtually any pattern directly on your property.

The Benefits for Coastal SC Properties

  • Permanent edge definition: Never needs to be reset, replaced, or pushed back into place after mowing
  • Weed barrier: A clean concrete edge stops grass runners from invading beds — the primary cause of bed maintenance headaches
  • Reduced edging labor: Weekly mowing services can run the mower wheel along the curb instead of hand-edging — saves time and keeps the line consistent
  • Mulch containment: Curbing keeps mulch in beds during heavy rainfall — a significant problem in coastal SC storm events
  • Curb appeal: Clean, defined beds immediately elevate the overall appearance of any property
  • Durability: Properly installed concrete curbing can last 20+ years in coastal SC conditions
Vacation Rental Value

Concrete curbing is especially valuable for vacation rental properties in Myrtle Beach — it makes the landscaping look consistently sharp with less ongoing maintenance labor, which translates directly to lower recurring costs over time.

What Does Concrete Curbing Cost in Myrtle Beach?

Pricing depends on the total linear footage, pattern complexity, and color options. Most residential projects in Horry County range from a few hundred to a few thousand dollars depending on scope. Bakerss provides free on-site estimates — we measure your beds and give you an exact quote with no obligation.

What Patterns Are Available?

The most common patterns for Horry County residential properties include single-slope (clean and modern), mower edge (functional and low-profile), and scallop (decorative, good for formal garden beds). We can also match stamped patterns and multiple color options.
