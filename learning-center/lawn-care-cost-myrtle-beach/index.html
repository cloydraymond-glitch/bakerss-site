How Much Does Lawn Care Cost in Myrtle Beach, SC?

If you're a Myrtle Beach homeowner or vacation rental owner wondering what professional lawn care should cost in Horry County, this guide gives you real local pricing — not national averages that don't apply to coastal South Carolina.

What Affects Lawn Care Pricing in Myrtle Beach?

Lawn care pricing in Myrtle Beach and Horry County is driven by a handful of factors that most companies consider before giving a quote. Understanding them helps you know what to expect and how to compare quotes fairly.

  • Property size — the biggest single factor. A small city lot takes 20 minutes. A large half-acre property takes 90 minutes or more.
  • Service frequency — weekly service is typically priced lower per visit than one-time or bi-weekly cuts because it's easier to maintain a consistently mowed lawn than one that's overgrown.
  • Scope of services — basic mowing and edging costs less than mowing plus trimming, blowing, and bed cleanup.
  • Property condition — a lawn that hasn't been cut in six weeks takes significantly more time and equipment wear than one on a regular schedule.
  • Add-on services — mulching, aeration, overseeding, and concrete curbing are priced separately from basic maintenance.

Weekly vs. Bi-Weekly — Which Is Better for Myrtle Beach Lawns?

In Myrtle Beach and coastal Horry County, weekly mowing is strongly recommended from April through September. Warm-season grasses like Bermuda, Zoysia, and St. Augustine grow aggressively in the coastal heat and humidity — lawns can go from neat to shaggy in under a week during peak season.

Bi-weekly service is appropriate in the cooler months (October through March) when growth slows. Many homeowners switch to bi-weekly in fall and return to weekly in spring.

Recurring vs. One-Time Lawn Service Pricing

One-time lawn cuts are typically priced higher per visit than recurring service for two reasons: there's no guarantee of ongoing work, and a lawn that hasn't been on a regular schedule is usually in worse shape and takes more time to bring up to standard. If you're looking for the best value, a recurring weekly or bi-weekly plan is almost always cheaper per visit than calling for individual cuts.

Vacation Rental Lawn Care Pricing

Vacation rental owners in Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, and Garden City typically need lawn service timed to their check-in schedule rather than a fixed day of the week. This flexibility has a small premium over standard residential service, but the convenience of not managing it yourself — especially for out-of-state owners — is well worth it.
