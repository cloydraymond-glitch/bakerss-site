Lawn Care for Airbnb & Vacation Rentals in Horry County, SC

Managing lawn care for a vacation rental in Myrtle Beach or Horry County is fundamentally different from standard residential lawn maintenance — and most lawn care companies aren't set up to handle the difference.

Here's what makes vacation rental lawn care unique, and what a good system looks like for Horry County property owners.

The Vacation Rental Lawn Care Problem

Standard residential lawn care operates on a simple weekly or bi-weekly schedule. Vacation rental lawn care has to work around something completely different: your booking calendar.

A lawn that's mowed on a Tuesday when checkout was Sunday and the next guest arrives Thursday looks great. A lawn that was supposed to be mowed on Tuesday but it rained, and now the next guest arrives Saturday to find a shaggy yard and wet clippings on the walkway — that's a problem. One that shows up in reviews.

The Key Difference

For vacation rentals, lawn appearance at the moment of guest arrival matters more than strict scheduling. Your lawn care team needs to be flexible enough to work around your check-in dates, not just their own route schedule.

What Vacation Rental Owners Actually Need

  • Flexible scheduling: Service timed to check-in dates, not fixed days of the week
  • Reliable communication: Confirmation when service is complete — especially for out-of-state owners who can't drive by to check
  • Consistent quality: The same standard every visit — not great one week and rushed the next
  • Fast response: If a storm knocks down branches or creates a mess before a guest arrival, you need someone who can respond quickly
  • Full-service capability: Mowing, edging, blowing clippings off all hardscapes, mulch refresh as needed, and shrub trimming on a seasonal schedule

What Your Lawn Should Look Like at Check-In

  • Grass mowed at correct height — not scalped, not overgrown
  • Clean, crisp edges along all walkways, driveways, and beds
  • All clippings blown off walkways, driveways, and patios
  • No visible debris, fallen branches, or storm damage
  • Mulch beds clean and weed-free
  • Shrubs trimmed and shaped

Coordinating Lawn Care with Cleaning Turnovers

The most seamless setup for vacation rental owners is having the same company handle both lawn care and interior cleaning. When both services are coordinated, the exterior and interior are both ready by the time guests arrive — and you're making one call instead of two when something needs attention.
