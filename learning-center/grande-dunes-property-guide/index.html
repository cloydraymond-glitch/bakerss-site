⛵ Property Owner's Guide to Grande Dunes — Myrtle Beach's Premier Community

Grande Dunes is Myrtle Beach's premier address — a 2,200-acre master-planned community stretching from the Intracoastal Waterway to the Atlantic Ocean, with two championship golf courses, a private marina, an oceanfront members club, and some of the highest residential property values in all of Horry County. For property owners in Grande Dunes, the community's premium positioning creates both elevated maintenance expectations and a straightforward calculus: the properties that are maintained at the community's standard hold their value and command premium pricing; those that fall short stand out conspicuously in a neighborhood where the competition is consistently high.

Grande Dunes Communities

Grande Dunes encompasses multiple distinct residential communities within its master plan — waterway-adjacent streets with ICW views and private dock access, golf course-adjacent lots with fairway views, interior residential streets, and oceanfront addresses along the resort corridor. Each location within Grande Dunes has slightly different maintenance pressures — ICW-adjacent properties face higher humidity, oceanfront properties face maximum salt air, and golf course-adjacent lots face the visual benchmark of the manicured course itself.

Maintenance in Grande Dunes — The Premium Standard

Grande Dunes HOA enforcement is active and specific. Lawn appearance — height, edge condition, bed maintenance — is the most actively monitored category. Properties with inconsistent lawn care become visible outliers in a community where the neighboring lots are maintained to a consistently high standard. Weekly professional lawn care during the growing season is the baseline expectation, not a premium choice.

Exterior appearance maintenance in Grande Dunes includes pressure washing on a regular schedule, consistent paint condition, and the kind of detail-oriented upkeep that reflects the property values the community commands. A home with algae-stained siding, a cracked driveway with biological growth, or peeling paint on the trim stands out in Grande Dunes in a way it wouldn't in a less premium community.

Epoxy garage floors are one of the most consistent service requests from Grande Dunes homeowners — the larger garage footprints (2 and 3-car are standard) and the community's interior aesthetic standards make a finished epoxy floor a natural upgrade. The 3D metallic and marble flake product lines are particularly popular in Grande Dunes for their high-end visual result.

Pool maintenance is consistently in demand throughout Grande Dunes — the warm season length, active occupancy, and the guest-facing nature of pools in a community where properties are frequently shown for sale or rental makes professional weekly pool service the standard rather than the exception.

How often should Grande Dunes homes be pressure washed?

Annual exterior washing is the minimum for Grande Dunes properties not directly adjacent to the ICW or ocean. ICW-adjacent and oceanfront properties benefit from twice-yearly washing given the elevated humidity and salt exposure those locations experience.
