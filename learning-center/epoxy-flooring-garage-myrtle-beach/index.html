Epoxy Garage Floors in Myrtle Beach, SC — What to Know Before You Start

A professionally coated epoxy garage floor is one of the highest-ROI upgrades available to Horry County homeowners — but coastal South Carolina's humidity creates specific considerations that make the installation process different from what you'd find in drier climates.

Why Epoxy Garage Floors Work So Well in Coastal SC

Myrtle Beach garages deal with sand, salt, humidity, and the constant tracking of beach and outdoor debris. A bare concrete floor in this environment is porous, stains easily, and becomes increasingly difficult to clean over time. Epoxy seals the concrete completely, creating a non-porous, chemical-resistant surface that wipes clean and doesn't absorb moisture, oil, or beach debris.

The Coastal SC Humidity Challenge

Here's the part most online guides don't cover: epoxy has a very specific humidity tolerance during application. If you try to apply epoxy coating during high humidity — common in Myrtle Beach from May through September — the moisture compromises adhesion and causes the coating to peel or bubble within months.

Important

Professional epoxy installation requires concrete moisture testing before application. In coastal SC, this step is non-negotiable. We test every floor before we start — if conditions aren't right, we schedule when they are rather than rushing a job that will fail.

The Epoxy Installation Process

  • Surface prep: Diamond grinding the entire floor surface to open the concrete pores and create mechanical adhesion — this is the most important step
  • Crack and joint repair: Filling any cracks or expansion joints before coating
  • Moisture testing: Confirming the slab is within acceptable moisture parameters
  • Base coat: 100% solids epoxy base coat applied evenly across the entire floor
  • Decorative flake broadcast: Vinyl flake chips broadcast into the wet base coat — covers imperfections and creates texture
  • Topcoat sealer: Clear polyurethane or polyaspartic topcoat for UV stability, chemical resistance, and shine

How Long Does Epoxy Flooring Last in Myrtle Beach?

A professionally installed epoxy floor with a quality topcoat can last 15–20 years in a residential garage with normal use. The topcoat is the critical layer for longevity — it protects the epoxy beneath from UV yellowing (a real concern in coastal SC) and daily abrasion.
