Conway Is Different

Airbnb Cleaning in Conway Requires Local Knowledge

Conway is not a beach town. It's Horry County's oldest city — founded in 1732 along the Waccamaw River — and the seat of county government. Guests who book Conway rentals are specifically choosing a slower-paced, historically rich rivertown experience over the tourist bustle of the Grand Strand. That distinction shapes everything about how rental properties here are used and what cleaning them requires.

The Conway Riverwalk — recently expanded in February 2025 to connect the waterfront to Main Street for the first time in the city's history — draws guests who explore the Waccamaw River cypress forests by kayak, dine at waterfront restaurants, and walk tree-lined historic streets. Coastal Carolina University brings an entirely different rental demographic: parents staying for graduation, families visiting students, and attendees of university events throughout the year.

Properties here are predominantly historic homes, craftsman bungalows, riverfront cottages, and residential properties near downtown — not beach condos. Cleaning them well means understanding wood floors, older surfaces, outdoor spaces for river recreation, and the specific expectations of a guest who paid a premium for a quiet, characterful stay rather than a beach party.
